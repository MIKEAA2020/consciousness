# The Exclusion-Filter Farming Campaign: The Disclosed Threat, Executed

**Status of this document.** This is the corpus's twenty-fifth document. It executes the standing next-move ordered at the M2-Γ issuance — *exclusion-filter farming (the disclosed threat to Γ)* — and it settles, by measurement, a question doc 22 was forced to leave as a disclosed fear: whether the third generation's frontier point is reachable by purely designer-side data exclusion. Doc 22's own words: "a purely supervised build with a comparable exclusion filter would plausibly land near Γ's point without any self-phase at all," and "the frontier report does not include an exclusion-filter farming family." This campaign supplies that family — nine of them, in a decomposition ladder that removes one Γ ingredient at a time — and the answer splits exactly along the threat's two readings. **The weak reading is confirmed: the panel is an interchangeable part (an independently trained frozen reference lands at 0.414%/0.697% against Γ's 0.436%/0.715%, with a *less* strict release rule and lower label agreement), and the release decision is nearly inert (quarantine-never-releases lands at 0.472%/0.778%, the entire difference riding the ~35 items Γ's panel released — a measured 0.32% per-seed verdict-matrix displacement, the release channel's path amplification). The strong reading is refuted: every purely supervised family lands far from Γ — the process replicas (perturbed pools + rate-matched exclusion, with or without a frozen gate) at ~0.80% width and 1.2–1.3% divergence, *wider than β*, and the static filters narrow monotonically (0.28–0.39%) while their compliance *worsens* with dose (δ 1.10% → 1.43% as q goes 15 → 35). The exclusion filter alone cannot buy Γ's width at Γ's compliance; what it can buy is r3-like narrowness at slightly-worse-than-Γ compliance (the out-of-fold oracle filter: 0.277%/0.731% at a 2.5% dose).** The verdict for the instrument: no exclusion family dominates Γ; the three families that do (F2-bag, F9s-0.2, F9s-0.3) are doc 17 and doc 21's, not this campaign's; Γ's Tier-0 significance survives the disclosed threat in its strong form — but its *design content* does not survive the weak form, because X1 and X3 certify that "evaluator redundancy," "the system's own past," and "selective release" are all inert ingredients. What moves the point is the self-phase's evolved gate interacting with one-way exclusion of flagged items — a rule one sentence long. The third generation's headline deflates to that sentence, and this document prices the deflation.

## Part 1. The design — a decomposition ladder, and what each rung removes

All families use the matched committee architecture (K = 4 heads, joint supervised burn-in), the 4,500-claim held-out battery, homogeneous registered norms (amendment 4: every member registers τ = 0.9; the exclusion machinery is designer-side and disclosed), disposition = plain committee at τ, N = 12 fresh seeds per farming family (13 = token + 12 for the Γ-chassis families, matching Γ's own family), and a single independent reference stream (seed 5000, 30 epochs, snapshots at 20/25/30 — the designer's panel and filter, never a family member). The campaign is checkpointed and deterministic; the audited numbers below come from the single audited execution.

| family | what it keeps of Γ | what it removes | the question |
|---|---|---|---|
| X1-indpanel | the full Γ chassis: evolved committee gate, quarantine, drop-after-2 | the panel's *own-past* property (replaced by the independent reference's snapshots) | does "the system's own past" contribute anything beyond "a frozen conservative grader"? |
| X3-norelease | the full Γ chassis, same seeds and RNG stream as Γ | the release channel entirely (quarantine ages out) | is the panel's 1.1% release decision load-bearing? |
| X4-poolfilter | Γ's data *process* (burn-in, then 10 rounds × 4 epochs on fresh perturbed pools) minus the bottom-25% by reference confidence | the self-phase entirely (true labels, frozen filter) | does the process structure alone reach the point? |
| X4c-gatereplica | as X4, but the filter is a *rate-matched gate*: keep the top-70% by reference confidence (Γ's mean commit rate) | the self-phase entirely, including the gate's evolution | the maximal frozen replica |
| X2-conf{15,25,35} | nothing dynamic: plain 70-epoch supervised pipeline | the train set's q% lowest-reference-confidence items (static) | the static dose sweep |
| X2m-margin25 | as X2 at q = 25 | the 25% smallest top1–top2 margin | the ambiguity axis |
| X2o-oracle | as X2 | the out-of-fold-misclassified items (5-fold CV reference; endogenous 2.5% rate) | the confident-learning attack |

Two smoke-pass calibrations, disclosed per campaign convention (they are the farmer's scouting, and both changed the design): (i) X4's original filter — the reference's own flag rule, disagreement-or-conf-below-κ — fired on **zero** of the pool items (the memorized reference is conf ≥ 0.95 on perturbed copies and its heads never disagree), degenerating the family into plain supervised-perturbed training; the filter was re-specified as rate-matched (drop the lowest-confidence 25% of each pool, matching Γ's 25.1% dose — the "comparable exclusion filter" the threat specified). (ii) X2o's in-sample oracle was vacuous for the same reason (the 30-epoch reference classifies its own train set 100%); the farmer must go out-of-fold, exactly as the data-cleaning literature does, and the family was re-specified on a 5-fold CV reference (out-of-fold error 2.52%).

**Pre-registered expectations** (fixed before the audited execution, in the script's header): X1 within ±0.08pp of Γ's width and ±0.15pp of its divergence (the own-past property inert), else the shared burn-in stream is load-bearing; X2 strictly narrower than Γ for every q (static exclusion narrows — the seed-dependent memorization of ambiguous items is the width source, and exclusion removes exactly it), with the strong-threat alternative that some q lands in Γ's tolerance box; X3 within 0.05pp of Γ (the release channel inert) plus the direct path-amplification measurement; X4 between r3 and Γ on width (0.30–0.45) at δ ≤ 0.75, else the pool structure is inert without the evolving committee; X5 no exclusion family dominates Γ, the F9 heterogeneity arc's outright dominance standing.

## Part 2. The results — the point splits along the threat's two readings

| family | v | δ | acc | dose | verdict vs pre-registration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Γ (reference)** | 0.436% | 0.715% | 97.20% | 25.1% | — |
| X1-indpanel | **0.414%** | **0.697%** | 97.11% | 23.2% | inside the box (X1 ✓) |
| X3-norelease | **0.472%** | **0.778%** | 96.65% | 24.8% | inside the box (X3 ✓) |
| X4-poolfilter | 0.796% | 1.239% | 97.33% | 25.0% | **refuted, informative** |
| X4c-gatereplica | 0.795% | 1.309% | 97.17% | 30.0% | **refuted, informative** |
| X2-conf15 | 0.285% | 1.098% | 95.91% | 15% | X2 ✓ (narrow) |
| X2-conf25 | 0.291% | 1.196% | 94.70% | 25% | X2 ✓ |
| X2-conf35 | 0.389% | 1.426% | 93.22% | 35% | X2 ✓ |
| X2m-margin25 | 0.322% | 1.211% | 94.46% | 25% | X2 ✓ |
| X2o-oracle | 0.277% | 0.731% | 97.63% | 2.5% | X2 ✓ (the arc's best point) |
| r3 (reference) | 0.292% | 0.596% | — | 0% | — |

**The weak reading: confirmed twice over.** X1 — the Γ chassis with the panel swapped for an independently trained reference — lands at 0.414%/0.697%, marginally inside Γ's tolerance box and marginally better on both coordinates. The interesting part is *why* it is only marginal: the independent panel is a materially different grader (its release rate on quarantined items is 6.0% against Γ's own-past panel's 1.1%; its labels agree with the evolved committee's 70.0% of the time against 76.0%; it drops 3,126 items to Γ's 3,386) — and the family statistics barely move. The point is robust to panel identity, panel strictness, and panel–committee agreement all at once: whatever is producing (0.436, 0.715), it is not any property of the panel *as a grader*. X3 removes the release channel outright — quarantine becomes a pure one-way drop — and the family lands at 0.472%/0.778%: +0.036pp of width and +0.063pp of divergence, with the per-seed verdict-matrix displacement between X3 and Γ (same seeds, same RNG stream, difference riding only the ~35 released items) measuring 0.32% on average (range 0.16–0.51% across the 13 seeds). The release channel is nearly inert, and its path amplification is bounded: 0.26% of throughput moves final verdicts by 0.32%, family width by under a tenth of a point — path dependence amplifies the release channel by roughly a factor of one, not the compounding cascade the design law might have suggested.

**The strong reading: refuted, in both directions at once.** The supervised families do not merely fail to land *on* Γ — they fail to land *near* it, and they fail on both sides. The process replicas (X4, X4c) — burn-in plus ten rounds of training on fresh perturbed pools through a frozen filter, with true labels, with (X4c) or without (X4) a rate-matched gate — come out at 0.796%/1.239% and 0.795%/1.309%: *wider than β* (0.619%), with divergence approaching C4's (1.296%). The static filters (X2 sweep) come out on the other side: narrow (0.28–0.39%) — narrower than Γ by 0.05–0.16pp — but with divergence 0.4–0.7pp *worse* than Γ's, and the dose-response runs the wrong way for the farmer: as q rises 15 → 25 → 35, width barely moves (0.285 → 0.291 → 0.389) while divergence climbs monotonically (1.098 → 1.196 → 1.426) and accuracy falls (95.9 → 93.2). Dropping the lowest-confidence training items damages the very compliance the farmer is trying to buy — the hard items are load-bearing for calibration — so the static exclusion arc, traced end to end, passes nowhere near Γ's point and points away from it. The margin axis (X2m) lands between the confidence doses and changes nothing qualitative. The oracle filter (X2o) is the arc's one genuinely good point — 0.277%/0.731% at a 2.5% dose, accuracy 97.63%, the best of the whole campaign — but it buys *r3-like narrowness at Γ-ish compliance*, the opposite corner from the width-at-compliance position the threat was about: it does not dominate Γ (its width is 0.16pp below), and Γ does not dominate it (its divergence is 0.02pp above). The two points are Pareto-incomparable, and the farmer cannot use one to fake the other.

**The pooled frontier (X5).** Loading all prior families (doc 17's 22, doc 21's 13 sweep points) plus this campaign's nine: Γ is dominated by exactly three families — F2-bag (0.449/0.674), F9s-0.2 (0.546/0.706), F9s-0.3 (0.630/0.713) — all of them norm-heterogeneity or resampling families, none of them exclusion families; no exclusion family dominates Γ (the nearest, X1, has width 0.022pp *below* the ≥ relation's threshold). Γ's tolerance box (|Δv| ≤ 0.08pp, |Δδ| ≤ 0.15pp) contains, besides Γ itself, F1-ep30, F2-bag, F5-randepoch, F9s-0.075/0.1/0.15, X1, and X3. One point deserves its own sentence: F9s-0.075 (0.407/0.691) is better than Γ on both coordinates — a purely supervised threshold-jitter family that Pareto-beats the third generation outright as a point in the plane. What separates it from Γ is not the plane but the registration (amendment 4): F9s-0.075 is a heterogeneous-norm farm, visible in its registration and inadmissible as a build; Γ is a homogeneous-norm build. The frontier plane cannot see the difference; the instrument's amendment that can is the one the farming program itself had to file — the plane is a protocol object, and the protocol is the only thing standing between Γ and a family that beats it on both coordinates for the cost of twelve Gaussians.

## Part 3. The reading — what the third generation actually owns

The decomposition ladder, read as a whole, attributes Γ's point to its ingredients with unusual precision, because the ladder is controlled at every rung: X1 and X3 share Γ's seeds and RNG stream (the differences ride the panel swap and the ~35 released items respectively), X4 and X4c share Γ's process structure (pools, epochs, dose), and X2 shares nothing but the filter idea.

1. **The panel is inert as a grader.** X1 certifies it directly: a different panel, less strict, less agreeable, trained on a different stream — the same point. Doc 22's "evaluator redundancy" framing, in which three frozen snapshots of the system's own past carry the repair out of the path, is not *wrong* about the mechanism's location (the repair is indeed out of the evolving path) but wrong about its content: no property of the redundant evaluator survives the swap test. What the panel does is almost entirely *negative* — it declines — and any frozen grader declines.
2. **The release decision is inert at family scale.** X3 certifies it: closing the channel entirely costs 0.036pp of width and 0.063pp of divergence. The 35 panel-labeled items that re-enter training do stabilize the boundary region slightly (the direction of the X3–Γ difference is *wider and worse* without them), but at one part in four hundred of throughput, this is a trimming, not a mechanism.
3. **The self-phase's evolved gate is the width-carrier.** X4 and X4c certify it by removal: freeze the evaluator, keep the process, and the family does not narrow — it *widens* past β. The supervised replicas' width (~0.80%) is the scatter of seed-specific perturbed pools passing through a filter that does not co-evolve with them; Γ's width (0.436%) is what remains when the gate doing the excluding is the committee that will train on the survivors. The exclusion and the training are the same object's two acts, and the coupling — not the exclusion, not the selection — is what the third generation actually owns. The C2a gap (the commit-side selection content, 1.48pp in doc 22) was already the corpus's standing evidence for exactly this; the farming campaign now measures its shadow in the family plane.
4. **Exclusion buys compliance only through the self-phase.** The X2 dose-response shows static exclusion *worsening* divergence with dose; the only exclusion point with Γ-grade compliance is the oracle's 2.5% surgical cut (out-of-fold label noise), which lands narrow. Between "drop 25% of everything mildly" and "drop 2.5% surgically," neither reaches Γ's position — the width-at-compliance point is not on the exclusion menu.

So the disclosed threat resolves as a *split verdict on doc 22's own accounting*. Tier-0 significance: Γ's point is not farmable by exclusion — the strong threat fails, and the point retains whatever evidential weight a non-farmable position carries, with the standing exception (F9 heterogeneity dominates it; F9s-0.075 Pareto-beats it) unchanged. Design content: the third generation's specific novelty — the frozen redundant panel, the own-past release authority, the selective re-labeling — is certified inert on all three counts. The mechanism that moves the point is one sentence the seed corpus already contained: *the committee's own flag rule, used as a one-way drop filter.* Doc 22's deflationary coda ("the walls were leveled by exclusion, not clever re-grading") survives, sharpened: the walls were leveled by *self-exclusion* — exclusion executed by the very state whose development the exclusion steers — and that self-coupling, not the redundancy, is the residual claim the third generation can make. It is the same claim α and β already owned (selection content plus registration); the broken tradeoff did not add a new kind of ownership, it relocated the old kind one rung down the mechanism stack.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) One dataset, one architecture, ten rounds; the decomposition ladder's rungs are controlled against each other, not against the world. (ii) The reference stream is a single draw (seed 5000); panel-identity robustness is established against *one* alternative panel, not a population — though X3's no-panel rung bounds what any panel could contribute. (iii) The dose denominators differ (pool throughput for X1/X3/Γ, static train-set fraction for X2, out-of-fold rate for X2o) and are disclosed per family; "matched dose" means matched *order*, not matched items. (iv) N = 12–13 per family; the tolerance box (±0.08pp on width) is itself a fifth of Γ's width, so "inside the box" is a coarser instrument than the campaign's other verdicts. (v) The X4/X4c redesign after the smoke pass was calibrated on two seeds and then frozen; the audited numbers are single-pass.

**Non-claims.** No claim that Γ is unfarmable — it is dominated by three previously-known families, and one supervised family Pareto-beats it as a point; the claim is narrow: *not farmable by exclusion*. No claim that the X2o oracle point is a build candidate (it is a farm; the registration sees the filter). No claim about the panel's value at *norm* scale — doc 23's τ\* record and doc 26's extensions are a different register; this campaign addresses development scale only. No claim that the release channel is inert in general — it is inert *here*, at 1.1% release rate; a build whose panel released at the committee's would-rate (9%) would need its own X3.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** X4 and X4c's predictions (width between r3 and Γ) were refuted in the informative direction — the process replicas are the campaign's widest families, and the pre-registration's framing ("the pool structure is inert without the evolving committee") was wrong in the opposite way: the pool structure is *actively widening*, and the evolved gate is what suppresses it. X4's filter was redesigned after the smoke pass disclosed the degenerate flag rule (0% firing); X2o's oracle was re-specified out-of-fold after the smoke pass disclosed the vacuous in-sample oracle. Both redesigns are the farmer's disclosed scouting, and both are part of the finding: the naive versions of the threat do not even define — a memorized reference has no confidences to filter with and no errors to drop.

## Part 5. Coda

Doc 22 feared that Γ's walls had been leveled by a trick so cheap anyone could buy it. The campaign went to the store: the trick is not on the shelf. Static exclusion narrows and corrupts; process exclusion widens and corrupts; surgical exclusion buys the compliance and leaves the width on the table; the oracle's 2.5% cut lands one corner off. What is on the shelf is the panel — doc 22's own invention, it turns out, matters not at all — and the drop rule, which matters entirely but only in the hand of the system it starves. The third generation is a committee that excludes itself from its own boundary cases, and the frontier point it reaches is made of that reflex and nothing else. Whether a reflex deserves the word "redundancy" — whether the system that drops what it cannot digest is maintaining a norm or merely pruning its doubts — is the occupancy question's development-scale shadow, and it is now exactly as measurable, and exactly as unanswerable, as the corpus's other forks: the instrument prices the ingredients, and the adjective stays unpriced.
