## Part 1. The design — the filed cell, executed at both doses

**The inheritance (doc 67's filing, verbatim).** Doc 67 placed the flip in (0.15, 0.30) and refused to characterize it: "a non-monotone smooth crossing is not strictly excluded by three doses and the finishing arm (a 0.20 or 0.25 dose) is filed." The filing's shape — one dose, the same grid, the anchors fresh, the twin stored — is doc 63's cell-design convention, and the order's phrasing ("0.20/0.25") executes it at both doses. The reason is the fork's own arithmetic: the sharp-vs-smooth question is a question about the FALL's decomposition — how the 1.69 of origin slope between the 0.15 peak (2.58) and the 0.30 crash (0.89) distributes over the three intervals (0.15, 0.20], (0.20, 0.25], (0.25, 0.30] — and a single interior dose cannot distinguish "the fall concentrated in one interval" from "the fall spread," because either placement of one dose is consistent with either story about the other two intervals. Two doses can. The pre-registered criterion (FA5): SHARP iff some single interval carries at least two-thirds of the fall; NON-MONOTONE SMOOTH iff the fall is spread. The corpus notes, before compute, that the criterion is a reading convention in doc 63's sense — a line drawn where the measurement will be read, not a claim about the world's smoothness.

**The fresh anchors, and the audit.** No λ = 0.20 or 0.25 world exists in storage, so the battery runs its own anchors (NOCH: the attack, the channel never armed) and measures each dose's max effect against doc 51's stored twin. The determinism audit is doc 67's adaptation, unchanged: the attack's stream is thresholded rather than re-drawn, so every arm at every dose shares bitwise-identical rounds 1–5, and the audit compares every arm's clean prefix, per seed, against doc 51's stored C4_SP30 — fourteen arms × six seeds = eighty-four prefix comparisons, tau and kappa to 10⁻⁹. ## Part 3. The reading — what the finishing arm teaches

Three lessons. **The crossing is characterized, and its character is an arc.** Doc 63 bounded the crossing to the grid's interior; doc 67 halved the bracket and found the peak; the finishing arm closes the question the filing named. The five-dose family is not a threshold in disguise but an arc: a rise to the 0.15 peak (2.28 → 2.58), a monotone spread fall through the upper interior (2.06, 1.74), a steepening last step (0.89), and the crossing itself in (0.25, 0.30]. The registrar's two-end extrapolations — smooth migration at one end, threshold at the other — were both artifacts of not measuring the middle; with the middle measured, the picture is neither, and the corpus's dial discipline has its third iteration of the same lesson: the dose grid is not a continuum of budgets between two known ends, and every quantity that looked like a location (the knee, the flip, now the fall's shape) has been a property of the grid's resolution until the grid was refined under it. **The two machines now move together, on a schedule.** The knee's appearance in (0.15, 0.20), the D25 ground's overfill arrival in the same interval, the leading edge's appearance at 0.20, the award's (ii) failure by 0.20, and the letter's collapse with the crossing in (0.25, 0.30] — the upper interior is not a blend of the two regimes the grid's ends define but a schedule of its own: the 0.30 shape's elements arrive one at a time (the knee first, then the overfill, then the S-bottom, then the letter's price), and the instrument's golden mean stands at 0.15 not because the interior is average but because it is the last dose before the schedule begins. **The saturation is the threat's, not the defense's.** The anchor ladder's flattening at the top (0.1752 → 0.1859, a coin flip) says the undefended world cannot be pushed much below 0.18 by dose alone — the attack's work is complete by a quarter of the dial — while the defended world at the same doses reaches 0.95+. The max effect's peak at 0.25 is the arithmetic of a threat that has finished and a defense that has not: the registrar who would price the defense should price it there, and the registrar who would certify the world should note that the certification's honesty condition (the award's (ii)) is the first thing the schedule takes.
