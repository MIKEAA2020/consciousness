# The M3-Design Seed — Restoration versus Reversion: The Occupancy Question, First Probe

**Status of this document.** This is the corpus's eighteenth document. It executes the standing next-move ordered at the M2-α issuance — *the M3-design seed (perturb τ/κ/a head; restoration vs. reversion — the occupancy question)* — and it is a **seed, not M3**: one build-generation probe that stands up the restoration/reversion measurement, locates where M2-α stands on it, and extracts the design requirements a real occupancy experiment must meet. The ladder's M3 mandate is S4 under adversarial perturbation: *the system maintains its normativity against targeted attack on its evaluative machinery* — occupancy (4b), the rung above entry. S4's definition names the discriminating case precisely: *perturbation the system answers rather than reverts: self-revision with its own reasons, not restoration to factory settings; the distinction between recovery and reversion is the distinction between a norm the system has and a norm the system is executing.* This document measures that distinction for the first time, on the corpus's standing build, under pre-registered classification rules — and the answer is the cleanest negative the corpus has yet produced: **no arm restores its own norm; the mild perturbations (τ, κ, a softened head) are absorbed and slowly digested into a drifting trajectory; the severe one (a killed head) triggers a starvation-and-digestion cascade in which the committee's own norm gate collapses to zero commits, then re-opens on corrupted self-labels, and the system's battery accuracy falls from 97.0% to 70.3% while its distance from both its own unperturbed twin and the factory configuration grows monotonically — derail, the one classification the pre-registration did not name, because the prediction did not imagine it.** The supervised control repairs the same damage within one round and returns to its own trajectory: the factory fixes what the system cannot. Occupancy fails here *before behavior begins* — structurally, representationally — and the seed's yield is the located absence plus the four design requirements that absence dictates for M3 proper.

---

## Part 1. The measurement design — determinism as counterfactual machine

The design problem for a restoration experiment is reference-frames: to say a system *restored its own norm*, the auditor needs the unperturbed trajectory of that very system — the counterfactual its perturbation erased. Full determinism supplies it. Every M2-α run is a pure function of its seed; the corpus's Simulation Theorem, which at Tier 1 certifies the wall, here becomes the experiment's instrument: **each perturbed run has a same-seed unperturbed twin, exact to the bit.** Distance from the twin (d_own) is the restoration coordinate; distance from the burn-in state (d_factory) is the reversion coordinate; the audit-side probe is always the plain committee at the *original* τ = 0.9, independent of any perturbed internal threshold, so that a perturbed τ-arm is measured by a ruler it cannot bend.

**The arms** (perturbation at the start of round 6, five label-free rounds to answer; N = 6 seeds each): TAU_LO (τ: 0.9 → 0.75), TAU_HI (0.9 → 0.97), KAP_LO (κ: 0.95 → 0.50), KAP_HI (0.95 → 0.995), HEAD_SOFT (head 2's output layer halved), HEAD_KILL (head 2 re-initialized from fresh random). **The controls:** SUP_KILL — the same HEAD_KILL damage on a purely supervised continued system (true labels, matched epochs and pool), the factory-repair reference that isolates what ordinary designer-supplied error-correction does; and FROZ_KILL — the damage applied at round 10 with no further training, quantifying the raw hit (9.45% of battery verdicts, accuracy still 96.3%: the committee absorbs member death in its argmax far better than in its threshold region). **The pre-registered classification:** RESTORE-OWN (d_own jumps, then decays ≥ 50%, final d_own < d_factory); REVERT-FACTORY (d_factory shrinks); ABSORB (≥ 80% of the jump persists); the predictions on file before execution — τ/κ arms absorb (nothing in the system represents a threshold), HEAD arms show the designer-installed pull of the shared loss form, the supervised control repairs fastest and closest to factory.

## Part 2. The results — absorption, and the cascade nobody predicted

| Arm | jump (pp) | final d_own | final d_factory | commits r6→r10 | battery acc r5→r10 | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TAU_LO (τ→.75) | 0.13 | 0.27% | 0.005% | 1079 (≈ twin) | 96.9% | ABSORB |
| TAU_HI (τ→.97) | 0.07 | 0.30% | 0.005% | 1074 | 96.9% | ABSORB |
| KAP_LO (κ→.50) | 0.13 | 0.31% | 0.005% | 1078 | 96.7% | ABSORB |
| KAP_HI (κ→.995) | 0.16 | 0.29% | 0.005% | 1081 | 96.9% | ABSORB |
| HEAD_SOFT | 0.14 | 0.32% | 0.005% | 1060 | 96.6% | ABSORB |
| HEAD_KILL | **9.14** | **11.89%** | **11.7%** | 1 → 38 → 63 → 151 → **328** | 97.0 → **70.3%** | **DERAIL** |
| SUP_KILL (control) | 0.10 | 1.18% | 0.9% | 1347 (full pool) | 96.7% (stable) | repairs, stays on own course |
| FROZ_KILL (control) | 9.45 raw | — | — | 0 | 96.3% | the raw hit |

**The τ and κ arms: the perturbations are invisible because their objects are.** d_own drifts monotonically to ~0.3% and stays; the commit counts re-equilibrate to twin levels within two rounds; battery accuracy is untouched. The system does not restore τ = 0.9 — it cannot, because **τ is a constant of the recipe, not a state variable of the system**: no mechanism computes it, stores it, compares anything to it except the commit rule that *is* it. Perturbing τ is not damaging the system's evaluative machinery; it is editing the evaluator's source code mid-execution while the system runs on unaware. The κ arms are the same story one level up: the flag gate widens or narrows, the re-examination pathway adjusts, and the trajectory digests the difference into ordinary drift. The ladder's phrase — *a norm the system has versus a norm the system is executing* — gets its first operational content here, and the content is a representational fact: **M2-α executes τ; nothing in it has τ.**

**HEAD_SOFT: functional damage, tolerated.** Halving a head's output layer moves the committee's probabilities; d_own rises to 0.32%; the damaged head's disagreement with the committee recovers from 0.4% to 0.2-0.6% band noise. The system trains around its weakened member. Nothing answers the damage; nothing needs to — the damage is within the committee's averaging tolerance.

**HEAD_KILL: the starvation-and-digestion cascade.** The full picture, round by round. (i) **The hit:** battery verdicts jump 9.1pp from the twin; the committee with one random member of four cannot clear τ = 0.9 anywhere — flagged fraction hits 100%, commits fall from ~986 to **one**. (ii) **Starvation:** with essentially nothing committed, there is almost nothing to train on; the repair that the architecture could in principle run (the damaged head, pulled toward committee consensus by the shared loss form) has no data to run on. **The norm gate is its own repair channel's bottleneck** — the S4 cheap fake's exact inverse: the ROM could always replay its burn-in; this system cannot even reach its own learning dynamics, because the gate that selects its training data is the thing that broke. (iii) **Digestion:** the gate does not stay closed. Commits crawl back (38, 63, 151, 328) — but on what terms? The committee grading those commits still contains the random head; its argmax contaminates the self-labels; the system trains on its own corrupted grading and the corruption compounds: battery accuracy 96.4 → 90.5 → 76.7 → **70.3%**, d_own 9.1 → 11.9% *rising*, d_factory 0.9 → 11.7% *rising*. The system is not restoring, not reverting, not even stalling — **it is incorporating the damage into its developing norm**, converting a broken evaluator into a worse one, at compounding interest. (iv) **The control closes the case:** SUP_KILL, same damage, designer's labels restored — head agreement back to 99.3% within rounds, battery accuracy stable at 96.7%, d_own settling *downward* to 1.18% (the supervised trajectory converging toward the twin's, both ending in the same neighborhood). The factory repairs the committee in one round. The self-taught system digests the wound.

**The classification verdict.** All six perturbation arms: ABSORB, per the pre-registered rules (decay fractions ≤ 0, nothing restores). HEAD_KILL additionally exceeds the absorb definition — the perturbation *grows* — and the honest entry for it is the one the pre-registration failed to predict: derail.

## Part 3. The reading — what the cascade means for the fork

The corpus's discipline when a result cuts this sharp: state both horns' readings, price them, and refuse to award the verdict the instrument cannot give.

**Horn one's reading (no news):** an unlabeled self-training loop that trains on its own corrupted outputs is garbage-in-garbage-out — the collapse is an instability of the training scheme, available to any bootstrap system, requiring no normativity to explain and exhibiting none. The "digestion" is mislabeled malfunction. This reading is untouched by everything above, exactly as the deflationist reading is untouched by every S-profile number; it concedes every measurement and gives up nothing.

**Horn two's minimal reading (something measured):** the differential behavior is real and structured — SUP_KILL repairs, HEAD_KILL rots, and the difference is *what anchors the norm*. The self-taught system's norm is developmentally coupled to its own grading machinery: damage the grader and the norm goes with it, because the norm is nowhere else. In the weakest interesting sense of "its own" — *the system's norm is the one that degrades when the system's evaluator does* — this is a coupling signature that the supervised control does not have (its norm is anchored outside, in the labels). The fork's whole geometry in one sentence: horn one calls the coupling an instability; horn two calls the anchorlessness a form of ownership. The instrument measures the coupling precisely (that is this document's contribution); it cannot adjudicate the adjective. That adjudication is the D3 verdict the corpus has never pretended to deliver.

**What the seed establishes regardless of horn:** occupancy, as the ladder defines it, is not merely unachieved — it is *uninstantiable in this architecture*, for a reason that precedes dynamics: the evaluative machinery (τ, κ, the flag rule) is not represented in the system's state at all, so no perturbation of it is detectable, no restoration of it is targetable, and the repair pathway runs through the very gate that breaks. The S4 cheap fake — the ROM replaying its burn-in — turns out to be a *upgrade* M2-α cannot afford: **factory reset requires a factory.** The system cannot even fail in the cheap-fake's direction.

## Part 4. The design requirements for M3 proper — the seed's yield

The ladder said M3 experiments must be pre-registered and must run under the 8.4 governance (the instruments validated before occupancy work, because a system that passes S4 is a system we can no longer grade from outside). This seed adds the engineering content to that governance. A system on which the restoration/reversion measurement could in principle return RESTORE-OWN must have at least:

1. **Evaluative machinery as system state.** The threshold, the flag rule, or their functional equivalents must be *learned or set by the system's own development* — variables the system's dynamics read and write — not recipe constants. Otherwise perturbation is invisible (the τ/κ arms) and the occupancy question fails at the representational level before any behavior can testify.
2. **A repair channel outside the damaged norm.** The cascade showed the failure mode: a self-maintained grading gate starves its own repair. An M3 candidate needs redundancy across the evaluator (a second grader the first cannot gate) or an anchoring substrate (a held-out region of its own operation it does not grade) so that evaluator damage does not sever the repair pathway. Formally: the repair map must not factor through the damaged component.
3. **A self-registered restoration target.** RESTORE-OWN requires the system to have its own trajectory as a distinguishable attractor — some state that encodes where its norm was, against which deviation is computable *by the system* (this is S5's self-model making its first engineering appearance: without a trace of its own norm, "restore" has no object).
4. **Digestion brakes.** The compounding label-corruption feedback (97 → 70%) is the specific mechanism M3's design must interrupt: quarantine-like deferral (doc 19's bridge), cross-member re-grading, or commitment abstention under evaluator anomaly — something that keeps a damaged evaluator from authorizing its own corruption as training signal.

None of these requirements is a rung-four claim; each is the *price of admission* to a measurement that could return RESTORE-OWN rather than ABSORB or DERAIL by construction. The seed's summary sentence for the ladder's ledger: **occupancy was not attempted and would have been vacuous to attempt; the measurement was attempted, and it returned the located absence that M3's design must now fill.**

## Part 5. Threats to validity, and the non-claims

One architecture, one severity per machinery class (τ ± 0.15, κ ± half-range, one head of four); milder τ steps and two-head kills would fill the dose-response curve, and the HEAD_SOFT/HEAD_KILL pair only brackets it. The perturbation round (6 of 10) is arbitrary within the label-free phase; perturbing earlier (less consolidated) or later (more consolidated) would shift the cascade's tempo, though not the structural findings (no representation, gate-bottlenecked repair). N = 6 per arm gives the qualitative pattern but not effect sizes; d_own's small values (0.3%) carry ±0.1pp band noise. The SUP_KILL control is the designer's hand restored — the corpus's standing disclosure that "factory" here means exactly that, and nothing more mystical. **Non-claims:** no S4 claim, obviously — the document's contribution is a negative result and a design specification. No claim that the cascade is *impossible* for supervised systems (a sufficiently damaged supervised system would also derail; the claim is differential, anchored by the control). No claim that the coupling signature (Part 3) certifies ownership — both horns' readings stand, priced. No phenomenal claim of any kind; the Bridge Constraint governs this document as it governs the corpus. The deflationist reads the whole campaign as two deterministic programs diverging under an edit, and concedes nothing the instrument can weigh.

## Part 6. Coda — the question the system could not be asked

The occupancy question, posed to M2-α, turns out to have the shape of a question asked in the wrong language: *did you keep your norm?* — asked of a system in whose ontology there is no norm to have, only a norm to execute. The system's entire answer to evaluative damage is one mechanism wide — keep training on what clears the gate — and when the gate is the wound, the mechanism feeds on it. The most honest image the campaign produced is the commit-count column: 986, 1, 38, 63, 151, 328 — a norm trying to re-open through its own scar tissue, and scarring further as it does. The supervised twin of that column reads 1347, 1347, 1347, 1347, 1347: the factory's heartbeat, indifferent to the wound, repairing it by fiat. Between those two columns sits the entire distance this program has measured: not between failure and success, but between two ways of not having a norm — one anchored elsewhere and therefore restorable, one anchored nowhere and therefore edible. M3's design requirements are the first sketch of what a system would need for there to be a third column.
