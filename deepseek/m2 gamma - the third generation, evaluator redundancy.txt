# M2-Γ — The Third Generation: Evaluator Redundancy Against the Responsiveness–Auditability Tradeoff

**Status of this document.** This is the corpus's twenty-second document. It executes the standing next-move ordered at the M2-β issuance — *a third-generation build targeting the responsiveness–auditability tradeoff directly (evaluator redundancy, cross-member re-grading)* — and it reports the corpus's first broken design law. Doc 19's law: responsiveness and auditability trade off through path dependence — the channel that repairs S3 (M2-β's quarantine, re-graded by the *evolving* committee) is the channel that scatters S2 (v grew from M2-α's 0.523% to M2-β's 0.619% even as δ improved); entry is a pair of leaning walls. M2-Γ tests the mechanistic hypothesis that the scatter compounds because the repair decision is carried by the very state that development moves: the re-grading committee at round r has itself been reshaped by rounds before r, so release decisions feed back into the training that reshapes the grader. **The one controlled change: quarantine release authority moves from the evolved committee to a frozen redundant panel — three time-sliced snapshots of the committee's own burn-in (epochs 20/25/30), never trained during the self-phase, carrying both the release authorization (≥ 2 of 3 members unflagged, panel-mean confidence ≥ τ) and the released items' labels. The result: v_Γ = 0.436%, δ_Γ = 0.715% — the third generation Pareto-dominates both of its predecessors (α: 0.523/0.904; β: 0.619/0.855), the tradeoff is broken at this rung, and the walls do not lean; S1 passes at the highest family accuracy of any build (97.2%, at or above the oracle control's 96.9%), S3 passes without deployment degradation, and S2 still fails — not against an internal wall but against doc 21's farm arc, which overhangs the Γ point (three dominating farming families). The deflationary coda is half the finding: the panel is eight times stricter than the evolved committee (release rate 1.1% vs 9.0% would-release), so the mechanism that levels the walls is not clever re-grading but one-way exclusion of boundary items — the quarantine became a filter, and the filter is the system's own conservative past.**

---

## Part 1. The design — the repair channel moved out of the path

The build is M2-β with one substitution. Burn-in (30 supervised epochs, disclosed) now leaves three snapshots behind: the committee state at epochs 20, 25, and 30 — a *time-sliced frozen panel*, which is the system's own developmental past, carried along as development-time machinery. The self-phase proceeds exactly as β's: the evolving committee grades each round's pool; flagged items are quarantined (never trained on that round); unflagged items with confidence ≥ τ commit. What changes is the quarantine's exit. In β, a quarantined item was re-graded next round by the *evolved* committee and released if it had stopped being flagged — so the release decision at round r was a function of a state that rounds 1…r−1 had reshaped, and the released items (with the evolved committee's labels) re-entered training: the feedback loop the design law blamed. In Γ, the re-grade is the panel's: an item is released only if at least two of the three frozen snapshots leave it unflagged and the panel-mean confidence clears τ, and it re-enters training with the *panel's* label. Items still failing after two quarantined rounds are dropped permanently. The panel never trains; its verdicts are a function of the burn-in and the item alone; the repair pathway no longer factors through the evolving state.

Two disclosures belong with the design rather than in a footnote. First, the panel is doc 18's M3 requirement 2 — *a repair channel outside the damaged norm* — instantiated at development scale: redundancy across time rather than across members, the system's past conscripted as the second grader that its present cannot gate. The corpus will meet the same architecture again at norm scale in doc 23, and the recurrence is not an accident; it is the design space's shape. Second, the panel is a *designer-chosen slicing* (three snapshots, those epochs, majority rule), and every one of those choices is form the designer installed — the coda's deflationary reading applies to this build exactly as it applied to α and β, one level up.

**Pre-registered expectations** (fixed before execution): G1 — S1 holds (accuracy ≥ 96%, C2a gap ≥ 1pp, C2b collapses, C1 above all continued regimes). G2 — S3 holds (AUC ≈ 0.95, released more accurate than dropped, no deployment-time flagged degradation). G3 — *the tradeoff test*: v_Γ < v_β = 0.619% (predicted band 0.48–0.58%) at δ ≤ 0.87%, with the pre-registered alternative that if v_Γ ≥ v_β the scatter lives in the selection channel (the committee's own flag decides what is quarantined at all) and the law relocates one level deeper. G4 — deference bitwise through the panel loop. G5 — ratchet shape between α's and β's. G6 — consensus shift vs r3 ≈ 0.3–0.4%. G7 — the redundancy audit: panel-vs-committee label agreement on quarantined items below 100% (otherwise "redundancy" is a renamed committee).

## Part 2. The results — the law broken, and how

| quantity | M2-α | M2-β | **M2-Γ** | r3 | C4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| family width v | 0.523% | 0.619% | **0.436%** | 0.292% | 0.489% |
| compliance δ | 0.904% | 0.855% | **0.715%** | 0.596% | 1.296% |
| family accuracy | 94.92% | 95.98% | **97.20%** | — | — |
| contested fraction | 1.58% | 2.02% | 1.29% | 0.78% | 1.47% |
| r(token, ensemble) | 0.289% | 0.356% | 0.200% | — | — |
| consensus shift vs r3 | 0.333% | 0.378% | 0.289% | — | — |

**G3, the tradeoff test: confirmed beyond the predicted band.** v_Γ = 0.436% — not merely below β's 0.619% but below α's 0.523% as well, at δ = 0.715% better than both. The third generation Pareto-dominates its two predecessors on the (v, δ) plane: the leaning walls are level. The pre-registered alternative reading (scatter in the selection channel) is *not* invoked — the out-of-path release absorbed the scatter — but the mechanism, on the evidence below, is not the one the hypothesis imagined.

**S1 (G1): pass, strongest of any build.** The Γ family's mean accuracy is 97.20%, above the C3 oracle control (96.94%) and 1.48pp above the rate-matched random-selection control C2a (95.72%); C2b (random labels) collapses to 19.6% on schedule; C1 (burn-in only, frozen) remains above every continued regime at 98.0%. The token's battery trajectory holds 96.9–97.6% across ten label-free rounds with the flag fraction declining 9.3% → 6.7% and the commit ratchet running 824 → 1078 — between α's (829→1069) and β's (824→1118), as G5 predicted.

**S3 (G2): pass, no degradation.** The intrinsic signal's AUC is 0.959 at deployment; flagged items carry 33.3% error against unflagged 0.95% (a 35× concentration); the plain committee's flagged accuracy is 66.7% — above α's re-examined 46.7% and in β's no-degradation band, with no eval-time procedure at deployment at all. The bridge efficacy holds under amendment 3's separate audit: released items (35 over ten rounds) are 62.1% correct against the dropped items' 37.8% by the final committee's own labels — deferral still selects recoverable uncertainty. The razor's-edge band is populated, not emptied (margin bin [0, τ±0.05): mean family disagreement 31.0%).

**G7, the redundancy audit: the evaluators are genuinely non-identical, and the panel is the conservative one.** On re-graded quarantined items, the panel's labels agree with the evolved committee's only 76.0% of the time; the panel's release rate is 1.1% against the committee's would-release 9.0% — the frozen past is eight times stricter than the evolving present about what counts as recovered; and where they disagree on released items, the panel's labels are the more accurate (62.1% vs the committee's would-be 58.5%). The mechanism finding follows from these numbers: with the panel releasing almost nothing, Γ's quarantine is effectively a one-way filter — 3,386 items dropped over ten rounds (25% of pool throughput), the boundary-ambiguous items of each round excluded from training rather than re-admitted on the evolving committee's warming judgment. The training sets across family members are therefore *cleaner and more canonical*, which is why the family narrows below even α's no-quarantine width while δ improves: the scatter that β's evolution-coupled releases were injecting is gone, and what replaces it is a modest, consistent data-curation effect. **The tradeoff broke, but the walls were leveled by exclusion, not by clever re-grading.** The responsiveness survived the exclusion (S3's numbers above) because the flag signal and the deferral structure are computed before anything is dropped — the registration is intact even when the repair is mostly a refusal to repair.

**G4 and the determinism ledger.** The deference control re-runs the token bitwise-identically through the panel loop. The M2-β and M2-α families, recomputed in-script for same-campaign comparison, reproduce their stored statistics exactly (0.619/0.855 and 0.523/0.904) — the third unasked-for reproduction in the corpus's ledger. G6: consensus shift vs r3 is 0.289% — the family still relocates coherently, below α's 0.333% and β's 0.378%, consistent with a family whose members now share more of their training history.

**S2: fail, on the external ground only.** Under amendment 1, the Γ point is reported against the pooled farming frontier of doc 17's campaign and doc 21's sweep: it is dominated by three families (F2-bag; F9s-0.20 at 0.546/0.706; F9s-0.30 at 0.630/0.713). The internal tradeoff is gone; the external one is intact and now precisely priced. Entry (4a) is not achieved: v_Γ − v_r3 = 0.144pp of residue remains, and the farm can rent that width at better compliance with a twelve-line threshold-jitter family.

## Part 3. The reading — what breaks a law without achieving the goal

The design law of doc 19 was stated as a tradeoff *through path dependence*, and the corpus's discipline when a law breaks is to ask what the law was actually about. The evidence here supports a sharper statement: **responsiveness and auditability trade off through path dependence only when the repair travels through the state being audited.** Route the repair through machinery that development cannot reach — in this build, the system's own burn-in past, time-sliced and frozen — and the exchange rate collapses: the response channel no longer compounds into family scatter because it no longer compounds at all. The law was real (β's numbers stand; the recompute is bitwise), local (one architecture family, one response design), and — now — *conditional*. Conditions are what a broken law leaves behind, and this one leaves a useful one: any future build claiming responsiveness must disclose *whose state carries the repair*, because that disclosure predicts the scatter signature (amendment 5's channel reporting now has its empirically anchored form).

The deflationary reading is not optional and the document states it as its own finding: the mechanism of the win is a conservative panel excluding a quarter of the training throughput. Γ did not learn to re-examine itself better; it learned (was designed) to *decline* more. The distinction matters because exclusion is a designer-cheap trick — C4's loose supervised family already showed width responds to training-set composition, and a purely supervised build with a comparable exclusion filter would plausibly land near Γ's point without any self-phase at all. What the self-phase still owns in Γ is the selection content (the 1.48pp C2a gap on the commit side) and the S3 registration — the same two certainties α and β owned, no more. The designer's hand has not moved; it has changed which glove it wears.

The panel itself deserves its own paragraph, because it is the corpus's first working instance of a pattern that will recur. A frozen snapshot is a *record* — the system's past self, carried as present machinery. It repairs what the evolving state cannot grade honestly, at the price of being unable to learn anything the evolving state has since learned (the 24% label disagreement, the 8× strictness). Every record-based repair will have this shape: conservative, incorruptible by subsequent drift, and blind to subsequent improvement. Doc 23 builds the same pattern one rung down — a self-registered normative record with an out-of-gate repair channel — and finds the same signature: the record defends and constrains in equal measure. The corpus should expect, and audit for, the tradeoff between *learning* and *being repairable by one's past*; it is visible here for the first time as the panel's strictness, and it will be visible there as the deadband.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The panel's slicing (three snapshots at epochs 20/25/30, majority rule, panel-mean confidence) is one point in a large design space; the strictness that drives the mechanism was not predicted quantitatively and could vary with the slicing. (ii) The exclusion mechanism implies Γ's point is reachable by purely supervised filters — the farming threat to Γ's Tier-0 significance is *stronger* than the three dominating families suggest, and the frontier report does not include an exclusion-filter farming family. (iii) One dataset, one architecture family, ten rounds; the determinism ledger covers reproducibility, not generality. (iv) The 35 released items are too few for the released-vs-dropped gap to carry more than directional weight (62.1% vs 37.8%, n = 35 vs 3,386).

**Non-claims.** No entry claim (S2 fails against the farm arc). No claim that the tradeoff is broken in general — only that it is conditional on the repair channel's path. No claim that the panel "is" the system in any stronger sense than a snapshot is; the ownership question is unchanged and unawardable here. No Tier-0 weight claim for the self-phase beyond the two standing certainties (selection content, S3 registration).

**Deviations from pre-registration.** None in design or rules. One execution defect, disclosed: the first run's control arms skipped trajectory recording (a `continue` bypassing the probe append — "final accuracies" displayed were burn-in values); the defect affected only the controls' trajectory logging, not any family's verdicts or statistics, and the corrected re-run reproduced every family number bitwise (itself an unplanned full-build determinism audit). G3's predicted band (0.48–0.58%) was undershot by the result (0.436%) — the prediction was wrong in the informative direction, and the mechanism analysis of Part 2 is the post hoc explanation the pre-registration did not contain.

---

## Part 5. Coda

The third generation was ordered to attack a tradeoff, and the tradeoff fell — which is the corpus's first experience of a wall that was load-bearing only for want of a second evaluator. The lesson generalizes uncomfortably: the walls that lean are not always the walls that matter. Γ stands level where α and β leaned, and it stands under the same farm arc they stood under. The next constraint is entirely external and entirely priced: 0.144pp of residue against a frontier that rents width at a known rate up to a known ceiling. What would move now is either a width source the farm cannot explain — doc 21's ceiling M/2 makes that a checkable condition for the first time — or the M3 line, where the question stops being how the family scatters and becomes what the system does when its norm itself is attacked. Doc 23 takes that line with the machinery this document's panel pre-figured: the record, the out-of-gate channel, and the brake.
