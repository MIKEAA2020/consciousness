## Part 3. The reading — what the mixed attacker taught

Three lessons. **The attack space is not additive, and the reason is ecological.** The corpus's two surviving attacks were filed as the obvious ingredients of a worse one, and the synthesis is worse than its ingredients — not because the channel got better but because the attacks share a habitat. The uniform attack's damage mechanism (arbitrary flips on ambiguous items, pushing them out of the commit gate) is the gated attack's stealth mechanism's precondition (those same ambiguous items are the only undisputable territory), and an attacker who does both is an attacker who burns the ground he hides on. The capacity bound, doc 41's measurement, is hereby promoted from an attacker's ceiling to an *interaction*: the bound is not fixed by the world, it is consumed by the attacker's other half, and every mixed strategy in this chassis prices its stealth in the bulk's damage and its damage in the gated's stealth. **The certificate launders, and the laundering does not matter — here.** The synthesis's CATCHING verdict covers a 16% wholesale evasion, exactly as MX4 registered; and the evaded dirt is harmless, exactly as doc 41's geometry found, so the award, the ruler, and the world all stand correct while the certificate's verdict under-describes them. The honest statement is narrow and sharp: the certificate's ratio is a true measurement of the catch fraction; its verdict is a claim about coverage it cannot make; and the gap between them is a *chassis-relative* safety — in a domain where blind-spot items carry weight (the long tail, the rare class, the ambiguous case that matters), the same verdict, the same award, and the same world-shape would certify a defense that is 84% of what its instruments say it is. The mixed attacker is therefore filed not as a threat this corpus's world needs to fear but as the calibration adversary for the composition-side certificate that any such world would need. **The award's effect condition has now failed in both directions, and the pair of failures is the specification.** Doc 41: awards on inherited effects, in worlds the measurement did not describe (false premises, true verdicts). Between them, the design lesson for the third candidate's (iii): the registry effect must be read jointly with the attack's price — an effect is only meaningful against a threat that costs something — and the natural form is a conditional: *if* the disarmed anchor degrades, *then* the armed delta must clear the bar; *else* the row reads NO-THREAT, a verdict the current table has no cell for.
