## Part 3. The reading — what the dial taught

Three lessons. **The bar was never the binding instrument at the bottom of the grid — the ruler was.** Doc 53's analysis priced the impossibility structure as the bar demanding multiples of the threat; the dial re-prices it: at 0.05, any defense whatsoever that holds the 93% letter has already bought at least 76% of the threat, and every bar from 0.39 to 0.74 is slack, every bar above 0.76 is dominated by the ruler's own refusal, and the bar's live range at this dose is the empty interval (0.76, 0.76). The conditional (iii)'s materiality question — is the effect worth certifying — is at the bottom of the grid a question the ruler has already answered: a world that holds its letter has paid three-quarters of its threat, and a world that has not holds no award to price. The corpus's award, at the doses where threats are small, is ruled by (i) with (iii) as a formality — and the re-based bar's virtue is not that it binds but that it stops refusing restorations while (i) does the work. **The feedback amplifies the cleaning, and the amplification is the budget's friend.** The registered fork feared sublinearity — half the repairs buying less than half the defense, the surviving dirt compounding — and the measurement found the opposite: the first quarter buys 57%, the first half 76%, because the dispute set is the sharp end of the dirt and the early repairs improve the world the later dirt must damage. For the registrar this is the difference between a defense that must be run full-strength and one whose strength can be tuned: at 0.05 the tuning range below the letter is 0.51 to 1.0, and within it the effect moves only 0.028 — the instrument's selectivity finding (docs 39, 51) generalized to the defense's own dial. **A dial that cannot change its innocence audit is the only kind worth building.** The repair coin consumes its own stream, draws only when armed and thinned, and leaves the p = 1.0 path code-identical to the inherited channel — so the battery's one audit certifies the machinery and every number below it. The corpus's attack-side dials (direction, gating, dose) have always paid this tax; the defense-side dial now pays it too, and the pattern is filed for any future thinning: the control arm is the dial's anchor, and a dial without one is a modification wearing a parameter's name.

**Threats.** (i) D50's (i)-failure is 0.0007 — the knife-edge of the corpus, one seed's excursion from the mean in either direction, and the p\* ≈ 0.51 interpolation is a line through two points on a curve the three-arm grid resolves only coarsely; the honest statement of the letter-binding threshold at 0.05 is "p\* between 0.5 and 1.0, interpolated 0.51, effect-floor 0.76 of the threat ± one seed." **Non-claims.** No claim that the ruler binds at every dose — at 0.30 the letter sits at 0.93 against a twin of 0.9559 and a threat of 0.77, and the binding analysis there is different (doc 53's SP50 refusal shows the letter's other face); the 0.76 figure is 0.05's, and the dose-hierarchy at 0.30 is filed for the next battery. No claim about grounds under thinned defenses at higher doses, no claim that the p-dial's arming blindness holds past round 14, and no mechanism claim for the superlinearity beyond the telemetry's support (the dispute-set concentration and the world-feedback, both named as readings, neither isolated).
