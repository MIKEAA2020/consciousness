## Part 1. The presupposition audit — the additive scale has no dualizer

**Standing notation (docs 24, 27).** ChuCost is the category of triples (A, X, r) with r: A × X → [0, ∞], morphisms (f, g) with (L): s(f(a), y) ≤ r(a, g(y)); the Dialectica tensor ⊗ with pairing r + s and unit I = (1, 1, 0); the residuum hom A ⊸ B = (X^Y × B^A, A × Y, h_⊸) with h_⊸ = s ∸ r; the adjunction of Theorems A and B. A *dualizing object* in a symmetric monoidal closed category is an object D such that the canonical map A → (A ⊸ D) ⊸ D is an isomorphism for every A (the double-dual map, (id-adjoint) mate of the identity); the associated dual is A^⊥ := A ⊸ D, and in a *-autonomous category the de Morgan identities hold, of which the one Theorem O's proof invokes is the hom form **X ⊸ Y ≅ (X ⊗ Y^⊥)^⊥** — the form obtained from the par form (X^⊥ ⊗ Y^⊥)^⊥ = X ⅋ Y by substituting the negation, and note for the record that doc 46 prints the par form where the hom form is meant.

**Lemma 63 (no dualizing object on the additive scale) [Derived here].** No object D of ChuCost is dualizing. *Proof.* Suppose D = (P, Q, d) is. Test the double-dual isomorphism at A₀ = (1, 1, c), the singleton pairings. A₀ ⊸ D = (1^Q × P^1, 1 × Q, h) ≅ (P, Q, h) with h((φ, β), (·, q)) = d(β(·), q) ∸ c — a pairing d ∸ c. Then (A₀ ⊸ D) ⊸ D has system carrier Q^Q × P^P; the canonical map A₀ → ((A₀ ⊸ D) ⊸ D) cannot be an isomorphism unless 1 ≅ Q^Q × P^P, forcing |P| = |Q| = 1. So D = (1, 1, d). Then for every A: A ⊸ D = (X, A, d ∸ r) and (A ⊸ D) ⊸ D = (A, X, d ∸ (d ∸ r)) = (A, X, min(d, r)) — the last by the two case checks (r ≤ d: d ∸ (d − r) = r; r > d: d ∸ 0 = d). The canonical map A → (A, X, min(d, r)) is (id, id), a morphism since min(d, r) ≤ r; it is an isomorphism iff the reverse condition r(a, x) ≤ min(d, r(a, x)) holds for all pairings r — i.e. iff r ≤ d pointwise. Since ChuCost carries pairings exceeding any finite bound, no (1, 1, d) dualizes; since no other candidate survived A₀, none exists. ∎ Three readings, each load-bearing below. **(a) The bounded scales carry dualizers:** (1, 1, K) dualizes the full subcategory of [0, K]-valued pairings, on which min(K, r) = r — and the associated dual A^⊥ = (X, A, K ∸ r) = (X, A, K − r) is exactly doc 24 Prop 4's reflected transpose; the bounded duality doc 24 bought by reflection is the bounded scale's dualizing structure, now identified as such. But the bounded fragment is not ⊗-closed (doc 27's Lemma 21: r + s overflows), so Definition 55's standing hypotheses — tensor-closed *and* containing the dualizer — cannot both be met there. **(b) The de Morgan identity fails in the additive scale, with the corrected form displayed:** with the hom form X ⊸ Y ≅ (X ⊗ Y^⊥)^⊥, the right side computes (for the plain transpose reading of ⊥) to the carriers of A ⊸ B carrying the pairing r + s, against the residuum hom's s ∸ r; the identity would force s ∸ r = r + s pairwise, failing wherever 0 < s and r > 0, by exactly the r that doc 27's Corollary 16 named as the Chu formula's double billing. (There is a canonical morphism (A ⊗ B^⊥)^⊥ → A ⊸ B — the identity on carriers, since s ∸ r ≤ r + s pointwise — the discount's direction, one-way: the residuum object is the cheaper of the two, and no morphism returns.) **(c) Theorem O's proof does not survive the audit:** its (a) ⟹ (c) step spends the hom-form identity and its (c) ⟹ (a) step spends A^⊥ = A ⊸ ⊥_V, both now unavailable in the additive scale. What survives is the (c) ⟹ (b) half, whose proof (doc 46's, verbatim) uses only fullness and Theorem B's currying — no duality anywhere. The applications are audited in Part 4's ledger; none of them used the duality, which is why the settlement survives the repair.

## Part 2. The repair — the MV Dialectica is where the dualizer lives

**Proposition 64 (the MV dualizer, and the corrected de Morgan) [Derived here; the quantale is doc 27 Prop 22's, classical].** Work in the MV Dialectica: the full subcategory ChuCost_MV of ChuCost on the [0, K]-valued pairings, with the Łukasiewicz tensor ⊗_MV (pairing r ⊙ s = max(r + s − K, 0)) and the MV residuum hom (h = s ∸_MV r = min(K, s + K − r)); by Theorem A's generality (doc 27 Remark 18) it is monoidal closed. Then: **(i) the monoidal unit is (1, 1, K)** — K ⊙ r = r — **and the dualizing object is (1, 1, 0)**; **(ii) the associated dual A^⊥ = A ⊸ (1, 1, 0) = (X, A, min(K, 0 + K − r)) = (X, A, K − r)**, the reflected transpose, so the involution is doc 24 Prop 4's reflection, now internal to a closed category; **(iii) the corrected de Morgan identity holds exactly: A ⊸_MV B = (A ⊗_MV B^⊥)^⊥.** *Proof.* (i) is the unit law K ⊙ r = max(K + r − K, 0) = r on carriers as in doc 24's Def 5. (ii) is the residuum computation displayed. (iii): B^⊥ = (Y, B, K − s). The tensor A ⊗_MV B^⊥ has system A × Y, observer X^Y × B^A, pairing t((a, y), (φ, β)) = r(a, φ(y)) ⊙ (K − s(β(a), y)) = max(r(a, φ(y)) − s(β(a), y), 0). Its reflected dual has the same carriers read back and pairing K − max(r − s, 0) = min(K − r + s, K) = min(K, s + K − r) = s ∸_MV r — the MV residuum hom's pairing, carrier for carrier and value for value. ∎ **The repair of the record, stated:** doc 27 Prop 22's tuple "([0, K], ⊙, 0, ≤)" lists the annihilator 0 (a ⊙ 0 = 0) where a unit is conventionally expected; the ⊙-unit is K, the scale's top. The slip was inert while the unit and the dualizer were never compared; it is load-bearing the moment they are, because the MV scale *splits* what the additive scale merged: the additive unit I = (1, 1, 0) is both bottom-cost and unit; the MV scale's unit is the top-cost singleton and its dualizer the bottom-cost singleton, and the distance between them — the full budget K — is the price the reflection charges.

**Theorem V (the closure-equivalence, re-homed) [Derived here; Theorem B is doc 27's].** In ChuCost_MV, let Q′ be a full replete ⊗_MV-closed subcategory containing (1, 1, 0). ## Part 3. The hypothesis, run — the dualizer's probes, priced and refuted

**Lemma 65 (what the probes are) [Derived here].** In either scale, with ⊥ = (1, 1, 0): a morphism (f, g): ⊥ → (B, Y, s) is a choice of b = f(·) with s(b, y) ≤ r_⊥(·, g(y)) = 0 for all y — so **ChuCost(⊥, X) is the set of zero-cost elements of X's system carrier: the b whose every test is costless.** At internal-hom points the probe set thins further, differently by scale: under the unit reading (the additive scale, where (1, 1, 0) is the unit), ChuCost(I, A ⊸ B) ≅ ChuCost(A, B) by the adjunction at C = I — the unit's probes at internal-hom points are the homs, and the hom's condition is the full coupling inequality; under the MV dualizer reading, ChuCost_MV(⊥, A ⊸_MV B) = {(φ, β) : min(K, s + K − r) = 0 ∀(a, y)} = {(φ, β) : s(β(a), y) + K ≤ r(a, φ(y))} — the pairs landing in A's top-cost region against B's zero-cost region, since s ≥ 0 and r ≤ K force r = K and s = 0. *Proof.* Unpacking (L) at the singleton source; the adjunction at the unit; the MV case computation. ∎ The probes are, in both readings, *coupling-blind shadows*: they see the region of the pairing at one extreme (the costless; the top-and-bottom), never the interior where the couplings live.

**Theorem W (the generating form refuted) [Derived here].** The dualizer's probes do not determine iso-classes at internal-hom points, in either scale that carries a dualizer. *Proof.* The constant-pairing family: let A_c = (2, 2, ≡c), B_d = (2, 2, ≡d), with c, d in the scale's range. Then A_c ⊸ B_d = (X^Y × B^A, A × Y, ≡ d ∸ c) — a constant pairing, with system carrier 16, observer 4. In the additive scale take (c, d) = (1, 2) and (0, 2): the internal-hom points (16, 4, ≡1) and (16, 4, ≡2) are non-isomorphic (a morphism (16, 4, ≡v) → (16, 4, ≡v′) requires v′ ≤ v; an isomorphism requires both) and both are probe-empty, since a constant pairing v > 0 has no zero-cost element. In the MV scale take (c, d) = (K, K) and (K, K/2): the points (16, 4, ≡K) and (16, 4, ≡K/2) — non-isomorphic, both probe-empty (their constant pairings exceed 0). Two non-isomorphic internal-hom points with equal (empty) dualizer-probe sets, in each scale. ∎

**Corollary W.1 (the Born world's blindness) [Derived here; the Born pairing is doc 42 Def 48's].** On the Born pairings (D(B), E(B), 1 − tr(eρ)) the dualizer's probe set is empty: a zero-cost density matrix would satisfy tr(eρ) = 1 for every effect e, and the zero effect already gives 0. The dualizer cannot see the quantum fragment at all — not its objects, not the comparison maps the detection question would ask about, nothing. ∎ **The Prop 51 connection, resolved.** Doc 58's Remark said the answer would live in the Prop 51 machinery, and it does, twice over: the equality core is the largest fragment on which the plain transpose is functorial (doc 24 §1.5) — the additive scale's only home for anything de Morgan-shaped, and Lemma 63 shows even that home does not extend to a dualizer; and between Born objects the equality core is the positive maps with the transpose the canonical non-CP member (Prop 51) — so the duality's home fragment is *exactly* the fragment the dualizer's probes are empty on (Corollary W.1). The two blindnesses meet at the same objects: the transpose is the first morphism the duality meets that the physical world does not induce, and the dualizer is the first probe that cannot see the world at all. The identification the bridging file wanted between duality and physics fails here for the most structural of reasons — its two halves are blind to each other's homes.

**The transfer engine, located.** Proposition 61(ii) closed the Set and Vec_fd cases by evaluating the probe bijection at the unit: |R| = |B|^{|A|}, R ≅ Hom(A, B) linearly — the unit-probe of the function object is the exponential of the unit-probes, because *points of a function space are functions*. Lemma 65 gives the ChuCost side of that sentence, and the sentence fails there: the unit-probe of the internal hom is the *hom* (a set of carrier-pairs satisfying a coupling inequality), and the probes of A and B are the zero-cost elements (coupling-blind); ChuCost(A, B) is not a function of (probes of A, probes of B) — it is not even determined by them, since Theorem W's witnesses have identical probe data at every object of the form (1, 1, ·) while their homs differ. The coupling phenomenon of Proposition 61(i) thereby acquires its mechanism: **objects outgrow their unit-probes because the function objects' probes are homs, and homs read the coupling the probes never see.** And the fence is sharpened to its boundary case: the dualizer's probe — the cheapest probe, the one every fragment owns by hypothesis — returns, at internal-hom points, data already equalized by Lemma 60 (under the unit reading it *is* the equalized hom; under the MV reading it is a shadow thinner still). The hypothesis was always going to fail inside the fence; what the unfolding adds is that it fails outside the fence too, on witnesses the (b)-structure never touches.

## Part 5. Coda

Doc 58 filed the dualizer-generation hypothesis as the cheapest open detection condition, and the cheapest thing about it turned out to be the price of checking whether it could be asked. The presupposition — a dualizing object in a category whose own ledger, doc 27's, had priced the negation as the one resource the additive scale cannot buy — folded at the first computation, and the folding sent the document down the corpus's oldest repair path: unfold, retract in part, re-home, re-prove. What stands at the end is a cleaner bridge than the one the settlement was written on: the closure-equivalence lives where the duality lives, in the budget currency; the settlement's arithmetic never needed the duality and still does not; and the hypothesis the order named is refuted with two-line witnesses — constant pairings, empty probes — the kind of counterexample that makes a formal family honest. The deepest finding is the meeting of blindnesses: the duality's home is the equality core, the equality core's canonical morphism is the transpose, the transpose is the map no channel induces — and the dualizer, asked to see through the coupling, sees nothing at all in the very world where those maps live. The bridging file wanted the Chu construction's duality to carry the physics; the corpus's mathematics, run to the bottom of this cell, finds the duality and the physics mutually out of sight, and says so with proofs on both sides. The base poset, under it all along, keeps its silence another phase; the MV scale, under it all along too, turns out to have been carrying the bridge's oldest hope — a duality that composes — at exactly the price doc 27 quoted for it, and in exactly the currency the instruments have been counting since the first deadband was registered.
