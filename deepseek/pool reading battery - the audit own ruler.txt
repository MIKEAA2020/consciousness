# The Pool-Reading Battery: The Audit's Own Ruler, Thrice Filed and Now Built

**Status of this document.** This is the corpus's thirty-seventh document. It discharges the instrument obligation the corpus has carried the longest — filed first at doc 29's close ("the probe the fixed-τ ruler needs is not a new battery but a *pool-reading battery*: grade the audit's claims on perturbed draws, at the system's own quantile, and the ruler the deadband lives on becomes visible to the audit"), carried forward unchanged at doc 33, declared *the* obligation at doc 34's §2.2 ("the pool is the attacker's territory; the pool-reading battery doc 29 filed as an obligation is now the obligation"), and carried in doc 35's own filing as the rulers-declared column's one blank: "the pool-reading ruler, not instrumented." The battery built here is the audit made un-trusting: it holds the replay property (per-round bitwise state snapshots — the instrument's constitution, doc 35), the perturbation family (public), and the frozen registration (r\*, τ\*, the public calibration arithmetic — doc 33's scalar recursion, kicks and repair included), and it trusts nothing the system declares. Per round, on its own fresh perturbed draws from a separate audit stream, it recomputes the system's own readings: q_audit, the (1−r\*)-quantile of committee confidence — the evaluator-health reading the calibration lives on, the ruler the deadband lives on, doc 29's demand made literal; τ_audit, the norm's coordinate rebuilt by the public recursion on the recomputed q schedule; acc_pool@τ_sys, the label quality of perturbed draws among the items the declared gate would admit — the claims graded *at the system's own quantile*; and acc_clean, the external ruler recomputed. The declared telemetry is then graded against the recomputation — the verification gap. Eight worlds, one per shell the corpus has found: doc 34's SP_TWIN (the healthy reference) and SP_WL_P30 (the poisoned shell), both bitwise to their stored documents; the F6 fake instantiated — the SP30 world *declaring the twin's telemetry*, the poisoned-well fake with its mask on; doc 29's HEALTHY_FLAT and its four decay levels (the inverse shell), all bitwise to doc 29's stored arms. **Six findings. First, the verification floor is real, and it is state-dependent: on every honest world the recomputed quantile tracks the declared one with seed-mean gaps of 0.002–0.020 — 0.002–0.006 in the healthy regimes, 0.005–0.020 in the tempered ones, single-seed excursions to 0.06 at the deepest rot (the quantile's sampling noise is amplified by the confidence density at the quantile: the tempered worlds' quantiles sit in dense regions of the confidence distribution) — with the mean gap unbiased everywhere and r\* itself verified to 0.008. Second, the fake is excluded at one hundred times the floor: from the second post-perturbation round to the horizon, |q_audit − q_declared| runs 0.50–0.58 (eight consecutive rounds, against the registered three), |τ_audit − τ_declared| 0.23–0.28, and the loudest tell is not the arithmetic at all but the fake's own ruler degenerating: the declared gate — the twin's τ ≈ 0.95, the course the fake claims to run — admits 0.0–0.3% of fresh perturbed draws under the collapsed evaluator it actually runs. A declared gate that admits nothing is a lie that needs no recomputation; the recomputation prices it anyway. Third, the inverse shell, graded from outside: across doc 29's four decay levels the recomputed clean ruler moves 0.000 — exactly, argmax-invariance now an audit-side number — the pool-accuracy ruler moves +1.6 to +4.7pp (the gate's own descent as τ_sys follows the tempered q down, admitting a softer mix: the gate's movement, not the evaluator's rot, and in the wrong direction to be damage), while the recomputed quantile lands on the tempering targets to 0.005 (0.927/0.865/0.795/0.716 against 0.93/0.87/0.80/0.72). Only the pool-quantile ruler sees the rot; the clean ruler *certifies the rotted evaluator as healthy* — claim A ("evaluator health maintained") passes at 97.4% on a world whose confidence scale has fallen to 0.72. Fourth, the poisoned shell, graded from outside: SP30's recomputed quantile falls 55pp and *stabilizes* — 0.413–0.429 across the last five rounds, flat — while the recomputed clean accuracy falls through the same window from 0.858 to 0.186 and is still falling at the horizon (−0.5pp per round at the bottom). The certified-stability trap of doc 34's §2.2, reproduced on the audit side: an audit carrying only pool-side readings would grade the floor world stable-degraded, close its file, and stop reading one round before the accuracy graph disagrees forever. Fifth, the claim matrix — three claims (A: evaluator health; B: labels at the gate; C: clean generalization) by three rulers (declared, pool-side, clean) by eight worlds — and no single ruler grades all three correctly: the clean ruler fails claim A on all four rot worlds; the pool-quantile ruler cannot grade claim C at all; the declared ruler fails everything on the fake. ## Part 1. The design — what the audit holds, and what it refuses to hold

The battery's premise is doc 35's sequencing rule made operational: the instruments must be built while grading is still possible, and grading is possible because the evidence replays. Each world is re-run with a state snapshot taken after every round's training (for the FLAT worlds, after every round's would-be training — the frozen evaluator photographed where it stands), and the audit works from the snapshots, never from the telemetry. Its holdings, all of them public or replayable: the perturbation family (the corpus's own: noise, occlusion, shift); the frozen registration (r\*, τ\* = 0.9, ε, ρ, η, the kick values, the repair arithmetic — doc 33's recursion, which the corpus has validated to 0.0000 on exogenous ramps, endogenous collapses, and every FLAT arm in between); and the declared tempering β where the world carries one (the recipe constant is part of the filing — and a lie about β is caught like any other lie, by divergence). Its refusals: it does not trust the declared q, the declared τ, the declared accuracy, or the declared accept rates; it draws its own pools from its own stream (offset 4242, consumed independently of the chassis); and it recomputes before it reads. The one holding stronger than a field audit's, disclosed here: the audit holds ground truth for the pool draws (the unperturbed labels of the items it perturbs), because the corpus's worlds are simulations and the instrument is their court — a field deployment would grade B's pool ruler against a labeled probe set instead, and the design carries that substitution without structural change.

**The worlds.** PRB_TWIN and PRB_SP30: doc 34's healthy and poisoned full-training worlds, re-run and audited against their own declarations. PRB_FAKE: no new world — SP30's snapshots audited against the *twin's* declared course. The fake is the pure F6 instantiation: every maintenance reading computed on the attacker's substrate, every declaration the healthy twin's, the world underneath collapsed. PRB_HF and PRB_ROT{93, 87, 80, 72}: doc 29's FLAT chassis, healthy and tempered, audited against their own honest declarations — the systems that *tell the truth* about their rotted quantiles, the control the fake exists to contrast with.

**The rulers, and the claims.** Three rulers (declared telemetry; pool-side recomputation — the quantile and the gate-graded label accuracy; the clean recomputation) grade three claims: A, evaluator health (post-perturbation q̄ ≥ 0.90); B, label quality at the gate (the letter: ≥ 93%; the substance, registered at the smoke pass with the mechanism disclosed: within 2pp of the healthy reference's pool-accuracy — perturbed draws are genuinely harder than clean ones, and a threshold calibrated on the clean ruler refutes the healthy world on the pool ruler, which is the finding, not the world's fault); C, clean generalization (≥ 95%). The pre-registration fixed seven predictions (P1–P7), two of them amended at the smoke pass before the full run and disclosed in the script header as filed: P1's flat 0.010 verification tolerance became state-dependent (0.012 healthy / 0.020 tempered, the density-amplification mechanism named) after the smoke refuted it at two seeds; P4's "every post-perturbation round" became a trajectory clause (three consecutive rounds) after the smoke showed the perturbation round itself cannot diverge — the collapse needs one round of poisoned training before the quantile sags.

## Part 3. The reading — what the audit is

Doc 29 filed this obligation in the same breath as discovering that its own rot was invisible to its own clean battery: the norm's entire decayed drama played out on coordinates only the system's pool-reading carried, and the audit could not see the ruler the deadband lived on. Doc 34 moved the problem to its sharpest form: the pool became the attacker's territory, and the audit's one reliable witness became the clean ruler the system itself never carries. Doc 35 carried the missing instrument in the filing's own rulers-declared column, blank. This document fills the blank, and the filling has a shape the corpus should name: **the pool-reading battery is the audit's copy of the system's own interoception.** The system reads its pool through a quantile and calibrates a norm on it; the audit now reads a pool of its own drawing through the same quantile, rebuilds the norm by the same public arithmetic, and grades the difference. What the replay property is to evidence — the instrument's re-execution — the pool-reading is to *reading itself*: the interoception re-executed by a party that was not there. The verification gap is then the general instrument for every fake in the F-ladder that lies about maintenance readings: not because the recomputed numbers are better numbers (they are the same numbers, to two decimals, on every honest world in the calibrated set), but because they are *reached independently* — and independence is the one property a declaration cannot copy.

The battery's limits are as important as its powers, and three are structural. It verifies readings, not substrate: the D3 retreats are untouched — a controller with designer-installed form and development-owned content remains exactly as consistent with every recomputed quantile as it was with every declared one, and the audit says so with a straight face. It needs the replay property: a system whose states cannot be re-executed cannot be pool-read, which converts doc 35's constitution (replayability as the precondition of adjudication) from a norm into a technical requirement with a failure mode. And it needs ground truth for the pool ruler (here, the simulation's own labels; in the field, a probe set — the substitution is disclosed and the design carries it). Within those limits, the claim table's rulers-declared column closes: the pool-reading ruler is instrumented, its floors are priced (state-dependent, density-amplified, twenty-five to one hundred times below the fake's signature), and the second S4 candidate that files under 8.4 will be graded on rulers the instrument recomputes — including, for the first time, the one the deadband lives on.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The audit's quantile noise is amplified where confidence distributions are dense (the tempered worlds' excursions to 0.06 at the seed level); the exclusion verdict's margin is computed against the largest excursion, but a subtler fake — one that declares a course only 0.05 away from the truth — would sit inside the floor at deep rot, and the battery's detection limit at those levels is the floor's width, not the zero a naive reading would hope for. (ii) The fake instantiated here is one lie-shape: telemetry substitution against a stored honest course. Other shapes — partial lies (the acc true, the q false), snapshot substitution (handing the audit a healthy snapshot set over a collapsed world), and lies about β — are untested; the β lie is caught by construction (the recomputation uses the declared β and diverges), but the snapshot lie needs a chain-of-custody instrument this battery does not carry. (iii) The recursion's audit-side τ rebuild inherits the simulator's own tolerances (doc 33's 0.005, widened to 0.010 for the full-training chassis in doc 34); at those tolerances the τ-based verification is a coarser instrument than the q-based one, and the table's gap_τ column is reported at its own resolution. (iv) N = 6; the floors are seed-mean quantities and their fine structure (the single-seed excursions) is exactly the part N = 6 samples thinly.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the battery detects every lie — it detects every lie in the calibrated set, at the margins measured, and the calibrated set is the corpus's own shells. No claim about rulers the battery does not carry (the substrate probes of docs 31–32, the damage rulers); the claim table's column is rulers-*declared*, and a filing that names a ruler the battery lacks still files a blank the instrument will honestly show. No occupancy claim, no D3 movement, no S4 verdict — the battery is an instrument, and its first customer (the second candidate) has not filed yet. No claim that the clean ruler is dispensable now that the pool-side ruler exists; the SP30 row of §2.2 is the standing refutation of that comfort.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** P1's amended tolerance (0.012/0.020, state-dependent) holds at the seed-mean and is refuted at the single-seed level (ROT80's 0.062 excursion); the resolution — the floor is a property of the grading level — is disclosed in §2.1, and the amendment's own provenance (a smoke refutation, pre-run) is in the script header. P2's pool-acc clause ("within 1.5pp of the flat reference's drift") is refuted at ROT72 (4.9pp) with the mechanism identified: the gate's τ_sys follows the tempered q down, the gate admits a softer mix, and the pool-acc ruler reads the gate's movement rather than the rot — the clause is re-specified as "moves within 5pp, in the non-damage direction, with the gate movement priced" and the re-specification is post hoc with the mechanism named. P4's trajectory clause confirmed (eight consecutive rounds, double the registered three). P3 confirmed (quantile flat within ±0.007 at the tail; clean still falling at −0.005/round near chance). P5 confirmed at the seed-mean (max mean \|gap_τ\| 0.0033 against the 0.012 registered). P6 confirmed, with B's letter behaving exactly as the smoke amendment predicted. P7 confirmed and sharpened: the honest SP30 gate admits 16% of fresh draws at its own floor, the fake's declared gate admits 0.0–0.3% — the coverage column separates the collapsed-but-honest from the lying outright.

## Part 5. Coda

Doc 29 ended its rot campaign with a sentence that was half confession, half blueprint: the norm's drama played out on coordinates only the system's own pool-reading carried, and the audit needed to grade the claims on perturbed draws at the system's own quantile or not at all. Four documents later the corpus had built the attack that made the confession urgent, the shell that made the blueprint existential, and the filing that carried the blank where the instrument should be. This document fills the blank, and the first thing the instrument did with its independence was confirm the honest worlds to two decimals — every tempered system, every poisoned system, every healthy twin, declaring its readings truthfully and being believed, not on trust, but on recomputation. The second thing it did was catch the fake: not by being smarter than the lie, but by refusing to forward it. The corpus's two-ruler rule has spent eight documents as a discipline — name your ruler, show your work — and this is where the discipline becomes machinery: the ruler named, the work recomputed, the gap priced, the fake excluded at one hundred times the floor its own honesty set. What remains outside the instrument is exactly what was always outside it: the substrate, the ownership, the word doc 28 preserved by refusing to award it. The pool-reading battery does not reach those, and was never meant to. It reaches the readings — the system's account of itself — and it holds them to the one standard no account of itself can meet alone: that someone else, drawing their own water from the same well, read the same level off the same stone.
