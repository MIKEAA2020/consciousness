## Part 1. Lemma 77 — the projection lemma

**Standing setup (docs 24/27/38/76).** ChuCost over the additive home V = ([0, ∞], +, 0) or the capped home ([0, K], ⊕, 0); objects (A, X, r), morphisms (f, g) with the lax condition s(f(a), y) ≤ r(a, g(y)); the Dialectica tensor with the unit I = (1, 1, 0); the fiber Q with dim aff Q(M_a, M_b) = a²(b² − 1) (Lemma 41). A fragment Q′ ⊆ ChuCost is full; the Con-equivalence E: Q′ → Q is fully faithful with every hom-map Q′(X, Y) → Q(EX, EY) affine; δ(X) denotes the dimension of EX. Write Z(X) for the zero-cost region of a profile object X = (A, r̄: A → [0, ∞]): Z(X) = {a | r̄(a) ≤ 0} = r̄⁻¹(0).

**Lemma 77 (the projection lemma) [Derived here].** Let X = (A, r̄) and Y = (B, s̄) be profile objects (one-point backward carriers). Then: (i) the coordinate map π_A: A × B → A, (a, b) ↦ a, together with the trivial backward map, is a ChuCost morphism X ⊗ Y → X, and likewise π_B; (ii) consequently, for any full fragment Q′ containing I, X, Y and their tensor, the map
  Φ = (c_{π_A}, c_{π_B}): Q′(I, X ⊗ Y) → Q′(I, X) × Q′(I, Y),  h ↦ (π_A ∘ h, π_B ∘ h),
is an affine bijection, where the codomain carries the product convex structure; in particular dim aff Q′(I, X⊗Y) = dim aff Q′(I, X) + dim aff Q′(I, Y). *Proof.* (i) The tensor of profile objects is the profile object (A × B, r̄ + s̄) with (r̄ + s̄)(a, b) = r̄(a) + s̄(b) (capped: min(r̄(a) + s̄(b), K)); the lax condition for (π_A, id) reads r̄(π_A(a, b)) ≤ (r̄ + s̄)((a, b), ∗), i.e. r̄(a) ≤ r̄(a) + s̄(b), which holds for every (a, b) because costs are non-negative (capped: r̄(a) ≤ min(r̄(a) + s̄(b), K) — the right side is ≥ r̄(a) when r̄(a) + s̄(b) ≥ r̄(a), i.e. always, and capped at K ≥ r̄(a)). (ii) The components c_{π_A} and c_{π_B} are post-composition maps, affine in the hom argument because the Con-enrichment's composition is affine in each variable; a set-map into a product of convex sets is affine if and only if its components are (the product structure is coordinatewise); hence Φ is affine. On underlying sets: a morphism h: I → X⊗Y is a choice of zero-cost point of the tensor — an element of Z(X⊗Y) = Z(X) × Z(Y) (the profile sum vanishes exactly where both summands vanish) — and Φ(h) = (π_A(h), π_B(h)) is that same pair, so Φ is the identity Z(X)×Z(Y) → Z(X)×Z(Y): a bijection. An affine bijection carries affine hulls onto affine hulls, and the hull of a product is the product of the hulls; dimensions add. ∎

The lemma's reading: the enrichment, however abstract its convex structures on the hom-sets, cannot disguise the tensor's product shape at the unit — because the projections are morphisms (fullness puts them in the fragment for free) and the enrichment must make post-composition by them affine. Where doc 76's Theorem BB had to grant the fragment an adjunction to force a dimension, Lemma 77 needs only the fragment's own tensor. And the companion observation, which will matter to the MV analogue (doc 80): the proof spends non-negativity of costs and additivity of the profile sum. Under a composition law that can REDUCE a cost — the Łukasiewicz ⊙, for which the composed pairing r̄(a) ⊙ s̄(b) = max(0, r̄(a) + s̄(b) − K) sits BELOW r̄(a) — the inequality r̄(a) ≤ r̄(a) ⊙ s̄(b) fails, the projections are not morphisms, and the lemma does not transport. The kill below is therefore a property of the additive-composition homes specifically.

## Part 2. Theorem CC — the filed cell is empty

**Theorem CC (no fiber-equivalent fragment exists, in the additive and capped homes) [Derived here; Lemma 41 doc 38's, Corollary BB.1's arithmetic doc 76's, Lemma 77 this document's].** Let Q′ be a full subcategory of ChuCost (additive or capped home) that is closed under the Dialectica tensor, contains the monoidal unit I, and carries a Con-equivalence E: Q′ → Q onto a subcategory of the fiber whose image contains some matrix algebra of dimension ≥ 2. Then no such Q′ exists. *Proof.* Suppose one does; write δ(X) for the fiber dimension of EX, and fix an object A with d := δ(A) ≥ 2.

**(1) The unit arithmetic.** Q′(I, I) is a single point (the unique pair of identity maps on one-point carriers; fullness makes ChuCost(I, I) = Q′(I, I)), so 0 = dim aff Q′(I, I) = δ(I)²(δ(I)² − 1), forcing δ(I) = 1. For every X: dim aff Q′(X, I) = δ(X)²(δ(I)² − 1) = 0, and the hom is nonempty (the fiber hom Q(EX, EI) contains the trace-then-prepare channel, and the affine bijection transports nonemptiness); a nonempty convex set of affine dimension 0 is a point. As a set, Q′(X, I) is the backward carrier X_X (the forward map A_X → 1 is unique, the backward map 1 → X_X picks a point, and the lax condition 0 ≤ r(a, x₀) is always true): so X_X is a single point — every object of Q′ is a profile object (A_X, r̄), exactly Corollary BB.1's constraint, rederived without the adjunction.

**(2) The transport.** For every profile object W ∈ Q′: dim aff Q′(I, W) = δ(I)²(δ(W)² − 1) = δ(W)² − 1 (Lemma 41 at input dimension 1: the fiber hom Q(M₁, M_{δ(W)}) is the state space of M_{δ(W)}).

**(3) The iterate equation.** By Lemma 77 and induction on n ≥ 1: dim aff Q′(I, X⊗n) = n · dim aff Q′(I, X), where X⊗n is any n-fold tensor bracketing (the projections onto each coordinate and onto the remaining block are morphisms at every step, and the joint map is the identity Z(X)ⁿ → Z(X)ⁿ on underlying sets). Combining with (2) at W = X and W = X⊗n:
  δ(X⊗n)² − 1 = n · (δ(X)² − 1),  i.e.  δ(X⊗n)² = n·m + 1,  where m := d² − 1 ≥ 3.

**(4) The kill.** Take n = m. Then δ(X⊗m)² = m² + 1. But m² < m² + 1 < (m+1)² for every m ≥ 1 (the right inequality: 1 < 2m + 1), so m² + 1 is not a perfect square, while δ(X⊗m) is a positive integer — its square is a perfect square. Contradiction. The m-fold tensor power X⊗m lies in Q′ by tensor-closure (it exists as an object), its fiber dimension is a positive integer, and the arithmetic of its own zero-cost region forbids it. ∎

**The witness, read aloud.** Take the fragment's nontrivial object and build its tensor powers: the zero-cost regions multiply (Z(X⊗n) = Z(X)ⁿ — set-level, immune to enrichment), so the affine dimension at the unit grows linearly, n·m; the fiber arithmetic demands the same dimension be a square minus one, δ(X⊗n)² − 1; and at the m-th iterate the two readings ask for δ² = m² + 1 — one above a square, below the next. Where Lemma 76's diagonal kill asked the ADJOINT for the square root of d⁴ − d² + 1 (an interval inside (d²−1)² and (d²)²), the iterate kill asks the TENSOR for the square root of m² + 1 (an interval inside m² and (m+1)²) — the same grammar, one level down, and one level earlier: the adjunction BB needed never exists to be killed, because the fragment does not exist to carry it.

**Where each hypothesis was spent.** Fullness gave the projections and the unit's endomorphism; tensor-closure gave the iterates; the unit gave the hom-from-unit channel and forced the profile shape; the Con-equivalence gave the dimension transport (†); nontriviality gave m ≥ 3 (any m ≥ 1 would do — even d = 2 dies at its 3rd power); repleteness, monoidality of E, adjoint-freeness — never consulted. The emptiness is robust to every weakening the filed cell could have hoped for.
