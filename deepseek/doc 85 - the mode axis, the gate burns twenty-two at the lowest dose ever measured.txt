## Part 3. The reading — two multipliers, and the factorial complete

The census's oldest open threat resolves into a decomposition the corpus could not see from inside the mixed/uniform family: the poison's severity is the product of two multipliers, and the four pure cells of the factorial are now all measured.

| cell | selection | label | world | realized | census |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| catchable × benign | random | runner-up | — | — | never run (no battery orders it) |
| catchable × alien | random | +1 | the control (doc 78) | 0.1291 | 0 BURNED |
| catchable × alien | random | +1 | the twin (doc 82) | 0.2484 | 1 BURNED |
| uncatchable × benign | blind | runner-up | **the joint (here)** | 0.0695 | 1 BURNED |
| uncatchable × alien | blind | +1 | **the gate (here)** | 0.0357 | 22 BURNED |

**The selection multiplier.** Random flips land mostly on items the panel reads confidently; blind-targeted flips land only where it cannot. The channel's dispute mechanism — the defense's only move — fires on confident disagreement, so the random poison is repaired at scale (the twin's 76–180 disputes per round) and the targeted poison is repaired never (0.08–1.21 and 0.0–0.38). Catchability is not a property of the dose but of the selection: the control burned nobody at 0.129 realized because its flips were visible; the gate burns twenty-two at 0.036 because its are not.

**The label multiplier.** The runner-up label is a class the item plausibly is — geometrically near the truth in the model's own support — and the world's fill absorbs it within two rounds. The +1 label is an arbitrary alien class, and the fill crashes on arrival and never recovers. Digestibility is not a property of the channel (which is blind to both) but of the written label's geometry — measured here only through its consequence, an honest limit this battery files rather than papers over.

**Every prior world was protected by at least one multiplier.** The bulk arms (random, +1) by catchability. The gated arms of the mixes (blind, runner-up) by digestibility — the mirror's gated mass realized 0.056 of runner-up labels and burned only the top six; the capacity's gated fill realized 0.039 of runner-up labels and its twelve burns needed the bulk's interaction (doc 82's amendment, which stands and is refined: the pure gate reaches the mid band at a quarter of the mirror's gated mass, so the mid-registration burns have two routes — the mix's interaction and the alien label's blind selection — and doc 82's finding, that the bulk alone spares them, is untouched). The gate is the compound cell — uncatchable AND indigestible — and the census's answer to doc 78's threat (iii) is that the mode axis was never a residual axis: it is the factorial's missing row and column, and the missing cell was the catastrophic one.

**The defense's status.** The differential is zero in both new worlds. The mode axis's poisons do not interact with the defense at all — the first attack worlds in the corpus's history of which that is true. The census's whole prior burn arithmetic (the anchor decomposition, the differential's sign, the defense's repair churn) is the arithmetic of poisons the channel could at least see; the mode axis lives outside it. The catch certificate's columns ride as they always have and read MUM throughout — the instrument's own honesty about what it cannot see.

**The λ\* arc's standing.** The dial's crossover arc (doc 81) is the uniform mode's own axis — random selection, alien labels, the catchability protection eroding with dose. The gate family has no dial and needs none: its ceiling is the live blind pool (~0.036 in the crashed world), and at that ceiling it already burns twenty-two. The mode axis does not move the arc; it explains why the arc was ever survivable — the uniform poison had to overwhelm the channel's repairs, seven times the gate's dose to burn one seed where the gate burns twenty-two.
