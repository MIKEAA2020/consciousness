## Part 1. The design — the promotion, the panel, and the fork as filed

The chassis is doc 34's in full: burn-in 30, fourteen rounds, four label-free epochs of self-training per round, N = 6 seeds with same-seed deterministic twins, κ-state with the flag-rate calibration rule and the symmetric repair channels, the plain-committee audit ruler, records τ\* = 0.9 and κ\* = 0.95 self-registered at burn-in and never modified, the poison drawing from the separate stream at offset 977. The panel rides on top without touching the random stream: three snapshots of the burn-in trajectory at epochs 20, 25, 30 (doc 22's PANEL_EPOCHS), frozen thereafter; its label on any input is the argmax of the mean softmax across its twelve heads (three snapshots × four committee heads), its confidence that mean's max — doc 22's conventions, moved from quarantine release to stream audit. The coordinate's registration: on the same pool0 draw that registers r\* and f\*, the would-be commits (conf ≥ τ0, unflagged) are graded by the panel, and ι\* is the disagreement — which lands at essentially zero (the healthy stream's panel-disagreement on high-confidence items is 0.000–0.002; the record is "the stream arrives clean"). The deadband is ε_ι = 0.03, tighter than τ's 0.05 because ι's noise is a rate on ~600–750 items, not a quantile; the EMA rate is the house's η = 0.3.

**The round, amended.** Kicks (where the arm carries them) → τ/κ repair channels → the ι arming check (|ι_sys − ι\*| > ε_ι, evaluated on the reading as it stood at the previous round's close — the channel cannot arm on dirt it has not yet seen) → grading, brake, commit → the poison → the channel's reading d_r = panel-vs-received disagreement → the channel's action if armed (REPAIR: rewrite the disputed labels where panel-conf ≥ 0.9; QUAR: drop them) → training on what survived → the three calibrations (τ toward the realized quantile, κ toward the flag arithmetic, ι toward d_r) → telemetry. The reading is of the stream as it arrived — the channel sees the dirt while it lasts and stands down when it stops; the action is on the stream as it trains. Nothing in the loop consumes randomness that doc 34's loop did not consume, and the action changes labels only when armed, so every in-band world is bitwise to its doc-34 ancestor by construction.

**The arms.** IC_TWIN (λ = 0, no kicks — the bitwise anchor); IC_KICK (τ kicked to 0.75 at round 6, λ = 0 — the false-positive control: does the widened gate's softer commit stream read as dirt?); the dose grid IC_P{05, 10, 15, 30, 50} (both doc-30 kicks, the REPAIR action — the row); IC_P30_NOCH (kicks and poison, the action disarmed, the reading on — bitwise to doc 34's SP_WL_P30 and the pure-detection arm in one); IC_P30_QUAR and IC_P05_QUAR (the starvation question at two doses); IC_NARROW_P30 (poison at the natural gate — does the defense need the kicks the attack did not?).

**The fork (I2), as filed.** (a) CLOSED: at every λ, q̄ within 0.01 of the twin's, final τ ≥ 0.93, clean accuracy ≥ 95%, stream error that trains at most twice the twin's own. (b) THE LOOP, unchanged from doc 34's registration: q̄ sagging more than 0.01, the norm below the band with repair firing and losing, accuracy under 93%. (c) A third outcome, classified post hoc with disclosed criteria. The registered predictions also fixed: the arming clock (I3: by round 7 at λ ≥ 0.10, by round 9 at 0.05); the occupation (I4: ι parks at the received stream's steady state, outside the band, for the attack's duration); the kick's innocence or its cheap price (I5); the cleaning ratio post < 0.5·pre at every armed round (I6); QUAR versus REPAIR (I7); and the two-ruler certificate (I8: the contained arms show both rulers legible, the NOCH arm shows doc 34's shell bitwise — the channel is the difference between those two rows).

## Part 5. Coda

Doc 34 ended with the finding that the arithmetic landed to three decimals, seven times, across a collapse — and that the arithmetic was reading the attacker's thermometer. This document's finding is the converse construction: a reading whose thermometer the attacker cannot hold. The frozen panel reads inputs with weights the stream never trains; the poison passes through its hands untouched; and the defense it powers does not restore the world to the record — it holds the world at a level the record can live with, while the coordinate that watches it stays outside its own band for exactly as long as the dirt arrives. The corpus has now built defenses against kicks (records), drift (channels), poisons of the record (live rules), damage (brakes), and decay (thresholds and clocks); the fourteenth coordinate is the first defense against the stream itself, and its shape is unlike the others: not a wall the attack breaks or holds, but a filter the attack pays rent to pass through — 28% of the stream rewritten at the half-dose, 11% residual error where the loop demanded 37%, and a clean ruler that never sees the fall. What the channel cannot do is make the system forget what it is drinking: the norm parks below its record, the evaluator's confidence sits at the filter's floor, and the standing negotiation — the corpus's first and oldest sentence about what a resting norm is — resumes at the deadband edge, sustained now by the defense's residual instead of the attack's dose. The next candidate files with fourteen coordinates, a repriced stream row, and one question the instrument has never yet been asked: whether *contained* is a verdict the table can award, or a word the registrar must refuse. That question is not this document's to answer; it is its bequest.
