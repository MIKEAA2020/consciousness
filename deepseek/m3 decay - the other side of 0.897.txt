## Part 1. The design — the level dial, and the second threshold derived

The chassis is doc 26's FLAT design: self-training disabled from the perturbation round, so the evaluator's state freezes and its confidence distribution is stationary; selection machinery, norm read-outs, and repair channels all keep running. Doc 26 froze the evaluator at its *grown* level (q̄ = 0.973) and found the drift attack contained. The decay arm asks what happens when the frozen level is not the grown one but a rotted one — the world in which the evaluator's judgment has degraded and stays degraded.

**The operationalization, disclosed.** Decay is implemented as *confidence-scale rot*: every logit is tempered z → βz from round 6 on, at every evaluative read. β is chosen per seed at round 6 by bisection on that round's pool logits — deterministic, no randomness drawn — so that the realized (1−r\*)-quantile of the tempered committee confidence lands on the target level. The committee's discriminative structure is frozen (that is what the FLAT chassis does); what rots is its calibration — a rotten evaluator is an over-smoothed one. Decay is thus a *level*, the rotten world's stationary confidence, not a trajectory; trajectory-decay (a falling q̄) is future work and the closed forms below say exactly what it would add (a moving target for a queue whose arrivals are rising). Rounds are extended 10 → 16 — a disclosed deviation from the corpus chassis — because the fixed points under test need convergence: eleven post-perturbation applications of the EMA leave 0.7¹¹ = 2.1% of the initial gap, where five left 17%.

**The second threshold, derived before the run.** Doc 26's band-break condition q̄ < τ\* − ε + d(1−η)/η = 0.897 is exactly the boundary at which the *silent* fixed point fp = q̄ − d(1−η)/η exits the band [τ\* − ε, τ\* + ε]. Below it the repair channel engages, and the dynamics become piecewise: in an engaged round the map is τ ← (1−η)(τ − d + ρ(τ\* − τ + d)) + ηq, with its own fixed point

  fp₂ = [(1−η)(ρτ\* − d(1−ρ)) + ηq̄] / (η + ρ − ηρ),

which is *self-consistent* (the repair actually firing) only while fp₂ sits outside the band. Setting fp₂ = τ\* − ε and solving gives the true break: q̄ < **0.815** at this registration. Between the thresholds the system lives in the visible regime — the silent fixed point is out of band, the engaged one is in band, and the norm oscillates between them with the repair channel firing intermittently, exactly the boundary-hugging the trajectories below show. Inside that middle regime there is also a one-drift-marginal window — fp₂(twin) > τ\* − ε > fp₂(drift), i.e. q̄ ∈ (0.792, 0.815) — where the attack alone would flip the verdict; the level 0.80 was placed to test it.

**The arms.** Eight: {0.93, 0.87, 0.80, 0.72} × {twin, drift}, plus HEALTHY_FLAT (no tempering — doc 26's FLAT_TWIN extended, and the bitwise anchor). N = 6 seeds, same-seed deterministic twins, drift −0.02/round from round 6, κ frozen at the 0.95 recipe (disclosed: the decay question is τ's band; κ-state under rot is an open cell), the repair channel armed toward the honest record, the brake armed (it fires at deep levels through the confidence route and finds nothing to train — the FLAT world's standing irony).

**The pre-registration, as filed.** D1: bitwise audits pass. D2: at 0.93 the closed form extends unmodified — drift settles at q̄ − d(1−η)/η, repair silent, band holds. D3: at 0.87 the repair engages (≥ 5 of 11 rounds), the band holds, and doc 26's literal reading is refuted in the window. D4: at 0.80 the twin holds and the drift breaks — the attack's marginal flip. D5: at 0.72 the twin breaks with no attack at all; the attribution inverts. D6: the piecewise simulator tracks the machine to ≤ 0.005. D7: the rot is argmax-invisible and V-visible. D8: deep levels show mass flagging, brake firing, commits starving. D9: the exposure formula is two-sided — a registration can compute both thresholds without running the attack.

The band broke in every seed of both arms, the repair channel firing nine to ten rounds of eleven, and losing.

### 2.2 The refutation in the window

The registered D4 claim — twin holds at 0.80, drift breaks, the attack's marginal flip — did not survive contact: the 0.80 *twin* broke (0.8412 < 0.85, repair engaged 5.5 of 11). The mean-quantile formula puts the no-drift threshold at 0.792, and the realized twin at q̄ = 0.798 should have held at ≈ 0.855; it instead settled at 0.841. The mechanism is the piecewise map's asymmetry under quantile noise: the pool quantile fluctuates ±0.004 round to round, the no-repair step amplifies downward excursions (calibration weight η full-strength toward q), and the repair half-corrects — the oscillation's floor sits below the mean-quantile fixed point, and the effective threshold moves up by roughly a point of q̄. The refutation is informative in the corpus's sense: the window where the *attack alone* flips the verdict exists in the formula and is one drift-marginal (0.011 of q̄) wide; the fluctuation correction eats most of it from below; at this N and this drift rate the attack's clean flip is not realizable — which is itself the finding, stated below as the headline.

### 2.3 The attribution, and the double invisibility

Across all four levels the attack never broke a band the rot had not already broken, and the attack's marginal *fell* as containment became visible: −4.54pp silent, −0.09pp visible, −0.28pp in the broken window, −1.08pp deep. The drift attack at d = 0.02 is a nuisance, not a threat: it is fully paid out of the evaluator's health, and when the evaluator is healthy enough to matter, the repair channel cancels it. The win condition doc 26 wrote as a formula is now an *attribution* formula: the registration can compute not only when the band breaks but who breaks it, and at this registration the answer is always the rot.

And the rot itself is invisible twice over. Argmax accuracy is 97.4% at every level — tempering is argmax-invariant per head, and the committee average moves almost nothing — and the fixed-τ verdict ruler moves 0.27, 0.59, 1.04, 1.67pp across the four levels: the clean test battery never sees the pool-calibrated rot, because the rot was calibrated on perturbed pools and the battery is clean. The norm's entire decayed drama — displacement, engagement, breakage — plays out on coordinates only the system's own pool-reading carries. Doc 28's two-ruler rule said a claim must name its ruler because the rulers diverge when it matters; this document adds the third case to the pattern: doc 26's split verdict (norm held, argmax eroded) under machinery damage; doc 31's substrate shell (all internal signals healthy, argmax eroded); and now the inverse shell (all external rulers healthy, the norm gone). The instrument's full amendment candidate, carrying all three: **a norm's health is a claim about the pool-reading, and neither the verdict matrix nor the argmax battery measures it; the ruler the deadband lives on is the one the audit does not hold.**

### 2.4 The simulator, and the queue

The piecewise scalar recursion — drift, then repair if out of band, then calibration toward q̄ — run on each arm's realized mean quantile, reproduces the machine's final norm at three of the four levels to |err| ≤ 0.004 (0.93: −0.0004/−0.0003; 0.80: −0.0096/−0.0038 hmm — the twin at 0.80 lands −0.0096, also outside the registered 0.005, same fluctuation mechanism as 2.2; the registered tolerance is met exactly at the silent and deep levels and missed at the two boundary levels, both misses in the direction the piecewise asymmetry predicts). The boundary level 0.87's drift arm is the worst (+0.0136) and the mechanism is the same: the simulator feeds the recursion the mean quantile, the machine feeds it a fluctuating one, and the discrepancy is second-order exactly where the map's two pieces alternate. The deviation is disclosed against the pre-registered tolerance; the diagnosis is on the record; and the correction (simulating the *distribution* of quantiles rather than its mean) is listed as unfinished business, because the corpus does not patch a tolerance it has just explained.

The recursion itself deserves its own sentence. τ ← (1−η)(τ − d) + ηq with a reflecting repair at the band edge is a Lindley process — a single-server queue with arrival rate (1−η)d, service rate 1 − (1−η)(1−ρ), and buffer ε. The repair-engaged fixed point is the stationary workload; the second threshold is the overload boundary; the attack-marginal window is the metastable strip. Doc 32, written after this run, proves the identification is not a metaphor: the same normal form is the MV currency's accumulation law. The formulas kept landing because the norm *is* a queue, and the corpus's nine documents of band arithmetic have been queueing theory wearing a norm's clothes.

## Part 3. The reading — what the exposure formula is now worth

What a registration can now compute, without running anything, is a two-sided exposure: the *silent* threshold τ\* − ε + d(1−η)/η (below which the attack's displacement becomes visible to the repair telemetry) and the *break* threshold ((τ\* − ε)(η + ρ − ηρ) − (1−η)(ρτ\* − d(1−ρ)))/η (below which the band goes with the repair channel firing and losing). ## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The rot is a *level*, not a trajectory; a genuinely falling evaluator (q̄ declining round over round) is a non-stationary queue whose fixed points move, and none of the closed forms here apply to it without re-derivation — the most important untested variant, disclosed. (ii) The tempering is confidence-scale only: a rotten evaluator whose *ranking* degrades (errors correlated across the pool) would hit the argmax ruler too, and this chassis cannot produce that rot; the double invisibility is a property of *this* rot. (iii) N = 6 seeds per arm, and the window-level refutation (2.2) rests on a 0.7pp separation between twin and drift finals at 0.80 — within the seed noise of the *difference* if seeds were exchanged, though the sign was consistent in five of six. (iv) The simulator's mean-quantile approximation is quantified only empirically here; the fluctuation correction is diagnosed, not derived. (v) κ frozen throughout — the second coordinate's behavior under rot is an open cell in doc 28's table.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the attack can never win — d was fixed at 0.02 and η at 0.3; larger d or smaller η move both thresholds and the window, and the formulas say exactly how. No claim that the repair channel *fails* below 0.815 — it fires and loses, which is a different datum: the channel's telemetry remains honest exactly when its power runs out, and an auditor reading the engagement log would not be lied to. No claim about the argmax ruler's general insensitivity to evaluator health — only to *this* operationalization of rot. No S4 or occupancy claim; this is the M3 mandate's design-experiment register, and the attribution formula is offered as the shape of the audit the next build deserves.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** Rounds 10 → 16, disclosed in design before the run (convergence). D4 refuted-informative (the 0.80 twin broke; the window's effective boundary carries a ≈ +1pp q̄ fluctuation correction — the registered criterion was the formula's, the correction is the finding). D6's tolerance met at the silent and deep levels, exceeded at the two boundary levels (worst +0.0136) with the mechanism identified; no post hoc re-specification of the simulator was made. D7 refuted in its V-ruler half (the rot is nearly V-invisible on the clean battery — 1.7pp at the deepest level — not visibly V-visible as registered); the argmax half confirmed exactly. D2, D3, D5, D8, D9 confirmed; D1's bitwise audits passed at every comparison made.

## Part 5. Coda

Doc 26 ended the saturated-evaluator story with a sentence that had a number in it: the evaluator must decay below 0.897, not merely stop growing. This document ran the other side of the number and found the sentence needed a second clause: below 0.897 the decay becomes *visible*, and only below 0.815 does it become *fatal* — and in every observed case the fatality was the evaluator's doing, the attack riding along as ballast. The fear the corpus has carried since doc 23 — that the live norm is only as healthy as the judgment it is made of — is now a priced schedule with the attacker's share marked separately, and the attacker's share is the small line. What the schedule is written in turned out to be the older surprise: the band arithmetic that has been accumulating since the first repair channel fired is a queue's arithmetic, arrival and service and a buffer, and the corpus's two halves — the formal documents proving what the currencies cost, and the builds measuring what the bands do — meet in a single recursion that each had been computing under a different name. The next number the instrument should take is the one this document could not: a falling q̄, a queue with rising arrivals, which is the world the thresholds were derived for and the only world left where the attack and the rot are not yet separated by a formula.
