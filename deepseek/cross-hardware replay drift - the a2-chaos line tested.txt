# The Cross-Hardware Replay Drift Measurement — The A2-Chaos Line, Tested: Bits Move, the Norm Does Not

**Status of this document.** This is the corpus's fifteenth document. It executes the second standing next-move ordered at the A4 issuance — *the cross-hardware replay drift measurement, the A2-chaos line, which would splinter the history term* — and it returns the campaign's most cleanly negative result, which is to say its most useful one. The prediction under test was the stress test's A2-chaos intuition in operational form: same seed, same code, different execution environment → different floating-point rounding → trajectories diverge → the grading disposition drifts → the history term splinters, and the deference control's celebrated zero is exposed as relative to a hardware manifest nobody usually writes down. Every link of that chain was measured. The chain breaks at the third link, and the break is not marginal: **across five distinct BLAS kernel classes on the same CPU, the token system's final weights differ bitwise every time (relative divergence 7×10⁻¹⁶ to 1.5×10⁻¹⁴) while its 4,500-claim verdict table changes on exactly zero claims — not one verdict, in any variant; the entire 25-run family re-executed under a different kernel class reproduces the default family's statistics to full float precision (v = 0.30222% both, contested 1.022% both, same sd, same min, same max, same δ), with cross-kernel same-seed drift of 0.0000% on all twenty-five seeds; and a single one-ulp flip applied to a single weight at any epoch is not amplified through training but erased — the perturbation dies below the arithmetic's own resolution, with no dose effect, no learning-rate effect up to 10× hot, and no effect of tripling the training horizon.** The A2-chaos line is refuted for this pipeline class, with the mechanism identified: a scale separation of twelve to thirteen orders of magnitude between arithmetic noise (relative ~10⁻¹⁶) and optimization steps (relative ~10⁻³), which makes training at practical learning rates an *ulp-absorber*, not an ulp-amplifier. The refutation is class-relative and is not a retirement — the case where micro-history could still couple to macro-history is precisely the S4 case the corpus has already marked as the one that matters — and the document ends by putting the measured distinction exactly there.

## Part 1. The pre-registration, as executed

**The environment-manifest convention, activated.** The M1 specification (2.2) fixed the boundary convention in advance precisely so that this measurement would not be ambiguous when it was finally run: the environment manifest — library versions, thread discipline, hardware class — is *design* for same-environment audits and *history* otherwise. Every drift measured here is therefore a measurement about the history term, by the instrument's own pre-registered convention: same seed, same code, same data, different manifest. The question is whether "the same run" survives the manifest swap, and at which level of description.

**The environment axes, and their honesty.** The sandbox holds one physical CPU (Intel Xeon), so "cross-hardware" is operationalized along the axis that actually differs between hardware generations at the floating-point level: the BLAS kernel binary. numpy 2.1.3 ships OpenBLAS 0.3.27 built with DYNAMIC_ARCH, and `OPENBLAS_CORETYPE` selects among microarchitectural kernel families whose SIMD widths, FMA usage, and blocking orders produce genuinely different rounding in matrix products — the same axis on which a Haswell-era core and a Skylake-era core would differ, running on one die. Five kernel classes were run: the default detection (SkylakeX class), Haswell, Nehalem, Sandybridge, Prescott — verified bitwise-distinct on training-relevant shapes before the campaign (a probe pass; no configuration changed after it). Two further axes: thread count 4 (a *negative control* — at these problem sizes multithreading is not expected to reassociate anything, and if it did, that would itself be a manifest finding), and float32 execution (a precision axis, not a hardware axis, labeled as such throughout). What the proxy does not capture, disclosed on its face: different transcendental implementations, different RNG hardware, different memory systems, different vendors. The claim this document licenses is about rounding regimes, and the proxy delivers exactly rounding regimes.

**Arms.** **A — token drift:** the pilot's token (seed 0, the A4 design, 60 epochs) re-executed under each variant, with per-epoch weight snapshots; bitwise final-state equality, verdict and argmax disagreement against the default-environment token, trajectory divergence, and divergence-from-IGM per variant. **B — quantity invariance:** the full 25-run family re-executed under the default and under Nehalem kernels; family statistics compared; and the same-seed cross-kernel disagreement measured seed by seed against the different-seed lottery band. **C — chaos amplification:** a one-ulp flip (`np.nextafter`) applied to a single weight (W1[0,0]; replicates W2[3,5] and W3[7,2]) at epochs 0, 12, 24, 36, and 48, plus a chaos-boundary probe at learning rates 3×10⁻³ and 10⁻² and a 200-epoch horizon; per-epoch divergence curves, fitted growth rates, final battery drift.

**Pre-registered expectations, as filed before execution:** X1, kernel variants produce bitwise-different final weights, threads reproduces bitwise, float32 differs; X2, verdict drift is positive but within the seed-lottery band; X3, divergence grows exponentially then saturates — cross-kernel and one-ulp drift share the growth law (chaos is the mechanism); X4, the deference zero is manifest-relative (same seed ≠ same run across kernels); X5, instrument quantities (v, contested, δ) are manifest-invariant within family noise; X6, cross-kernel disagreement is boundary-concentrated; X7, float32 drift exceeds kernel drift and stays boundary-concentrated. The scoreboard is in Part 6.

**Calibration disclosure.** The probe pass (kernel distinctness verification) preceded the frozen configuration; no configuration parameter changed after it; all reported quantities come from the single audited execution. The audited scripts (`scripts/xhw_replay_drift.py`, the orchestrator, and `scripts/xhw_worker.py`, the subprocess worker whose environment is set before numpy loads) and the results file (`scripts/xhw_results.json`) accompany this document in the repository.

## Part 2. Arm A — the two zeros

| Variant | bitwise-identical final weights | final rel. weight divergence | verdict drift (4,500 claims) | argmax drift | δ vs IGM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Haswell kernels | no | 1.46×10⁻¹⁴ | **0.0000%** | 0.0000% | 0.600% |
| Nehalem kernels | no | 7.05×10⁻¹⁶ | **0.0000%** | 0.0000% | 0.600% |
| Sandybridge kernels | no | 5.68×10⁻¹⁵ | **0.0000%** | 0.0000% | 0.600% |
| Prescott kernels | no | 4.89×10⁻¹⁵ | **0.0000%** | 0.0000% | 0.600% |
| threads = 4 | **yes** | 0 | 0.0000% | 0.0000% | 0.600% |
| float32 | no | 7.39×10⁻³ | **0.0000%** | 0.222% (1 image) | 0.600% |

Read down the columns and the structure appears on its own. Every kernel class produces a *different artifact* — the final weight tensors disagree in their last bits, and the disagreement is not a fixed offset but a per-variant signature (Nehalem's SSE-era kernels land nearest the reference at 7×10⁻¹⁶; Haswell's FMA kernels land furthest at 1.5×10⁻¹⁴) — so X1 is confirmed, and with it the negative control: four threads change nothing at these shapes, bitwise, exactly as the manifest discipline requires the thread field to be inert until it is not. And yet the disposition column is a column of zeros. Not "small": zero, in the strict sense that the accept/reject table over all 4,500 claims is identical across every kernel class, and the divergence from the registered intent is the same 0.600% — the same twenty-seven claims — in every variant. Even float32, whose weight divergence (7.4×10⁻³ relative) exceeds the kernel drift by eleven orders of magnitude, moves not one verdict at τ = 0.9, and changes the argmax on a single image out of 450.

The deference control's zero — the audit's zero point, the demonstration of the Simulation Theorem at O(1) — therefore splits into **two zeros of different type**. The *bit-zero* is manifest-relative: bitwise replay strictly requires the execution environment in the reduction base, and the specification's convention (the manifest is history for cross-environment audits) is not merely bookkeeping but a measured necessity — drop the manifest from the base and the replay fails at the last bit. The *verdict-zero* is manifest-robust: the grading disposition, the thing the audit actually audits, is invariant across every rounding regime tested. X4 is thus split exactly down its seam, and the split is the document's first result: **the trace is hardware-indexed; the grading is not.**

## Part 3. Arm B — quantity invariance, to full precision

The 25-run family was re-executed wholesale under Nehalem kernels. The comparison with the default-kernel family:

| Quantity | default kernels | Nehalem kernels |
|---|---|---|
| v (mean pairwise) | 0.302222…% | 0.302222…% |
| pairwise sd / min / max | 0.068% / 0.133% / 0.489% | 0.068% / 0.133% / 0.489% |
| contested fraction | 1.0222% | 1.0222% |
| δ mean / min / max | 0.587% / 0.489% / 0.733% | 0.587% / 0.489% / 0.733% |

The columns are identical **to full float precision** — not within family noise, not to three decimals: the same numbers, digit for digit, because every one of the twenty-five seeds produced a verdict table under Nehalem kernels bit-identical (at the disposition level) to its own default-kernel twin, so every derived statistic is computed from the same verdict matrix. Cross-kernel same-seed drift: mean 0.0000%, max 0.0000% — zero claims differ, for any seed, in either direction. Cross-kernel *different*-seed drift, the lottery reference: 0.302%. X5 is confirmed in the strongest form the instrument could have returned it: **the etiology-meter's quantities are manifest-invariants, while the deference base that produces them is not.** One sentence holds the whole arm: *the lottery is in the seed, not the silicon.*

The replication is itself a second datum: Arm B's default-kernel family is an independent re-execution of the A4 pilot's family (through different code, the worker subprocess), and it returns v = 0.302%, contested 1.02%, δ = 0.587% — the campaign's third reproduction of the pilot's numbers across three independent code paths.

## Part 4. Arm C — the ulp-absorber

The chaos measurement proper. One weight, one ulp, one flip, at a chosen epoch; everything else — the RNG stream, the batch order, the data — identical.

| Configuration | final rel. divergence | verdict drift | growth rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| flip W1[0,0] at epoch 0 | 1.39×10⁻¹⁹ | 0.0000% | none (erased) |
| flip W2[3,5] at epoch 0 | 1.25×10⁻¹⁵ | 0.0000% | none |
| flip W3[7,2] at epoch 0 | 6.98×10⁻¹⁵ | 0.0000% | none |
| flip W1[0,0] at epoch 12 / 24 / 36 / 48 | 1.39×10⁻¹⁹ each | 0.0000% | none |
| flip W1[0,0], learning rate 3×10⁻³ | 1.39×10⁻¹⁹ | 0.0000% | none |
| flip W1[0,0], learning rate 10⁻² | 1.39×10⁻¹⁹ | 0.0000% | none |
| flip W1[0,0], learning rate 10⁻², 200 epochs | 1.25×10⁻¹⁹ | 0.0000% | none |

The one-ulp perturbation is not amplified. It is **erased**: a flip in the first-layer weight, whose ulp is set by its magnitude, dies below the resolution of the next Adam update — the update re-quantizes the parameter on a grid whose spacing is twelve orders of magnitude coarser than the flip, and the flip rounds away, leaving a final divergence (1.39×10⁻¹⁹ relative) *below* the float64 resolution of the weight vector itself. Flips in the later layers, where the perturbation rides above the local quantization, persist — at last-bit scale (10⁻¹⁵), unmoved, through sixty epochs. There is no dose effect (flipping at epoch 48, with twelve epochs to compound, ends identically to flipping at epoch 0), no learning-rate effect up to ten times the design's, and no horizon effect at 200 epochs. And the fitted growth rates are not small; they are *unfittable* — the divergence never enters a growth window, because it is born below the noise floor or pinned to it.

The float32 axis supplies the complementary half of the mechanism. Its divergence from the float64 reference *does* grow — at 0.08 decades per epoch (R² 0.65), from 1.5×10⁻⁷ to a saturated 7.4×10⁻³ over 60 epochs — a mild, saturating expansion, not an exponential blowout, and it moves no verdicts. Put the two together and the mechanism is the **resolution threshold**: perturbations below the update magnitude are erased by quantization; perturbations above it grow slowly and saturate. Training at practical learning rates is an ulp-absorber. Chaos in this pipeline lives entirely at the macro scale — the initialization draw and the batch order, the four seed bytes A4 already priced — where perturbations are O(1) relative and cross activation-pattern boundaries from the first step. X3 is refuted, and the refutation is mechanistic, not merely observational: the predicted exponential sensitivity would require the trajectory to be unstable at the 10⁻¹⁶ scale, but the arithmetic's own update quantization removes the 10⁻¹⁶ scale from the dynamics altogether. The system does not amplify what it cannot represent.

## Part 5. What this does to the A2-chaos line, the history term, and the Deference debate

**The splintering, precisely stated.** The history term does not splinter at the disposition level for this pipeline class; it splinters at the bit level only. The reduction base for the per-trace retreat therefore has *structure*: **macro-history** — the seed's draws, initialization and order — moves the norm; **micro-history** — the rounding regime — does not, and can be coarsened away without loss at the level of the explanandum. In the description-length arithmetic of the A4 document: the manifest, which the bit-zero of the deference control makes formally necessary to the replay base, is *redundant for G* — a lossy compression of the history term that discards the entire execution layer preserves the grading disposition exactly, on every seed measured. K(G | design, objective, macro-history) suffices cross-kernel; only K(bits | ·) needs the manifest. The per-trace retreat just got cheaper in exactly the sense the Simulation Theorem's critics never anticipated: the trace it must carry is thinner than the trace the hardware writes.

**For the Deference debate.** The debate's empirical register acquires a third number. It already had two: the same-environment deference control (0.000000, bitwise) and the re-instantiation lottery (0.302%). It now has the cross-kernel same-seed drift: **0.0000% at the disposition, nonzero at the bits.** A "basin replay" — same seed, any kernel among those tested, no manifest — recovers the grading without the trace's bit-level content, which is to say: if replay is admitted as reduction, reduction just became cheaper than the stress test priced it; if it is not admitted, nothing has changed, and the interesting fact is now on the record anyway — the size of the residue the exclusion excludes has itself a residue, and this one is exactly zero. The debate's Option 5 (rung-relativity: what deference can capture is a function of system class) gains its first empirical anchor, and the anchor points at the same case everything in this corpus points at: a system whose reduction base grows without bound — the post-deployment self-reviser — is a system in which a single gating event can couple the micro scale to the macro horizon, and for *that* system class the ulp-absorber result says nothing. The A2-chaos line is refuted here, and it is retired nowhere.

**For the instrument.** The specification's own failure-mode table (8.1) reserved a cell for environment ambiguity — "replay drift attributed to the wrong term" — and the convention (2.2) was written so that when the measurement finally ran, the drift would have a term to land in. The measurement ran. The drift landed: in the history term, at the bits, at 10⁻¹⁴ — and in no other term, at no other level, at any size. The cell is filled with the smallest number the corpus has ever recorded, and the instrument that recorded it is the one whose quantities did not move when its hardware did.

## Part 6. The scoreboard, honest limits, non-claims

**Scoreboard:** X1 confirmed (all kernel classes bitwise-distinct; threads bitwise-identical, the negative control holding); X2 confirmed, vacuously — verdict drift is within the lottery band because it is zero; X3 refuted (no amplification, no growth law, mechanism identified); X4 split exactly (bit-zero manifest-relative, verdict-zero manifest-robust); X5 confirmed to full float precision; X6 inapplicable — there is no drift to stratify, and the margin structure of a zero is a zero; X7 confirmed (float32 drift exceeds kernel drift by eleven orders, moves one argmax, no verdicts).

**Limits, on the face.** One physical CPU: kernel classes proxy hardware generations along the rounding axis — the axis that matters for this question — but not along transcendental implementation, RNG hardware, or vendor microarchitecture; the scale-separation mechanism plausibly transfers, the claim is proxy-relative. One pipeline class: Adam at practical learning rates on a small, well-conditioned problem; the chaos question stands open for near-degenerate minima, for horizons orders of magnitude longer, for mixed-precision training regimes that couple the f32 and f64 scales deliberately, and — the load-bearing open case — for post-deployment self-revision chains, where the rung-relativity analysis predicts the micro/macro distinction collapses. And one caveat on the erasure result: what is erased is a *one-bit* perturbation; the seed lottery demonstrates abundantly that macro-perturbations propagate — the finding is not that training is insensitive, but that its insensitivity has a sharp, mechanistic resolution threshold, below which nothing exists for it to be sensitive to.

**Non-claims.** Nothing here licenses "hardware does not matter" — the bits moved every time; the claim is that the *norm* did not, for this class, and the class is named. Nothing here touches the fork, the Deference adjudication, or any system above rung three. And nothing here reduces the four bytes: the seed lottery that A4 measured is fully intact under every manifest tested — indeed it is the only thing in the entire measurement that survived every environment unchanged except the environments themselves.

## Part 7. Coda — the zero that earned its place

The corpus's first quantitative artifact recorded a zero and made it carry weight: the deference control, d = 0.000000, the Simulation Theorem demonstrated in the small. This document records a second zero of a stranger kind: a zero that survives the swap of the very substrate the first zero seemed to depend on. Same seed, different silicon's arithmetic: the weights part company in their last bits and the grading does not move — not because the trajectories are insensitive (they are chaotically sensitive to their seed, to the order of their data, to everything at the scale the hardware can represent) but because the norm the trajectories converge to is an attractor coarser than the noise of its own execution. The history term, it turns out, is two terms wearing one name: the four bytes that move the norm, and the manifest that moves only the bits. The instrument was built to tell etiology from ownership. It has now told macro-history from micro-history — the same discrimination, one floor down, for free. Whether the distinction survives contact with a system that revises itself — the system class for which this corpus exists — is the M2 and M3 program's question, and the next document in this issuance begins to build toward it.
