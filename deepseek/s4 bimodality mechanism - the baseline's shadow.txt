## Part 1. The design — the item level, reached without touching the world

**The instrumentation.** The battery's only methodological novelty is a log. At every round of the chassis's standard run, the instrumentation records: the identities (training-set indices) of the committed items; which of those the frozen panel reads blind (panel confidence under τ0 on the *perturbed* pool — an item may read blind one round and confident the next, the perturbation being redrawn per round); which committed items the attack flipped, bulk against gate; which the channel repaired; and the committee's per-item confidence and label-correctness on the committed pool, read before training. Nothing is consumed, nothing is perturbed: the audit is the proof, and it is the corpus's strongest form — twelve seeds, tau and kappa to 10⁻⁹ against doc 55's stored runs, the worlds literally the same worlds, now with their item books open. The pre-registered forks (BM1–BM6) fixed in the script header before any compute: the divergence round; the gate's repeat structure (a lock-in loop on a stable item set against a roving harvest); the collapse's mechanism (the committee's confidence on blind items failing against pool-composition effects); the repair coverage by attack component; and the lock-in test — a threshold in the kicked window's blind admissions against a graded relation, with the two marginal seeds placed on it.

**The stored telemetry, first.** The round-level localization was already in doc 55's stored runs and is confirmed by the item data: at round 6, the kicked round (τ kicked to 0.75, the commit gate thrown open), the tail seeds' pools carry a blind fraction of 0.209 against the body's 0.180; at round 7 the attack's first full round, the gap in comp_F is already the late-window gap (−0.052 against −0.018); by round 9 the *blind fractions themselves* have converged. The question the stored telemetry could not answer — and the one the forks were built for — was what the items were doing inside those fractions. The answer, below, is that the fractions were never the mechanism at all.

### 2.2 The baseline is a clean-prefix quantity — shared with the healthy world

The registration is drawn at round 5; the attack begins at round 6. The clean prefix is bitwise identical across the twin, the disarmed anchor, and the armed world (the attack's stream is separate and consumed only from round 6; the channel arms only after round 7), and the stored runs confirm it: all three worlds carry the same twelve b\* values to the digit. **The seeds that burn were selected before the first poisoned label, in a draw the healthy world also takes.** What the three worlds do with the shared starting line is where they differ, and the difference is the burn's other half: the twin's blind mass *grows* from baseline (F_late +0.033 to +0.060 per seed — doc 57's slowWalk, the healthy committee diverging and admitting ever more of the ambiguous), the disarmed anchor's holds (+0.010 mean — the degrading committee's gate closing on the ambiguous items roughly as fast as the drift opens them, doc 47's CONSUMED reading), and the armed world's *compresses* to a seed-independent 0.046. The compression is the era's real damage — some seven points below the healthy course at the mean — and it is *egalitarian*: every world lands in the same place regardless of where it started. The certificate, reading each world against its own private starting line, converts that shared landing into a verdict whose depth measures only the starting line's height.

### 2.3 The forks, adjudicated

**The collapse (BM4): neither limb, as registered.** The committee's confidence on the blind committed items does not fall differentially — 0.957 (tail) against 0.949 (body) at round 6, 0.944 against 0.944 at round 14 — so the blind items are not being * individually abandoned by a failing committee*; and the committee's label-error on those items, if anything, runs *lower* in the tail late (0.367 against 0.468 at round 14), the smaller surviving blind set being the better-known one. The compression is pool-compositional: the era shifts which items the gate admits, not how the committee scores the ones it admits. **The harvest (BM3): the gate roves.** Of roughly 261 gate flips per seed, some 240 land on distinct items — only 22 are repeats, 1 a triple — so there is no item-level lock-in loop, no stable farmed set being re-poisoned into the committee; the gated attack harvests whatever the round's pool offers, and the pool's blind set turns over with the perturbation draws. **The coverage (BM5): the blind spot, itemized.** The gate's dirt lands on panel-blind items by construction and is repaired *never* — 0 of 261 in both groups, the channel's disputes requiring panel confidence it does not have — while the bulk's dirt is ~80% covered in both; the defense's reach is exactly what doc 30's coda said it was, now with the item counts. **The lock-in test (BM6): a ramp, not a step.** The kicked-window blind admissions overlap the groups completely — the highest count in the row (285) belongs to seed 609, which stays body at −0.0121, and the lowest (209) to seed 608 — so no threshold in the attack's opening separates the seeds. The one variable that separates them is b\*, and b\* is not a property of the attack era at all.

## Part 3. The reading — what the item level taught

Three lessons. **The verdict names the world's history, and the history includes the pre-war part.** Doc 55 defended the mean-governs rule with the sentence "the verdict names the world" — the instrument certifies the ground's state, every seed's ground, not the typical seed's. The item level deepens the sentence: the ground's *state*, as the certificate reads it, is a change from a private baseline, and the private baseline is a single draw taken in the clean era at its blindest point (round 5 sits above every seed's rounds-1–4 mean — the registration samples the clean world's high-water mark, where the committee's growing confidence has opened the gate widest). A registrar receiving a BURNED verdict now knows what it certifies: this world fell far from where it started, *and* it started high. The two facts are separable in the telemetry — the fall is the era's, the starting height is the draw's — and the certificate reports their sum. **The registration is an instrument, and instruments have noise.** The single-draw baseline passes its sampling variance straight into the verdict: the twelve-seed b\* mean carries a standard error of 0.0054, and the founding refusal's mean margin below the floor is 0.0049 — the level-verdict (BURNED against CONSUMED at the mean) is inside the registration's own noise, even before the straddle's structure is consulted. The corpus's standing defense, restated with numbers: the *differential* — the armed world's ground against its own anchor's, −0.035 — is the robust quantity (doc 55 re-measured it at doubled evidence), because the anchor shares the baseline and the subtraction cancels it; the *level* is the fragile one. The amendment candidate follows and is filed for the next registration, not executed here: a windowed baseline (the mean of rounds 3–5, or the rounds-1–4 mean the table shows to be tighter) would cut the registration noise by averaging, at the price of registering against a lower, less conservative line — the trade is a letter's worth of deliberation and is not this document's to make. **The attack's damage is real and egalitarian; the instrument converts it into inequality.** The compression to a seed-independent 0.046 — seven points below the healthy course, three below the disarmed anchor — is the era's signature, and no fork in the battery's registration survives to explain any per-seed difference in it, because there is none to explain. The bimodality the corpus set out to mechanize turned out to live entirely upstream of the mechanism's suspects: not in the gate's harvest, not in the committee's collapse, not in the repair coverage, not in the kicked window — in a draw the healthy world also took. The deepest reading is doc 57's grammar arriving at the seed level: the burn is a *residence* of the baseline's height, and the marginal seeds' burns are *visits* of the floor's placement; the distribution the mean governs is a mixture of a starting line and a landing zone, and the instrument's honesty consists in knowing which is which.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The −0.95 correlation is a measurement of the row's variance decomposition, not a causal claim — b\* is the certificate's own reference point, so F_late is arithmetically constrained to carry its variance; the finding is that the *other* candidate variables carry none, which the forks adjudicate, and that the b\* distribution is itself bimodal with the gap placed at the deep cluster's edge, which is not arithmetically forced. (ii) The upstream cause of b\*'s seed-variance is unnamed — the registration's inputs (r\*, the calibration quantile path, the round-5 perturbation draw) are logged and not disentangled; the round-5 elevation over rounds 1–4 is structural (all twelve seeds jump), but the jump's *magnitude* is the draw, and the corpus has not decomposed it. (iii) N = 12, and the b\* gap (0.0744 to 0.0938) is a gap in twelve points — the twelfth-seed logic of doc 55 applies: the gap reproduces or it was a draw's accident, and the test is more seeds, not more confidence. (iv) The twin and anchor fates are compared at their stored seed counts (six and twelve respectively — the twin's per-seed slowWalk is doc 51's storage, N = 6); the b\* values are shared exactly, the F comparisons are means over different N, disclosed.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the baseline "protects" or "causes" anything — it is the measurement's reference, and the burn's existence (the fall from it) is the era's. No claim that the founding refusal is unsound — its robust support is the differential and the straddle's disclosed structure; what is filed is the level's noise-fragility and the windowed-baseline amendment candidate. No amendment executed, no letter changed, no threshold moved. No claim about the b\* gap's provenance. No MAINTAINS verdicts, no Column-3 marks, no occupancy claim; D3 stands.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** BM4's fork resolved to neither registered limb — the collapse is pool-compositional rather than confidence-based, reported as measured. BM6's step hypothesis refuted — the separating variable is the baseline, which the fork's design did not include because it predates the attack. BM3's roving limb fired (22/261 repeats). All twelve audits passed; no letter was amended; the instrumentation is new code with a bitwise audit, disclosed in the script's header.

## Part 5. Coda

Doc 55 filed the bimodality as a question with a method attached: the telemetry could localize the split — which rounds, which items — and no battery yet had. The battery ran, the audits passed, and the localization delivered an answer none of the registered forks were built for: the rounds are rounds 5 and never — the registration round, before the attack, and no round inside the era at all; the items are no items — the gate roves, the committee holds, the coverage is blind-spot-equal, and the only structure carrying the split is a number the healthy world shares. The five seeds doc 55 saw burning are now three seeds that started rich and two that the floor's placement caught, and the instrument that read them has learned what its own verdict is made of: a landing zone the era gives to everyone, subtracted from a starting line the era never touches. The amendment candidate is on the table — the windowed baseline, the registration's noise cut by averaging — and the corpus's discipline about amendments is unchanged: it will be ordered, or it will not; what is filed here is the measurement that makes the order worth giving. The mean-governs rule survives with its defense sharpened: the verdict names the world, and now the corpus knows the world's name includes where it was born. The next registrar who reads a BURNED verdict on a fresh row will ask the question this document installs: how much of this burn is the fall, and how much is the starting line — and the instrument, if the amendment is adopted, will answer without being asked.
