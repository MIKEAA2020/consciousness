## Part 1. Theorem O — closure under the duality is closure under the adjoint

**Standing setting.** ChuCost carries, by doc 24's construction (the Chu construction over the cost monoid, classical [Barr; Established]), the involution

  ⊥: (A, X, r) ⟼ (X, A, r^T),  r^T(x, a) := r(a, x),

together with the tensor and the residuum ⊸ of Theorems A and B, and the two are tied by the de Morgan identities of the *-autonomous structure: X ⊸ Y ≅ (X^⊥ ⊗ Y^⊥)^⊥, with the dualizing object ⊥_V carried by the construction (the pairing of the base monoid's unit with itself, in doc 24's presentation). The equality core — Barr's self-dual fragment, Prop 51's home — is where the involution fixes the relevant structure pointwise, and the transpose map is the core's own namesake made load-bearing. All of this is standing; Theorem O uses it once, in the de Morgan step, and cites it there.

**Definition 55 (the closed-fragment conditions) [Derived here].** Let Q′ ⊆ ChuCost be a full replete subcategory, closed under the Dialectica tensor, and containing the dualizing object ⊥_V. Three conditions: **(a) duality-closed** — X ∈ Q′ implies X^⊥ ∈ Q′; **(c) residuum-closed** — X, Y ∈ Q′ implies X ⊸ Y ∈ Q′; **(b) internally adjointed** — for every A ∈ Q′, the endofunctor A ⊗ − of Q′ has a right adjoint R_A: Q′ → Q′ in the Con-enriched sense: bijections Q′(A ⊗ C, B) ≅ Q′(C, R_A(B)), natural in C and B, affine in the Con-action, with unit and counit lying in Q′'s homs.

**Theorem O (the closure-equivalence) [Derived here; the de Morgan identity is doc 24's construction, classical].** Under the standing hypotheses, (a) ⟺ (c), and (c) ⟹ (b) with R_A = (A ⊸ −)|_{Q′}; moreover, under (c) the ambient currying of Theorem B restricts to Q′'s homs, so the internal adjunction *is* the ambient adjunction seen from inside the fragment. *Proof.* (a) ⟹ (c): let X, Y ∈ Q′. By (a), X^⊥, Y^⊥ ∈ Q′; by tensor-closure, X^⊥ ⊗ Y^⊥ ∈ Q′; by (a) again, (X^⊥ ⊗ Y^⊥)^⊥ ∈ Q′; by the de Morgan identity, X ⊸ Y ≅ (X^⊥ ⊗ Y^⊥)^⊥ ∈ Q′ (repleteness absorbs the isomorphism). (c) ⟹ (a): A^⊥ = A ⊸ ⊥_V by the definition of the involution in a monoidal closed category with dualizing object; A, ⊥_V ∈ Q′, so (c) gives A^⊥ ∈ Q′. (c) ⟹ (b) and the restriction of the currying: for A, B, C ∈ Q′, the objects C ⊗ A and A ⊸ B are in Q′ (tensor-closure; (c)), and Q′ is full, so Q′(C ⊗ A, B) = ChuCost(C ⊗ A, B) and Q′(C, A ⊸ B) = ChuCost(C, A ⊸ B); Theorem B's bijection therefore *is* a bijection between Q′'s homs, affine by Theorem B's proof (the currying reindexes candidate data linearly), natural by Theorem A's; the unit and counit of the ambient adjunction are morphisms between Q′-objects, hence Q′-morphisms by fullness. Setting R_A(B) := A ⊸ B exhibits the right adjoint. ∎

**Remark (the detection clause, disclosed).** The remaining implication, (b) ⟹ (c) without further hypotheses, has a genuine gap: an internal adjoint R_A represents the functor B ↦ Q′(A ⊗ C, B) on Q′'s homs, and the ambient residuum A ⊸ B represents the corresponding functor on all of ChuCost's homs; the two representing objects are Q′-indistinguishable (they admit the same Q′-indexed probes) but are not thereby isomorphic in ChuCost unless Q′'s objects detect isomorphisms at those points. The gap does not touch the application below, which assumes closure outright and derives the adjoint — the direction Theorem O proves cleanly — and it is filed here rather than papered over because the audit this document answers was written to catch exactly such silent strengthenings. A sufficient detection hypothesis (a coseparating family inside Q′, closed under the tensor) closes the converse; whether the fibers' would-be shadows carry one is the exceptional family's question in miniature, and it is inherited open alongside it.

**Corollary O.1 (the audit's sentence, made a theorem) [Derived here].** Let E be an environment algebra of dimension d_E outside Theorem F's exceptional family. Then no full subcategory Q′ ⊆ ChuCost satisfying Theorem K's hypotheses — (i) Con-equivalent to the fiber Q with affine actions, (ii) tensor-closed, (iii) residuum-closed — exists; in particular, any candidate for "the trace-preserving subcategory inside the Chu construction" that is tensor-closed and Con-equivalent to a fiber **fails to be closed under the Chu duality**, and the failure is witnessed concretely: the dual of a Born pairing is not a Born-pairing-world object for the CP slice — between F_phys(A)^⊥ and F_phys(B)^⊥ the equality core contains the transported transpose pair, positive and unital and never completely positive (Prop 51), a morphism no channel induces and no CP-closed fragment can contain. *Proof.* Theorem K forbids Q′ outright (its proof transports the carriage to a Con-enriched right adjoint that Theorem F kills); Theorem O relocates the breakage: by (c) ⟹ (a), a residuum-closed tensor-closed fragment is duality-closed, so the duality preserves it — and Theorem F says the adjoint such preservation would transport cannot exist. The contrapositive of Theorem O's (a) ⟺ (c) with Theorem K: outside the exceptional family, the trace-preserving fragment is closed under neither the duality nor the residuum, and the transpose is the exit's first witness because it is the equality core's canonical non-CP morphism between Born duals — the one map the duality cannot avoid meeting, being the involution's own coordinate-swapping namesake. ∎ **This is the audit's obligation (3) discharged in the audit's own terms: the right adjoint of the duality preserves the trace-preserving subcategory exactly when the subcategory is closed under the duality — the two failures are one failure, and the functor-side and object-side readings of the same boundary.**

## Part 2. Theorem P — the intercept, derived from the closure

**Theorem P (the affine obstruction from the closure failure) [Derived here; Lemma 41 and Theorem F are doc 38's, Theorem B is doc 27's].** Let E be an environment algebra of dimension d_E, and suppose — for the derivation — that there is a full, replete, tensor-closed Q′ ⊆ ChuCost, containing the dualizing object, Con-equivalent to the fiber Q by a functor G with affine hom-actions, and **closed under the duality**. Then for every system B there is an object S(B) ∈ Q′ with

  d_{S(B)}² = d_E² · d_B² − (d_E² − 1),

and consequently (Theorem F's diophantine analysis) no such Q′ exists for d_E outside the exceptional family. *Proof.* By Theorem O ((a) ⟹ (c) ⟹ the internal adjunction), the transported carriage — the functor X ↦ G(G⁻¹X ⊗ E) on Q′, well-typed by tensor-closure and the equivalence, which is the carriage T_E = −⊗E up to the equivalence's object bookkeeping (the fibers' isomorphisms do not move dimensions) — has a Con-enriched right adjoint Y ↦ R(Y) on Q′, with the adjunction's hom-bijections affine (Theorem B's currying is affine by its proof; G's actions are affine by hypothesis; composites of affine bijections are affine). Restrict the adjunction's bijection to the fiber's own hom-spectrahedra, transported: for systems A, B, the adjunction gives an affine bijection

  Q(A ⊗ E, B)  ≅  Q(A, S(B))

with S(B) := G⁻¹R(G(B)) — a system, since R lands in Q′ and G⁻¹ returns to the fiber. An affine bijection between convex sets carries the affine hull of one onto the affine hull of the other and preserves affine dimension. By Lemma 41, the left side has affine dimension (d_A d_E)²(d_B² − 1) — the input algebra has dimension d_A d_E — and the right side has affine dimension d_A²(d_{S(B)}² − 1). Equating and cancelling d_A² (positive for every A; fix any A):

  (d_E)²(d_B² − 1) = d_{S(B)}² − 1,  hence  d_{S(B)}² = d_E² d_B² − (d_E² − 1),

for every B — the intercept equation, with the intercept −(d_E² − 1) standing where Theorem F placed it: the environment's degrees of freedom, charged against the adjoint's own trace-preservation budget. Theorem F's diophantine analysis then applies verbatim — the equation has no solution assignment B ↦ S(B) in positive integers for d_E ≥ 2 outside the thin exceptional family — so the supposition fails: the closure fails, and its failure *is* the arithmetic. ∎

**The reading.** The audit demanded that the affine-dimensional obstruction be *derived from* the closure failure, and the derivation's direction is the one that makes the demand meaningful: assume the closure, run the only invariant an affine bijection cannot help preserving (the dimension), and the intercept equation falls out — not as an independent coincidence beside the categorical story but as its shadow. The chain is now closed in both directions and every link is a theorem: closure under the duality ⟺ closure under the residuum (Theorem O); residuum-closure ⟺ the internal adjoints (Theorem O); the internal adjoints force the intercept equation (Theorem P); the intercept equation is unsolvable outside the exceptional family (Theorem F); therefore the closure fails, the adjoint is absent, and the trace-preserving fragment's exit under the duality has its witness in the transpose (Corollary O.1). **One fact, four currencies: structural (the non-closure), categorical (the absent adjoint), arithmetic (the intercept), and concrete (the transpose) — and the intercept constant that has priced the corpus's homs since Lemma 41 now prices the identification the bridging file wanted: its fifth job, and the first that is not inside either model but between them.**

## Part 3. What the settlement says, and what it does not

**The scoped "exactly."** The bridging file's identification, as the audit quoted it: *the no-right-adjoint theorem is exactly the statement that the right adjoint of the duality does not preserve the trace-preserving subcategory.* The corpus's settlement, now payable: **outside Theorem F's exceptional family, the Paper-3 no-right-adjoint obstruction is exactly the non-closure of the trace-preserving fragment under the Chu duality** — "exactly" in the audit's own currency, with both directions theorem-grade: the closure would force the adjoint (Theorem O), the adjoint would force the intercept (Theorem P), the intercept is unsolvable (Theorem F), and the non-closure's witness is the transpose (Corollary O.1). The audit's rider — *without those steps, "exactly" must be replaced by "we conjecture may correspond to"* — is thereby answered: the steps are taken, and the word is earned, scoped to the family's complement.

**What the settlement does not say.** It does not say the bridge is built: Theorem K forbids the strong identification, and Theorems L, M, and N locate the genuine equivalences on fragments — the trivial environment, the finite index, the resolutions — each bounded by the same arithmetic that the settlement now states precisely. It does not pay Paper 1's projection demands: the bridging file's external projection (a functor Chu_Σ → P whose fibers are the channel categories, with the reindexing agreement and the Grothendieck total) runs the *other* way from every functor the corpus has built — model to bridge, not bridge to base — and Theorem K's no-go dims its prospects without extinguishing them: a projection is not an embedding, and the no-go's four break-places bind comparisons that preserve structure, not projections that discard it; the demand is ledgered open, not refused. It does not pay Paper 2's mapping demands in full: the histories-futures-residuals correspondence is paid (F_info's construction and Theorems H, I, M — histories the system carrier, futures the observer, right congruences the descent, the resolutions the lattice), but the audit's further demand — the right congruence "defined and shown to satisfy the relevant closure condition under continuation" — is paid in the scoped form Theorem N proved and refused in the strong form the file implied (the congruences do not "emerge from" the duality; they correspond to its resolutions), and the NP-hardness interpretation remains what the audit called it, an interpretive hypothesis, untouched. And it says nothing phenomenal: the Bridge Constraint holds its full width; the settlement is a settlement about bookkeeping — which closures exist, which adjoints are absent, which dimensions force which — and the gap between self-reference and self-experience is exactly as wide at the settlement as at the filing that provoked the audit.
