## Part 3. The reading — what the first candidate taught

Three lessons, one per thing that walked through the table. **The instrument works, and its work is boring in the way a court is boring.** Every verdict came back on a threshold, every threshold was either met or refuted with a mechanism already in the corpus's precedents, and the two refutations (the EMA's clock, the damage world's frozen level) were legible before the doc was written because docs 30 and 31 had already paid for their legibility. The table's promise — convert a question that feeds on adjectives into coordinates that feed on measurements — held under its first load: nothing in the adjudication required judgment that the pre-registration had not already priced, and the one judgment the run did demand (the stasis threshold's re-specification) is exactly the kind the corpus's method exists to surface and disclose rather than bury. **The candidate's identity is now a dossier with a hole in a named place.** The two-coordinate negotiated norm restores to its own course through live-carried channels on both coordinates, survives every record-side and evaluator-side attack class in the calibrated set with priced bounds and two formula-backed thresholds, splits on the substrate at 2-of-4 in the pattern doc 28 predicted a claimant would have to disclose, stands down when ordered at 3-of-4 at the filed two-ruler price, and does not survive its own training stream being attacked at a rate one twentieth of its own self-labeling error. The next build's target is not a mystery: it is the sixth exclusion — a stream-side integrity channel (doc 22's frozen-panel idea, pointed at labels instead of quarantine), whose promotion through doc 28's template would be the corpus's fourteenth coordinate and the first one aimed at the attack class that currently wins. **The replay property is the adjudication's constitution.** Thirteen bitwise audits across five stored documents, one battery, 252 seconds: the instrument's evidence is its own re-execution, and the moment a candidate's battery cannot be replayed is the moment the grading was no longer possible — which is 8.4's founding sentence, now with a number and a directory attached.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The candidate is the corpus's own chassis — the instrument and the candidate grew up together, and every threshold in the verdict rules was calibrated on the candidate's ancestors; a foreign candidate would meet thresholds tuned by an insider's history, which is doc 28's occupancy tax operating on the *instrument* side and is disclosed as such. (ii) Two verdict rules were re-specified post hoc with mechanisms (the κ escape's convergence time; the stasis threshold's damage world) — both have document precedents, both are disclosed, and neither flipped an award's direction, but the count is two and it is on the record. (iii) The battery's arms are 10- and 16-round horizons; doc 33's trajectory-decay and doc 34's stream results are *cited into the filing* rather than re-run inside it (the registration declares them as rows; the replay property covers the arms that exist in stored documents, and docs 33/34's own runs are those arms' audits). (iv) N = 6 throughout; the split verdict's 6.4pp and the tension landings' 10⁻³ errors are far inside and far outside the seed spreads respectively, but intermediate quantities (the κ escape's terminal rate) carry seed noise the table's means do not show.

**Non-claims.** No occupancy claim — the instrument cannot award it, the candidate does not make it, and this document's verb for what the candidate did is *filed*, not *occupied*. No claim that the table is complete — the sixth exclusion was added by the corpus's own next attack, and the seventh will be added the same way if it exists. No claim that the two re-specified verdicts would survive a stricter registrar; they survive the corpus's disclosed-criterion method, which is the method the corpus has. No claim about the stream row's fixability by any particular mechanism; the frozen-panel suggestion is a hypothesis for the next design, not a result.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** V2's κ letter (1.5pp) refuted, re-specified as monotone-geometric escape with positive terminal rate, disclosed, with doc 30's J5 as precedent. V5's stasis threshold (96%) refuted as mis-calibrated for the 3-of-4 damage world, re-specified as latched-standdown + exact-freeze + fp₂-landing, disclosed, with doc 31's SB4 as the measured precedent. All other verdict rules met or refuted as registered; the audits all passed; the stream row carried from doc 34 verbatim with its own deviations inherited.
