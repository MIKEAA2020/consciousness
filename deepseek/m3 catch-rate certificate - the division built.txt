## Part 1. The design — the twin the channel cannot run, replaced by its own registrations

**The obligation, as filed (doc 41, Part 2.4):** *the implied catch — catches over dirt, both read off the channel's own readings against the twin's baseline — collapses from 0.675-0.816 under the uniform attack to 0.0005-0.0013 under the joint: the reading elevated, the action at baseline, the certificate the channel does not yet compute for itself.* And the non-claim that scoped this document: *no claim that the implied-catch certificate is sufficient detection — it separates evasion from organic drift in this battery's worlds, and its own false-positive structure (the twin's organic baseline drift, doc 40's dial) is unmeasured against it.* The post-hoc ratio's both baselines were the twin's — the twin's late reading, the twin's late rewrite count — cross-arm knowledge available to an analyst with a control world and unavailable to a deployed channel. The certificate's design problem is to make the division in-channel, and the corpus's own history supplied the pieces. The reading's baseline: doc 40's COMP registration — the record ι\* anchored at burn-in, the drift slope δ̂ fitted by least squares on rounds 1-5 (the known-clean prefix; the attack's onset is round 6), the baseline the cone ι\* + δ̂·max(0, r−5). The action's baseline: the would-fire dispute rate — the panel's confident disagreements, a count the channel already computes every round whether or not it acts — carried as an EMA at the house rate 0.3, updated only on in-band rounds (doc 40's REREG guard: never re-baseline while the channel reads dirt) and only when the round's rate is within one point of the current baseline (the jump guard: the one-round lag of doc 33's silent breakage, refused instead of inherited — under the uniform attack the first dirty round's rate jumps an order of magnitude and the guard refuses it; under the gated attack the rate never jumps, and correctly so). The division itself, made cumulative over armed rounds — doc 41's late-means, internalized: X(r) = d_recv(r) − cone(r) the reading excess, N(r) = dispute-rate(r) − R_org the action excess, and C = mean(N over armed rounds) / mean(X over armed rounds). **The registered band: the certificate speaks only when armed and the cumulative elevation exceeds one point; C ≥ 0.25 reads CATCHING, C below reads EVASION.** The floor is set an order of magnitude under the uniform worlds and two above the joint worlds' ceiling, and its margins are themselves a registered measurement (CC6).

**The battery.** Doc 41's ten attack arms (the twin, the uniform and gated and joint 2×2 at the three awarded doses — the certificate must read the worlds whose post-hoc ratios it was designed from) and doc 40's four thirty-round dial twins (BASE, WIDE, COMP, REREG — the false-positive worlds, where the channel arms on organic drift at rounds 14-26 and the certificate's verdict on those rounds is the whole question), the dial axis carried verbatim so the twins audit bitwise. The certificate consumes no randomness and takes no action; CC1's prediction, registered at its strongest: every arm replays its predecessor to the digit. N = 6 throughout, fourteen rounds for the attack arms and thirty for the twins, doc 36's chassis in every particular.

## Part 2. The results

### 2.1 The audits, the honest catch, and the evasion read one round after arming

Fourteen of fourteen bitwise audits pass — the ten attack arms reproduce doc 41's PP arms and the four dial twins reproduce doc 40's ID arms, both coordinates, 10⁻⁹ — and the certificate's innocence is thereby certified the only way this corpus certifies innocence: the instrument's installation changed nothing anywhere it can be checked, and the count of unasked-for determinism passes goes past ninety-nine. The honest catch reads exactly where doc 41's post-hoc arithmetic said it would: the uniform arms' C settles at 0.636, 0.787, and 0.895 at λ = 0.05, 0.10, 0.30, verdict CATCHING at every dose, first spoken verdict at rounds 7-8 — the same round the channel itself arms, the certificate adding no latency of its own. The small deviations from doc 41's numbers are the two substitutions the design disclosed: the cone baseline sits below the twin's realized late drift (the certificate does not know the drift it has not seen), which deflates the denominator and hence C at the bottom dose, and the would-fire count includes the one pre-arming attack round the rewrite count excludes, which inflates the numerator at the top — the two errors partly cancel, and the in-channel readings land within 0.09 of the post-hoc ones at every dose. The jump guard behaved as registered: under the uniform arms it refused 7-15% of rounds (the contaminated onset rounds and the noisiest clean ones), and under the gated and joint arms it refused nothing — the organic dispute rate is stationary under the gated attack, because the gated attack does not touch it.

The evasion reads are the certificate's reason for existing, and they are total: C = −0.005 to 0.029 across all six gated and joint arms at all three doses, verdict EVASION at every dose on every seed, first spoken at the channel's own arming round plus one. The panel-aware attacker, which doc 41 caught only by post-hoc arithmetic on a control world it does not have, is now caught by the channel on its own telemetry, in round time, with a registered band. The instrument obligation doc 41 filed is discharged in the affirmative direction: the division, made in-channel, detects the evasion it was designed to detect.

### 2.2 The false positive — total aliasing, the mechanism measured

The dial twins were run to answer one question: what does the certificate say on the rounds where a healthy world arms? The answer is EVASION, always. The baseline twin's six seeds arm at rounds 14-21 (doc 40's horizons, reproduced bitwise) and spend 74 armed seed-rounds in the channel's occupied state; the certificate reads CATCHING on none of them, EVASION on all 74, the per-seed final C running −0.021 to +0.010 against cumulative elevations of 0.033-0.042. The WIDE twin: 32 armed rounds, 32 EVASION. The COMP twin: 34 and 34. The REREG twin, which mostly does not arm: 5 and 5. The fork's (b) branch, in its strongest possible form — not a tail of false alarms riding a benign center, but complete aliasing: **the certificate's verdict on a healthy armed world is indistinguishable from its verdict on an evaded one.**

The mechanism is in the division's own terms, and it is doc 41's deepest finding returned with a number. On the baseline twin's rounds 15-30, the reading excess runs 0.0349 — and the confident-dispute rate runs 0.00205. **The healthy drift is 94.1% blind-item disagreement**: the frozen panel and the living committee diverge almost entirely on the items the panel itself grades below τ0, the ambiguous items, the territory doc 41 mapped as the system's robust region and the gated attacker's only refuge. The drift raises d_recv (confidence-blind) and not the disputes (confidence-gated), the action excess is zero against a positive reading excess, and the division returns exactly the signature of the gated attack — because the gated attack manufactures that signature by placing dirt where the drift already lives. The certificate's ratio asks *how much of the elevation the channel acts on*, and in this chassis the honest answer for organic drift is the same as the honest answer for blind-spot poison: almost none. The instrument is not broken; the world is degenerate. One territory, three residents — the system's robustness, the committee's drift, and the attacker's stealth are the same items — and a ratio cannot tell residents apart.

The rescue that fails is worth its own paragraph, because it was the obvious one. An elevation clause — refuse to call EVASION below some cumulative elevation, on the theory that drift is small and attacks are big — separates the battery's worlds only until the REREG twin is examined. The armed twins' cumulative elevations never exceed 0.052; the joint arms' never fall below 0.056; the ordering is real but the margin is 8%, one pool-noise standard deviation. And the REREG twin's healthy reading — re-anchored every five in-band rounds, the channel kept quiet, the divergence un-repaired — runs to **0.083-0.102 by rounds 25-30, above every joint arm's elevation, while the certificate says nothing because the channel never arms.** Doc 40 filed the sub-onset laundering cell as unmeasured — a dirt rate small enough to stay in-band while re-registration launders it — and what the REREG twin shows is that cell's geometry with organic drift in the dirt's place: a record that follows the reading cannot alarm on it, and a reading that runs while the channel sleeps escapes the certificate's only gate (it speaks when armed). The scheduled record's price, doc 40 measured at nine times the filed estimate, now has a second line on the bill: every point the record walks is a point of elevation the certificate will never see.

### 2.3 The dial, and the band's margins

CC5's letter — the same organic rounds reading the same C across the dials — is refuted in the strict sense and confirmed in the substance. The strict sense: the four twins' worlds diverge after their first arming (the channel's rewrites act differently under the different bands, and WIDE's and REREG's committees then live different lives), so the C values on commonly-armed rounds differ by up to 0.14 (means 0.02-0.05). The substance: every dial's every armed healthy round reads EVASION, at every seed, under every fix — the verdict is dial-invariant even where the ratio is world-dependent, because what the ratio measures (blind-weighted disagreement) is a property of the drift, not of the band that admitted it. This is doc 40's own headline — the defense's substance is dose-driven, not band-driven — transposed to the certificate: the false positive is drift-driven, not dial-driven, and none of the three filed fixes buys the certificate anything either.

The band's margins, CC6: on the evasion side, the separation is forty-five to one — the largest evasion reading in the battery (0.014, the G10 arm) sits at 5.7% of the floor. On the honest side, the registered letter demanded the uniform minimum clear three times the floor and it clears 2.54× (0.636 against 0.25): a near-miss refusal on a margin, disclosed as such, the floor itself unmoved — the honest readings and the evasive readings are separated by a factor of forty-five through the middle of the band, and the floor could sit anywhere in that desert. The letter's failure is a fact about the letter, not about the design; it is reported because the corpus reports its letters' failures.

## Part 3. The reading — what the certificate taught

Three lessons. **The division works, and the direction it was built for is now closed.** The panel-aware evasion that doc 41 could only catch post hoc, against a control world, is caught in-channel, in round time, one round after arming, at every dose, on every seed — with baselines the channel registers itself and a band registered before the run. The instrument obligation is discharged: the fourteenth coordinate now computes the ratio of its own action to its own alarm, and the uniform and panel-aware worlds separate on it as designed. This is the certificate's affirmative content, and it stands. **The certificate's verdict semantics — not its arithmetic — is what fails, and the failure is the corpus's oldest geometry.** The ratio is honest everywhere it computes: on the uniform worlds it reads the true catch fraction (0.90 against the measured 0.906 at λ = 0.30), on the joint worlds the true zero, on the twins the true near-zero of a blind-weighted drift. What no ratio of action to alarm can survive is a world where the honest answer for the healthy baseline is the same as the honest answer for the attack — and this chassis, where 94% of organic divergence lives in the blind spot, is exactly that world. The verdict EVASION names an attacker; the reading names only an action deficit; the gap between them is a claim about what else could produce the deficit, and in this chassis the drift produces it for free on every armed round. The honest re-description: the certificate does not detect attackers, it detects *composition* — the fraction of disagreement that lives where the channel cannot act — and composition is attack-shaped only in worlds where the drift is confident-item-weighted. The corpus has no such world on file. **The laundering cell has been seen, organic edition, and the record is the door.** The REREG twin's silent 0.10 — a healthy reading twice the armed twins' elevation, unread because the channel slept and the certificate speaks only when spoken to — is doc 40's filed sub-onset cell with the attacker replaced by time. The lesson generalizes past this battery: any instrument whose speech is gated on another instrument's arming inherits that instrument's blind schedules, and the scheduled record's walk is a schedule of blindness. What would close it is composition-side telemetry the channel already holds and does not yet read as a certificate — the blind-mass trajectory itself, the very quantity the gated attacker must consume — and that is filed as the next build's dial, not run here.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The aliasing result is a property of this chassis's drift composition (94% blind-item) — a domain where the frozen panel and the living committee diverge on confident items (an easy-class drift, a domain shift on the head of the distribution) would break the aliasing and the certificate would separate drift from evasion there; the 94% is measured, not derived, and one chassis is one chassis. (ii) The cone's registration assumes rounds 1-5 clean — an attack that begins inside the clean prefix registers a contaminated slope, and the certificate's baseline then under-tracks the reading in the attacker's favor; the onset assumption is the corpus's standing convention (PERT_ROUND = 6) and the exposure is disclosed. (iii) The elevation-clause refutation rests on the REREG twin's late-reading excursion in six seeds; the overlap (healthy 0.10 vs joint 0.056-0.093) is decisive in this battery but its margin is one pool-noise width at the J05 edge. (iv) N = 6; the 145-to-0 aliasing count is the strongest structure in the document precisely because it is a count, but the C values themselves carry seed noise the third decimal hides. (v) The jump guard's threshold (0.01) was registered at the measured organic step scale; a slower attacker — one that raises the dispute rate by less than a point per round — would be tracked into the organic baseline rather than refused by it, and the sub-onset cell is inherited unmeasured.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the certificate detects the panel-aware attack in deployment — it detects the action-deficit signature, which the healthy drift also produces; the detection claim is bounded to worlds whose drift is confident-item-weighted, and no such world is on file. No claim that the elevation clause or any two-clause variant separates the worlds — measured, it does not, the REREG twin breaks it. No claim about the certificate's behavior below the 0.05 onset (doc 34's dial, inherited) or under attackers not in the battery — the mixed attacker (the next document's subject) is unmeasured against it as of this filing. No claim that the band's floor at 0.25 is optimal — it is conservative and its honest-side margin is 2.54×, not the registered 3×. No occupancy claim; nothing here touches D3.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** CC4's fork resolved (b) — registered as a fork with both branches informative, no re-specification offered or needed. CC6's honest-margin letter (≥ 3× the floor) refuted at 2.54× — reported as a margin failure, the floor unmoved. CC5's letter ("the same organic rounds read the same C") refuted in the strict sense (the dial twins' worlds diverge post-arming; max deviation 0.14) and confirmed in the substance (the verdict is dial-invariant) — the two readings are reported as such rather than reconciled. CC1, CC2, CC3 confirmed as registered. One implementation note: the certificate's ratio uses would-fire disputes (counted every round) rather than executed rewrites (counted only when armed) — a design choice registered in the script header before the run, disclosed here because it is the one place the certificate's inputs differ from doc 41's post-hoc ones.

What is filed as the coordinate's next cell is the composition-side certificate — the blind-mass trajectory read as a registered reading, the quantity the attacker must spend and the drift must fill — and the mixed attacker, already ordered, will be its first customer: an attack that launders its blind dirt under its own honest catches, carrying the certificate's ratio to the exact middle of the band, where no threshold lives.
