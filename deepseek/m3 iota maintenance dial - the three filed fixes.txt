## Part 3. The reading — the noise, the schedule, and what files where

Three lessons. **The false positive is a noise phenomenon wearing a drift timescale.** Doc 36 filed the horizon as ε_i/drift because the drift was what it could see — a steady divergence between a frozen panel and a living committee — and the dial's two refutations (the law's optimistic overshoot; the cone's failure) share one mechanism: the crossing is done by the excursions, not the center. This is the same structure the corpus has met three times — the deadband's check one round behind the violation (doc 33's silent breakage), the EMA lagging a moving target (doc 33's lag law), the frozen panel diverging from the live committee (doc 36) — and the dial adds the fourth face: a band wide enough for the mean is not wide enough for the tail. The filed replacement is empirical and honest: the measured slope (≈ 250 rounds per unit of ε_i in this chassis), the mechanism (first excursion to clear), and the open cell (the excursion tail's law — Part 4). **The record can be a course, and the corpus now knows its price.** Re-registration is the only fix that buys the horizon back (four of six seeds clean through thirty), and it does so by conceding doc 23's deepest distinction: a constant record is a claim about the world's stationarity, and the healthy self-training world is not stationary — the committee and the panel diverge because the committee is alive. The scheduled record's price is nine times the filed estimate because the snap inherits the noise with the drift, and the honest summary is that the corpus's first scheduled record works, is priced, and walks upward. Whether a course-following record (COMP applied to ι\* itself — re-anchor to the fitted line, not the reading) would pay less is the obvious synthesis and is filed as the dial's own next cell, unrun. **The dial does not touch the defense.** Every dose arm's substance — catch rounds, cleaning ratio, clean accuracy, the loop's absence — is invariant or near-invariant across the dial, because the channel's work is done by the dirt it catches, not the band's geometry that admitted it. The maintenance question and the defense question are orthogonal axes of the same coordinate, and the dial is the instrument that separated them: doc 36's channel needed a maintenance schedule; doc 39's candidate needed the schedule not to matter; both are now measured statements.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The horizon law's refutation rests on two band widths (0.03, 0.05) and six seeds — the measured slope (≈ 250 rounds per unit) is a two-point chord, not a curve, and the excursion-tail mechanism is inferred from the refutations' pattern rather than fitted to the excursions themselves; a dedicated excursion study (high-frequency reading snapshots, fitted tail) would price it properly and is filed as the dial's unmet obligation. (ii) The thirty-round twins extend doc 36's fourteen-round chassis past its registered horizon — the burn-in, self-training rate, and panel age are unchanged, but rounds 15-30 are outside every prior audit's span, and the drift rate fit (rounds 6-30) inherits the longer window by construction. (iii) The REREG price measurement (net record movement) is a net, not a path: a record that oscillates and a record that ratchets measure the same net, and the two imply different laundering risks; the in-band guard's safety is certified only at the measured doses (0.05 and above), and the sub-onset laundering cell remains unmeasured (doc 34's dial, inherited). (iv) N = 6 throughout; the two residual REREG false positives are two events, and their rate is not a rate.

**Non-claims.** No claim that any fix *solves* the false positive — the dial's cleanest result is that none does, and the choice it leaves the designer is a priced trade (five rounds for a wider band; the horizon for a scheduled record; nothing for the cone). No claim that the scheduled record is safe below the onset dose — the laundering cell is filed open. No claim about the panel-aware attacker (the next document's subject; the dial's every arm assumes a uniform poison). No claim that the measured slope generalizes beyond this chassis's panel age, committee size, or self-training rate.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** ID1's letter (first arming 13-17) refuted — two seeds at 20 and 21; the mechanism (the noise's excursions crossing before the drift's center) is the same one that refutes ID2 and ID4, and no re-specification is offered because the prediction is simply wrong in the direction the mechanism says. ID2's letter (23-27, else censored) refuted — the measured 18-24 with zero censored. ID5's record-price letter (0.006-0.010) refuted — measured 0.054-0.106 net; the fix's benefit clause stands. ID3's fork resolved at its (a) branch (armed by round 12) as registered. ID4's twin clause refuted; its dose clauses confirmed bitwise. ID6 confirmed. The three BASE-arm audits pass bitwise; all deviations are disclosed in place.
