## Part 0. The suspicion, stated plainly

The registrar's question is not "Is Russellian monism true?" It is: **"Did doc 90's tribunal assume the conclusion?"** The suspicion has a mechanism, and the mechanism is on the record before the audit lifts a finger: the four campaigns are the corpus's own instruments; the corpus's own Status header at doc 90 declares that "the corpus's own earned results are Russellian-shaped (earned at docs 32, 38, and 89; disclosed at doc 87 Part 5)," and that the Camp-Marker "stands up for the whole session." A tribunal that grades the field with one camp's instruments should be *expected* to file that camp at the top — unless the instruments are neutral in the one sense that matters here: neutral with respect to the grammar in which they were proved. That is testable. The test is whether the verdict survives when the criteria are replaced by questions no grammar owns.

The audit question, sharpened to one sentence: **was "Russellian monism is the sole survivor" a finding about the nine theories, or a finding about the four criteria?** If the former, it will survive re-scoring under de-theorized criteria by scorers who have never seen the corpus. If the latter, it will invert, and the inversion will localize — the failures that fall will be the ones filed on the criteria that carry the grammar's shape, and the failures that stand will be the ones filed on evidence. The registrar asked for the test to be run hard enough that if the bias is there, it shows. It was run at five-scorer strength, with the persona spread including the one camp the first draft of the battery omitted — the camp whose instrument the tribunal is. The bias showed. This document is the record of where, and of what survives it.

## Part 1. The steelmen — the nine at full strength

The method's first stroke: no theory is scored from its strawman. Each of the nine was restated in its strongest form and checked against doc 90's own trial versions, because an audit that upgrades the field's entries after the fact would be auditing its own softballs. The check files cleanly at six of nine: doc 90 already tried physicalism with the phenomenal concepts strategy ("the position's best card and deserves its respect"), already credited IIT with "the field's most serious measurement campaign," already honored GWT's retreat as "the field's most honest act of retreat," already gave higher-order theories "the field's highest distinction: the closest approach to the missing instrument," already tried the placed form of Russellian monism (the form that won), and already conceded, in its own Part 3, the convergence of the two banks. Six of nine entries were tried at steel grade. The tribunal was not a strawman tribunal.

The three upgrades the audit registers, with the reason each matters:

**Physicalism — the Type-B form, with the concept strategy load-bearing.** The strongest version does not retreat from the identity; it *owns* it: one thing, two vocabularies, quotational concepts explaining the gap's *feel* without denying the identity's *truth*. Doc 90 tried this and priced it as "a placement, not a traversal" — the pricing, not the steelman, is what the audit re-tests (Part 6).

**Functionalism — the constitutive reading of the dark-system reply.** The strongest version answers "the system could run in the dark" with "the intuition presupposes a phenomenal fact beyond role, and the theory denies that fact — the objection begs the question against us." Doc 90's own entry records the field's forty-year war as "the dispute, conducted at scale" and files both sides' persistence as camp data. The steelman's point: the question-begging charge runs both ways, and the tribunal charged only one side (Part 6).

**Illusionism — the executed elimination.** The strongest version does not default on the datum; it accounts for it: the seeming is real, the seemer is not, the misrepresentation mechanism is documented (confabulation, choice blindness, introspective error), and the "hard problem" is predicted to *feel* insoluble. Doc 90 filed the deletion as coherent, C1-protected, "the standing partner at the fork's left edge" — but charged it a price ("the question returned to sender"). Whether that price is a fact about illusionism or a fact about the criterion is exactly what the battery measures (Part 3).

The full steelmen, as scored, are registered in the battery's instrument file; the doc 90 trial versions stand as the corpus's own record. The audit's finding at this stroke: **the tribunal steelmanned the field honestly — the suspicion, if it is real, does not live in the entries. It lives in the questions.**

## Part 2. The neutral criteria — the registrar's five questions

The method's second stroke: replace the campaigns' vocabulary — bridge, dark system, traversal, quotient, complement, invocation — with questions that no grammar owns and every camp can read. The registrar supplied the questions; the audit operationalized each into a 0/1/2 rubric and pre-registered both before any scorer ran:

**N1 (primitive link).** Does the theory posit a primitive — brute, unexplained — relation between physical structure and experience that is not entailed by the theory's own ontology? (2: no such link, or fully entailed; 1: a primitive, but motivated, structured, or internal to the core identity claim; 0: a brute external link doing the load-bearing work.)

**N2 (first-person reports).** Does the theory explain what subjects say and show about their experience, eliminate it, or leave it unexplained? (2: explains with a supported account, or eliminates with a well-supported deflation; 0: leaves it brute; 1: partial.)

**N3 (causal compatibility).** Does the theory make consciousness causally efficacious in a way that conflicts with physics? (2: fully compatible; 0: closure or conservation violations; 1: tension.)

**N4 (observer).** Does the theory require a special observer — homunculus, inner witness, privileged point of view — for consciousness to occur? (2: no; 0: a regress-inducing observer; 1: observer-like but non-regressive and motivated.)

**N5 (falsifiability).** Does the theory make at least one claim that could be falsified, and has any relevant test actually been run? (2: falsifiable and tested at least indirectly; 0: no falsifiable content; 1: falsifiable in principle, untested or only very indirectly tested.)

Two de-theorizing decisions were made in the rubric, both disclosed now because the scorers flagged both later: **the elimination license** — N2 counts a well-executed elimination as a pass (doc 90's campaign counted deletion as a survival *with a price*); and **the parity pricing** — N1's middle bucket scores a motivated internal primitive at 1, whether it is called an identity (physicalism's word) or an identification (Russellian monism's word). The audit is not neutral simpliciter — the scorers' own meta-receipts (Part 3, finding 6) show these five questions tilt deflationist. The claim made here is narrower and sufficient: **the criteria are neutral with respect to the corpus's grammar** — which is the only neutrality the registrar's suspicion requires. The trade is the audit's own marked cost, filed at Part 10.

The pre-registration record: the five criteria, the rubric, the nine steelmen, the output format, and the one-standard-consistently instruction were fixed before the first scorer ran. The battery's one protocol amendment is disclosed in the same breath: the fifth scorer (4-e, the Russellian) was added after the first four returned, when the missing cell became visible — the battery had four adversarial personas and one neutral control, but no defender of the camp whose instrument the tribunal is. Adding the fifth scorer cannot rescue the audit's blind mean for RM (one voice in five); it can only measure the spread. It measured it (Part 3, finding 3).

## Part 4. The rival constructions — can a non-Russellian pass?

The method's fifth stroke: take the theories the tribunal failed and try to build versions that pass. Four constructions, two of which pass.

**R1 — Labeled Type-B (passes, and its passing is the audit's sharpest finding).** Take Type-B physicalism and apply the corpus's own discipline to it: state the identity as the theory's one labeled jump — "identity holds; the crossing is not derived; we label it" — and answer the dark-system objection with RM's own dissolution, verbatim: the dark twin, as specified, is an extensional abstraction, an abstraction does not duplicate a tokening, and whether abstractions instantiate is the labeled jump. No bridge is *claimed*; the observer is not *read*; the cut is *placed* — on the physical face rather than the intrinsic one, which the corpus's own formalism (C2: no object without its observation column) declines to rank. Does this rival survive the four campaigns? Doc 90's own Part 3 already answered, in the tribunal's one "genuine surprise": "the physicalist's best defense (the phenomenal concepts strategy) and Russellian monism's epistemic wing are converging on the same sentence from opposite banks — 'physics gives structure; the residue question is not settled by argument.'" The tribunal handled the convergence by absorption: "the corpus watched physicalism's best defense drift toward the Russellian epistemic wing while filing this entry, and the convergence is recorded, not exploited." The audit's cross-examination of that sentence: **a rival that survives by adopting the survival-shape is reclassified into the surviving family — so the sole survivor's uniqueness is partly definitional, uniqueness by absorption.** If Labeled Type-B is filed as physicalism, it fails a tribunal whose own text says it converges on the survivor's sentence; if it is filed as "the Russellian epistemic wing," then the survivor's name covers two camps and the field's frontier, by the tribunal's own Part 3, is "no longer at the bridges" but at the fork — where both banks stand. Either horn costs the cap. The rival construction does not need to win; it needs only to pass, and doc 90 concedes the pass in its own words.

**R2 — executed deletion (passes).** Illusionism under the licensed N2: the elimination is executed, the mechanism is documented, the theory makes discriminating predictions (introspective misrepresentation patterns), and the battery returns 9.20 — the field's second-best mean, first or second for four of five scorers. Doc 90 filed it as survivor-by-deletion with the price of "the undischarged performance, the globally lost contrast, and the question the registrar asked... returned to sender." The battery's answer to that pricing: the debt is criterion-relative — the neutral control scores the deletion as *executed* (N2 = 2), not defaulted, and the "performance" argument is Theorem II applied reflexively, which is the corpus's grammar, not the field's. The fork's left edge stands; what falls is the asymmetry that filed one edge as the sole survivor and the other as a survivor carrying a debt the rubric invented.

**R3 — the access retreat (passes, as what it always was).** GWT-as-law, battery mean 9.40, the field's best score under any unaligned lens. The tribunal filed the retreat with the highest honesty in the failure column — "this is what every bridge-theory looks like after it audits itself" — and kept the bridge-failure. The neutral control's meta-observation on the same retreat: "a Lakatosian could read [it] either as honest discipline or as a degenerating problem-shift — the rubric cannot distinguish the two." The tribunal's grammar distinguished them, in its own favor: a retreat toward measurable structure is *discipline* inside a grammar whose walls are structural, and *evasion* inside one whose residue is the datum. The audit re-files GWT exactly as the battery scored it: the field's most evaluable program, the standing survivor of the operational turn.

**R4 — the dualist patch (fails; the audit refuses to build it).** The attempt was made: correlationist dualism with the phenomenal domain causally idle keeps physics clean — and every scorer, including the ones sympathetic to realism, files the cost at the same cell (N2: report-tracking becomes brute covariation, "content validated, mechanism hand-waved"), while the interactionist branch already died on earned ground ("the anomalies never arrived" — doc 90's words, and the audit's confirmation). The construction is refused. **This is the audit's one clean confirmation of the tribunal: the failure that was filed on evidence, not on the bridge criterion, is the failure that survives every lens.**

The stroke's verdict: at least two non-Russellian constructions pass (R1 by adoption, R2 by execution), and one of them passes *the corpus's own criteria* once the absorption move is refused. **The uniqueness claim fails the rival test — which is the test the registrar designed to break it, if it was breakable.**

## Part 5. The neutral-case tests — the criteria run on heat and on computation

The method's sixth stroke: run the criteria on theories that have nothing to do with consciousness, and see whether they are too strict, too loose, or honest.

**Test 1 — the kinetic theory of heat (1845–1905).** "Temperature is mean molecular kinetic energy" is an a posteriori identity — the P\*b cell in the corpus's own taxonomy — held brute from Clausius through Maxwell through Boltzmann: the ergodic hypothesis that would have *entailed* it was postulated, not derived, and was only partially justified decades later. No instrument ever reads molecular kinetic energy directly; everything measured is a macroscopic proxy. Run doc 90's four campaigns against the kinetic theory as it stood in 1870: the argument campaign files the identity premise as "the conclusion wearing a premise's clothes" (doc 90's own sentence against physicalism, and the water/H2O disanalogy is doc 90's own — "for water, the appearance/reality distinction absorbs the modal pressure"); the measurement campaign files the instruments as proxies at the reader's boundary (the horizon lesson, verbatim); the structure campaign files the identity as a placement, not a traversal. **FILING: FAILURE (bridge).** The most successful reduction in the history of science fails the tribunal's bridge criterion at precisely the stage — brute identity held while the program earns its derivation — at which reductions are actually won. Brownian motion (1905) and the specific-heat anomalies (which forced the quantum revision, absorbed by the program, not fatal to it) came later and did the earning. The lesson, filed at the strength the registrar's test demands: **a criterion that would have failed the kinetic theory in 1870 is a criterion calibrated against early-stage reductions — and every theory of consciousness is in 1870.** Doc 90's own two-threshold discipline ("survival is a verdict about shape, not about truth"; "the failures keep... the right to say the crossing is earned" — denied, not falsified) is the correct internal reading of the tribunal, and the audit honors it. But the SOLE SURVIVOR cap was stated at one threshold, and the heat test shows what the one-threshold statement is worth.

**Test 2 — the theory of computation.** "A Turing machine is a physical causal transition structure" runs into Putnam's trivialization theorem: under liberal interpretation rules, any physical system implements any finite automaton, and the ascription of computation to physics is not free — it needs an observer/interpretation discipline to be non-trivial. Here the observer criterion (N4; the tribunal's third question, "does it read an observer?") does genuine, load-bearing work: it is what distinguishes real computational ascription from trivialization, exactly as it distinguishes higher-order theory's monitor (battery N4: 1, observer-like, non-regressive, motivated) from a homunculus (0). **The observer wall is honest.** So is the causal-closure wall (test R4 above; the audit upholds it). The audit's locating finding: **the loading is not in the tribunal's walls — it is concentrated in the bridge criterion and the dark-system criterion, the two that carried RM's uniqueness.** The walls stand; the rubric built on two of them was a camp's. A tribunal whose surviving theory passes by declining to claim what the heat precedent says science vindicates, and whose failing theories fail for claiming it, has its strictness calibrated to a camp — and now the calibration is measured, not asserted.

## Part 7. The axioms, formalized — and the theorem the audit adds

The tribunal's reasoning, written as the proof it implicitly is. Premises:

**A0 (structural completeness).** Physics, completed, is the extensional grammar's content — structure and relations; silence on intrinsic nature. *Status: the Russellian family's own premise, and the corpus's own C2/theorem — load-bearing, not neutral. It is what makes the Part 3 reduction ("the field collapses onto the corpus's open fork") derivable at all: under A0, the residue question has exactly two coherent completions — the complement is empty, or it is carried.*

**A1 (anti-brute-links).** A theory may not hold an unearned structure-experience link. *Status: neutral as stated; gerrymandered in application — aspect-identity (RM's word for its identification) was excused as placement, concept-identity (Type-B's word for the same shape of fact) was charged as a bought bridge. The parity cells show the application differential is a camp differential.*

**A2 (causal closure).** No consciousness-driven violations of physics. *Status: neutral; legitimately eliminates interactionism. Upheld by every lens.*

**A3 (anti-homunculus).** No special inner observer. *Status: neutral; vindicated by the computation test; mildly stretched against higher-order theory (a representational monitor is not a homunculus — the battery's N4 middle bucket exists for exactly this).*

**A4 (the datum must be explained, not eliminated).** First-person seemings are data. *Status: begs against illusionism — it is A0's flip side; the illusionist's entire position is that the datum is a misrepresentation. Doc 90's C1 protected the camp; its rubric still priced the position's key move as a debt.*

**A5 (the labeled-jump discipline).** Unearned claims must be labeled as jumps. *Status: the corpus's house epistemics — a discipline criterion, not a content criterion. It rewards candor about crossing, and candor is orthogonal to truth: the kinetic theorist of 1870 who *asserts* the identity flatly is 35 years from vindication, and the quietist who labels his jump is not one day closer to a crossing.*

**The derivation, and its two auxiliaries.** From A0–A5 the sole-survivor conclusion follows only with two auxiliary premises the tribunal never stated: **(i)** aspect-identity is not a brute link, where concept-identity is; **(ii)** elimination is not explanation. Strike auxiliary (i) and the conclusion becomes "several survivors" (RM-placed, Labeled Type-B, and — under the licensed N2 — executed deletion). Strike auxiliary (ii) and illusionism's deletion prices as a pass. Strike A0 and the Part 3 reduction fails outright: the field no longer collapses onto the fork, because nothing forces the residue question into two completions. **The uniqueness was never derived from neutral premises.
