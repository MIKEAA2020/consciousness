## Part 3. The findings

Four findings, in descending severity. Each is stated with its evidence, because a finding without its evidence is an assertion, and the corpus does not file assertions.

**Finding 1 (HIGH — narrative fidelity; the one sentence the documents contradict).** The map's Part 7, on amendments: *"Three executed in the census line alone (doc 78's cold-road attribution, doc 68's windowed-baseline reading, doc 60's bimodality mechanics), each refuted at the item level, each disclosed in the amending document's Status header."* The filed record supports **one**, not three. Doc 82's Status header: *"One amendment executed against the corpus's own filed reading: doc 78 Part 3's attribution 'the bulk churn burns the mid registrations' is REFUTED at the item level."* Docs 72, 75, and 78 each file the same sentence in their own headers: *"No amendment executed, no registration changed, no letter moved."* The windowed-baseline affair — which the finding's parenthetical misattributes to doc 68 — was doc 64's, and it was an amendment **refused**, not executed: doc 60 registered the candidate (a windowed baseline, the mean of rounds 3–5, "registered as the next candidate filing" in doc 60's own header), doc 64 executed it as a bitwise pure re-read and then filed *"the amendment is REFUSED as the registration; the single draw stands, with the decomposition it bought filed beside it as the reading."* Doc 68's actual role was explanatory, not amendatory — it explained doc 64's partial success ("the window averages draws and trims the gate's climb — the temperature passes through"). And doc 60's bimodality mechanics were never amended by anyone: the map's own Part 5 tells the baseline story as standing, and Part 5 itself says *"One amendment was executed against the corpus's own filed reading"* — so the map contradicts not only the documents but itself, Part 7 against Part 5. Under the map's own charter this is the one sentence where "the document wins and this map is wrong."

**Finding 2 (MEDIUM — a label that mis-scales the bracket).** The map's headline: *"λ\* in its final eighth, (0.2875, 0.30]"*; and its Part 5 line for doc 81: *"the address filed to its final eighth."* Doc 81's own filing: *"THE CROSSING NOT AT 0.2875: λ\* ∈ (0.2875, 0.30], the bracket halved to a quarter of the original interval."* The bracket's **value** is correctly reported everywhere the map states it numerically — but the fractional **label** is wrong under every frame the corpus has attested: against the original bracket (0.25, 0.30], the interval (0.2875, 0.30] is the final **quarter**; the final eighth would be (0.29375, 0.30] — precisely the cell doc 82 declined to run ("the next convention's cell, not an expectation"); against doc 77's coda frame (which counts (0.275, 0.30) as "the twelfth of the dial," a dial 0.30 wide), it is a twenty-fourth. The label overstates the resolution by one halving. A reader who navigates by the label rather than the number will mis-scale what remains.

**Finding 3 (LOW — a compression that reads two ways).** The map's Part 5 line for doc 81: *"the cliff's last half-interval concentrated three-quarters of the final fall in the last quarter."* Doc 77 placed three-quarters of the last fall in (0.275, 0.30); doc 81 found 97.6% of that half-interval's fall inside (0.2875, 0.30] (2.4% in (0.275, 0.2875]; "a fortieth of the dial carries thirty-eight percent of the total fall"). The faithful reading — the campaign found the three-quarters *concentrated* into the quarter — is available; the literal reading — three-quarters of the fall is in the last quarter — is not the filed arithmetic. The sentence is ambiguous in the direction that flatters the compression.

**Finding 4 (LOW — a count stale at publication).** The map's Status: *"two hundred forty-six files in the repository's deepseek/ folder."* True of the corpus the map maps (the task-96 count); false the moment the map shipped — its own push (batch 19: the map, its figure, the figure script, and the numbering instrument) raised the folder to 250.
