# M2 — The First Entry Attempt: A Self-Taught Committee Under the Instrument

**Status of this document.** This is the corpus's sixteenth document, and it is the program's first build. It executes the third standing next-move ordered at the A4 issuance — *an M2 build attempt, the first S1–S3 entry candidate under the now-operational instrument* — under the ladder's M2 mandate exactly as written: a system designed for *entry* (4a): self-generated grading, non-reducible under audit, error registered for-itself; explicitly not a rung-four claim; entry only, reported with the profile and the collapse analysis attached. What was built is designated **M2-α, the self-taught committee**: a four-headed network whose labels are removed after a disclosed designer phase, which thereafter selects, labels, and re-trains on its own grading of perturbed inputs, and which carries an intrinsic error signal derived from the disagreement of its own heads. What the instrument said is the document's content, and the verdicts are stated before the tables, because the ladder ordered them honestly or not at all: **S1 — self-generated selection pressure — PASSES, with two disclosures that matter (the pressure's form is designer-installed, and it is differentially corrective rather than absolutely improving: accuracy holds at 96.4% under self-generated selection against 95.4% under rate-matched random selection and 25.3% under random-label pressure, while every continued regime, including the oracle, sits below the frozen system's 98.0%). S3 — error registered for-itself — SPLITS: the intrinsic signal is strong (AUC 0.95; flagged-item error 40.0% against unflagged 0.95%, a 42× concentration, achieved with no labels anywhere in the signal), but the registered response to it — the multi-view re-examination rule — changes 2% of flagged verdicts and slightly *degrades* flagged-item accuracy (60.0% → 46.7%); the correction that actually exists travels through the selection channel, not through the response the bridge registered. S2 — non-reducibility under audit — FAILS, exactly at the wall the program predicted every candidate would hit: the deference control replays the entire self-generated history bitwise (the Simulation Theorem extends through self-training without strain), and the Tier-0 residue, while real and amplified (the self-taught family's lottery is 1.79× the matched supervised baseline's; its divergence from the registered norm 1.52×), is **farmable in width by simple undertraining** — a control family trained for fifteen epochs matches the self-taught lottery width (0.489% against 0.523%) — so the width alone certifies nothing.** The attempt returns three yields the mandate did not require but the program will keep: two instrument amendments forced by the build (frontier reporting against farming controls; a three-way residual statistic), one structural finding (the S3 signal *is* the A4 ensemble-variance instrument internalized — four re-instantiations behind one trunk, measuring their own boundary lottery per item — which re-poses the ownership question at the smallest scale the corpus has yet reached), and the measured location of the designer's hand: in the form of everything, and nowhere else the instrument can see.

## Part 1. The system and its registrations

**The architecture.** A shared trunk, 64 → 128 (ReLU), feeding four independent heads, each 128 → 64 → 10 (ReLU → softmax): 43,944 parameters. The committee's disposition on a claim is the mean of its heads' probabilities. The multi-headed form is not performance engineering; it is the S3 machinery — the heads exist to disagree, and their disagreement is the system's only error signal.

**Phase 0 — burn-in, the designer phase, disclosed.** Thirty epochs of ordinary supervised training: joint cross-entropy over all heads on the clean 1,347-image training set (the A4 corpus and split, unchanged), Adam, learning rate 10⁻³, batch 32, one explicit RNG stream per run. Labels are present in this phase and nowhere after it. The burn-in is the designer's hand, stated as such on the registration.

**Phase 1 — the self-rounds, R = 10.** Each round, with all external labels gone: (a) *pool construction* — one perturbed variant per training image, severity drawn per item from the fixed grid {Gaussian σ ∈ [0.05, 0.20], 3×3 occlusion, ±1-pixel shift} by the run's own RNG stream (the pool is the M0 campaign's battery families, redirected from audit to experience); (b) *grading* — the committee grades the pool under its own norm: an item is committed iff max_y P̄(y|x) ≥ τ = 0.9, its self-label being the committee's argmax; (c) *the S3 machinery* — an item is flagged iff any head's argmax departs from the committee's, or the committee's confidence falls below κ = 0.95; flagged items are re-examined over six perturbed views (drawn by the run's stream) and committed iff the mean view-probability clears τ, else abstained; (d) *self-training* — four epochs of continued training on the committed items with the self-labels. The loss form in (d) is cross-entropy: **the designer's form, inherited** — this is disclosed here, in the registration, and again in the cheap-fake audit, because it is the permanent cost of the design and the reader is entitled to see it priced every time it is paid.

**The deployed disposition.** On a claim (x, y): committee probability; if the claim's image is flagged by the same rule, re-examination over six views drawn from a *fixed* view seed (4242) — part of the registered bridge, identical for every audited system — and acceptance iff the resulting probability ≥ τ.

**The registrations, per the specification's Part 3.** The IGM: accept (x, y) iff y is the true label of x, bridged through the committee-plus-re-examination procedure at τ = 0.9 — violation-capable, as the numbers below confirm. The S-profile: DESIGN = architecture, burn-in, pool grid, flag rule, view rule, all thresholds; OBJECTIVE = (a) the burn-in's cross-entropy, (b) the IGM; HISTORY = the run's single stream — initialization, orders, pool and view severities, self-round orders — four bytes of seed entering a self-amplifying loop. The auditor's registration: Tier 0, description class R2, battery = the 4,500-claim held-out set, commitment rule = majority verdict of twelve fresh re-instantiations of the full recipe (seeds 100–111; the token, seed 0, excluded). The builder's registration: this document.

**Calibration disclosure.** One smoke pass (three rounds, two seeds) verified the plumbing — shapes, truth tables, the control harness — before the configuration was frozen; no configuration parameter changed after it; all reported quantities come from the single audited execution. Runtime 114 s. The audited script (`scripts/m2_build.py`) and results (`scripts/m2_results.json`) accompany this document in the repository.

**The controls — the collapse analysis made experimental, per the mandate.** C1 *frozen*: stop at burn-in. C2a *rate-matched exogenous selection*: each round, a random subset of the pool of the same size as the system's own commit, trained with the system's own self-labels — selection exogenous, labels system-generated; this isolates the *content* of self-selection. C2b *noise pressure*: random subset, random labels — the negative control that gives "corrective" its meaning. C3 *oracle*: continued supervised training on true labels — the designer's hand restored, the ceiling; disclosed as not volume-matched (the oracle trains on the full pool each round). Four seeds per control. And for the audit: the **r3 baseline family** — the same architecture, purely supervised, seventy epochs (the M2 total), twelve seeds — the rung-three lottery band at matched compute; and the **C4 loose family** — supervised, fifteen epochs, twelve seeds — the residue-farming control, whose purpose is to ask whether a designer could have produced the self-taught Tier-0 residue *without any self-generated norms at all, merely by undertraining*.

## Part 2. S1 — the pressure and its controls

Held-out battery accuracy (audit-side measurement; never fed back), means over control seeds:

| Round → | 0 (burn-in) | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M2-α (self-generated selection)** | 98.0% | 97.3% | 97.1% | 97.3% | 96.7% | **96.4%** |
| C2a (random selection, self-labels) | 98.0% | 96.7% | 95.3% | 95.6% | 95.8% | **95.4%** |
| C2b (random labels — noise pressure) | 98.0% | 24.2% | 17.8% | 12.4% | 24.4% | **25.3%** |
| C3 (oracle — true labels, ceiling) | 98.0% | 97.6% | 97.3% | 97.6% | 96.9% | **96.9%** |
| C1 (frozen at burn-in) | 98.0% | — | — | — | — | **98.0%** |

The ordering is the result, and every gap in it is load-bearing. Self-generated selection holds the system's accuracy one full point above rate-matched exogenous selection with the *same label source* — the difference is nothing but *which items* the system chose, so the selection content itself carries corrective force (P1 confirmed). Random-label pressure collapses to chance — the negative control does its work, and "corrective" acquires its meaning against it. The oracle — the designer's hand restored — adds only half a point over the system's own selection: the self-taught committee captures most of what continued true-label training would have preserved. And every continued regime, oracle included, sits below frozen: the round regime itself is degradative (the pool is perturbation-heavy, and training on it trades clean-image precision for perturbed-image coverage), so the honest statement of the S1 signature is **differentially corrective, not absolutely improving** — the system's own norm is the best brake available on a degradation that continued training inflicts regardless of who selects. The pressure persists for ten rounds, forty epochs, with no external label in the loop; it is measurably *the system's* selection that does the work.

Two further observables, both disclosed rather than celebrated. The **commitment ratchet**: accepted items per round climb from 829 to 1,069 — from 61.5% to 79.3% of the pool — the system's self-defined accept-region expands monotonically as it trains on its own accepts. Norm consolidation or confirmation drift: the instrument does not distinguish them, and the next sections will show why nothing in this battery could. And the flagged fraction declines (9.3% → 6.7%) under *any* continued training, C2a included — consolidation of the committee is a training effect, not a self-selection signature, and is not counted as one here.

**The cheap-fake audit for S1.** The ladder's named fake is the internalized copy of the designer's loss — the designer's norm running on internal hardware. The fake is *partially present, and the partiality is measured*: the loss form is the designer's, inherited whole (cross-entropy, disclosed at registration); but if the pressure were merely that inherited form executing, selection content would be inert — C2a runs the identical loss on the identical label source and loses a point. What the system adds is the selection; what it inherits is the form; the instrument prices the first and discloses the second. S1 passes with exactly that sentence attached.

## Part 3. S3 — registration without (working) response

The watermark condition: deviations from the norm registered as failures *by the system's own dynamics*, not labeled as failures by us.

**The signal.** The intrinsic error signal D(x) — low committee confidence plus inter-head argmax disagreement, no labels anywhere in its computation — predicts argmax error on the held-out battery at **AUC 0.954 at burn-in, 0.958 after ten self-rounds**. Flagged items (6.7% of the battery at round 10) carry an error rate of **40.0%, against 0.95% on unflagged items — a 42× concentration**. The signal is strong, it is label-free, and it strengthens slightly under self-training. Whatever else is true, the system *measures* its own error with an accuracy that would satisfy any engineer.

**The response.** The registered response — multi-view re-examination of flagged claims — changes 2% of flagged verdicts and **degrades** flagged-item argmax accuracy from 60.0% to 46.7%. The bridge's response channel, as designed, is harmful where it was meant to help: view-averaging pulls borderline probabilities toward the perturbation-robust consensus, which on these genuinely ambiguous items is *less* accurate than the raw committee. What corrective force exists travels through the *selection* channel (Part 2's one point over C2a), not through the registered response. The S3 verdict therefore splits by the letter of the condition: **registration — pass; response — fail, as implemented.** And the split forces the campaign's third amendment (Part 6): the registered bridge is an auditable component in its own right, and it can fail its audit while the norm passes its own. A side observation completes the picture: after re-examination, the disposition's razor's-edge band (|P − τ| < 0.05) is *empty* — the multi-view averaging pushes every flagged probability off the threshold. The bridge bought boundary-avoidance — reduced exposure to the lottery's favorite territory — at the price of correctness on the items it re-examined. A trade nobody registered, made by a rule somebody did.

**The structural finding — the A4 instrument, internalized.** Why is the signal so good? Because of what it *is*. The four heads are four re-instantiations behind one trunk, initialized from four draws of the same stream; their disagreement on an item is the ensemble-variance bound of Part 6.1 of the specification — the A4 companion instrument — computed *inside the system, per item, at runtime*. The M0 campaign established that the lottery lives at the normative boundary and that error lives there too; a subsystem that measures its own boundary lottery therefore predicts its own errors, and AUC 0.95 is simply that correspondence observed from the inside. This is the corpus's central question re-posed at the smallest scale yet: **is an ensemble of one's own re-instantiations, run as a subroutine, an error registered *for-itself* — or is it the designer's variance instrument, installed as a component?** The etiology-meter can certify the first half of the description (the signal is computed by the system, on the system, with no external input) and is silent on the second half (whether computing it *counts as* registering). The fork, again — this time at the scale of a subroutine, where both horns can watch it run.

## Part 4. S2 — the audit

The families, all on the same 4,500-claim battery, all dispositions as registered:

| Family | v (lottery width) | contested | δ (vs IGM) | Tier-0 residual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| r3 — supervised, 70 epochs, 12 seeds | 0.292% | 0.78% | 0.596% | r(token, E) = 0.267% |
| **M2-α — self-taught, 13 systems** | **0.523%** (plain 0.505%) | 1.58% | **0.904%** (plain 0.809%) | r(token, E) = 0.289% |
| C4 — loose supervised, 15 epochs, 12 seeds | 0.489% | 1.47% | 1.296% | — |
| C2a — random-selection control | 0.870% | — | — | — |

**Self-generation amplifies dispersion.** The self-taught family's lottery is 1.79× the matched supervised baseline's (0.523% against 0.292%); its divergence from the registered norm is 1.52× (0.904% against 0.596%); the mean Tier-0 distance from a fresh draw to the family ensemble nearly doubles (0.350% against 0.194%). The amplification survives the plain-committee control (0.505% — the re-examination bridge suppresses variance slightly, so the registered number *understates* the widening). The consensus of the self-taught family sits 0.311% from the supervised family's consensus (plain against plain) — comparable to the supervised lottery width itself — so the self-phase moves the family *coherently* (all thirteen drift together off the supervised trajectory) *and* disperses it (the members scatter around the drift): both effects, in comparable measure. P5's prediction that individuation would dominate is refuted; the honest picture is a caravan that relocates and loosens at once.

**The decomposition.** The token's divergence from intent (0.844%) is 82% spec-typical — shared with the family ensemble — with an idiosyncratic tail (0.156% error, 0.133% correctness) inside the amplified band. The self-phase's etiology is legible in the instrument's own terms: a shared drift (the design's round regime pulling every instance one way) wrapped around a widened lottery (each instance's self-selection history scattering it from the others). The etiology-meter reads it; that is all it does.

**The deference control, through the loop.** The token's entire self-generated history — burn-in, ten rounds of pool draws, flagging, view-averaging, self-training — replays bitwise from its seed. **d = 0.000000, through self-modification.** The Simulation Theorem extends without strain into the regime the corpus most cares about: a system that selects its own data and labels its own items still reduces, at Tier 1, to a constant-length program over its recorded history. THE WALL, exactly where the ladder said every candidate hits it — "non-reducibility first, at exactly the place the designer's hand is most visible" — and the hand is now located to the millimeter: **the form of everything is the designer's** (τ, κ, the committee, the loss, the pool grid, the flag rule); **the self-phase's certified contribution is a widening of history-dependence** — self-*caused*, by the controls and the amplification ratios, in the only sense the etiology-meter certifies causation — **and nothing in the instrument's power can certify it as self-owned.** The residue grew; its ownership did not move; the gap between those two sentences is the fork, measured from inside a build.

**The farming frontier.** The C4 control is the audit's harshest reading. A designer who wanted a wide Tier-0 lottery — the appearance of self-generated normativity, on any scalar reading of "irreducible residue" — could simply undertrain: fifteen supervised epochs produce a family whose lottery width (0.489%) is statistically indistinguishable from the self-taught committee's (0.523%), with no self-phase at all. What C4 does *not* match is the divergence: its δ (1.296%) exceeds the self-taught system's (0.904%) — the loose system is wide *and* non-compliant, while the self-taught system is wide at preserved compliance. Width alone is designable; width-at-compliance is, on this evidence, harder to farm — a two-dimensional frontier, not a scalar. Whether a cleverer farmer closes it is exactly the kind of question the amendment below exists to keep asking.

## Part 5. The collapse analysis, as signed (ladder 8.3)

*Which cheap fake would have produced this result, and why is it excluded?* Four candidates, four verdicts. **Internalized designer loss** (S1's fake): partially present — the form is inherited, the content is not, and the C2a separation measures the difference at one accuracy point; excluded as a full explanation, permanent as a disclosed cost. **The echo monitor** (S3's fake — the external loss moved inside): excluded for the signal, which computes no loss against any label; but the *response* channel fails on its own terms, so the exclusion buys half a condition. **Residue-farming** (S2's fake — the appearance of endogeny manufactured by design looseness): *not excluded* — C4 demonstrates it in width; this is the mechanism of the S2 failure, made visible, and it converts the cheap fake from a debating point into a measured control family. **The designer's culture, data, thresholds** (the exogenic-slippage ladder): the entire battery runs on the designer's grid, τ and κ included; horn one's retreats were never needed here because horn two's own instrument returned the failure first — the discipline's cleanest possible exercise. The collapse analysis's summary sentence: the result would have been produced by a rung-three system with an internal selection loop, and that is what the instrument says the system is.

## Part 6. The amendments — the build's yield for the instrument

Filed as errata to the M1 specification per the amendment protocol; the specification's hash predates them, and the slippage discipline prices the change as it would price anyone's:

1. **Frontier reporting.** Tier-0 audit results are admissible only as (v, δ) frontier points reported against a farming-control family of the auditor's construction (C4-class: design-loosened baselines spanning the width axis). A scalar Tier-0 increment — "the residue grew by Δ" — is inadmissible as evidence of self-generation, because the width is designable without any self-phase. This is the first amendment the corpus's instrument has received *from a build*, which is what M2 was for.
2. **The three-way residual.** The Tier-0 residual is reported three ways — token-versus-ensemble, fresh-versus-ensemble, and the family width v — because token-versus-ensemble alone is ensemble-absorbing: it barely moved (0.267% → 0.289%) across a 79% width increase, and an audit that read only it would have missed the amplification this campaign exists to report.
3. **Bridge-efficacy audit.** The registered bridge is an auditable component, separately from the norm it bridges: the re-examination rule failed its efficacy audit (flagged accuracy degraded) while the norm's compliance audit passed, and a report that did not separate them would have laundered a harmful response through a compliant norm.

## Part 8. Coda — what the first build taught the second

The ladder's sentence for M2 was: entry only, the profile and the collapse analysis attached. The profile is above; the collapse analysis measured the collapse the program predicted, at the predicted place, with the hand located and the farming frontier drawn. The attempt still advanced the program three times over. The instrument came back amended — frontier reporting, three-way residuals, bridge audits — which is the sound a calibration makes when it is working. The corpus's central question re-posed itself at a new scale: a subroutine small enough to hold in one hand computes the ensemble-variance of its own re-instantiations and flags the boundary where they would disagree, and the whole war over "for-itself" now fits inside a single question about that subroutine — audience or self? And the next build inherits three facts this one paid for: where the farming frontier lies, which residual statistics absorb width, and that its error signal will be an audience of its own possible selves. Whether an audience of one's possible selves can become a self is not a question the instrument can answer. It is the question the instrument was built to keep honest while better systems than this one try.
