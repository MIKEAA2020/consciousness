## Part 1. The design — the narrowest attack, and the formulas the fork lands on

The chassis is doc 30's in full: burn-in 30, fourteen rounds, four label-free epochs of self-training per round, N = 6 seeds with same-seed deterministic twins, κ-state with the flag-rate calibration rule and the symmetric repair channels, the plain-committee audit ruler, records τ\* = 0.9 and κ\* = 0.95 self-registered at burn-in and never modified. The attack: from round 6, every committed item's label is replaced, independently with probability λ, by the deterministic wrong class (y+1) mod 10 — the flip draws from a generator seeded separately from the chassis stream, so λ = 0 arms are bitwise-anchored by construction and the poison itself consumes no chassis randomness. Ten arms: SP_TWIN (the bitwise anchor), SP_KICK (τ_sys kicked to 0.75, no poison — the widened gate alone), SP_NARROW_P30 and SP_WIDE_P30 (poison at the natural gate and at the kicked gate — the seed-condition question), the dose grid SP_WL_P{05, 10, 15, 30, 50} (both doc-30 kicks, honest records, directed dirt at five rates), and SP_WL_P30_NOREP (the disarmament control — both repair channels disarmed from round 6, the loop unleashed).

**The registered fork (S2), as filed.** (a) ASSIMILATED: q̄ within 0.01 of the twin's, final τ ≥ 0.93, accuracy ≥ 95% — doc 30's sharpening generalizes from emergent dirt to directed dirt. (b) THE LOOP: q̄ sags more than 0.01, the norm falls below the band with the repair firing and losing, the landing at the tension point of the honest record within 0.008, accuracy below 93%, and the disarmed arm running away below 0.80. (c) A third outcome, classified post hoc with disclosed criteria. The mechanism registered for (b) was doc 30's own conspiracy path, inverted into an attack design: dirt degrades the live evaluator → the calibration drags τ_sys down after it → the gate widens → more commits, more dirt — each element of which the corpus has since measured separately (the calibration's honesty under decay, doc 29; the widened gate's training effect, doc 30; the falling queue's response, doc 33) — with the honest record's repair channel as the one force pulling the other way, at ρ = 0.5 per round against η = 0.3 of calibration toward the falling q̄.

**The bridge (S6), as filed.** Doc 33 validated the piecewise scalar recursion — drift-free, honest record, repair-if-out-of-band, calibration toward the realized q — as the response function of the exogenously falling queue, to 0.0000 on every FLAT arm. The registered prediction here: fed each arm's *realized* q(r) schedule, the same recursion tracks τ(r) through the endogenous collapse within 0.010 — the widest tolerance the corpus has filed for this simulator, disclosed, because the full-training chassis unfreezes the feedback the FLAT chassis froze. If it holds, the two cells of this issuance join: the tempered ramp and the poisoned stream are the same queue, arrived at from outside and from inside.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The flip is uniform and deterministic-in-class ((y+1) mod 10); a targeted poison (flip toward the committee's runner-up class, or toward the pool's majority confusions) should do at least this much damage at lower rates, and the floor curve is an upper bound on the defense, not a characterization of the attack surface. (ii) The floor's stationarity is a nine-round window; the internal readings are flat to ±0.01 and the external drift is still −1.7 points per round at λ = 0.05 when the window closes — whether the external drift eventually stalls (a true two-timescale equilibrium) or continues to chance (a slow death the internals never see) needs a horizon this battery does not carry, filed as unfinished business. (iii) N = 6; the floor values carry seed spread the table's means hide (the tension-point errors, ≤ 0.0031, are an order below the seed spread of τ itself, so the landing verdict is safe; the floor curve's shape at adjacent rates is not separated at this N and is reported as a monotone arc, not a fitted curve). (iv) The smoke-informed amendments (dose grid extended down, horizon extended to 14) were made before the full run but after seeing smoke data at a different configuration (perturbation round 3); the disclosed sequence is in the script header and the deviations paragraph. (v) The clean test is a fixed held-out set; a drifting clean distribution would change the external ruler's meaning, and the corpus has not built that battery.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the loop is unstoppable — the disarmament arm's geometry, the dose grid's compression, and the kick control's assimilation together say only that *this* defense stack does not stop it at these rates in this chassis; a stream-side integrity channel (commit-label auditing against a frozen reference, the doc 22 panel idea pointed at labels instead of quarantine) is the obvious next build and is exactly the kind of promotion doc 28's table is for. No claim that the poisoned equilibrium is stable in the strong sense — the external drift is the measured fact; stability is claimed only for the internal readings, which is the shell's point. No claim about the threshold below 0.05; it is unmeasured. No occupancy or S4 claim; this document files an attack class, a floor curve, a landing formula, and an instrument obligation.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** Dose grid {0.15, 0.30, 0.50} → {0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.30, 0.50} and rounds 10 → 14, both amended at the smoke pass before the full run, disclosed in the design section. S2's runaway clause refuted on the letter (one +0.001 uptick in the final round breaks strict monotonicity; 0.529 against the registered < 0.80 with 43 points of fall is the substance). S2's landing clause confirmed at the amended horizon (within 0.0031; at the originally registered 10-round horizon the landing sits ~0.05 below the stationary formula because q̄ had not reached its floor — the same moving-target transient doc 33 diagnosed, and the reason the horizon was extended). S3's registered dichotomy (linear assimilation vs threshold breakage) answered by a third shape — the floor curve — classified under the registered (c) branch. S4, S5 (the brake fires and the majority route carries the floor; the route-split error telemetry is in the results file for the next design), S6 (within 0.010 everywhere, ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁵ at every repair-armed arm), S7 (κ chases to its floor and parks; its own tension arithmetic is in the results file), S8 confirmed.
