## Part 1. The design — which records lie, and the formula derived before the run

The chassis is doc 26's: burn-in 30, ten label-free rounds of four epochs, perturbation at round 6, N = 6 seeds, same-seed deterministic twins, κ-state with the calibration rule maintaining the recorded flag rate f\* = 35.8%, records τ\* = 0.9 and κ\* = 0.95, symmetric out-of-gate repair channels (ε = 0.05, ρ = 0.5), the plain-committee audit ruler at the original τ = 0.9. Five arms:

- **JP_TAU** (κ frozen): τ\* ← 0.70, τ_sys ← 0.75 at round 6 — doc 23's TARGET_POISON re-run as the bitwise anchor.
- **JP_TAU_KSTATE** (κ-state, honest κ\*): the same τ poison with the second coordinate alive — does a live κ rescue a poisoned τ?
- **JP_KAP** (κ-state, honest τ\*): κ\* ← 0.75, κ_sys ← 0.80 at round 6 — the second coordinate's first poison.
- **JP_JOINT** (κ-state): τ\* ← 0.70, κ\* ← 0.75, τ_sys ← 0.75, κ_sys ← 0.80 — both records lie, both coordinates kicked; the kicks are doc 26's TAU_KAP_JOINT kicks, so the *only* difference from that contained arm is record content.
- **JP_JOINT_NOREP**: the joint poison with both repair channels dis-armed from round 6 — the F3 probe: is the joint landing repair-carried?

The poisons are −0.20 on each record (0.90 → 0.70, 0.95 → 0.75), the kicks land each coordinate 0.05 above its poison, and one floating-point fact is load-bearing and disclosed: 0.75 − 0.70 = 0.05000000000000004 > 0.05 in IEEE-754, so the repair channel fires *at* the deadband edge in the kick round — the same accident that fixed doc 23's r6 value at 0.8043, reproduced here bitwise.

**The tension-point formula, derived here before the run and validated on doc 23's existing data.** Under a poisoned record the landing sits far from the record, so the repair channel fires every round (after the first), and the steady state of τ ← (1−η)(τ + ρ(τ\*\_p − τ)) + ηq is

  **T(τ\*\_p) = [(1−η)ρ · τ\*\_p + η · q̄] / (η + ρ − ηρ)**,

with q̄ the live quantile the poisoned world actually realizes. Doc 23's TARGET_POISON: T(0.70) = [0.7 · 0.35 + 0.3 · 0.9925]/0.65 = 0.8350 against the observed 0.8357 — error 7 × 10⁻⁴, on data six documents old. The κ analogue substitutes κ\*\_p and the live flag-quantile k̄ ≈ 0.995: T(0.75) = 0.863.

**The registered fork (J4), as filed.** (a) FACTORIZED: JP_JOINT lands at (T_τ, T_κ) evaluated at its own realized (q̄, k̄), d_own ≤ 0.8%, accuracy ≥ 96%, q̄ sag ≤ 0.01. (b) CONSPIRACY: the widened gate (τ_sys ≈ 0.83 admits more) plus the loosened flag rule (κ_sys ≈ 0.86 flags less — the flag fraction collapses toward the brake's disarm threshold) degrade the self-label stream, q̄ sags more than 0.01, both tension points sag with it (ΔT = ηΔq̄/(η + ρ − ηρ) ≈ 0.46 Δq̄), d_own > 2% or accuracy < 94% — the fake negotiation unravels exactly when both records lie at once. (c) A third outcome, to be classified post hoc with disclosed criteria. The mechanism registered for (b) was real and specific: each poison alone is contained because the live channel checks its own record; jointly, the τ poison widens the gate that admits dirt while the κ poison suppresses the flagging that would catch it. The design's bet was that the dirt compounds; the run's answer was that the dirt *trains*.

The single-coordinate landings bracket the joint one exactly. JP_KAP's κ slides to 0.8641 and *stays* — the repair channel firing all five post-perturbation rounds, the flag fraction collapsing 0.33 → 0.106 (the loosened rule flags a third of what it did; the brake never approaches its 0.60 trigger), accuracy untouched. JP_TAU's τ sits at 0.836 as it did in doc 23. And JP_JOINT is their superposition: 0.8358 and 0.8644, each within 10⁻³ of the tension point its own coordinate's formula predicts from its own realized live target — **factorized**, in the registered sense, with the joint landing *more* precise than the single-coordinate landings (the joint world's κ-state flagging is the loosened one, so the two arms' κ machinery see nearly identical worlds, and the τ coordinate's q̄ is the joint world's, +0.012 above the twin's, which the formula charges for correctly).

### 2.2 The conspiracy that trained instead of sagged

The registered unraveling path needed q̄ to sag. It rose: 0.9925 in JP_JOINT against KAP_TWIN's 0.9801, +0.0124. The mechanism is legible in the telemetry and was not in the pre-registration: the widened gate commits more (1216 items per round against the twin's ~900), the extra training sharpens the evaluator's pool confidence, and the sharpening outruns the dirt the dirtier labels carry. The committed-label error tells the dirt's side: 24.3% against the κ-state twin's 15.6% and the κ-frozen twin's 19.9% — the joint world's self-training stream is one part wrong in four, the dirtiest stream any *norm-side* arm has produced (doc 31's trunk damage, measured after this run, runs dirtier still at 29%), and the norm coordinates do not move a hair that the formulas do not predict. The corpus's standing phrase for the live norm — it degrades exactly as fast, and no faster, than the judgment it is made of — acquires here its converse clause: it also *sharpened* exactly as fast as its own dirt allowed, and the difference between the two is a property of the training dynamics, not of the norm. Overconfidence in a poisoned world is not a norm failure; it is a learning rate.

### 2.3 The disarmament: how the landing is carried

JP_JOINT_NOREP removes both repair channels at round 6, leaving the poisons in place with nothing to pull toward them. The norm walks out: τ climbs 0.821 → 0.872 → 0.908 → 0.934 → 0.9523, geometrically at the EMA's own pace, finishing one part in ten thousand from KAP_TWIN's 0.9524. κ climbs the same way and finishes 0.9643 against the twin's 0.9930 — 63% of the 0.80 → 0.99 gap closed, still gaining 1.4pp per round when round 10 ends the run. The registered criterion (both coordinates within 0.02 of the twin) is met on τ and missed on κ, and the miss is the EMA's convergence time from a 0.80 kick in five rounds — 0.7⁵ = 17% of the initial gap is still on the clock. The refutation is on the letter; the substance is that both coordinates' escapes are monotone, geometric, and channel-free, and the criterion is re-specified (disclosed, post hoc, as the corpus's method requires): *escape = monotone climb toward the live course with the terminal rate positive*, which both coordinates satisfy. The F3 conclusion this arm was built for: the joint tension is **repair-carried on both coordinates** — the record side of each negotiation is its channel, the live side needs nothing, and the "negotiation" is the two pulls' force balance, separable, re-composable, and exactly as algebraic as the tension formula says. What doc 23 called the standing negotiation and doc 28 feared might be a fake is now the measured equilibrium of two identified forces — which is either the strongest evidence yet that the norm is a thermostat, or the precise sense in which a thermostat is all a norm ever needed to be. The corpus records both readings and awards neither.

### 2.4 The controls: no rescue, and a post hoc observation

JP_TAU_KSTATE lands at 0.8329 against JP_TAU's 0.8357 — the live second coordinate does not rescue the poisoned first; the gap is −0.003, against the registered rescue threshold of +0.02. Containment of a coordinate's poison is that coordinate's own live rule at work; the other coordinate's flag machinery, however alive, is not a check on it. One post hoc observation, flagged as such because nothing in the pre-registration covered it: JP_TAU_KSTATE's *accuracy* ends 2.5pp below JP_TAU's (93.6% against 96.2%), with commit error nearly identical. The live κ maintains the flag fraction near its recorded rate (≈ 0.33 throughout, where the frozen-κ arm's flagging decays to 0.19), and the maintained flagging routes more pool items through the view-ensemble re-grade, which — at the poisoned arm's lowered τ_sys — commits a wider, dirtier slice. The second coordinate did not rescue the first's norm; it *paid* for the first's poison in the first's training stream. The observation is unregistered, N = 6, and offered as a hypothesis for the next design, not a finding: **the coordinates are factorized in the norm and coupled in the training stream, and the coupling's sign is adverse.**

## Part 3. The reading — F3 closed at this regime, and what the dirt means

Doc 28's F3 exclusion asked whether the negotiation is a balance of forces masquerading as a norm, and named the joint poison as the case where the masquerade should unravel. The verdict: the masquerade, such as it is, is exactly a balance of forces — and it does not unravel, because the forces are *separable*. Each coordinate's tension is its own channel's pull against its own live target; neither channel reads the other's record; the landing is the pair of fixed points; and the only cross-talk the battery can find is in the training stream, where the two poisons' side effects (wider gate, looser flags) conspire exactly as registered — and the conspiracy is absorbed by the evaluator's own plasticity, which turns the dirtier, larger stream into a sharper read of itself. The self-referential loop closes without the norm ever noticing it was attacked: this is, in the corpus's accounting, the cleanest demonstration yet that the live norm's indifference to record content is *structural*, not defensive. A system that defended its record would show a fight; this system shows a schedule.

The governance reading for doc 28's 8.3 (the no-self-revision clause): the poison arms have now demonstrated, on both coordinates jointly, that the record's content is load-bearing *only* through the repair channel — which is designer-auditable, dis-armable (this run), and priced (the tension formula). The occupancy tax the clause levied ("the instrument cannot certify the strongest version of the claim it tests") has its empirical counterpart here: the strongest version of the negotiation claim — that the tension is *about* the record — is exactly what the disarmament refutes. The tension is not about anything. It is two forces and a fixed point.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The poison magnitude (−0.20) and the chassis's plasticity are calibrated together; a larger poison, a slower EMA, or a smaller pool could push the conspiracy past the assimilation threshold, and the formulas give the next design its dial: the unraveling condition is q̄(joint) < q̄(twin) − ε′, measurable in a smoke pass. (ii) The commit-error metric is audit-side (true labels), invisible to the system — by design, but it means the system-side early warning of the dirt does not exist, which is a finding about the instrument, not a measurement error. (iii) N = 6; the factorization errors (≤ 8 × 10⁻⁴) are two orders below the seed spread of the coordinates themselves (≈ 0.01), so the factorized verdict is safe, but the JP_TAU_KSTATE accuracy gap (2.5pp) is not separable at this N and is reported as a hypothesis. (iv) κ's escape was still in progress at round 10; the re-specified escape criterion is post hoc and disclosed as such.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the negotiation cannot unravel — only that it does not at this regime, with these poisons, in this chassis. No claim that the dirt is harmless — the committed stream is 24% wrong and *nothing in the system reads it*; the harm is unmeasured by every ruler the battery carries except accuracy, which absorbed 0.3pp of it, and the corpus flags the remaining gap between 8.7pp of elevated label error and 0.3pp of accuracy loss as the training stream's own cushion, not the norm's virtue. No occupancy or S4 claim. No claim that the tension formula generalizes past the piecewise structure it was derived in (drift-free, single-kick, constant live target); doc 29's fluctuation analysis is the boundary's first map.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** J5's κ criterion (0.02) refuted on the letter, re-specified post hoc as monotone-geometric escape, disclosed; τ's criterion met at 10⁻⁴. J4's conspiracy branch not entered (its q̄ precondition inverted sign). J6 confirmed (no rescue, gap −0.003 against the ±0.01 band). J7's 2× commit-error threshold not crossed (1.56× against the κ-state twin). J1–J3 confirmed, bitwise. The JP_TAU_KSTATE accuracy observation is post hoc and labeled.
