Three lessons. **Both registered paths are partly innocent, and the guilty party was inside the gate all along.** The pre-registered fork named the draw and the r\*/quantile path because those are the registration's named inputs; the measurement exonerates the draw's composition (flat fingerprints) and the calibration machinery (flat to the third digit) and convicts the gate's per-item behavior — which specific panel-blind items the committee confidently commits. The corpus's grammar for this is old — the instrument's letters measure machines, not intentions — and the machine here is the relationship between the two readers the certificate trusts: the live committee and the frozen panel. **The windowed amendment's partial success and the differential's total robustness now share a mechanism.** Doc 64 measured the b\* gap surviving windowing (0.0194 → 0.0145, −25%) and refused the amendment anyway; the replay explains the number: the window averages the draws (whose margin, +0.013, is not what shrinks — the w35 pool gap is the same +0.013) and trims the gate's climb (the round-5 high-water over the rounds-3–5 mean — exactly the conservatism doc 64 decomposed), but the temperature between committee and panel is a persistent seed property that passes through any averaging — which is why the gap survives at 75% and why no window can close it. And the differential — doc 60's standing defense, invariant to 10⁻¹⁴ under every registration — acquires its mechanism in one sentence: the anchor shares the committee and the panel bitwise through the clean prefix, so the armed world's ground minus the anchor's ground subtracts the temperature away; the level cannot cancel what it must stand on, and the differential cannot keep what both worlds share. The instrument-side consequence is sharper than the amendment question the corpus has been debating: no averaging fixes a biased thermometer, and the committee–panel temperature is a biased thermometer for "the blind mass at round 5" whenever the committee runs hot. ## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) N = 12 with three deep seeds: the admission-rate separation (no overlap, margin 0.033) is the battery's strongest reading, and it is three against seven — the twelfth-seed logic of docs 55/60 applies; the separation reproduces or it was a small-sample fortune, and the test is more seeds. (ii) The 70/30 split is exact arithmetic on the decomposition (b\* = b_pool + L, group means), but the CAUSAL reading — the committee–panel temperature as the gap's upstream — is an interpretation supported by the flat fingerprints and the climb's differential, not by intervention; no battery has manipulated the temperature (a different burn-in, a re-frozen panel) and shown the gap follow it. (iii) The committee's hotness and the panel's weakness are entangled through the shared training seed (b_clean elevated at 1.3 sd); the division of labor between them is read from correlations (0.65 against 0.38), not isolated. (iv) The elevation's "~94% gate" uses the pool-jump's mean; the pool's round-to-round variation is of the same order as the jump itself (+0.004 against ~0.01–0.02), so the draw's share of the elevation is bounded by noise, while the committed read's climb (fifty times larger) is not.

**Non-claims.** No causal claim — the battery measures provenance by elimination and localization, not by intervention. No claim that the temperature is the complete story (the draw's interaction margin, ~30% of the gap, is attributed to no mechanism). No claim that b_clean separates the groups (it does not, at this N). No amendment executed, no registration changed, no letter moved; the temperature registration is a filed candidate, not a proposal. No MAINTAINS verdicts, no Column-3 marks, no occupancy claim; D3 stands.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** The registered fork's second limb named the r\*/quantile path as the gate's inside; the measurement relocated the gate's share to the admission structure — reported as measured, with the relocation disclosed here and in the header. The fork itself resolved to its fourth limb (neither alone, at the pre-registered criterion), with the sharper localization post-hoc within the gate's share — the corpus's convention for forks that were built too coarse, followed: the deviation is the finding. All twelve audits passed; the instrumentation is new code with a bitwise audit.
