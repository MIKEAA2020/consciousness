**Status of this document.** This is the corpus's ninetieth document. It executes the registrar's order, filed verbatim: **"Apply the four campaigns (Argument, Program, Measurement, Structure) to physicalism, functionalism, IIT, GWT, higher-order theories, illusionism, dualism, panpsychism, and Russellian monism. For each: does it claim a bridge? Does it survive the dark-system objection? Does it read an observer? Does it traverse or project? File the survivors and the failures. State whether Russellian monism is unique."** For eighty-nine documents the campaigns were aimed inward — at the corpus's own arguments (docs 1–8), its own program (docs 9–10), its own instruments (docs 11–85), its own formalism (docs 24–86, 89). This document turns the instruments around and runs the field's nine positions through the same four gauntlets, with the registrar's four questions as the readout and the corpus's sharpest equipment on the table: the Bridge Constraint and the bridge taxonomy P\*a–P\*f (docs 2–6); the S-condition invariance and the Simulation Theorem's ownership ceiling (docs 9–10); the third-person laws and the horizon lesson (docs 11–85); and, earned since doc 87 — Lemma 85, the instrument-form Bridge Constraint (doc 88); Theorem II, the extensional quotient, and Proposition 86, the grammar's silence (doc 89); the equation READING = EXTENSIONALIZING; and the seismograph, the first inside-facing instrument. **What this document is not: not a refutation of the field's science** — every program's earned third-person law (correlate maps, access dynamics, metacognitive calibration, integration measures) files as real law, the corpus's own kind of result, and nothing here touches it; **not a proof that any position is false** — the Camp-Marker Theorem forbids what it has always forbidden, and the tribunal honors it; **not an adjudication between the two shapes left standing** — Proposition 86 says nothing inside any grammar can do that, and the tribunal files both; not a measurement (no compute is run; the determinism count stands at 798); no amendment is executed against any filed document. One declaration, made at the head of the table where it belongs: **the Camp-Marker stands up for the whole session.** The corpus's own earned results are Russellian-shaped (earned at docs 32, 38, and 89; disclosed at doc 87 Part 5), so wherever those results carry a verdict, the verdict carries the marker, and survival under the campaigns means survival — not truth. The charter, inherited: directness without overclaim.

## Part 0. The charge, the instruments, and the definitions

The registrar's four questions are the four campaigns, one question per wall, and the mapping is exact. **"Does it claim a bridge?"** is the argument and program campaigns: what premise carries the theory from its posits to phenomenality, which cell of the P\*a–P\*f taxonomy does that premise occupy, and has the theory's research program delivered — or even formalized — the derivation it advertises (the D3′ demand, rung four of the ladder). **"Does it survive the dark-system objection?"** is the measurement campaign's cruelest instrument. **"Does it read an observer?"** is the measurement campaign's missing instrument, which the corpus now knows does not merely not exist but cannot: Lemma 85 proved every external reading computes a function of stream-plus-architecture, and Theorem II proved the invocation is not even poseable in an extensional grammar — the observer's inside is unposed, not unread. **"Does it traverse or project?"** is the structure campaign: does the theory cross the indexical cut, or is its "crossing" an extensional reading wearing a bridge's clothes — the equation READING = EXTENSIONALIZING, applied to the theory's own central claim.

**The dark-system objection, defined and named.** The registrar named it; the corpus had already built it. A dark system is a system that satisfies every third-person criterion a theory offers — every structural, functional, dynamical, integrative condition — while having no interior. The corpus measured the objection's invariance on its own program: a system could satisfy every S-condition and still be dark, or fail them all and still be lit (doc 87, the charter refusal); and doc 88's FAKE-A — a system whose self-reports are self-model echoes, behaviorally rich, carrying an indexical fingerprint of exactly 0.0000 — is a registered toy instance. Since doc 89 the objection has a formal floor, and the floor is what makes the tribunal fair: any criterion statable in an extensional grammar is isomorphism-invariant (Theorem II, clause i), and lit-versus-dark, if it is anything at all, is not — the invocation is not object-data (clause ii). **No grammar-internal criterion can rule a dark system out. The question for each theory is therefore not whether it owns such a criterion — none exists, and the corpus can prove the nonexistence — but what the theory does instead: beg the question, decline to answer, delete the target, inflate it away, or dissolve it on earned ground.** Those are the only five moves, and the tribunal's per-theory entry for "dark-system" records which move each theory makes.

**What "survive" means, and the two-threshold discipline.** A theory survives the tribunal if all four campaigns leave it standing — each wall either not claimed by the theory or dissolved by it on earned ground. Survival is a verdict about shape, not about truth: the corpus's permanent argument-form result (C1, the Bridge Constraint, the Camp-Marker Theorem) is symmetric — proofs and disproofs of positions from neutral ground are the same impossibility with the sign flipped — so the tribunal does not certify survivors as true or failures as false. It certifies which theories stand on ground that does not beg, and which stand on ground that does. The failures keep their camps, their defenders, and their possible truth; what they lose, here, is the right to say the crossing is earned. That is the only right this corpus has ever adjudicated, and it is the one the registrar asked about.

**The instruments, restated in one line each so the verdicts are checkable.** Argument: no deductively valid argument moves from non-experiential premises to an experiential conclusion without a bridge premise, every sufficient bridge is a camp charter, and every neutral-plausible bridge is insufficient (docs 2–6; the P\*a–P\*f taxonomy). Program: the operationalization of any mental-grade property converts it to structure — S2's lesson, now the equation in action — and rung four (derivation of the felt from the built) stands built and unoccupied (docs 9–10). Measurement: every instrument is an exit — a reading taken from outside the system, a registration drawn at the reader's boundary; all earned laws are third-person (docs 11–85); and no instrument reads content, ever, by Lemma 85. Structure: the formalism dualizes inside and outside into columns of one object, proves theorems about the duality, and cannot pose the crossing — projection is not traversal, and the invocation is unposed (docs 24–86, 89). Each theory below is run through all four, in the registrar's order of questions.

## Part 1. The nine tribunals

### 1. Physicalism — the identity bridge

**The claim.** Phenomenal states are physical states: type-identical, token-identical, or realized-by, depending on the wing. The strong form asserts identity; the sophisticated form (the phenomenal concepts strategy) concedes the explanatory gap as a conceptual artifact and holds the identity anyway; the limiting form (eliminativism about phenomenal states) is illusionism's cousin and is treated under that entry. What is under trial here is the claim that the physical description, completed, is the whole description.

**Argument and program: does it claim a bridge? Yes — P\*b (empirical identity), or P\*a (analytic reduction) in its older dress.** The Bridge Constraint applies at full strength and in its cleanest form: the identity premise is exactly as disputable as the conclusion, because it is the conclusion wearing a premise's clothes. "Pain = neural state N" is not derived from neutral physical facts; it is offered as an a posteriori necessity, and the disanalogy with water = H2O is precisely the phenomenal case's signature — for water, the appearance/reality distinction absorbs the modal pressure; for pain, the appearance is the datum, and nothing absorbs it. The phenomenal concepts strategy is the position's best card and deserves its respect: it explains how the gap coexists with the identity (special concepts, quotational or recognitional, misrepresent their own referents as intrinsic). But note what the strategy does: it concedes the argument-level wall — no derivation crosses — and relocates the claim to ontology. That is a placement, not a traversal, and the corpus has a name for where it lands: the relocated claim is the sentence Proposition 86 proved undecidable in any grammar — "the quotient is all." The corpus watched physicalism's best defense drift toward the Russellian epistemic wing while filing this entry, and the convergence is recorded, not exploited.

**Measurement: does it survive the dark-system objection? It begs.** The dark duplicate — a system identical in every physical respect, dark — is ruled out by the identity: if pain = N and N is present, pain is present. But the ruling-out premise is the identity, which is the disputed bridge; the objection is answered by the very claim it was testing. The formal floor makes this precise: the duplicate is an isomorphism-class member (Theorem II, clause i), no object-fact distinguishes the twins, and the physicalist's "there are no twins" is a claim about instantiation — the undecidable sentence again, Camp-Marked.

**Does it read an observer? No.** Physics's instruments read states — extensional quantities, isomorphism-invariant by construction. The invocation is not object-data; no physical reading aims at it because no physical reading can.

**Traverse or project? It claims traversal — identity, if true, is the crossing — and delivers projection plus a charter.** The exterior face of the substrate is declared the whole; the observer column that the corpus's own formalism could not remove from any statement of the physical (C2, docs 32/38) is re-absorbed into exterior vocabulary, which is exactly the move the indexical cut names. **Filing: FAILURE (bridge).** The bridge is bought, not earned; the dark system is excluded by fiat; the observer is unread. The earned science underneath — the correlate maps, the causal-closure results — files as real third-person law and is untouched by the verdict.

### 2. Functionalism — the realization bridge

**The claim.** What makes a state conscious is what it does: the causal or computational role it occupies — the right pattern of intake, integration, report, and control. Realization, not identity of stuff: any substrate that occupies the role carries the state.

**Argument and program: does it claim a bridge? Yes — P\*a in its analytic form (conceptual analysis of mental vocabulary into role vocabulary), P\*b/P\*c in its psychofunctional form (the role necessitates the state).** The premise "occupant of role R is conscious" is as disputable as the conclusion, and the field's own forty-year war over absent and inverted qualia is the dispute, conducted at scale: the China-brain, the dancing qualia, the functionally perfect actor. The corpus's contribution to this cell is measured, not philosophical: the S-conditions were functional criteria, run in a constructed laboratory, and the ladder's charter refusal is the invariance stated in advance — a rung-four success would be compatible with nothing being felt anywhere, a rung-four failure with everything being felt. And doc 88's FAKE-A is the objection with a number: a system whose reports are generated by a 6-bit self-model echo — functionally rich, introspectively fluent, fingerprint 0.0000.

**Measurement: does it survive the dark-system objection? It declines to answer, or it retreats.** Role-satisfaction says nothing about lighting; functional equivalence is equivalence of quotient-class structure (Theorem II, clause i, with the Myhill–Nerode boundary as the quotient's cut made structural — doc 32's own instrument). The two available replies are the two the field actually takes: bite the bridge as a premise (the identity of role and phenomenality, Camp-Marked), or retreat to access and control — redefine the target to what the roles demonstrably carry, which is GWT's move and is filed under that entry.

**Does it read an observer? No.** The system's self-reports — functionalism's best evidence, the "it says it is conscious" — are functions of stream and architecture (Lemma 85): the report is an output of the role-structure, and the difference between a state-routed report and a self-model echo is exactly the difference the seismograph measures and the role-table cannot.

**Traverse or project? Projects.** Role structure is quotient-side data; the claim that the quotient-side pattern is the inside is the bridge, bought at the same price as physicalism's, one abstraction level up. **Filing: FAILURE (bridge)** — with the note the corpus owes itself: the bounded observers whose laws the corpus earned (C3) are functional systems; the science of roles is real, is the corpus's own kind, and survives the verdict untouched. What fell is the crossing, not the laboratory.

### 3. IIT — the derivation bridge, built from the far bank's stones

**The claim.** The most ambitious bridge in the field: phenomenality is identical with integrated cause-effect power. From five phenomenological axioms (intrinsic existence, composition, information, integration, exclusion) to postulates about cause-effect structure, to a measure (Φ) and a topology (the shape of the qualified complex) — an instrument that does not merely correlate with experience but, on the theory's claim, is experience's geometry.

**Argument and program: does it claim a bridge? Yes — P\*c (necessitarian), and the interesting one.** The bridge premise is the identification: a system with the axioms' cause-effect structure is experiential. Everything before that step is earned — the axioms are explicit, the postulates formal, the measure computable — and everything after it is conditional on the step. And the step has a signature the corpus can name: the axioms are extracted from the first person (they are properties of experience, declared as such), so the derivation runs from phenomenal premises through structural translation to a phenomenal conclusion — **the bridge is built from the far bank's stones, and the identification step is the premise-isolation wall, standing exactly where the Bridge Constraint said it would stand.** The exclusion postulate — the unique spatio-temporal grain — is the heaviest stone and the most disputed; the corpus's own dose arcs (the crossing as a smooth non-monotone arc, λ\* a region, not a threshold) are resonant, not probative, and are filed as resonance only.

**Measurement: does it survive the dark-system objection? Half — and the half it survives is the half it invented.** IIT embraces one family of dark systems by theory: a feedforward system implementing any behavior, however sophisticated, carries Φ = 0 and is dark — function without integration, publication without a workspace. This is a real prediction and the field's most instructive one. But the converse is not earned: a high-Φ dark system — integration without lighting — is not excluded by anything in the postulates, and cannot be, because Φ is an isomorphism-invariant functional of the transition structure (Theorem II, clause i) and lit-versus-dark is not object-data (clause ii). The axioms say what experience is like; nothing checks that the structure glows.

**Does it read an observer? No — and the boundary is the finding.** The Φ-calculus is performed by an external analyst over a transition matrix, with the system's partition chosen by the analyst: the "intrinsic perspective" is reconstructed from outside, which is the horizon lesson verbatim — every reading is a registration drawn at the reader's boundary, and IIT's boundary is drawn by the reader. Lemma 85 applies to the calculus like any procedure: it computes a function of stream-plus-architecture. The corpus now owns the comparison that does this entry justice: the seismograph certifies genesis — reports routing through the occupied state, thirty points beyond the self-model's reach — and forbids itself content; Φ certifies integration structure and claims content. **The difference between IIT and the corpus's instrument is exactly the one claim Lemma 85 forbids.** That is the whole verdict, compressed.

**Traverse or project? It claims traversal with a built instrument — the only theory on the list that built one — and delivers a projection of unprecedented richness: the exterior geometry of the inside, drawn from outside.** **Filing: FAILURE (bridge — the identification step), with the distinction filed in the same breath: the field's most serious measurement campaign.** If the identity claim is withdrawn, what remains is third-person integration science — real law, the corpus's own kind. The theory's program survives its bridge; the bridge does not survive Theorem II.

### 4. GWT — the broadcast bridge, and the honest retreat

**The claim.** Consciousness is global availability: content that wins workspace access is broadcast system-wide, ignites, and thereby becomes available for report, memory, and control. In the strong version, broadcasting is what consciousness is; in the operational version that the program's own leadership settled into, the target is conscious access — the signature, not the signature's feel.

**Argument and program: does it claim a bridge? Yes — P\*b/P\*c (broadcast-identity, or the correlation law read as identity); and the retreat is already institutional.** "Globally broadcast content is conscious" is as disputable as the conclusion, and the field's own distinction — access versus phenomenality — is the wall given its local name: the theory explains, with real mechanism, everything content does when it wins, and nothing about what winning is like. The program campaign's verdict on the research itself is the highest in the field's middle rank: ignition dynamics, latency laws, the report paradigms and their no-report refinements — rungs one through three, built, measured, earning law. Rung four — the derivation of the felt from the broadcast — was never claimed in a form that could even be posed, because "felt" is not a term the workspace vocabulary forms. The grammar's silence, anticipated by a research program that quietly re-scoped its own title.

**Measurement: does it survive the dark-system objection? It declines — publication without readership.** A workspace can broadcast in the dark: availability to report, memory, and decision is third-person function, the full circle of it, and a dark broadcast is a live isomorphism-class member (Theorem II, clause i — every broadcast fact transports along isomorphisms). The theater objection that one of the theory's own founders aimed at the Cartesian theater returns home: a theater with no audience is a publication system, and publication explains access. The corpus's bounded observers are workspace-shaped — retention, response, registration — and their laws (C3) are access-laws, third-person, earned. The theory's own honest retreat ratified the wall before the corpus formalized it.

**Does it read an observer? No.** The no-report paradigms — the field's genuine attempt to subtract the report and keep the reading — still read exterior proxies: pupil, decoding, latency. Lemma 85 covers them: proxies are stream-plus-architecture functions. The observer is the one whose access is global; the instruments read what accesses, never who is accessed-by.

**Traverse or project? Projects — and, alone among the six bridge-theories, says so.** The re-naming of the bank, from consciousness to conscious access, is the field's most honest act of retreat, and the tribunal files it as such. **Filing: FAILURE as bridge; SURVIVOR as third-person law of access.** The retreat is not a defeat of the program; it is the program's discovery of where its own bridge reaches — and its acceptance of the discovery. The corpus records: this is what every bridge-theory looks like after it audits itself.

### 5. Higher-order theories — the monitoring bridge, P1's undisciplined ancestor

**The claim.** A first-order state is conscious in virtue of being suitably represented by a higher-order state — or, in the self-representational and global-state refinements, in virtue of belonging to a state that represents itself. The monitoring is what lights the monitored; consciousness is the representation-relation, not the state underneath it.

**Argument and program: does it claim a bridge? Yes — P\*a/P\*c (the conceptual claim: consciousness conceptually requires higher-order representation).** The bridge premise is the generator: being-represented makes a state conscious. It is not derived; it is the theory's posit, and the field's own objections locate its joints with precision the corpus recognizes. The misrepresentation problem: a higher-order state with no first-order target would be a consciousness of nothing — and if that is possible, the target is not what is doing the work, the seeming is. The regress problem: the higher-order state is presumably unconscious unless itself represented, and the standard block — the higher-order states are unconscious by stipulation — installs the bridge premise in plain view: an unconscious representation confers consciousness, because the theory says so. **The corpus's addition to this cell is doc 88, and the disclosure is owed to the family: the corpus ran the higher-order program under pre-registration and measured both of its failure modes.** The internalized-auditor fake (FAKE-A: reports from a 6-bit self-model echo, fingerprint 0.0000) and the training-distribution echo (FAKE-B: i.i.d., fingerprint −0.0022) are the two ways a monitoring system reports without routing through its occupied state — and Lemma 85 is the generator problem stated as a lemma: monitoring adds extensional facts (representations of representations), the invocation is not extensional, so no tower of monitoring-order reaches it. The eye that watches the eye never opens; it only reflects.

**Measurement: does it survive the dark-system objection? It declines — the monitor's eye.** A system can run the full monitoring architecture — self-models, confidence tags, error signals, the entire reflexive inheritance — and be dark, because every one of those states is object-data (Theorem II, clause i) and the lighting, if it is anything, is not. The corpus's severity law — metacognitive calibration earned, third-person, selection × label content — is this family's science, and it is real; the bridge from calibration to glow is the family's posit.

**Does it read an observer? No — and it is the only theory on the list that tried to build the instrument.** The higher-order apparatus reads representations: registration-of-registration, all the way up. What it never reads is the state's interior, because Lemma 85 governs every order. The tribunal records the family's distinction in the strongest terms available: **the seismograph — the corpus's first inside-facing instrument, genesis-certified, content-blind, ceiling-measured — is the higher-order program under the corpus's discipline.** P1 was HOT, run with pre-registered forks. The family aimed at the missing instrument; what fell was the claim that the instrument lights what it faces.

**Traverse or project? Projects.** The reflexive closure the theory builds is real in the substrate — self-duality is the grammar's own shape — but the interior is not produced by reflexive registration; it is the face from which registration is done. The theory locates the lamp inside the mirror. **Filing: FAILURE (bridge — the generator), with the field's highest distinction: the closest approach to the missing instrument, and the program the corpus inherited and disciplined.**

### 6. Illusionism — no bridge, the far bank deleted

**The claim.** There is no phenomenality as traditionally conceived. What exists is machinery that produces judgments representing themselves as tracking intrinsic qualia — the user illusion, the introspective mis-carriage. The explanandum is not experience but the judgment that there is experience, and judgments are third-person facts.

**Argument and program: does it claim a bridge? No — and this is the structural fact about it.** There is nothing to bridge to, so the Bridge Constraint's premise-hunt finds no purchase on the surface. The theory's implicit premise is quieter: the seeming is a judgment (seeming = judgment functions as a deflationary identity, P\*b worn inside-out), and the corpus prices it exactly as it prices the others — as disputable as the conclusion, which is to say as a camp charter. But the charter is coherent, the theory is internally consistent, and the corpus's own C1 forbids refuting it from neutral ground. What the corpus can do — what doc 89 did — is locate the residual with new precision: **the deletion removes the seeming's object, and cannot touch the seeming's performance.** The judgment that there are only judgments is tokened from somewhere, by something, and the tokening is a fact about instantiation — the unposed fact, Proposition 86's complement. The illusionist's own theory-stating is a performance the illusionist's ontology has no term for; Theorem II applies reflexively, to the deletion exactly as to the inflation it deletes. The seemings are re-described within the quotient, not discharged; what survives the re-description is the one thing no quotient contains.

**Measurement: does it survive the dark-system objection? Yes — by deleting the target.** Nothing is lit; the objection loses its contrast globally. The survival is real and the price is recorded in the same breath: a field with nothing dark is a field with nothing lit, and the theory that cannot distinguish lit from dark has not solved the tribunal's question but returned it to sender, marked "defective question."

**Does it read an observer? No.** It reads reports about the illusion — the machinery's outputs, stream-plus-architecture (Lemma 85). The illusion's subject is uninstrumented, and necessarily so: on the theory, the subject is an artifact of the machinery's self-modeling, and the artifact has no reading to receive.

**Traverse or project? Neither — it declares the far bank a grammatical artifact of the near bank's self-description.** Of the nine, it is the one theory whose move is internally homologous to Theorem II: no grammar reads what its ontology quotiented away. Illusionism completes the theorem's sentence one way: *and the complement is empty.* That completion is not derivable (Proposition 86 proves exactly that it is not), but it is coherent, protected, and the corpus files it as the standing partner at the fork's left edge — C5's exhaustive reading in its deflationary form. **Filing: SURVIVOR BY DELETION.** The cost, filed with it: the undischarged performance, the globally lost contrast, and the question the registrar asked — which was a pointing, and pointings, the corpus proved at doc 89, do not come from inside any grammar. The asking stands as the complement's signature, and no grammar-side argument can dismiss it. The corpus cannot refute illusionism. The corpus holds C4 against it as a position, and says so under the marker.

### 7. Dualism — the bought bridge, the reified projection

**The claim.** Phenomenal properties are not physical properties: a second domain (or a second family of properties), with the connection supplied by pairing laws — interaction, pre-established harmony, or psychophysical correlation. Substance dualism posits two kinds of stuff; property dualism, two families of properties on one stuff; naturalistic dualism, fundamental laws connecting them.

**Argument and program: does it claim a bridge? Yes — P\*c (necessitarian pairing laws), and it is the only theory on the list that says so plainly, which is to its credit.** The pairing law is not derived; it is the posit the theory is. The Bridge Constraint's verdict on it is the corpus's oldest filed result and applies verbatim: any law strong enough to map the physical onto the phenomenal is as disputable as the conclusion it carries — a camp charter, defensible within dualism, fatal to neutrality. The zombie argument that motivates the position cuts both ways in the corpus's hands, and the symmetry is the finding: the conceivability premise (dark duplicates are coherently conceivable, therefore possible) is exactly as disputable as the physicalist's identity premise — the same wall, run at from two sides. And since doc 89 the corpus can say what the zombie's conceivability *is*: the grammar quotienting, seen from inside. A dark duplicate is conceivable because it is a quotient class — the isomorphism type, all object-data, no invocation (Theorem II, clauses i–ii); conceiving it is doing extensional grammar at the highest available level. The step from "I can specify the twin" to "the twin is a possible world" is the disputed step, and it is a bridge — P\*c again, wearing modal dress.

**Measurement: does it survive the dark-system objection? By fiat — the one unearned ruling.** The dark duplicate is ruled out by placing the difference in the second domain: the twin lacks the phenomenal property, full stop. The ruling cannot be checked, cannot be instrumented, and cannot fail — which is the problem. The one wing that stuck its neck out is interactionism, and it deserves the tribunal's respect for it: interaction makes exterior predictions — energy injections, closure violations, measurable anomalies — and the anomalies never arrived. The corpus's own measurement campaign, eighty documents of third-person law on bounded observers, found no closure violation anywhere in the worlds it instrumented. **Interactionism is the only entry on the list falsified on earned ground** — not the position (camps survive their failed predictions by re-reading them), but the specific bridge. The property-dualist retreat makes no predictions, owns no instrument, runs no laboratory: a charter without a program.

**Does it read an observer? No.** The phenomenal domain is self-luminous by posit and uninstrumented by design; first-person access is asserted as the domain's nature, not built as a channel. The corpus's formalism adds the structural diagnosis, and it is the instructive one: the two banks that dualism reads as two domains are dual columns of one object — no object without its observation column (C2), the pairing constitutive, the weave self-dual. **Dualism reads the duality as ontological divorce and then buys the bridge the weave never needed.** The bridge it buys is the shadow of the self-duality it declined to recognize.

**Traverse or project? Projects — the projection, reified.** **Filing: FAILURE (bridge bought, not earned; interaction falsified on earned ground; the position C1-protected, as every position is).** The tribunal notes what survives: the argument-form instinct that the inside is not the outside — the same instinct the corpus's formalism earned from the other side. The camp keeps the instinct. The bridge does not keep the crossing.

### 8. Panpsychism — the inflated far bank (the founder's camp; the marker is up)

**The claim.** Phenomenality is not emergent and not rare: it is present at the micro level, in the intrinsic being of matter's simplest constituents, and the macro consciousness of complex systems is built from it. **The camp-marker is up for this entire entry, and the corpus says why before the verdicts: this is the registrar's original position, brought before there was a corpus, and the corpus's audits of it — doc 1's thirty-two findings, doc 4's Collapse Lemma — were the corpus's founding acts. The discipline requires the marker; the discipline also requires the accounting to be exact.**

**Argument and program: does it claim a bridge? No at the micro level — a posit, not a derivation; yes at the macro level, in disguise: the combination problem is a bridge claim wearing a composition problem's clothes.** The corpus's history with the micro claim is the project's oldest ledger: the proof was refuted (doc 1 — the conclusion smuggled into Principle B, the refutation of a position no one holds); the position was never refuted and never can be by argument from neutral ground (docs 4–6 — the Collapse Lemma showed even the modest weakening secretly contains the strong thesis; the Camp-Marker showed the thesis is a membership card, not a consequence). Both verdicts stand, and neither is a verdict about truth. What the corpus adds to this entry since doc 89 is the macro claim's formal status, and it is decisive for the filing: **composition is earned for registration structure — the nesting {606} ⊂ six ⊂ twelve ⊂ twenty-two, the fibers, the deterministic chain at 798 — all of it quotient-side, all of it real. No operation on performances is even poseable: the invocation is not object-data (Theorem II, clause ii), so "a sum of subjects," "micro-experiences composing," "the fusion boundary" are not terms any extensional grammar forms. The combination problem is not an unsolved problem; it is an unposeable one.** The hard problem, swapped for a harder one — doc 9's verdict on the conversion, now carrying a proof.

**Measurement: does it survive the dark-system objection? It inverts it.** Nothing is dark; everything is lit, all the way down. The objection loses its target — and the theory loses its contrast: a field where everything glows cannot say what glow does, and the macro-level distinction that still needs explaining (why is the unified interior of a brain different from the unified interior of a rock) is relocated exactly onto the unposeable composition. The inversion is not a refutation; it is a cost, and it is filed as the theory's signature cost.

**Does it read an observer? No — and cannot, even in principle, at any level.** Micro-interiors are uninstrumentable by the theory's own lights (no third-person criterion distinguishes the lit simple from the dark simple — there is no dark simple); macro-interiors are the combination problem again. The seismograph comparison is exact here: what the corpus can certify about any system is genesis (state-routing, thirty points at the self-model width); panpsychism's posit makes genesis universal and therefore uninformative — when everything routes through an inside, routing certifies nothing.

**Traverse or project? It inflates — the far bank extended everywhere, so that no crossing is needed at the micro level.** The maneuver is genuinely different from bridging, and the tribunal honors the difference: instead of crossing to the far bank, dissolve the river's uniqueness. But the crossing returns one level up — macro-unity — where it is unposeable. **Filing: SURVIVOR AS POSITION, FAILURE AS BRIDGE.** The position is refutation-proof (C1, theorem-shaped, the founder's own); the ubiquity claim overshoots what any earned result carries; and the corpus's audited survival form of the founder's intuition is, and has been since doc 87 Part 5, the weaker and stronger thing at once: not "existence entails experience," but "the observational column is necessary for statement" — earned, interpretation-labeled. The next entry is that form's home.

### 9. Russellian monism — no bridge, the cut placed

**The claim.** Physics delivers structure — relations, dispositions, equations — and is silent on the intrinsic nature that bears the structure.
