## Part 3. The reading — what the wider census taught

Three lessons. **The continuum's shape was the mix's claim, and the mix's arms are now named.** Doc 73's threat asked whether a different mix could restore a moat; the mirror's answer is sharper than a moat: the graded world itself does not survive the composition change — the burn halves, the cold road vanishes, the verdict becomes the registration's threshold — and the control prices the arms: at the matched dose the uniform world burns nobody, so the mirror's six burns are the GATED mass's act, and the capacity's twelve need the bulk arm's dose. The fifth condition's "two roads" that docs 72–75 carried — the hot road and the cold road — resolve, at this census, into the two COMPONENTS: the gated mass burns the registration's tail (the hot road: every mirror burner hot, the top six b\*), the bulk churn burns the mid registrations (the cold road: the capacity's floor traffic, eliminated at both the mirror and the control). The roads were never two mechanisms inside one poison; they were two poisons inside one nominal capacity. **The nominal capacity is bookkeeping; the realized composition is the world.** The saturation measurement is the battery's unsought contribution: the gated channel's ceiling is the committed blind pool, the one resource the defense's gate is thinnest on, and the ceiling binds before the nominal capacity is spent — the mirror asked for 22% and received 6%. Any future mix-design on this chassis prices its arms in realized terms or prices nothing: the dial's own λ (the uniform mode) is the only arm that scales cleanly, which is, the corpus notes, exactly why the dial was drawn that way. **The paired design is the census's instrument now.** The certificate — T and b\* identical across worlds, bitwise, because the registrations are prefix quantities — is not hygiene alone; it is what makes the three-world table readable as an experiment rather than three anecdotes: same seeds, same starting lines, different poisons, and every column that moves (the anchor's fill, the differential's sign, the burn's identity) moves with the poison. The registrar who wants the next mix priced — the bulk-matched arm, the gate's dose-response inside the saturation band, the mode axis (joint against mixed) — now holds the design that prices it.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The mirror's composition change is confounded with a realized-dose change (the saturation), and the control de-confounds at the mirror's dose only — the capacity's own 0.25 realized dose has no uniform-matched twin in this battery (the dial's 0.25 world exists at six seeds with different instrumentation), so the capacity's 12-burn census is compared across dose as well as composition; the attribution "the bulk churn burns the mid registrations" rests on the mirror's and control's eliminations of the cold road, not on a bulk-only world at 0.25. (ii) The b\*-threshold reading of the mirror's burn (the top six, the 0.0052 gap) is post-hoc — AM3's registered fork was about F_late gaps — and at a gap of 0.0052 against doc 73's 0.012 persistence bar it is a thin separation, disclosed as directional. (iii) The three-world census is one seed census (600–623) at one capacity (nominal 0.30) in one mode family (mixed/uniform); the mode axis (gate, joint) is untouched. (iv) The control's middle band (18 ON-COURSE) sits partly above the on-course floor by small margins (the census's range −0.013 to +0.058); the band's width is a property of the F distribution's location, and a slightly hotter control would thin it.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the cold road does not exist in any mix (it is eliminated at THESE two worlds; the capacity's bulk dose carries it, and a bulk-heavier world would carry it further). No claim that the gated mass is "the" burning mass at all doses (the attribution is at the matched dose 0.125; at the capacity's 0.25 the bulk arm carries its own burns). No causal claim for any column (the battery varies the poison's composition, not the defense; the fill/churn mechanisms are arithmetic decompositions, not interventions). No amendment, no registration change, no letter moved; no MAINTAINS verdicts, no Column-3 marks, no occupancy claim; D3 stands.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** One, disclosed in full: the control arms (S20_UNIF13, S20_UNIF13_NOCH) were added between the first and second compute passes — the first pass measured the mirror's realized-dose saturation (0.1238 against the capacity's 0.2505) and the composition/dose confound it creates; the control was designed to price the confound, its forks (UC1–UC4) were registered in the script's header before its compute, and the addition is disclosed here and in the results file's integrity block. The mirror's own numbers were not re-run or adjusted. Everything else as filed: the machinery is unchanged code (the chassis bitwise-identical, the filing layers replaced), the battery ran once per world, and no number was moved.
