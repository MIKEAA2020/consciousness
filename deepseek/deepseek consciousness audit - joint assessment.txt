## 2. Head-to-Head Contradictions, Adjudicated

### C-1: Is S's argument repairable?

- **D:** Yes. Provides per-issue fixes plus a full "Revised Positive Argument" (§6 of D), concluding panexperientialism is "the most plausible ToE."
- **Z:** No. The load-bearing premise is question-begged; the "eight converging arguments" are one circular move double-counted; the advertised conclusion is not derived.

**Adjudication: Z.** D's own reconstruction fails D's own diagnoses — three times over:

(a) D's Issue 3 rules Epistemic Availability circular and demands it be weakened *and* C established independently. D's §6 revised positive argument then re-states the premise **unweakened** ("The only intrinsic nature we know directly is experience") and supplies no independent argument for C.
(b) D's Issue 6 declares the form–substance dichotomy false ("modern physics rejects this"), while D's §6 justifies Concrete Grounding by re-asserting it ("A purely relational entity is an abstraction, not a concrete thing").
(c) D's Issue 10 concedes proto-experience is undefined, while D's Issue 4 fix asserts panexperientialism "avoids unknowns."

A repair that re-commits the diagnosed fallacies is not a repair. Furthermore, no D fix supplies the two things the positive argument cannot close without: an extrapolation rule from minds to electrons (Z C10) and any answer to the combination problem (Z C1). Verdict: S's argument is not repairable at the level of rigor S claims. What survives is a much weaker conditional (see C-7).

### C-2: The modal formalization — drop □(P→C)?

- **D (Issue 1b):** The necessity operator is "unjustified"; strict materialism claims only sufficiency; replace □(P→C) with P→C — "the refutation still holds."
- **Z (B4):** □ is the only non-trivial part of the derivation; the real flaw is S's conflation of a priori derivability with a posteriori necessity.

**Adjudication: Z.** Three reasons.

(i) *Dialectical:* reductive physicalism asserts identity (pain = C-fiber firing), and Kripkean identities are necessary if true. Demanding □(P→C) is therefore the materialist's **strongest** charter, not a strengthening of a straw man. Dropping it attacks a weaker position than the live one.
(ii) *Internal to D:* D's Issue 1a complains the refutation is tautological; D's Issue 1b fix makes it **more** tautological (from P∧¬C infer ¬(P→C) — pure truth-functional bookkeeping). The fix re-introduces the defect diagnosed.
(iii) *Substantive:* the actual defect Z's B4 isolates — treating failure of a priori deducibility from structural description as failure of necessitation — survives D's fix untouched. D's side-remark ("confusing logical necessity with metaphysical possibility") gestures at the right distinction but draws the wrong repair.

### C-3: Does physics refute the form–substance dichotomy?

- **D (Issue 6):** "In physics, form *is* substance. A wavefunction **is** a physical entity... You are assuming a Cartesian dualism between 'form' (math) and 'substance' (matter), but **modern physics rejects this**."
- **Z (B3, C5):** Denial of the dichotomy is the structural realist's research program (OSR, MUH) — a live, contested stance. S (and any reconstruction) simply assumes the dichotomy: question-begging.

**Adjudication: Z, with credit to D's spirit.** Physics qua physics is silent on whether description exhausts nature; that question is metaphysics *interpreting* physics. D's categorical "modern physics rejects this" overreaches — wavefunction realism itself is contested (ψ-epistemic vs. ψ-ontic; configuration-space worries; spacetime emergence in quantum gravity). The correct statement is Z's: the dichotomy is denied by a serious live program, and eliminating OSR by citing Concrete Grounding is circular. D repeats exactly this circularity in Issue 7 (structural realism "violates Concrete Grounding" — the very principle OSR denies). D's underlying instinct — that S conflates model with entity — is sound and matches Z's B2/B3; D's formulation overshoots into a false claim about what physics has established.

### C-4: Parsimony — reorderable or decorative?

- **D (Issue 4):** The counting flaw is fixable: let Concrete Grounding eliminate strict materialism first; parsimony then only adjudicates dualism-with-X vs. panexperientialism.
- **Z (B7):** The metric is gerrymandered (chosen after the winner); parsimony does no independent work; by other natural metrics panexperientialism is *less* parsimonious (a universal experiential category plus unexplained binding facts).

**Adjudication: substantially Z; D's fix is correct as description, wrong as defense.** D's reordering silently concedes Z's structural point: all eliminative work is done by Concrete Grounding alone — the "elegance and parsimony razor," S's advertised engine, is decorative. D draws no such conclusion and presents the reordering as a rescue. Worse for both D and S: after grounding eliminates strict materialism, the parsimony comparison between Candidate 2 (unknown X) and Candidate 3 (undefined Proto, per D's own Issue 10) is a **wash** — both posit one unknown intrinsic kind. D never notices that its Issue 4 fix and its Issue 10 finding cannot both stand (see §4, SC-3/SC-6).

### C-5: Mental causation — is non-reductive physicalism the fix?

- **D (Issue 5 sub):** The causal-symmetry critique misfires because strict materialism does not imply epiphenomenalism; "non-reductive physicalism (e.g., emergent consciousness) allows mind → physics"; epiphenomenalism is a subset of dualism.
- **Z (A5, A6, C7):** Agrees on the misattribution (convergent finding #4) and adds: (i) NRP's mental causation is itself contested (Kim's causal exclusion problem); (ii) identity physicalism dissolves the asymmetry trivially and is missing from S's candidate list; (iii) S's own favored dual-aspect "solution" is epiphenomenal about the phenomenal *as such*.

**Adjudication: Z.** D's fix oversells a contested position: under Kim's exclusion argument, if every physical event has a sufficient physical cause, NRP's distinct mental causes are excluded or redundant. Presenting NRP as cleanly permitting mental causation treats an open problem as settled. And D's passing concession — "reductive physicalism (consciousness = physical processes) **does** allow mind → physics (since mind *is* physics)" — names exactly the theory that satisfies causal symmetry at zero cost, yet D never adds it to the candidate set in Issue 7 or §6, where it would defeat Candidate 3's claimed uniqueness. D diagnoses the right disease and withholds the standard medicine.

### C-6: Proto-experience — definable or structurally doomed?

- **D (Issue 10):** Proto-experience is vague; fix = define it ("non-conscious but capable of becoming conscious," or "neither physical nor mental"); revised claim: "Existence entails an experiential or **non-physical intrinsic** ground."
- **Z (A4, C8):** The dilemma is structural, not lexical: if proto is experience-like, electrons carry proto-feelings and the combination/absurdity problems arrive; if it is not, Candidate 3 collapses into Candidate 2. No definition escapes both horns (the collapse dilemma for panprotopsychism).

**Adjudication: Z — demonstrably, by D's own text.** D's first proposed definition ("non-conscious but capable of becoming conscious") inherits the combination dynamics (becomes conscious *how*, under *what* law?) that D never addresses. D's second ("neither physical nor mental"), and the revised claim built on it ("experiential or non-physical intrinsic ground"), literally re-labels Candidate 2's unknown X — the theory D's Issue 4 rejected *for positing an unknown kind*. D's repair converts its winner into its rejected loser. This is the sharpest single demonstration in this adjudication that the problem is structural, not terminological.

### C-7: Final verdict on S's thesis.

- **D:** After repairs, panexperientialism is "the most plausible ToE," softened to "not logically necessary."
- **Z:** The argument fails on its own terms; nothing follows about what is true; the honest residue is agnosticism pending engagement with combination/collapse and the rival candidates.

**Adjudication: Z, with one concession to D.** D is right that an abductive case for panexperientialism could *in principle* be made, and right that S's own §3 caveat marks the honest reading of S. But D's reconstruction does not deliver even plausibility, because it re-commits the circular premise (C-1a), never engages the position's canonical unsolved problems (combination, decombination, collapse), and never engages its strongest rivals on their merits (OSR, illusionism, identity physicalism, the ignorance hypothesis). The maximum defensible conclusion from S's materials, after the best repairs from **both** audits:

> IF concrete entities require non-structural intrinsic natures (denied by OSR), AND IF first-person acquaintance reveals such a nature (denied by illusionism and representationalism about introspection), AND IF parsimony is truth-conducive in unconstrained metaphysics (undemonstrated), THEN panprotopsychism is one candidate worth taking seriously — alongside neutral monism and dualism-with-X, which by then are its equals.

That is a research suggestion, not a ToE verdict. S's rhetoric of proof, impossibility, and necessary entailment is unsupported — on this, both audits agree at the diagnostic level; the adjudicated difference is only that D's reconstruction fails to overturn the diagnosis it shares.

- **Taxonomy inflation:** Z's count of 9/11/12 items is aggressive; some overlap (A5 and B6 share the candidate-set issue; C4 and B7 overlap on ignorance and parsimony). A stricter editor might consolidate. D's eleven issues are flatter but cleaner.
- **Tone:** Z is prosecutorial; D is editorial. Tone does not track truth, but it affects usefulness to an author sympathetic to S.

## 5. Meta-Observation

All three documents are machine-generated: S (DeepSeek), D (DeepSeek), Z (Super Z / GLM). Two structural facts emerge.

First, the ten-point convergence between the two audits (§1) is the strongest available evidence that S's diagnosed defects are real. Models with different training and no shared context do not converge on ten findings by coincidence — and notably, D converges with Z even though D shares a vendor with S.

Second, the divergence pattern is instructive. D audits charitably and constructively but never checks its repairs against its diagnoses; Z audits prosecutorially and offers no repairs, so it cannot re-commit them. The failure family D exhibits in §4 — *diagnose-then-recommit* — is the same family Z attributed to S (escalating confidence across restatements; formal decoration of informal moves). The lesson generalizes: in machine-generated philosophy, the binding constraint is not intelligence but **self-consistency discipline across an argument's own restatements and repairs**.

## 6. Final Adjudicated Verdict on S

1. S's negative argument is definitionally vacuous — **upheld** (Z B1; D Issue 1 at the diagnostic level; D's repair fails per C-2/SC-5).
2. S's positive argument is circular at its load-bearing premise (Epistemic Availability), as both audits independently found; it additionally begs the question against structural realism, equivocates on "symmetry" and on Exp, and derives less than its headline.
3. S's position is uncredited Russellian monism / panprotopsychism, and the century of objections to that position (combination, decombination, collapse, ignorance, illusion) is absent — from S, and also from D.
4. Even after the strongest repairs from either audit, the defensible residue is a heavily conditionalized plausibility claim for panprotopsychism as one candidate among several — not a proof, not an impossibility result, not a ToE verdict.
5. S's rhetoric of "proof," "impossible," "mathematically and metaphysically impossible," and "necessary entailment" is unsupported. Both audits concur at the diagnostic level; the adjudicated difference is only that D's reconstruction does not overturn the diagnosis it shares with Z.
