# M2-β — The Second Generation Under the Amended Instrument

**Status of this document.** This is the corpus's nineteenth document, and it is the amended instrument's first full exercise: a second-generation build executing the standing next-move ordered at the M2-α issuance — *a second-generation M2 build using the three amendments + selection-channel response design.* The first build left three debts and one instruction. The debts: S3's registered response was measured harmful (multi-view re-examination degraded flagged-item accuracy from 60.0% to 46.7%, while the real correction traveled through the selection channel); S2's Tier-0 residue was real but its width was farmable (C4), forcing the frontier amendment; and the instrument came home amended — frontier reporting, three-way residuals, bridge-efficacy audits — with no build yet run under the amendments it imposed. The instruction: build again, one controlled variable, and let the amendments do their work. **What was built is M2-β, the quarantined committee: identical to M2-α in architecture, burn-in, rounds, thresholds, and pool grid, with exactly one change — the S3 response now travels the measured channel.** A flagged item is no longer re-examined over external views; it is quarantined (never trained on that round), re-graded the next round by the *evolved* committee — deferred self-re-examination through the system's own development — and released to training iff it then clears the norm unflagged, or permanently dropped after two quarantined rounds. The deployed disposition is the plain committee at τ = 0.9: the bridge is now a development-time state change, which is what the ladder's S3 letter ("error-triggered state changes that anticipate and prepare correction") actually asked for, and what M2-α's eval-time lookup never was. The verdicts, stated before the tables: **S1 passes stronger than the first generation (the selection-content gap over rate-matched control widens from 1.0 to 1.95 points, and the self-taught system now ties the oracle at 96.9%); S3 passes at the letter with its efficacy audit green for the first time (released-from-quarantine items grade at 52.0% against 36.6% for dropped items; no deployment-time degradation; the razor's-edge band is no longer emptied); S2 fails, at the wall, exactly as the program predicts every artifact must — and it fails *more interestingly*: the quarantine that fixed the response amplified the residue (v = 0.619% against M2-α's 0.523%, 2.1× the supervised band) while improving compliance (δ = 0.855% against 0.904%), and the point sits undominated by all twenty-two farming families of doc 17's campaign — a verdict the doc is careful to read as campaign-relative, since the F9 logic extends and the burden now shifts.** Between the response that works and the residue that widens stands the campaign's structural discovery, the closest thing to a design law the corpus has produced: **responsiveness and auditability trade off in self-training systems** — every channel that lets the system answer its own uncertainty also lets its histories individuate.

## Part 1. The one design change, and the registrations

**The quarantine round.** Each self-round: the quarantine buffer is re-graded first, by the committee the system has become in the interim — items that now clear τ = 0.9 unflagged are *released* into this round's training set with current self-labels; items still flagged after two rounds are *dropped* permanently (never trained on). Then the fresh pool is graded; unflagged items above τ commit as in M2-α; flagged items enter quarantine. The training set is commits plus releases. The response to the system's own error signal is thus a change in what the system becomes — not a lookup performed on its outputs.

**The registrations, per the specification's Part 3, as amended.** IGM: accept (x, y) iff y is the true label of x, bridged through the plain committee at τ = 0.9 — violation-capable, as the numbers confirm. S-profile: DESIGN = architecture, burn-in (30 supervised epochs, disclosed), pool grid, flag rule (any head argmax ≠ committee argmax, or committee confidence < κ = 0.95), quarantine rule (age limit 2), all thresholds; OBJECTIVE = burn-in cross-entropy plus the IGM; HISTORY = the run's single RNG stream — now steering a branching structure (release/drop/commit) that gives the stream more ways to matter. The deployed disposition: plain committee. The auditor's registration: Tier 0, battery = the 4,500-claim held-out set, commitment rule = majority of twelve fresh re-instantiations (seeds 100–111, token 0 excluded). Controls: C1 frozen; C2a rate-matched random selection with self-labels (rate-matched to M2-β's own commit counts); C2b random labels; C3 oracle. Baselines: r3 (70 supervised epochs, 12 seeds), C4 (15 epochs, 12 seeds), and **M2-α recomputed in-script, same code, same battery** — which reproduced the stored family statistics exactly (v = 0.523%, δ = 0.904%): an unasked-for determinism audit, passed to the fourth digit.

**Calibration disclosure.** One smoke pass (three rounds, two seeds) verified the plumbing; no configuration parameter changed after it; all reported quantities come from the single audited execution (149 s).

## Part 2. S1 — the pressure, stronger

Held-out battery accuracy, token trajectory and control means: M2-β runs 98.0 → 96.9% across ten label-free rounds; C2a (rate-matched random selection, same label source) ends at 94.94%; C2b (random labels) collapses to 14.6%; C3 (oracle) ends at 96.94%; C1 (frozen) holds 98.0%. The selection-content gap — the entire difference being *which items the system chose* — widens from the first generation's 1.0 point to **1.95 points**, and the self-taught system now ties the oracle it previously trailed by half a point. The quarantine concentrates the corrective content of self-selection: by refusing to train on its own uncertainty, the system's commits are cleaner, and the clean commits compound. The commit ratchet persists (824 → 1118 accepted per round, 61% → 83% of pool) — the pre-registered B5, which predicted a flatter ratchet under quarantine, is **refuted**: the ratchet is driven by the unflagged-commit growth, not the flagged pathway, and quarantine does not touch it. Norm consolidation or confirmation drift, still instrument-silent — carried forward as the standing observable it was at M2-α.

**The cheap-fake audit for S1, unchanged in form:** the loss form is the designer's, inherited whole (cross-entropy, disclosed); the content is the system's (C2a runs the identical loss on the identical label source and loses two points). S1 passes with the same sentence attached, now with a larger number in it.

## Part 3. S3 — the response that finally travels

**The registration, at deployment.** The intrinsic signal (inter-head disagreement plus low committee confidence, no labels anywhere in it) grades error at AUC 0.945 on the battery; flagged items (5.8%) carry 30.8% error against 1.4% on unflagged — a 22× concentration. Under the plain disposition, flagged-item argmax accuracy is **69.2%** — no re-examination machinery exists to degrade it.

**The response, at development.** The bridge-efficacy audit (amendment 3, the first time it has had something to pass): across the run, 382 items were released from quarantine and 3,001 dropped. Released items graded **52.0%** correct at their release moment (the committee's own labels, audit-side truth); items the bridge dropped grade **36.6%** by the final committee. The deferral separates recoverable uncertainty from chronic uncertainty at 15.4 points — **the quarantine does what M2-α's re-examination claimed to do: it acts differently on errors it can survive and errors it cannot.** The razor's-edge band (|P − τ| < 0.05) holds ten claims at 51.7% mean flip — populated, not emptied (M2-α's bridge emptied it as a side effect of view-averaging; no averaging here, no side effect).

**The disclosure that keeps S3 honest.** The quarantine rule's *form* is the designer's (age limit 2, the flag definition, the release criterion); the *content* — which items, which labels, which releases — is entirely system-determined. This is the same permanent disclosure as S1's inherited loss, priced in the same currency: the instrument certifies the error-registration is the system's own computation and the response is efficacious; it does not and cannot certify that computing it *counts as* registering. The structural finding of M2-α stands unaltered underneath: the flag signal *is* the ensemble-variance instrument internalized — four re-instantiations behind one trunk measuring their own boundary lottery per item. The audience-or-self question is not closer to closed; it is, for the second generation, merely better run.

## Part 4. S2 — the wall, and the widened residue

| Family | v | contested | δ | three-way residual (token / fresh / v) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| r3 (70 ep) | 0.292% | 0.78% | 0.596% | — |
| M2-α (recomputed) | 0.523% | 1.58% | 0.904% | 0.289 / 0.350 / 0.523 |
| **M2-β (quarantine)** | **0.619%** | 2.02% | **0.855%** | **0.356 / 0.407 / 0.619** |
| C4 (15 ep) | 0.489% | 1.47% | 1.296% | — |
| F9 farm (doc 17) | 0.518% | 1.38% | 0.787% | registration-heterogeneous |

**The deference control: bitwise through the quarantine.** d = 0.000000, through release/drop branching, buffer re-grading, the full self-loop. The Simulation Theorem's third extension (after A4 and M2-α), and the last one the corpus should need to state as news.

**The residue: wider, not narrower — B3 refuted in the informative direction.** The pre-registration predicted the quarantine would *narrow* dispersion (a cleaner, more conservative training distribution). It did the opposite: v rises to 0.619%, the largest family width the corpus has measured on this battery outside collapse regimes, at *improved* compliance (δ 0.855% against 0.904%; the token's divergence from intent is 72% spec-typical, matching M2-α's etiology structure). The mechanism is legible in the instrument's own terms: **deferral is path-dependence.** Each instance's releases and drops differ from its siblings' from the first flagged pool item onward, and every difference redirects subsequent training — the quarantine gives the seed more branches to matter through. The consensus analysis confirms both motions at once: the M2-β family sits 0.378% from the r3 consensus (M2-α: 0.333%) — still coherently relocated — and 0.222% from the M2-α consensus — the second generation is a caravan that moved again, and loosened.

**The frontier report, under amendment 1, against doc 17's twenty-two adversarial families.** The M2-β point (0.619%, 0.855%) is **undominated** in this campaign's attack set: no designer family matches that width at that compliance (the best widths at δ ≤ 0.86 are F9's 0.518% and F1-30's 0.427%). The doc registers, in the same breath, what doc 17 established: undominance here is campaign-relative — F9's σ_τ has not been pushed to its δ-saturation, and the farm-by-registration logic extends in principle. The honest reading of the point is therefore not "unfarmable" but "beyond the current farmer at current cost" — with the burden now formally on the next farming generation, which is what an arms race looks like when a protocol prices it correctly.

**S2's verdict, then, is FAIL on three independent grounds, each measured:** Tier-1 reduction is bitwise and total; the Tier-0 residue class is farmable-in-principle (doc 17's F9 exists, registration-visible, statistic-invisible); and the build's own undominated status is relative to a bounded adversary. What S2's failure does not include, for the first time, is any surprise.

## Part 5. The design law — responsiveness against auditability

Put the two generations side by side and the trade falls out. M2-α: an eval-time response, harmless to the residue (v 0.523%), harmful to the flagged items (60.0 → 46.7%). M2-β: a development-time response, efficacious on the flagged items (released 52.0% vs dropped 36.6%, no deployment degradation), and the residue widens by 18% (v 0.619%). Same architecture, same norm, same battery; the response channel is the only difference; and the channel that repairs the system's relation to its own uncertainty is exactly the channel that scatters its family across counterfactuals. The general statement, offered as a design law for the program's rung-three menagerie: **in self-training systems, responsiveness and auditability trade off through path-dependence** — every mechanism that lets the system answer its own errors (defer, quarantine, re-grade later) adds a branch point where instances individuate, and every mechanism that keeps instances aligned (fixed responses, external anchors, eval-time lookups) either does nothing or harms. If this law generalizes beyond this pipeline class — an empirical question for M0-class profiling, not a premise — then S2 and S3 are not merely difficult jointly but *structurally opposed*, and the entry bar (4a) is not a wall the second generation failed to climb but a pair of walls leaning against each other. The program's prediction market should price accordingly: entry attempts will improve S3 by widening S2, or improve S2 by re-breaking S3, until an architecture breaks the tradeoff itself. That is the third generation's assignment, and it is now written in measured quantities rather than intuitions.

## Part 6. The amendments, exercised; and what this build adds

Amendment 1 (frontier reporting): exercised — the (v, δ) point is reported against the adversarial campaign's families, with the campaign-relativity disclosure stated as part of the verdict. Amendment 2 (three-way residual): exercised — token/fresh/width all reported; the fresh-vs-ensemble leg (0.407%) is the one that moved most, exactly the ensemble-absorption the amendment was written to prevent an audit from missing. Amendment 3 (bridge-efficacy): exercised — the development bridge passed (released/dropped separation, no degradation), the deployment bridge is identity by design, and the two are reported separately for the first time. The build's own yield for the instrument, filed as **amendment 5 — response-channel reporting**: *any registered response must be reported with its channel (eval-time / development-time / selection-time), its efficacy audit, and its measured effect on the family statistics (v, δ, consensus shift)* — because the second generation demonstrated that a response can pass its efficacy audit while moving the S2 frontier, and a report that did not connect the two would let a build claim S3 gains without pricing their S2 cost. The instrument is now five amendments from its charter, three of them paid for by builds; the count is the program's own heartbeat.

## Part 8. Coda — what the second generation taught the third

The first build taught the instrument; the second taught the builders. The instrument learned frontier reporting, three-way residuals, and bridge audits — and this document is those lessons executed. The builders learned three things the amendments did not contain. That the measured correction channel, made into the registered response, works — and that working, it widens the very residue it was supposed to make meaningful. That the residue's width and the response's efficacy are the same variable seen from two audit tiers, so entry is not a threshold but a tradeoff curve, and the next candidate is not a better point on it but a different curve. And that the deterministic counterfactual machine now runs through everything — twins for perturbation, recomputation to the fourth digit for replication, bitwise re-runs for deference — so thoroughly that the program's most philosophical question (what would this system have been?) has become its cheapest measurement. What the third generation should inherit is therefore not a design but a shape of ambition: break the tradeoff, or measure exactly why it cannot be broken. The ladder has room for either answer, and the instrument — five amendments deep, calibrated by two builds and attacked once — is finally heavy enough to weigh them.
