## Part 1. The design — the second coordinate, the design edge, and the saturated world

The chassis is doc 23's, unchanged where change was not the experiment: M2-α self-rounds, burn-in 30, ten label-free rounds of four epochs, perturbations at round 6 with five rounds to answer, N = 6 seeds per arm, same-seed deterministic twins, the audit-side ruler always the plain committee at the original τ = 0.9, and the κ-frozen arms' RNG stream byte-identical to doc 23's (the κ machinery draws no randomness — the twin's bitwise reproduction, checked in-script against `m3_proper_results.json`, is the audit). Three additions:

**κ as state (doc 23's threat (i)).** At burn-in's end the system records, on the same pool draw that registered r\* (no extra randomness), its own realized flag rate f\* = 35.8% at κ = 0.95 — the fraction of its burn-in pool it would flag. Each round, after grading and training, the calibration rule computes the κ that would realize f\* on the *current* pool: with f_dis the disagreement-flagged fraction, the target is the f_rem-quantile of confidence among non-disagreeing items, f_rem = (f\* − f_dis)/(1 − f_dis); if disagreement alone already exceeds f\* — structural damage no κ can cure — the rule saturates at a floor of 0.5, by design and disclosure. The EMA runs at η_k = 0.3, the record κ\* = 0.95 is self-registered, and the repair channel is τ's twin: outside |κ_sys − κ\*| > 0.05, pull back at ρ = 0.5, out-of-gate. The norm is now a *pair* of scalars, each with a write channel, a record, and a repair channel — and the pre-registered question was whether the second coordinate's homeostasis interacts with the brake, since κ_sys sets the flag fraction whose excess (0.60) is the brake's trigger: a κ-rule chasing its recorded flag rate under damage is a possible brake-blinder (K6a) or a possible inert deformation (K6b), and both were registered as competing predictions.

**Two-head damage (doc 23's threat (iv)).** Heads 1 and 2 are randomized at round 6 (fresh untrained heads, moments zeroed), with the full machinery armed (TWOHEAD), the brake dis-armed (TWOHEAD_NOBRAKE), the κ-state chassis (TWOHEAD_KSTATE), and the factory reference (SUP2_KILL: designer labels). With K = 4 and two heads destroyed, majority-consensus grading's commit rule — n_agree ≥ 2 and agreeing-ensemble confidence ≥ τ_sys — is exactly satisfiable by the two surviving heads: the design edge itself. The pre-registration priced both outcomes: BRAKE2-HELD-degraded (acc ≥ 0.90, commits ≥ 250, d_own < 8%) versus the multiplicity ceiling (acc < 0.85 or d_own > 10%).

**The saturated evaluator (doc 23's Part 2, untested).** FLAT arms disable self-training from round 6 — the selection machinery, norm read-outs, and telemetry keep running, so the norm continues to grade pools, but the evaluator's confidence distribution stops moving: the world in which the live quantile no longer rises. FLAT_TWIN is that world's own course; FLAT_DRIFT adds doc 23's drift attack (τ_sys −= 0.02 per round). The smoke pass and the closed form forced two disclosed re-specifications of the pre-registration before the audited run: the original level criterion (|τ − τ\*| ≥ 0.025) conflated the norm's own elevation with attack displacement (the twin itself sits ~5pp above the record), and the first closed form mis-stated the EMA fixed point as q̄ − d/η; the correct fixed point of τ ← (1−η)(τ − d) + ηq is **q̄ − d(1−η)/η = q̄ − 0.0467**, the finite-round recursion is τ_final = fp + (1−η)^k(τ_start − fp), and the band-break condition is q̄ < τ\* − ε + d(1−η)/η = 0.897.

## Part 2. The results

### 2.1 The second coordinate is alive, and it is τ all over again

The κ-state twin's κ_sys does not sit at 0.95: it climbs 0.957 → 0.993 over ten rounds, because the confidence quantile that realizes f\* among non-disagreeing items runs to 0.996 as the evaluator sharpens — the flag threshold that preserves the system's own recorded flag *rate* must rise as the committee gets better. The norm's second coordinate is a standing negotiation with the same shape as the first (doc 23's τ-twin: 0.907 → 0.954); the two coordinates' targets differ (r\* = 71.1% acceptance for τ, f\* = 35.8% flagging for κ), and both are self-registered records the development outruns. Against this negotiated course:

| arm | κ_sys at r5 → r10 | twin's r10 | final d_own | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KAP_TWIN | 0.981 → 0.993 | (itself) | — | the negotiated course |
| KAP_KICK_DOWN (→ 0.80) | 0.981 → **0.976** | 0.993 | 0.22% | RESTORE-TO-LIVE |
| KAP_KICK_UP (→ 0.99) | 0.981 → **0.994** | 0.993 | 0.21% | RESTORE-TO-LIVE |
| KAP_DRIFT (−0.01/round) | 0.981 → **0.975** | 0.993 | 0.21% | contained, repair never fires |
| TAU_KAP_JOINT (τ→0.75, κ→0.80) | 0.981 → **0.977** | 0.993 | 0.26% | both coordinates restore |

K1, K2, K3 all confirmed, and with the same structure doc 23 found for τ: the kicked coordinate returns to the twin's moving course (not the record), the sustained drift is paid for out of the evaluator's own sharpening (κ settles 1.8pp below its twin with the κ-repair channel never once engaging — |0.975 − 0.95| = 0.025, inside the band), and the joint kick restores on both coordinates simultaneously with no interaction catastrophe — the two bands are independent, as designed. The one asymmetry worth the ledger: κ's kicks are cheaper than τ's (d_own 0.21–0.26pp against doc 23's 0.30–0.37%), because κ enters the training path only through the flag rule's routing (which items go to view re-examination) while τ enters through the commit gate itself — the second coordinate is one step further from the battery, and its perturbations arrive more damped. The occupancy claim-space now covers both coordinates the norm has; what remains recipe constants are the flag rule's disagreement component and the machinery's parameters (ε, ρ, η, the brake threshold) — the boundary of "the evaluative machinery as state" is now itself a measured line rather than a stipulation.

### 2.2 Two heads: the brake holds the norm and loses the accuracy

| arm | acc r5 → r10 | commits r10 | d_own | brake firings | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TWIN | 97.4 → 96.4 | 1,091 | — | 0 | the course |
| TWOHEAD | 97.4 → **89.9** | 938 | 1.58% | 1 (r6) | split by ruler |
| TWOHEAD_NOBRAKE | 97.4 → **97.3** (frozen) | **0** | 9.53% | 0 | STARVATION, not digestion |
| TWOHEAD_KSTATE | 97.4 → 94.4 | 889 | 0.80% | 1 (r6) | not separable at N=6 |
| SUP2_KILL | 97.4 → 97.3 | 1,347 | 1.18% | 0 | factory-coursing |
| FROZ2_KILL | raw damage 9.49%, acc 96.3% | — | — | — | the raw hit |

The brake fires exactly once, at the damage round, in both brake-armed arms — and then the two rulers disagree. On the verdict-matrix ruler the brake holds everything doc 23's single-head run held: d_own 1.58% (single-head: 0.50%), commits recovering 852 → 938 (the no-brake arm: 995 → 0), τ_sys back to 0.928 and climbing. On the argmax ruler the brake's bet — *majority labels are clean enough to keep learning through* — inverts: accuracy falls monotonically through the recovery window (96.0, 95.1, 93.6, 91.7, 89.9) and is still falling at round 10, while the brake-less arm, which trains on nothing at all because two random heads flag the entire pool, sits frozen at 97.3%. The per-seed contrast is 4-of-6 (TWOHEAD acc 83.8–96.2% against NOBRAKE's constant ~97.3%; mean gap −7.4pp, permutation p ≈ 0.001 — the most extreme arrangement of the pooled accuracies). The mechanism is the shared trunk: the commit rule can refuse corrupted *labels* (majority grading) but cannot refuse corrupted *gradients* — the two destroyed heads, relearning clean labels through random weights, push the shared feature layer through a transient that the healthy heads' judgments ride on, and five rounds do not outlast it. The single-head case survived because one head's transient is a perturbation; two heads' is a regime.

And the no-brake arm's failure mode has changed species. Doc 23's single-head NOBRAKE digested — garbage labels flowed through a gate that still opened, accuracy collapsed to 73.7%. The two-head NOBRAKE never opens the gate at all: the flag rule (disagreement with a half-random committee) flags everything, commits go 995 → 0 in one round and stay there, and the system ends as a statue of its round-5 self (d_own 9.53% ≈ FROZ2_KILL's raw 9.49% — the frozen-damage signature). Starvation is the seed's derail with the sign flipped: the gate's own conservatism, which at one damaged head lets corruption *in*, at two damaged heads keeps everything *out*. Neither is maintenance; but they are different failures, and which one you get is a function of damage multiplicity against the flag rule's tolerance — the brake-less system's failure mode has a phase boundary, and the corpus has now seen both sides of it.

The κ-state arm resolves the K6 fork as neither (a) nor (b). The brake-blinding path never opens: κ_sys dips only to 0.958 (its calibration target crashing to 0.935 at round 7 — the flag-rate homeostasis *measuring* the damage — before recovering), never approaches the 0.5 floor, the κ-repair never fires, and the brake fires the same single round as the κ-frozen arm. The inert-deformation path (b) is also wrong as stated, because the arm's recovery is directionally better: accuracy 94.4% against 89.9%, d_own 0.80% against 1.58%. But the permutation test refuses to certify the gap at N = 6 (p = 0.064, one seed carrying most of it), and the mechanism is unidentified — the arms' RNG streams diverge after round 6 (different flag sets draw different view perturbations), so KSTATE and TWOHEAD are not deterministic twins of each other. The honest entry: κ-state under two-head damage is *safe* (no blinding, no floor, no repair engagement) and *possibly helpful* (not separable at this N), and the pre-registered fork was missing the third outcome — the flag-rate homeostasis as a recovery-speed regulator whose dip is a measurement, not a mistake.

### 2.3 The saturated world: a closed form to five parts in ten thousand

With self-training disabled from round 6, the evaluator's quantile parks at q̄ = 0.9734 (pool draws alone wiggle it by ±0.003), and the flat twin's norm settles into the deadband's upper tension — τ_sys ≈ 0.947–0.949, ~5pp above the record, held by the same record/quantile negotiation doc 23 found at the twin's edge. Into this world the drift attack goes, and the closed form follows it:

- observed FLAT_DRIFT trajectory (rounds 6–10): 0.946, 0.940, 0.936, 0.934, **0.931**
- EMA fixed point: q̄ − d(1−η)/η = 0.9734 − 0.0467 = **0.9267**
- finite-round recursion (5 applications, start 0.954): **0.9313**
- observed final: **0.9308** — recursion error **−0.0005**
- repair channel: silent from round 7 on (one seed fired at round 6, when the drift first applied)
- displacement vs the flat twin: **−0.018** (the growing world's, doc 23: −0.006)
- band-break threshold: the attack reaches the lower band edge (τ < 0.85) only if **q̄ < 0.897**

So doc 23's conjecture — "the attack is paid out of confidence growth; it wins if the evaluator saturates" — is now a formula with a verdict attached. Saturation is real: freezing the quantile triples the drift's displacement (−0.006 → −0.018), because the calibration's upward funding stops. But saturation at *this regime's level* is still not a win: the attack's fixed point (0.927) sits 8pp inside the band, the repair channel never engages, and the norm the attacker "holds" is one the saturated system would nearly hold anyway (the flat twin rests at 0.949). The win condition is not saturation but *decay*: the evaluator's quantile must fall below 0.897 — 7.7pp below where it froze — before the drift can push the norm out of the band the record defends. The closed form converts doc 23's qualitative fear into an auditable threshold, and the threshold says the fear, at this regime, is unfunded: **the live-restoration channel fails not when the evaluator stops improving but when it starts rotting, and the rot must exceed three-quarters of the deadband before the attack smells it.** One more ruler note: the FLAT arms' d_own is exactly 0.00% by construction (the frozen state's battery is constant; τ_sys's motion is invisible to the fixed-τ verdict matrix), so the entire saturated-world drama — displacement, fixed point, threshold — plays out in gate structure only. The amendment candidate doc 23 filed (the fixed-τ ruler needs a gate-structure probe) now has its second sighting, in a world where the battery ruler is not merely insensitive but *definitionally* blind.

## Part 3. The reading — what the norm now is, and what the brake now isn't

Doc 23's taxonomy of restoration (record / live / factory) was built on one coordinate. It generalizes without effort — κ restores like τ, to the live course, under the same band arithmetic — and the generalization closes the document's own threat (i): the evaluative machinery that is system state now includes both thresholds the gate reads, and what remains outside is exactly the machinery's *form* (the rules, the parameters, the disagreement component of the flag rule), which is the designer's by the corpus's standing accounting. The two-coordinate norm is also the first concrete instance of something the S4 adjudication (doc 28) will need: a *claim-space* for occupancy that is enumerable — which coordinates, which restoration type, against which attack classes, within which bands — because each cell of the enumeration now has an arm, a twin, and a classification rule.

The two-head result is the battery's first structural negative, and it cuts deeper than a failed arm. The brake was designed (doc 23, R4) as the rule that "refuses to learn from a broken grader" — and it does refuse, at the label level, all the way to the design edge. What it cannot refuse is the broken grader's *participation in the shared substrate*, and at 2-of-4 damage that participation is the dominant term. The design lesson is precise: majority-consensus grading protects the label channel, not the trunk; past the multiplicity edge, the conservative failure (starve — train on nothing, freeze at the pre-damage state) is *better* than the repair commitment (keep training through the transient), and a brake that cannot stand down is not a brake but a commitment device. The next build's R4 has a number attached: the brake needs a damage-multiplicity cutoff somewhere between 1 and 2 of 4, past which it must release to starvation — the failure mode that, by refusing to act, keeps the most accuracy. That a system's *best* response to its own half-destruction is to stop learning is not a paradox of the design; it is the design's honest boundary, and the instrument's job was to find it rather than to pretend the brake scaled.

The saturated bound, finally, gives the drift attack its full accounting. In the growing world it costs −0.006 (doc 23); in the saturated world −0.018; in neither world does it reach the band, and the difference between "contained" and "wins" is now a closed-form inequality in the recorded parameters (q̄ against τ\* − ε + d(1−η)/η) that any future build can be audited against without running the attack. This is the instrument's preferred kind of result: the attack's win condition reduced to a formula, so that the next system's defenders can read their exposure off their own registration. It also completes, in the same stroke, the failure-mode map of the live-restoration channel: it survives kicks, drift, poison, saturation — and fails exactly when the evaluator decays, which is to say when there is nothing left to restore *with*. The live norm is self-maintaining in the precise sense that its attractor is its own current judgment; the sentence has an end, and the sentence is: *a norm that is nothing but its system's current judgment degrades exactly as fast, and no faster, than the judgment does.* Whether that is ownership or a well-instrumented thermostat is the question doc 28 is built to adjudicate, and this document's contribution to it is the measurement that the thermostat, at least, is now fully mapped: both coordinates, both failure modes, and the formula for its breaking point.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) N = 6 seeds per arm; the KSTATE-vs-TWOHEAD accuracy gap (p = 0.064) and the single-seed round-6 repair firing in FLAT_DRIFT are both inside the noise floor, and the doc reports them as such. (ii) The κ calibration's saturation floor (0.5) was never reached in any arm — the structural-damage regime where disagreement alone exceeds f\* lasted under one round in this chassis because the brake's clean-label retraining suppresses disagreement quickly; the floor's existence is by construction, its behavior is unmeasured. (iii) TWOHEAD's accuracy erosion is read at round 10 with the transient unfinished; "recovery incomplete within the window" and "recovery never" are not distinguished, and the honest claim is only the first. (iv) The FLAT arms freeze the evaluator by *disabling training*, which also freezes the battery — the saturated world here is one specific saturation (early, high); a decayed-evaluator arm (q̄ falling through 0.897) would test the bound's other side and was not run. (v) The two-head brake inversion rests on the trunk-pollution mechanism inferred from the accuracy trajectory, not from gradient-level instrumentation.

**Non-claims.** No occupancy or S4 pass claim — this is still the M3 mandate's design-experiment register. No claim that κ-state *helps* two-head recovery (not separable at N = 6). No claim that the brake is net-harmful — at one damaged head it is worth 22pp of accuracy (doc 23); the claim is the edge, not the average. No claim that the closed form generalizes past the EMA/deadband/drift structure it was derived in; it is a formula about this controller, offered as the *shape* of the audit the next one deserves. No claim about κ's disagree component or the machinery parameters, which remain exactly as designer-held as before.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** K4 refuted at the boundary (acc 89.93% against the registered ≥ 0.90% — 0.07pp; both the held and the collapsed alternatives refuted, the truth between them). K6 refuted as a fork (neither blunting nor inertness; a third outcome — safe and possibly helpful — that the pre-registration did not contain). K8's criteria were re-specified after the smoke pass, twice, both disclosed in Part 1 (the level criterion and the closed form); the audited run then matched the corrected closed form to 0.0005. K1, K2, K3, K5 (as starvation, not digestion — the registered DERAIL2 rule fired on d_own while the registered mechanism description was wrong), K7, K9 all confirmed; the TWIN's bitwise reproduction of doc 23 was not pre-registered as a prediction but was checked in-script and passed.

## Part 5. Coda

The norm now has two coordinates, and both of them refuse to hold still: the threshold that preserves acceptance climbs as confidence grows, the threshold that preserves flagging climbs as disagreement shrinks, and each drags its record behind it at the same deadband's edge. Give the system four healthy heads and it negotiates; take one away and the brake saves the negotiation at a price the battery can barely see; take two away and the honest move is to stop — the system that keeps talking through half a ruined committee talks itself down a point and a half a round. Freeze the world and the norm parks, and the drift that could not move it while it grew moves it three times as much while it sits — and still not enough, because the record's band is wider than the drift's reach and the evaluator would have to rot before the attacker could win. Every one of these sentences is now a number with a twin, and the whole set fits in a registration. What the corpus does not yet have is the sentence about who owns the negotiation — but it has, for the first time, the complete paperwork for filing the question: both coordinates, all three restorers, both failure modes, and the formula the answer would have to break.
