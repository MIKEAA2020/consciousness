## Part 3. The reading — what the fourth cell taught

Three lessons. **The factorial is complete, and the composition is the burning variable.** Two doses, two compositions, four worlds, one seed census, every world audited bitwise to the family's chain: at ~0.125 realized the mixed world burns six and the uniform zero; at ~0.25 the mixed world burns twelve and the uniform one. The composition's marginal contribution is +6 burns at the low dose and +11 at the high; the dose's marginal contribution inside the uniform world is +1 (606) and inside the mixed world +6. The dose does not burn the census — it amplifies whatever composition carries it, and the composition that carries it furthest is the mix. The capacity's own twelve, read against its twin, are eleven parts interaction and one part the registration's top: neither arm alone, at any realized level this corpus has run, produces the mid-band burns. Which is the amendment this battery owes doc 78, and executes: Part 3 of that document attributed the mid-registration burns to "the bulk churn" on the strength of two eliminations; the twin's measurement refutes the attribution at the item level — the bulk arm alone, fed past the capacity's own realized level, spares every mid-band seed — and replaces it with the interaction's name. The two roads were not two poisons after all; they were one poison's two arms, acting together, on a registration whose ordering the gate reads and the bulk presses.

**The burn is an ordered set, and the composition sets its depth.** The nesting {606} ⊂ the mirror's six ⊂ the capacity's twelve is the battery's cleanest structure, and its reading generalizes the mirror's own: the b\* ordering is the world's fragility axis, the same axis in every world (the registrations are prefix quantities — the design's gift, again), and what the attack composition controls is HOW FAR DOWN the ordering the burn reaches. The uniform world at the full dose reaches the top one. The gate-heavy mix at half dose reaches the top six — exactly, a threshold. The bulk-heavy mix at full dose reaches the top six plus six more from the middle band, and the middle band's burns are graded, not thresholded (the capacity's continuum, doc 73's reading, standing). The letter's two grammars — threshold at the mirror, continuum at the capacity — were never two mechanisms; they are two depths of the same ordering, and the depth is the mix's to set.

**The defense can burn, and the corpus now has the receipt.** The twin's single burn is not the poison's direct act — the undefended world at 0.25 fills +0.151 and burns nobody — but the repair traffic's: −0.197 of differential on the one seed whose registration was highest to start. The mirror's burns were arithmetic without defense (neither fill nor churn, the starting line's own heights under the floor); the twin's is defense without arithmetic (the fill high, the churn higher). A registrar pricing this defense's side-effects now holds both limits: at the gate-heavy mix the defense is a spectator to the burn; at the uniform full dose the defense IS the burn, and the seed it takes is the world's most fragile one. The budget sheet's recurring irony — the defense worth the most exactly where it costs the letter — acquires its census-side counterpart: the defense works hardest exactly where its work is the only danger left.
