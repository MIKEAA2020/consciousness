## Part 0. The obligation, and what "independently" costs

The roadmap's Phase 3 list is quoted in the status paragraph, and its discipline is worth restating because it did work in doc 32 and does work here. The bridging file's original sin — as the GPT audit located it — was the habit of asserting that Paper 2's structures "emerge" from the Chu construction, substituting vocabulary for construction; doc 32's repair was to build the information model with the comparison functors forbidden, and the repair paid: the model assembled itself out of classical pieces, and the complexity theorem arrived as a pair with a boundary the roadmap never drew. The same repair, run on the quantum side, has a sharper target, because the bridging file's quantum claims are its boldest: that the opfibration of quantum channels over a causal poset is "the object/projection side of the Chu construction," and that Paper 3's no-right-adjoint theorem "is exactly the statement that the right adjoint of the duality does not preserve the trace-preserving subcategory." The audit's response to the second claim is Phase 3's specification in miniature: to prove any such identification one needs the exact functor whose right adjoint fails, the exact subcategory, a proof that closure under duality is equivalent to the adjoint's existence, and a derivation of the affine-dimensional obstruction from the closure failure — "without those steps, 'exactly' must be replaced by 'we conjecture may correspond to.'" This document supplies the first two items (the functor is the carriage T_E; the subcategory is the fibers themselves, every morphism trace-preserving by fiat, with the intercept Lemma 41 pricing exactly what trace preservation costs) and leaves the equivalence and the derivation to Phases 4–6, where the roadmap puts them. The discipline costs what it always costs: the model below cannot borrow so much as a symbol from the bridge, and where the physics literature's results are used (the Choi correspondence, the diamond norm's subadditivity, the Grothendieck construction) they are used as *classical* results with their provenance disclosed, not as bridge material.

## Part 1. The base poset, the environments, and the fiber

**Definition 38 (causal poset; arrow category) [Derived here; the object is the papers'].** A *causal poset* is a locally finite poset (P, ≼): for all p ≼ q the interval [p, q] = {r : p ≼ r ≼ q} is finite. The *arrow category* Arr(P) has objects the arrows p ≼ q and morphisms (p, q) → (q, r) the composable pairs. The running example throughout is (ℕ, ≤) — the corpus's own round structure, and time; the intended physics is a finite causal set with its causal order. Local finiteness is what makes "the environment accumulated over an interval" a finite-dimensional object, and it is assumed rather than derived, as an operational finiteness postulate.

**Definition 39 (interval environment assignment) [Derived here; instantiates Paper 1's transport data].** An *environment assignment* is a monoidal functor E from (Arr(P), composition) to (Alg_fd, ⊗, ℂ): each arrow p ≼ q carries a finite-dimensional matrix algebra E_{pq} = M_{k_{pq}}, with E_{pp} = ℂ and E_{pr} = E_{pq} ⊗ E_{qr} *strictly* (k_{pr} = k_{pq}k_{qr}). Concretely: any strictly monotone capacity function w: P → ℕ with p ≺ q ⟹ w(p) | w(q) generates one, by k_{pq} = w(q)/w(p); the trivial assignment (all k = 1) is permitted and is the no-environment limit. The reading: transporting a system from p to q along the causal order accrues an environment of dimension k_{pq}, multiplicatively — the paper-1 growth, now a strict monoidality rather than a metaphor.

**Definition 40 (the fiber; the enrichment) [Established, assembled; the enrichment observation is classical].** The *fiber category* Q has objects the finite-dimensional matrix algebras M_d, d ∈ ℕ (written A = M_{d_A}); hom Q(A, B) is the set of trace-preserving completely positive maps Φ: A → B; composition is the usual channel composition; the identity on A is the trivial channel. Each hom is a compact convex set — under the Choi correspondence, the spectrahedron {J ∈ Herm(B ⊗ A) : J ⪰ 0, Tr_B J = I_A} — and composition is bilinear in its two arguments, so **Q is enriched over Con**, the monoidal category of convex sets and affine maps (the enrichment's monoidal structure is the pointwise one on function spaces; only bilinearity of composition and affinity of the tensor-action are used below, so the reader may take "Con-enriched" to mean exactly: homs are convex sets, composition and the functor actions are affine in each variable).

**Lemma 41 (the hom intercept) [Derived here; the count is standard, the reading is the papers'].** For A = M_a, B = M_b with a, b ≥ 1: the affine hull of Q(A, B) has dimension exactly **a²(b² − 1)**. *Proof.* The Choi correspondence is an affine isomorphism Q(A, B) ≅ {J ∈ Herm(B ⊗ A) : J ⪰ 0, Tr_B J = I_a}. The real vector space Herm(B ⊗ A) has dimension a²b²; the linear map Tr_B: Herm(B ⊗ A) → Herm(A) is surjective (for any Hermitian H, the matrix J = I_b ⊗ H/b has Tr_B J = H), so the affine constraint Tr_B J = I_a is a slice of codimension exactly dim Herm(A) = a². The slice is nonempty (J = I_b ⊗ I_a / b has Tr_B J = I_a) and contains a strictly positive point, so the feasible spectrahedron is full-dimensional in the slice: its affine hull is the slice. Hence dim aff Q(A, B) = a²b² − a² = a²(b² − 1). ∎ The **trace-preservation intercept** is the −1: per unit of input algebra (there are a² of them), the output pays one dimension of bookkeeping that no scaling of the output reproduces — b² becomes (b² − 1), never (b² − c) for c ≠ 1. Every obstruction in Part 4 is this count wearing different clothes.

## Part 2. Transport, the total category, and the exact opfibration

**Definition 42 (transport functors) [Derived here].** For p ≼ q with environment E = E_{pq}, the *carriage functor* T_{pq}: Q → Q acts by A ↦ A ⊗ E and Φ ↦ Φ ⊗ id_E. That Φ ⊗ id_E is trace-preserving: Tr_{B⊗E}[(Φ ⊗ id)(ρ)] = Tr_B Φ(Tr_E ρ) = Tr_A(Tr_E ρ) = Tr ρ, using that partial trace commutes with the identity factor and that Φ preserves trace. T_{pq} is a Con-functor (affine on homs), and the strict monoidality of E makes T_{qr} ∘ T_{pq} = T_{pr} exactly — so p ↦ Q, (p ≼ q) ↦ T_{pq} is an *indexed category* on P in the roadmap's sense, a genuine functor P → ConCat.

**Definition 43 (the total category) [Derived here; the construction is the standard Grothendieck construction for a covariant indexed category — non-novelty disclosed; the instantiation is the papers' structure made exact].** The *total category* 𝓔 has objects the pairs (p, A); a morphism (p, A) → (q, B) is a channel Φ ∈ Q(A ⊗ E_{pq}, B) if p ≼ q (and none otherwise); the identity on (p, A) is id_A ∈ Q(A ⊗ E_{pp}, A) = Q(A, A); the composite of Φ: (p, A) → (q, B) and Ψ: (q, B) → (r, C) is Ψ ∘ (Φ ⊗ id_{E_{qr}}) ∈ Q(A ⊗ E_{pq} ⊗ E_{qr}, C) = Q(A ⊗ E_{pr}, C), with associativity inherited from Q's and the strict monoidality. The reading is the physically loaded one and it is exact: **a channel from a system at p to a system at q is a map that may consume everything the interval accumulated.** The projection π: 𝓔 → P, (p, A) ↦ p, is a functor.

**Proposition 44 (the opfibration; and the exact failure of the fibration) [Derived here].** (i) π is a Con-enriched opfibration: the cocartesian lift of p ≼ q at (p, A) is the identity channel id: A ⊗ E_{pq} → A ⊗ E_{pq}, typed (p, A) → (q, A ⊗ E_{pq}); the factorization condition is satisfied uniquely because a morphism (p, A) → (r, B) *is* already a morphism (q, A ⊗ E_{pq}) → (r, B) under the interval algebra, and the only factorization through the identity is the trivial one. Forward transport is free — the identity channel costs nothing, which is why the opfibration exists. (ii) π is not a fibration as soon as some k_{pq} ≥ 2: a cartesian lift of p ≼ q at (q, B) would be a channel ℓ: (p, A) → (q, B) such that every h: (r, C) → (q, B) with r ≼ p factors uniquely as ℓ ∘ g with g: (r, C) → (p, A); taking the candidate A = B and ℓ = the discard Tr_{E_{pq}}, the factorization demands h = Tr_{E_{pq}} ∘ (g ⊗ id_{E_{pq}}) for some g — i.e., that h not correlate the C ⊗ E_{rp} factor with the E_{pq} factor before discarding. Channels that do correlate them exist as soon as k ≥ 2 (compose any entangling unitary on (C ⊗ E_{rp}) ⊗ E_{pq} with any channel to B), and they form a set of positive — indeed full — affine dimension inside Q(C ⊗ E_{rq}, B), while the factorable ones form the affine image of the lower-dimensional space Q(C ⊗ E_{rp}, A). So the cartesian lifts fail, and they fail on a full-dimensional set: **the total category is an opfibration over the causal poset and not a bifibration, and the non-liftable morphisms are exactly the ones the physics calls irreversible — channels that have interacted with their interval's environment.** This is Paper 1's opfibration and Paper 3's obstruction, separated cleanly: the forward structure exists for free, the backward structure fails for an arithmetic reason, and Part 4 prices it.

**The discard, and its section [Derived here].** The partial traces assemble into a Con-natural transformation δ: T_{pq} ⇒ Id_Q, δ_A = Tr_E: A ⊗ E → A (naturality is the commutation Tr_E ∘ (Φ ⊗ id) = Φ ∘ Tr_E, the same computation as Definition 42). And δ has a Con-natural *section* s: Id ⇒ T_{pq} given by any fixed state σ₀ of E: s_A(ρ) = ρ ⊗ σ₀ (naturality: (Φ ⊗ id)(ρ ⊗ σ₀) = Φ(ρ) ⊗ σ₀, automatic because Φ ⊗ id acts factor-wise). So at the level of states, recovery is free and natural: one can always prepare a fresh environment. **The irreversibility is not about states, and the document will not pretend otherwise** — it lives in the fibration failure and in the adjunction, both of which are about channels.

## Part 3. Instruments, and the two branching rates

**Definition 45 (instruments) [Established, assembled; the sum rule is Davies–Lewis].** An *instrument* with outcome set I on (A, B) is a finite family {Φ_i}_{i ∈ I} of completely positive trace-non-increasing maps Φ_i: A → B with Σ_{i∈I} Φ_i trace-preserving (the *sum rule*). Instruments compose sequentially with outcome product: ({Φ_i}, {Ψ_j}) ↦ {Ψ_j ∘ Φ_i}_{(i,j) ∈ I × J}; the outcome set of the composite is I × J. Every instrument dilates to a single channel with a *recorder*: Φ̂ = Σ_i Φ_i ⊗ |i⟩⟨i| ∈ Q(A, B ⊗ ℂ^{|I|}), the classical recorder; replacing ℂ^{|I|} by a matrix algebra M_e allows superposed records.

**Proposition 46 (the branching-rate split) [Derived here].** (i) The space of I-outcome instruments on (A, B) has affine dimension |I| · a²b² − a² = a²(|I|b² − 1): |I| independent Choi matrices, one sum-rule slice of codimension a². (ii) The classical recorder's dilation Φ̂ lives in Q(A, B ⊗ ℂ^{|I|}); since dim(B ⊗ ℂ^{|I|}) = b|I|, the full channel space there has affine dimension a²(b²|I|² − 1), and the dilations form the block-diagonal slice of dimension a²(|I|b² − 1) inside it. (iii) A quantum recorder M_e sits in a²(b²e² − 1). Setting e = |I| (equal recording capacity): the classical record costs |I|b² − 1, the quantum record costs |I|²b² − 1, and the ratio grows like |I|. *Reading:* **classical branching is linear in outcomes; quantum branching is quadratic in dimension.** The intercept −1 is common to both — it is the same trace-preservation budget — and the *scale* is what the recorder's physics multiplies. This is the instrument-side member of the intercept family, and it is the precise home of the bridging file's observation that Abramsky's three-valued reductions preserve symmetry groupoids: symmetries are records of the classical kind (outcomes, |I|-linear); amplitudes are records of the quantum kind (dimension-squared), and no three-valued reduction touches the square.

## Part 4. The adjunction under investigation, and the exact obstruction

The adjunction the roadmap names is the *recovery adjunction*: whether the carriage functor T_E: Q → Q (Definition 42, for a nontrivial interval environment E with d_E ≥ 2) has a right adjoint G — the functorial compression, the would-be right inverse of environment-carriage, the categorical form of "recover the channel from the world it dissipated into." The vacuum section of Part 2 shows the *object* level recovers; the fibration failure shows the *morphisms of the total category* do not; the adjunction question is the fiber-level remainder, and it has an exact answer.

**Theorem F (the intercept; no enriched right adjoint to carriage) [Derived here].** Fix E with d_E ≥ 2. Suppose G: Q → Q is a Con-enriched right adjoint to T_E. Then for all A, B there are affine bijections Q(T_E A, B) ≅ Q(A, G B), natural in B. Taking affine-hull dimensions (Lemma 41) on both sides with A = ℂ: d_E²(d_B² − 1) = d_{GB}² − 1, i.e. **d_{GB}² = d_E² d_B² − (d_E² − 1)** for every B. Hence: (i) if the diophantine equation has no solution at some B, no such G exists — and it has no solution at most (d_E, d_B): for d_E = 2 the equation reads d_G² = 4d_B² − 3, and 4d_B² − 3 lies strictly between (2d_B − 1)² = 4d_B² − 4d_B + 1 and (2d_B)² for d_B ≥ 2 (since 4d_B − 1 > 3), so it is not a square; the general case factors as (m − d_G)(m + d_G) = d_E² − 1 with m = d_E d_B, and any solution with d_B ≥ 2 requires d_E d_B + d_G ≤ d_E² − 1, i.e. d_B ≤ d_E − 1 together with an exact factorization of d_E² − 1 into two factors of equal parity summing to 2d_E d_B. (ii) The exceptional family is thin but nonempty: (d_E, d_B, d_G) = (4, 2, 7) and (6, 3, 17) are exact solutions (4²·2² − 4² + 1 = 49 = 7²; 6²·3² − 6² + 1 = 289 = 17²; equivalently (8−7)(8+7) = 15 = 4²−1 and (18−17)(18+17) = 35 = 6²−1), and at these dimensions the dimension count does not decide the adjunction. (iii) At the exceptional dimensions the obstruction must be refined, and the refinement is *open*: the conjecture is that the Con-naturality in B and the coherence with the discard δ (Part 2) kill the exceptions — a putative G at (4, 2, 7) must moreover be a Con-functor, compatible with composition, and mate to δ under the adjunction, and no assignment of object dimensions satisfying the count is known to survive those demands. The obligation is filed, not discharged. ∎ *(Proof of (i)–(ii) is the arithmetic above; the theorem's honest scope is: the adjunction is killed outside the exceptional family, and the exceptional family is exactly characterized.)*

**Corollary F.1 (the set-level honesty) [Derived here].** As bare sets, |Q(A, B)| = |ℝ| for all a, b ≥ 1, so no cardinality obstruction to any adjunction exists anywhere in the model: **the intercept is genuinely a property of the convex-linear structure** — the enrichment is not decoration but the carrier of the obstruction, and any would-be proof of the papers' identification that passes through bare Set must fail. (This is the answer to the audit's demand that the affine-dimensional obstruction be *derived*, not assumed: Lemma 41 derives it, Theorem F spends it, and the corollary fences off the empty route.)

**Theorem G (what recovery is, and where it fails) [Derived here].** (i) The discard δ: T_E ⇒ Id has a Con-natural section (the vacuum embedding, Part 2): state-level recovery is free. (ii) The fibration failure of Proposition 44(ii) is the channel-level statement: the non-factorable channels — those that correlate a system with its interval environment before the discard — form a full-dimensional subset of the relevant hom, so the failure is generic, not exotic. (iii) The fiber-level remainder is exactly Theorem F: the recovery adjunction would be the functorial form of factorization, and the intercept prices it out of existence outside the exceptional family. The three together are the document's replacement for the bridging file's sentence "the failure of the right adjoint is what gives the arrow of time its structural bite": the bite, made precise, is that *forward carriage is free, backward factorization is generic-impossible, and the adjunction that would mediate between them is arithmetically excluded* — at the state level nothing is lost, which is why the physics had to be a channel-level statement in the first place.

## Part 5. The currency question, inherited and answered

Doc 32 closed by asking, of the quantum sibling it had just built the classical sibling of: *which quantale do the fibers carry?* The model answers in three steps.

**Proposition 47 (the additive currency is native) [Established, assembled].** The diamond norm ‖·‖_◇ makes Q a category enriched over ([0, ∞], +): ‖Ψ ∘ Φ‖_◇ ≤ ‖Ψ‖_◇ + ‖Φ‖_◇, and ‖id‖_◇ = 0. The fibers therefore carry the additive currency natively — per-use errors accumulate by addition, exactly as doc 32's Definition 36 specified for the information side, and the quantum and classical halves of the corpus now share a composition law for costs.

**Proposition 48 (the MV layer, and the invariance of the intercept) [Derived here; Interpretation labeled].** Layer the budget currency on the same homs: for a trajectory of uses with per-use diamond errors e_t ∈ [0, K], define the MV accumulation x_t = max(x_{t−1} + e_t − K, 0) — doc 32's Lindley recursion, the mean-payoff budget. The zero level of this accumulation — all x_T = 0, free on every suffix window — coincides with the exact world e_t = 0 for all t, which is the additive currency's zero level. The intercept theorem (F) is a statement about the affine hulls of the homs, i.e. about the exact world's geometry; it is therefore *currency-invariant*: both quantales carry the same obstruction at the zero level, and they part company only where doc 32's Theorem E located the parting — the complexity of the finite-quotient problem, which is a positive-level statement. **The answer to the inherited question: the fibers can carry either currency; the choice is free at the intercept and binding only at the complexity boundary; and the two theorems — E here, F there — are orthogonal axes of the same skeleton.** [The first sentence of that answer is theorem-grade; the orthogonality reading is an interpretation, and is labeled as one.]

The Bridge Constraint (doc 24, held since): every theorem above is compatible with both the primitivist reading (the intercept is what the physics *is*) and the bookkeeping reading (the intercept is what the *formalism* charges for bookkeeping, and any sufficient statistics-free formalism would charge it too). Phase 3 was not designed to break the constraint and does not.

## Part 6. What Phase 3 buys, and what it does not

**Bought, at theorem grade.** The roadmap's list is discharged: base poset (Definition 38), environment assignment (39), indexed category and fibers (40, 42), transport maps (42), total category (43), the opfibration with its exact non-bifibration (44), the channel class (the CPTP homs, with their affine dimension priced in Lemma 41), instruments with the branching-rate split (45, 46), the adjunction under investigation with its exact obstruction (Theorem F, Corollary F.1, Theorem G), and the currency inheritance (47, 48). Both endpoints of Phase 4's comparison now exist and are well-typed: F_phys: 𝓔 → ChuCost can for the first time be written against a target whose enrichment (docs 24/27) and whose classical sibling (doc 32) are themselves theorem-grade. The papers' central metaphor — forward free, backward priced — is now three exact statements (Proposition 44, Theorem F, Theorem G) whose common constant is the trace-preservation intercept.

**Not bought, and said so.** The identification with the Chu duality's treatment of trace-preserving subcategories: conjecture-shaped, Phase 4–6, with the audit's four proof obligations unchanged (the exact Chu functor; the subcategory's closure equivalence; the derivation of the affine obstruction from closure failure; the *-autonomy connection). The exceptional-family refinement of Theorem F: open, with the conjecture stated. The infinite-dimensional and algebraic-QFT settings: untouched — the model is the finite-dimensional operational skeleton, local finiteness is a postulate, and any field-theoretic claim would need an environment assignment that grows along lightlike structure, which is exactly the kind of extension the roadmap's later phases would order, not assume. And nothing about the corpus's empirical half is claimed as *derived* here: the connections drawn below are interpretations, labeled as such, and the formal documents' discipline — no empirical warrant borrowed for a theorem, no theorem offered as an empirical verdict — holds on both sides of the corpus.

## Part 8. Coda

Doc 32 ended by noting that the roadmap's discipline had been the whole gift: freed of the bridge, the information model assembled itself out of a congruence, a triangle, and a queue. The quantum model, freed the same way, assembles itself out of a slice, a square, and a count: the trace-preservation slice of the positive cone (Lemma 41), the recorder's square (Proposition 46), and the diophantine count that no tensoring of the output can counterfeit the one dimension the trace pays (Theorem F). The corpus's empirical half has spent eight documents learning that defenses are budgets and attacks are arrivals; the formal half now says, in finite dimensions, the thing that budget always was: an intercept, invariant under every scaling the environment can buy. The two halves do not touch in this document, and that is the discipline too — but the corpus is allowed to notice, as an interpretation and nothing more, that the norm's records (docs 23–26) are intercepts of exactly this kind: constants the system pays once and cannot dilute, while everything around them compounds. Phase 4 will be ordered or it will not; if it is, the comparison functors meet, for the first time, a quantum model that can be wrong in numbered places — an exceptional family here, a refinement there — which is the only kind of model worth bridging to.
