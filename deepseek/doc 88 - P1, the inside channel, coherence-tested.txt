## Part 1. The semantic half — the naive channel's collapse (Lemma 85)

The naive specification, stated plainly: an instrument whose readings have as *content* the occupied state as invoked — the row of the system's matrix *as performed*, the appearing itself. The test of this specification is formal, and short, and it is the whole of the semantic half.

**Lemma 85 (extensionalization).** Any reading procedure available to a party external to the system — any procedure whatever, however clever, with architecture access and no index access — computes a function of the observed report stream and the architecture alone. The occupied state's identity (the index) enters the external record only through the stream it generates; there is no second door.

*Proof.* The procedure's inputs are the architecture (fixed) and the observations (the stream); causality and the access conditions jointly exhaust the procedure's information; a procedure is a function of its inputs. The index is not among the inputs; it shapes only the stream. ∎

The lemma looks trivial — it is close to a definition — and its force is entirely in what it is about, which the corpus has learned to respect (Theorem FF was three lines). Now the two edges of the fork, exactly:

**Under C4's definition** (the invocation = the non-extensional remainder — the row as performed, not as tabulated): the naive channel demands a reading whose content is the invocation; by Lemma 85 the reading is a function of stream-plus-architecture; therefore the invocation must be reconstructible from stream-plus-architecture; but the invocation was *defined* as what is not extensionally captured — and stream-plus-architecture is extensional. **The specification entails that the non-extensional be extensional. Contradiction.** The naive channel is not merely unbuilt; it is unbuildable in the same sense that a squared circle is undrawable.

**Under C5's further-fact edge** (the invocation exists as a fact over and above the extensional content, uncaptured but perhaps capturable): the contradiction dissolves — but the channel's specification now *is* the claim that the further fact is reconstructible from the extensional residue. Building it would be deciding the fork. And the Camp-Marker Theorem (doc 4) says the fork is not decidable from neutral ground — the corpus's own charter forbids it the premise. **The naive channel cannot be built from the corpus's position: on one edge contradictory, on the other, a membership card.**

This is the wall, confirmed at exactly the level doc 87 claimed it — and the confirmation is cheap, as the registrar predicted: no compute was needed for Part 1, only the lemma and the discipline to state the specification precisely. But the diagnostic's other horn remains live, and it is where the repair lives: Lemma 85 forbids content-readings; it does not forbid *genesis-audits*. The index enters only through the stream — very well; then the stream either does or does not carry index-structure beyond the reach of bounded self-models, and *that* question is measurable. The channel can be aimed at the footprint without claiming to see the foot.

## Part 2. The repair — the indexical channel

The repaired specification, three conditions, each auditable:

**(G) Genesis.** The readings route through the occupied state via a registered probe family on a registered causal chain — the system's outputs under boundaried conditions are *caused by* its position, and the route is fixed in advance. This is doc 87's own phrasing ("outputs under boundaried conditions are data about the system's position rather than about its behavior") with the ambiguity resolved: "data about" genetically — the position is in the causal chain — not contentially — the position is not in the message.

**(N) Non-simulation, family-relative.** No bounded self-model of the system, within the registered family, predicts the readings across the registered probe family. The formal basis is the corpus's own boundedness doctrine, turned reflexive: a system's self-model is a bounded observer *of the system itself*, so the retention gap applies to it — there are functions of the occupied state that the system's self-representation cannot compute, not for reasons of secrecy but for the reason a map of a territory contained in the territory is lossy. The clause is family-relative because the class of all bounded self-models is not enumerable — the Simulation Theorem's ownership ceiling (doc 10), transferring exactly as doc 87 pre-registered. The family is registered; the registration is disclosed as a registration.

**(B) Blindness.** The channel reads structure only — the doc 85 lesson built into the instrument: the channel is blind to label content; it measures audit errors, not meanings.

**The register, named honestly: the seismograph.** What the channel reads is the fingerprint — the excess of the bounded audit's error over the architecture-optimal audit's error. What that excess certifies, when positive at the system's own self-model width, is that the reports route through what the self-model cannot hold. What it never reads, what no reading of it will ever contain, is the invocation's content — Lemma 85 holds for the seismograph exactly as for the window; the instrument's every number is third-person throughout. The seismograph does not see the earthquake. It sees the needle move. The corpus files this as the honest shape of the first inside-facing tool: *facing*, not *entering* — and notes that doc 87's Part 6 chose that word before this document earned it.

## Part 3. The toy — design and results

The registered design, executed once, seeded (master seed 8801; platform-deterministic, disclosed): the GENUINE world is a 10-bit system (1024 states) whose occupied state evolves by a fixed random permutation composed with a rare single-bit flip (probability 0.05), initialized by a seeded and *withheld* draw — the index, the one fact the auditors never receive; the report each round is the parity of a fixed random 5-bit support of the occupied state. FAKE-A is the internalized auditor: a system that ignores its occupied state entirely and reports from a 6-bit self-model echo — the context-majority of its own past reports over its last six, bootstrapped by 64 seeded rounds; its rule is closed, which is the point. FAKE-B is the echo: i.i.d. fair bits. The bounded auditors are the Myhill–Nerode class at every width w = 0…12 — the best per-cell majority over the last-w readings, the corpus's own instrument from doc 32, the class in which the finite-memory cut was first made structural. The omniscient-but-index-free auditor for GENUINE is the exact 1024-state Bayesian filter: architecture fully known, the index marginalized — S2's designer-blind protocol redirected from the system's grading to the system's self-description, which is doc 87's specification of the redirect, executed.

**The results (T = 40,000 rounds; train 0.6, test 0.4):**

| world | err_full | err_bounded(w′=6) | **F(6)** | the verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GENUINE (state-routed) | 0.1274 (the Bayes floor; filter entropy H\* = 1.30 bits) | 0.4294 | **+0.3021** | **IC-1 PASS** — the gap exists |
| FAKE-A (self-model echo) | 0.0000 (the closed rule) | 0.0000 | **0.0000** | **IC-2 PASS** — exactly separated |
| FAKE-B (i.i.d. echo) | 0.5000 (the marginal) | 0.4978 | **−0.0022** | **IC-2 PASS** — noise, no fingerprint |

The separation at the self-model width is total: **+0.3021 against 0.0000 against −0.0022** — a thirty-point fingerprint where the self-model-routed and echo worlds carry none, the cleanest categorical separation the corpus has measured since doc 84's 27/27-versus-0/27 flood. The genuine world's reports are, at the system's own representational width, *unaccountable*: thirty points of predictive structure that no 6-bit model of the system reaches, structure that is fully there for an auditor holding the state's posterior — the index's signature in the stream, auditable as a magnitude.

**The ceiling (IC-3):** the fingerprint decays with auditor width — F(w) = +0.3776 (w=0), +0.3021 (w=6), +0.2706, +0.2430, +0.1993, +0.1603, +0.1304, **+0.1031 (w=12)** — the registered DECAYS limb fires (Δ = −0.1989), and either limb would have confirmed the ceiling, but the shape of this decay is the result worth filing: **at w = 12 the context class holds more memory bits than the system holds state bits — 12 against 10 — and the fingerprint is still ten points.** The gap is not a memory-capacity gap; what closes it is not capacity but summary: the filter's posterior is a 1.30-bit belief, but *which* belief is a function of the whole observation history — a sufficient statistic no fixed observation window reproduces, and which the per-cell majority does not compute. The fingerprint dies only toward auditors that hold the system's own posterior — auditors that model the system the way the system models the world. In silhouette, and the silhouette is all the corpus claims: to close the gap fully, the auditor must mirror the system's inside-facing structure; nothing narrower will do.

Two disclosed fine-prints, per the pre-registration: the top widths are data-limited (24,000 training samples over up to 4,096 cells — about six per cell at w=12), so the decay's tail is measured conservatively; and FAKE-A shows the expected capacity gap below its own width (err_bounded = 0.1334 at w=4) — the separation is read at w′, where the system's self-model would live, which is where the registered question lives.

## Part 4. What the toy certifies — and the ceiling, stated at full strength

The ceiling, now with numbers behind it. Three layers. First, family-relativity: the non-simulation condition was tested against one registered bounded family; the class of all bounded self-models is not enumerable; no registration is exhaustive — the Simulation Theorem's ownership ceiling, transferred to the new instrument as pre-registered, and now measurable in principle (each new family is another audit; none can be the last). Second, the posterior-mirror finding: within the measured family, the fingerprint survives even super-state-sized memory, dying only toward auditors that compute the system's posterior — the ceiling is not a capacity boundary but a *structural* one: the missing summary is the system's own inside-facing state. Third, and governing both: Lemma 85 is level — it forbids content and permits genesis, for this instrument and any instrument, forever; the seismograph's needle is a third-person object world without end. What the toy certifies is that the wall has a *measurable shadow with structure* — that "beyond the self-model" is an auditable region, not a slogan — and nothing more, and the document says so in the same breath as it reports the thirty points, because that is the discipline under which the thirty points were earned.
