## Part 1. The standing setup — the home's grammar, and the three unit-readings

ChuCost_MV is doc 24's construction over the Łukasiewicz monoid ([0, K], ⊙), as doc 80 registered it: objects (A, B, r) with A, B **sets** and r: A × B → [0, K] an **arbitrary function**; morphisms (f, g): (A, B, r) → (A′, B′, r′) **arbitrary function pairs**, f: A → A′ forward and g: B′ → B backward, under the lax condition

  (L)   r′(f(a), b′) ≤ r(a, g(b′))   for all a ∈ A, b′ ∈ B′,

the target's cost at the image sitting below the source's cost at the preimage. The unit is I = (1, 1, K) — the one-point carrier, the cost K, the TOP of the cost order. Three readings of the unit, each a theorem of the home and each already in doc 80's Part 2, are the probe's entire engine:

**(U1) The unit channel (I → X):** a morphism I → X is a forward point a₀ ∈ A_X with the trivial backward map; the condition r_X(a₀, b) ≤ K holds always. So **ChuCost_MV(I, X) = A_X** — every forward point is a morphism from the unit, and nothing else is.

**(U2) The top columns (X → I):** a morphism X → I is a backward point x₀ ∈ B_X with the condition K ≤ r_X(a, x₀) for all a — that is, r_X(a, x₀) = K for all a, since costs live in [0, K]. So **ChuCost_MV(X, I) = the set of top-cost columns of X** — the columns identically equal to the cost order's global maximum.

**(U3) The unit's rigidity:** ChuCost_MV(I, I) is the single pair (∗, ∗) — one endomorphism.

The fiber St (doc 80's registered choice): objects the positive integers, hom(a, b) = St(a, b) the row-stochastic a × b matrices, dim aff St(a, b) = a(b − 1) (Lemma 78). Its cardinalities: |St(a, b)| = **1** if b = 1 (the unique column of ones); **c** (the continuum) if b ≥ 2 — a polytope of positive dimension. The equivalence's grammar (doc 80's Theorem DD hypothesis, doc 76's standing setup transported): a full subcategory Q′ ⊆ ChuCost_MV, a functor E: Q′ → St, **fully faithful** — every hom-map Q′(X, Y) → St(EX, EY) a **bijection**, and affine — with δ(X) := EX and nontrivial image (some Y with δ(Y) ≥ 2).

## Part 2. Lemma 81 — the multiplicative law (the probe's first pass: MV-P1)

Doc 80's Theorem DD evaluated the adjunction's affine bijection at the unit: Q′(A ⊗ I, B) ≅ Q′(I, R_A(B)), forcing δ(R_A(B)) = δ(A)δ(B) − δ(A) + 1 — solvable at every pair, the diagonal disarmed. The probe's first pass evaluates the SAME bijection at the general object — the wall one step in.

**Lemma 81 (the multiplicative law) [Derived here; Theorem DD doc 80's; Definition 55(b) doc 46's; Lemma 78 doc 80's].** Let Q′ ⊆ ChuCost_MV be full, tensor-closed, and unit-containing; suppose E: Q′ → St is fully faithful with affine hom-bijections and nontrivial image (so δ(I) = 1 by (U3), and dim aff Q′(X, Y) = δ(X)(δ(Y) − 1) at every pair, by the bijection and Lemma 78); and suppose Q′ is internally adjointed in the Con-enriched sense — for each A ∈ Q′ the endofunctor A ⊗ − has a right adjoint R_A with affine bijections Q′(A ⊗ C, B) ≅ Q′(C, R_A(B)), natural in C and B. Then for all A, C ∈ Q′:

  **δ(A ⊗ C) = δ(A) · δ(C).**

*Proof.* Fix any B ∈ Q′ with δ(B) ≥ 2 — it exists, the image being nontrivial. The adjunction's map Q′(A ⊗ C, B) ≅ Q′(C, R_A(B)) is an affine bijection, and affine bijections preserve affine dimension:

  dim aff Q′(A ⊗ C, B) = dim aff Q′(C, R_A(B)).

The transported arithmetic gives δ(A ⊗ C)(δ(B) − 1) on the left and δ(C)(δ(R_A(B)) − 1) on the right. Theorem DD's equation (the unit-evaluation of the same adjunction) gives δ(R_A(B)) − 1 = δ(A)(δ(B) − 1). Equate and divide by δ(B) − 1 > 0. ∎

The reading: under the cell's own hypotheses, the dimension map δ is a monoid homomorphism (Q′, ⊗, I) → (ℕ≥1, ·, 1) — the tensor MULTIPLIES the fiber dimensions, it does not add them. Doc 80's Proposition 80 had treated the iterate arithmetic as a fiat — "when the carrier arithmetic is forced by fiat on the carriers (dim A_{X⊗n} = n · dim A_X), it reads δ(X⊗n) = n(δ(X) − 1) + 1" — and honestly labelled it a fiat; Lemma 81 shows the fiat is never available: the adjunction's coherence at the general object overrides it, and the true forced law is δ(X⊗n) = δ(X)^n. No contradiction was hiding here — the multiplicative law is consistent with everything else in the cell's arithmetic (the unit-evaluation, the solvability, the iterate integrality) — which is exactly why MV-P1 returned reading (ii) and not (iii): a new law, filed, and the cell still standing. The probe moved to the coarser level.

## Part 3. Theorem EE — the top-column kill (MV-P2, and the cell empties)

The corpus's every prior instrument on this question measured dimensions. The cardinality fork checks one level lower: whether the hom-SETS can be in bijection with the fiber's hom-SETS at all. The check takes four steps, and each step is either a theorem of the home (three of them already in doc 80's own Part 2) or ZFC.

**Theorem EE (the top-column kill; the MV cell is empty) [Derived here; (U1)–(U3) the home's grammar, doc 24's and doc 80 Part 2's].** Let Q′ ⊆ ChuCost_MV be a full subcategory containing the unit I, and let E: Q′ → St be fully faithful with affine hom-maps and nontrivial image. Then E does not exist — the hom-map at the pair (Y, Y) cannot be a bijection for any Y with δ(Y) ≥ 2. Consequently there is no full, replete, tensor-closed, unit-containing, St-equivalent, internally-adjointed fragment of ChuCost_MV with nontrivial image: **the hypothesis class of the MV fiber-equivalent unit-inclusive implication is empty, and the implication is vacuously true.**

*Proof.* Let Y ∈ Q′ with δ(Y) ≥ 2.

**(1) The unit's rigidity.** ChuCost_MV(I, I) = {(∗, ∗)} by (U3); the hom-bijection at (I, I) gives |St(δ(I), δ(I))| = 1, and St(d, d) is a single point only at d = 1 (its dimension d(d − 1) vanishes only there). So δ(I) = 1.

**(2) The carrier's cardinality.** By (U1), ChuCost_MV(I, Y) = A_Y; the hom-bijection at (I, Y) gives A_Y ≅ St(1, δ(Y)) — the (δ(Y) − 1)-simplex, of cardinality c since δ(Y) ≥ 2. So **|A_Y| = c**.

**(3) The unique top column.** By (U2), ChuCost_MV(Y, I) = the top-cost columns of Y; the hom-bijection at (Y, I) gives a bijection with St(δ(Y), 1) — a single point. So Y has **exactly one** b₀ ∈ B_Y with r_Y(a, b₀) = K for all a ∈ A_Y. (This is doc 80's own Part 2 derivation, verbatim in content: "every object has exactly one top-cost backward column.")

**(4) The collapse.** Let f: A_Y → A_Y be ANY function — arbitrary, as the home's grammar demands — and let g: B_Y → B_Y be the constant g ≡ b₀. The lax condition (L) for the pair (f, g) reads r_Y(f(a), b′) ≤ r_Y(a, b₀) = K for all a, b′ — and the left side is a cost, and costs live in [0, K]. The condition holds ALWAYS. So (f, g) ∈ ChuCost_MV(Y, Y) for every function f, and distinct f give distinct pairs: **|ChuCost_MV(Y, Y)| ≥ |A_Y|^{|A_Y|} = c^c = 2^c.**

**(5) The contradiction.** The hom-bijection at (Y, Y) requires |ChuCost_MV(Y, Y)| = |St(δ(Y), δ(Y))| = c (a polytope of dimension δ(Y)(δ(Y) − 1) ≥ 2). Cantor: 2^c > c. No bijection exists; fully faithfulness fails at (Y, Y); the equivalence does not exist. ∎

The instrument is the audit trail for the mechanism; the cardinality step is ZFC and stays in the proof.

**Where the kill sits, and why nothing found it before.** The kill spends NOTHING that the cell's definition stockpiles: no repleteness, no tensor-closure, no adjointment, no enrichment, no dimension arithmetic.
