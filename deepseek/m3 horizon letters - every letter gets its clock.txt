## Part 3. The reading — the registrar's clock, discharged

Three lessons. **The letters divide by how they age, and the taxonomy is the registration.** Horizon-stable: the catch certificate's floor (a proportion — its grammar is a rate, and rates do not accumulate) and, this battery's cleanest result, the burn floor at the mean level (the fall from baseline is a completed event whose depth the era holds fixed). Row-dependent: the bracket's slope, the ruler's margin. The registrar's obligation, discharged, is the table itself: no letter is read again without its clock beside it, and the corpus's 14-round convention — defended in doc 57 as the window inside which the attacks act and outside which the drifts compound — is now the table's header rather than its footnote. **The straddle persists through time as it did through evidence — and that persistence is the boundary's strongest credential.** Doc 55's test (double the evidence; if the split reproduces, it is a boundary) is run here on the horizon axis: the split reproduces at double horizon with rotating membership, the mean holding to three ten-thousandths. The boundary is not an artifact of the seed draw (doc 55) nor of the horizon (this battery) — it is the row's shape: a landing zone the era fixes, a set of starting lines the draw fixes, and a floor that cuts between them. The mean-governs rule's defense acquires its third column: the verdict names the world (doc 55), the world's history (doc 60), and now the world at any horizon the registrar can afford (this battery) — the level is the stable thing, and the stability is measured, not assumed. **The anchor is an instrument too, and it has its own slowWalk.** The fifth condition's design grades the armed world against its disarmed anchor, and the anchor has now been caught aging: the same drift that fills the healthy twin fills the disarmed mix, one clock slower, crossing the CONSUMED letter at thirty rounds and heading for the ceiling on some seeds. ## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) Thirty rounds is double fourteen and still finite — the burn floor's hold is measured over one doubling, and the anchor's growth (+0.045 over sixteen rounds) extrapolates to the ceiling around round forty-five on the mean, a projection the corpus does not make as a claim. (ii) The per-seed churn (5/12 → 7/12) moves two seeds by less than four ten-thousandths each — the membership rotation is a real re-draw (603's 1.2-point recovery is not noise) but its edges are thin, and N = 12 bounds the resolution. (iii) The slope comparison (CAP30 holds, RR_U10 fails) is two rows — the row-dependence reading is a hypothesis with two points, filed as such. (iv) The twin reference at thirty rounds is doc 40's stored run at a different seed set (six) and a different dose-path; the ruler's drift comparison is indicative, not paired.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the burn floor is horizon-stable everywhere — one row, one doubling, the level held; other rows (the mixes at other doses, the gated worlds) are unmeasured beyond fourteen rounds and the anchor's aging suggests the reference side moves under them. No claim that the anchor's ON-COURSE reading at thirty is an error — it is an honest reading of a drifting reference; the finding is the drift, not a mis-grade. No claim about the effect bars at any horizon beyond fourteen — the table's last row is open, filed. No amendment executed: the windowed-baseline candidate (doc 60) and the anchor-re-read question are filings for the next registration. No MAINTAINS verdicts, no Column-3 marks, no occupancy claim; D3 stands.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** The burn floor's fork resolved to the hold limb (+0.0003, disclosed as to all intents a hold); the anchor's fork to the drift limb; the slope's to the hold limb — all three as registered, none amended. The membership churn (5/12 → 7/12) was not a registered fork — the split's reproduction was, and the churn is reported as the measurement it is. All twenty-four audits passed; no letter was amended.
