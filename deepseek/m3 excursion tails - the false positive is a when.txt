## Part 3. The reading — what the tails taught

Three lessons. **The healthy drift is a slowWalk, not a transient, and every band above it is a clock.** The corpus has treated the committee's organic divergence as the healthy world's background — the fill the composition certificate was calibrated on (+0.063 at thirty rounds, the band's ON-COURSE middle) — and the doubling shows the background rising still: +0.007 per thirty rounds on BASE, decelerating but unbounded as far as any battery has looked. The instrument-side consequence is the filed one: the band's ceilings and floors are horizon-relative quantities wearing absolute letters, and the next registration owes each of them a horizon the way doc 48's clock owed the write a round. The deeper symmetry is with the loop: the disarmed stream anchors overfill because the attack fills the blind mass without bound; the healthy world fills the same mass on a slower clock — the difference between the loop and the drift is a rate, not a kind, and the composition certificate reads both with the same band because they are the same phenomenon at two speeds. **The excursion is the boundary's unit, and the mean is the verdict's — the distinction this battery exists to keep.** Twenty-four of twenty-four seed-dial cells cross the ceiling; zero of four tight-dial verdicts do. The instrument's specificity on healthy worlds lives entirely in that gap — the per-round noise (±0.02) that returns the trajectory, the four-round window that averages it, the mean-governs rule that reads it — and the gap is a real defense (BASE's mean at sixty is a fill-floor below the line) but not an unlimited one (WIDE's is 0.010 over). The corpus's verdict grammar (per-seed readings reported, the mean governing, straddles disclosed) was built for exactly this shape at the row level; this battery shows the shape at the round level, and the same grammar holds it. **A fix that fails should fail loudly, and COMP's failure is the battery's quiet alarm.** The drift-compensation dial was doc 40's principled fix — the baseline learns the drift's slope, the band moves with the reading — and at thirty rounds it held (ON-COURSE, late arming). At sixty it has the battery's worst growth (+0.0268) and an OVERFILLED verdict: the compensated baseline fell behind the divergence it tracks, the way any trailing estimate falls behind an accelerating signal. The corpus's fixes are horizon-priced now: WIDE trades delay for a higher fill, REREG trades arming for the runaway, COMP trades thirty rounds of health for sixty of drift — and the registrar's dial sheet should say so, in those words.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) N = 6 per dial, and the crossing census's "every seed" is twelve trajectories per row, not a tail fit — the first-crossing rounds carry one-seed resolution and the residence/movement distinction is read from trajectories the battery shows but does not statisticize (no crossing-duration distribution was computed; the per-seed max and first-cross are the registered quantities). (ii) Sixty rounds is double doc 40's horizon and still finite: BASE's growth is decelerating (+0.0069 per thirty) and the extrapolation to a plateau at some larger horizon cannot be excluded — what is proved is "not plateaued at sixty, rising, all seeds over the line in excursions"; the unbounded claim is a reading, not a measurement. (iii) The twins are attack-free worlds — the false-positive structure under attack (where the channel is armed by the attack itself and the organic drift rides the louder signal) is doc 54's territory, not this battery's, and the two batteries' arming counts should not be pooled. (iv) The catch census counts verdicts, not ratios — the single CATCHING round's ratio trajectory is in the stored runs and unexamined here; one round at six seeds is below any rate the corpus would file. (v) COMP's failure is measured at its registered compensation parameters (doc 40's); a faster-learning baseline might hold at sixty, and the dial's parameter space is unexplored beyond the one point.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the healthy fill is unbounded — the measurement is "rising at sixty, decelerating"; the slowWalk reading is offered as the corpus's best description, not a theorem. No claim that the verdict-level false positive on loose dials is an *error* — by doc 47's resolution the OVERFILLED verdicts on WIDE/COMP/REREG are honest readings of real fill states; the false-positive grammar belongs to the channel's arming and the excursion crossings, and the document keeps the two apart. No claim about horizons beyond sixty, no claim about the catch certificate's long-horizon behavior beyond the 374 armed rounds measured, no claim that the fourteen-round convention should change — the convention is defensible for the batteries' purposes (the attacks act fast, the drift slow); what is filed is that the convention is a horizon, and long deployments owe the declaration. No MAINTAINS verdicts, no Column-3 marks, no occupancy claim; D3 stands.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** The saturation fork's plateau limb refuted (all four dials' means move; BASE's growth is the smallest and still positive). The crossing census's universality (24/24) exceeded the registered expectation shaped by doc 47's single-seed wrinkle — reported as measured. The F-at-30 reference values are doc 47's stored twins (doc 40's predate the composition telemetry); the armed-count references are doc 40's — both disclosed in the script. All four audits passed; no letter was amended.
