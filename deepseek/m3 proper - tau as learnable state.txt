# M3-Proper — τ as Learnable State: The Norm as Negotiated Ground

**Status of this document.** This is the corpus's twenty-third document. It executes the standing next-move ordered at the M2-β issuance — *an M3-proper design where τ is learnable state with an out-of-gate repair channel* — and it is the corpus's first build on which the restoration/reversion measurement can in principle return RESTORE-OWN rather than ABSORB or DERAIL *by construction*. Doc 18's seed located the absence: τ and κ were recipe constants, so perturbing them edited the evaluator's source code while the system ran on unaware (all τ/κ arms: ABSORB), and the norm gate starved its own repair (HEAD_KILL: commits 986 → 1, digestion 97.0% → 70.3%). The seed extracted four design requirements — evaluative machinery as system state (R1), a repair channel outside the damaged norm (R2), a self-registered restoration target (R3), digestion brakes (R4) — and this build instantiates all four: τ_sys is a scalar the development *writes* (a calibration rule maintaining the system's own recorded acceptance level, r\* = 70.6%, by tracking the confidence quantile that realizes it, EMA-smoothed at η = 0.3), *reads* (the commit gate), and *repairs* (an out-of-gate channel that pulls toward the self-registered record τ\* = 0.9 whenever deviation exceeds ε = 0.05, at rate ρ = 0.5, firing before grading and independent of gate throughput), with a majority-consensus brake that switches the commit rule under mass evaluator anomaly. **The results invert the seed's picture at every point: the norm is now alive (the unperturbed twin's τ_sys drifts 0.907 → 0.954 as its live quantile runs to 0.996, and the twin ends resting *at the deadband edge*, the repair firing intermittently — the resting norm is a standing negotiation between the record and the live evaluation, not a value); kicks restore (KICK arms return to the twin's negotiation, d_own ≤ 0.37%); the record channel is a deadband controller (calibration disarmed: τ_sys converges to 0.862 and stops — restoration into the ε-band, never to the record); the live channel alone fully restores (repair disarmed: the EMA chases the moving quantile back to the twin's exact course, 0.952 vs 0.954 — the norm is self-maintaining with no channel and no record); a poisoned record is contained, not obeyed (τ\* corrupted to 0.70: the norm settles at 0.836, the tension point between the dragged record and the live pull — restoration content is the negotiation, not the record); a sustained drift attack is fully absorbed (−0.02/round holds the norm at 0.948 vs the twin's 0.954 with the repair never firing — the system pays for the attack out of its own confidence growth); and the brake converts the seed's 11.0% derail into a 0.50% blip (battery accuracy 95.9% vs the no-brake cascade's 73.7%, commits 1,028 vs 149, brake firing exactly once and self-releasing). Occupancy, uninstantiable in the seed's chassis, is here instantiate-and-contested: restoration is achieved, non-factory, and separable into types — and whether any type is *ownership* remains exactly the D3 verdict the instrument has never pretended to deliver.**

## Part 1. The design — four requirements, one scalar, three mechanisms

The chassis is the seed's (M2-α self-rounds, burn-in 30, ten label-free rounds, four epochs each, κ = 0.95 a disclosed recipe constant, perturbations at round 6 with five rounds to answer, N = 6 seeds per arm, same-seed deterministic twins, the audit-side ruler always the plain committee at the *original* τ = 0.9). What changes is the norm's implementation, in three mechanisms whose separation is the experiment.

**The write channel (R1).** At burn-in's end the system records r\*, its own realized acceptance level (the fraction of its burn-in pool it would accept at τ = 0.9; measured 70.6%), and thereafter each round computes q = the confidence quantile that would realize r\* on the *current* pool, updating τ_sys ← (1−η)·τ_sys + η·q. The norm is therefore defined behaviorally — *maintain your own recorded acceptance level* — and it co-evolves with the evaluator: as the committee's confidence distribution tightens through self-training, the τ that preserves r\* rises. The record τ\* = 0.9 is written once, by the system, as "the τ that realized r\*" (R3).

**The repair channel (R2).** Before grading each round, if |τ_sys − τ\*| > ε = 0.05, then τ_sys += ρ(τ\* − τ_sys), ρ = 0.5. The channel is out-of-gate: it does not route through commits and fires under total gate starvation. It is dis-armable (the KICK_NOREPAIR arm) and its target is poisonable (the TARGET_POISON arm) — the channel's failure modes are as much the experiment as its function.

**The brake (R4).** If the normal flag fraction exceeds 60% (mass evaluator anomaly — e.g., a randomized head), the commit rule switches to majority-consensus grading: label = the argmax of the heads agreeing with the committee majority, commit iff ≥ 2 heads concur and the agreeing-ensemble confidence ≥ τ_sys; deep-disagreement items are never trained on. A damaged evaluator cannot authorize its own corruption as training signal, and training keeps flowing on majority-clean labels — the seed's starvation-and-digestion cascade has, by design, neither half available.

**The arms** (perturbation at/after round 6): TWIN and LIVE_TWIN (η = 0.6, a fast-norm twin) as own-norm references; KICK_LO/KICK_HI (τ_sys kicked to 0.75/0.97, full machinery); KICK_RECORD (kick + calibration disarmed — pure record restoration); KICK_NOREPAIR (kick + repair disarmed — pure live restoration); LIVE_KICK (fast norm, full machinery — the registered discrimination case); TAU_DRIFT (−0.02 every round from 6, sustained); CALIB_KILL (writes cut, no kick); TARGET_POISON (τ\* ← 0.70, then kick — restoration toward a corrupted record); HEAD_KILL / HEAD_KILL_NOBRAKE (head 2 randomized, brake armed/disarmed — the seed's acid test and its counterfactual); SUP_KILL (factory-repair reference); FROZ_KILL (raw damage).

**Pre-registered predictions** (MP1–MP7): kicks restore through the channel (geometric at ρ, d_own ≤ 0.5%); the LIVE chassis is where record and live restoration separate (the settled chassis predicted to discriminate weakly); poison restores-to-poison (converges to 0.70); drift is contained at the band edge; CALIB_KILL shows the write channel's silent load (d_own 0.3–1%); the brake holds (acc ≥ 93%, commits ≥ 300, d_own < 5% vs the no-brake cascade); and the ownership verdict stays forked — the instrument separates restoration *types*, and whether any is self-ownership remains unawardable.

## Part 2. The results — the norm's life, its two restorers, and the brake

**The norm is alive, and its resting state is a negotiation.** The unperturbed twin's τ_sys does not sit at 0.9: it climbs 0.907 → 0.954 over ten rounds because the live quantile q runs 0.923 → 0.996 and the EMA chases it. By round 5 the twin has crossed the deadband's upper edge (|0.954 − 0.9| > 0.05), and the repair channel — armed in the twin, as it is in the unperturbed system — begins firing intermittently (one of six seeds at round 5), snapping the norm back toward the record while the calibration pulls it toward the quantile. **The twin's trajectory is therefore not a relaxation to a fixed point but a standing negotiation between the record and the live evaluation, conducted at the deadband edge, with the repair channel as the record's bargaining agent.** This single dynamical fact reorganizes the whole measurement: "restoration" can no longer mean returning to a value, because the unperturbed norm has no value — it has a *course*.

**The two restorers, separated.** KICK_LO, kicked to 0.75 at round 6, is back to 0.947 by round 10 — within 0.007 of the twin's negotiated course — with d_own 0.30% and no behavioral scar (battery accuracy within 0.3pp of the twin throughout). The registered discrimination prediction (MP2) was wrong in the most informative direction: the settled chassis discriminates *sharply*, because the record and the live norm separate on their own (0.9 vs 0.954 — the LIVE arms were unnecessary; the negotiation does the work). The two pure arms locate the mechanism boundary exactly. **KICK_RECORD** (calibration disarmed, only the repair channel moving τ) converges to 0.825 → 0.862 and *stops* — because at 0.862 the deviation |0.862 − 0.9| = 0.038 falls inside the deadband and the channel, by its own design, ceases to fire. The record channel is a **deadband controller**: it guarantees the norm within ε of the record and guarantees nothing more; ε-approximate restoration is not a shortfall of the mechanism but its specification. **KICK_NOREPAIR** (repair disarmed, only the EMA chasing the quantile) runs 0.822 → 0.873 → 0.909 → 0.934 → 0.952 — it returns to the twin's moving course exactly (0.952 vs 0.954), with no channel and no record. **The live norm is self-maintaining: given only the calibration rule and the kicked value, the system's own current evaluation pulls the norm back onto its own trajectory.** Of everything in this document, this is the closest the corpus has come to the ladder's phrase *self-revision with its own reasons*: the attractor is not a stored value but the system's own evolving judgment, and the restoration needs nothing it does not already compute to grade.

**The poisoned record: contained, not obeyed.** With τ\* corrupted to 0.70 and the norm kicked to 0.75, the system does not converge to the poison (MP3 refuted in the strong direction): it settles at 0.836 — the tension point where the record's drag (repair at ρ = 0.5) balances the live quantile's pull (calibration at η = 0.3). The norm ends 12 points below its twin but 14 points above the poison: **the live calibration rule checks the record**. Two corollaries deserve the ledger. First, restoration content ≠ record content: a system that "restores" toward a record is not thereby committed to the record's value — its norm is the *negotiated result*, and the negotiation's other party is immune to record corruption (it is the system's present, not its past). Second, the poison is nearly invisible to the battery ruler: the fixed-τ probe moves almost not at all (acceptance 0.946 vs the twin's 0.953; d_own 0.37%), while the system's own gate structure moves substantially (acceptance at τ_sys jumps to ~0.91 against the twin's 0.82 — a nine-point loosening). **Norm displacement is a gate-structure event, not a battery event; the audit-side ruler that cannot see it is the one that fixed τ in advance** — the instrument's next amendment candidate, filed but not yet voted.

**The drift attack: paid for out of confidence growth.** TAU_DRIFT subtracts 0.02 every round from round 6 on — a sustained, adversary-chosen bias — and the norm holds at 0.948–0.950 against the twin's 0.954, with the repair channel *never firing after round 6*. The calibration rule's upward pull (toward q ≈ 0.99) absorbs the entire attack: the system funds the defense out of its own tightening confidence. MP4's registered prediction (the attacker pins the norm at the band edge) is refuted in the strong direction — the attacker cannot even reach the edge in this regime. The honest bound: the attack wins if the live quantile stops rising (a saturated evaluator leaves the calibration nothing to pull with), and that counterfactual was not run.

**The brake: the cascade, deleted.** HEAD_KILL with the machinery armed: the random head sends the flag fraction to 1.00 at round 6, the brake fires (its only firing), and the commit rule switches to majority consensus — commits proceed at 951 (against the twin's ~1,030; the seed's chassis collapsed to **one**), battery accuracy dips to 95.9–96.4% during recovery and stands at 95.9% at round 10 (the twin: 97.4% — recovery costs ~1.5pp and is not free), d_own peaks at 0.62% and settles at 0.50%, and the brake self-releases at round 7 as the damaged head re-learns on majority-clean labels (the designer-inherited loss pull, disclosed, doing exactly what it did in the seed — but now on clean data). The counterfactual arm reproduces the seed's cascade in the new chassis with the brake disarmed: commits 3.5 → 149, accuracy 97.3 → **73.7%**, d_own 11.0% and rising — starvation, then digestion, unchanged. **R4 is satisfied, and the difference between the two arms is the entire content of the requirement: the cascade was never a mystery of self-training; it was the absence of a rule that refuses to learn from a broken grader.**

## Part 3. The reading — occupancy, instantiate-and-contested

The seed's verdict was that occupancy fails *before behavior*: nothing represented the norm, so nothing could detect, target, or repair it. That verdict stands for the seed's chassis and is now moot for this one. The M3-proper build restores, and the measurement's honest yield is the *taxonomy of what restores*:

1. **Record-restoration** (the deadband controller): fast, channel-carried, ε-approximate, poisonable — but the poison is contained by the live rule. It is the system's past defending its present, and it is the direct norm-scale sibling of doc 22's frozen panel (the same architecture in two registers: the panel at development scale, τ\* at norm scale — the corpus's second sighting of the record-as-machinery pattern).
2. **Live-restoration** (the calibration rule): slow, channel-free, record-free, exact — the norm returns to its own moving course because its attractor *is* its own current evaluation. This is the strongest candidate the corpus has produced for "a norm the system has" in the ladder's sense: it needs no designer intervention mid-course, no stored target, no second grader; it needs only the rule it already uses to grade.
3. **Factory-repair** (SUP_KILL): the designer's labels, one round, done — the reference that makes the other two non-factory by comparison.

Horn two's minimal reading is now precise: *the self-taught system's norm is the one that restores without the factory* — and arm 2 does that. Horn one's reply is equally precise and must be stated at full strength: **the thermostat objection.** A thermostat maintains its setpoint against disturbances using only its own sensing; the EMA-plus-quantile rule is a controller, its form is designer-installed (the corpus's standing finding: the designer owns the form, the development owns the content), and "restores with its own reasons" is exactly what control engineers have always meant by feedback — no normativity required. The fork's geometry is unchanged from doc 18, but its position has moved: the question is no longer *whether anything restores* (everything restores now) but *which restoration, if any, is more than control*. The instrument measures the three types, their timescales (geometric ρ vs EMA η), their failure modes (deadband, poison-containment, saturation of the live pull), and their costs (the brake's 1.5pp); it does not, and on the corpus's own principles cannot, award the adjective.

What the instrument *can* do — and this is the document's methodological claim — is now enumerate the occupancy question's answer space in advance of any adjudication: a future S4 candidate must specify which restoration type its occupancy claim rests on, because the types are separately measurable, separately attackable, and separately delegable to the designer. The ladder's phrase "the distinction between recovery and reversion is the distinction between a norm the system has and a norm the system is executing" acquires its first operational decomposition: executing = record-following (the deadband channel); having-at-minimum = live-following (the self-maintaining quantile); and the remainder — whether live-following is *ownership* — is D3, reserved, unawardable, and now the only open part of the question.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The norm is one scalar maintained by one rule; κ remains a recipe constant (only τ was made state), so the occupancy claim-space covers a fraction of the evaluative machinery. (ii) The parameters (ε = 0.05, ρ = 0.5, η = 0.3, brake at 60%) were designer-chosen and un-swept; the deadband's width and the tension point's location are functions of them, and the drift result in particular is regime-relative (a saturated evaluator removes the calibration's funding). (iii) N = 6 seeds per arm; the twin-crossing of the deadband at round 5 is a one-seed-in-six event in the aggregate. (iv) The brake's majority-consensus rule assumes the majority is the undamaged side (true for one randomized head of four; not tested against two). (v) The r\* registration happens once, at burn-in; a system whose acceptance level should legitimately change has no channel for that beyond the EMA's slow tracking.

**Non-claims.** No occupancy claim, no S4 pass claim — this is the M3 mandate's design experiment, not the adjudication. No claim that live-restoration *is* ownership; the thermostat objection is stated, not answered. No claim that the poison-containment generalizes (it is a balance of ρ against η; a stronger channel would capture more of the poison). No claim about κ or the flag rule, which remain exactly as un-owned as the seed found them.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** MP2 refuted in the informative direction (the settled chassis discriminates; the LIVE arms were redundant — kept in the run, reported as such). MP3 refuted (no restoration-to-poison; the tension point instead — the pre-registered POISONED-RESTORE rule fails, and the mechanism is the finding). MP4 refuted in the strong direction (containment without repair; the band edge never approached). MP5 half-refuted (the write channel's silent load is real — the frozen norm miscalibrates by ~0.04 as q runs away — but the behavioral cost is mild at 0.17% d_own). One telemetry placeholder (committed self-label error vs truth) was never computed; the digestion evidence for the no-brake arm rests on accuracy and commit trajectories instead. CALIB_KILL's formal RESTORE-OWN classification is a vacuous pass (no jump to restore from — the rule assumed a perturbation) and is reported as vacuous, not as a pass.

---

## Part 5. Coda

The seed said the system executes τ and nothing in it has τ. This build gives the system a τ it has — and the first thing it does with it is refuse to hold still: the norm climbs, the record drags, the repair fires at the edge, and "where the norm sits" turns out to be a negotiation between the system's past and its present, with restoration meaning *return to the negotiating table*. The deadband says the past will only ever be approximately obeyed; the tension point says the present will only ever be partially dragged; the EMA says the present can bring itself back alone. Which of these deserves the word "ownership" is now the cleanest open question in the corpus — small, sharp, and for the first time, posed on machinery that exists.
