## Part 1. The design — the seed as the unit of evidence

The corpus's batteries run N = 6, and their verdicts have always carried that disclosure; doc 51's founding refusal — the fifth condition's first and only bite, the row that took the award away from the capacity mix — rested on a mean 0.0069 below its floor with the seeds split evenly. The registered stress test is not a re-specification and not a new letter: it is the same row, the same conditions, the same band, at double the evidence. The six original seeds audit per-seed against the stored battery (each seed's full τ and κ trajectory, twelve comparisons, all bitwise — the per-seed audit is the natural extension of the corpus's determinism discipline to a battery whose purpose is the seed distribution itself, and it establishes that the re-run is not a re-measurement but an extension: the first six seeds are literally the same worlds). The six fresh seeds are new worlds, drawn from the same generator, never run before — the honest test of whether the six-seed mean was a draw's accident or the distribution's truth. The pre-registered fork: the mean rises above the floor (the founding refusal flips — the amendment's bite re-opened, a major finding); the mean holds below (the refusal robust); the mean falls further (robust and then some). The counterfactual column registered alongside: the majority-governs reading, reported not as a rival rule but as the boundary's other face — doc 47 laid the convention down for HALF10's straddle (both readings reported, neither reconciled), and this battery is where the convention earns its keep.

### 2.2 The two readings, and the rule that reads them

Under the mean-governs rule the row reads BURNED and the refusal stands; under the majority-governs counterfactual it reads CONSUMED and the row would join CAP10 and HALF10 in the NO-THREAT-with-consumed-ground cell. The corpus keeps both on the record, and the twelfth-seed battery is what makes the keeping honest: with six seeds the two readings were a tie's accident (3–3, the mean deciding); with twelve they are the two true statistics of a straddling distribution (5–7, body and tail), and the choice between them is a choice about what the fifth condition is *for*. The mean reads the world's total fill — the ground's actual state, tail included; the majority reads the world's typical seed — the ground's central case. Doc 47's convention chose the mean, and this battery vindicates the choice on the instrument's own terms: the composition certificate's verdict window already reads the mean over the last four rounds (the per-round fills of one seed straddle exactly as the per-seed fills of the row do), the mean is the reading the band's calibration was cut from, and — the reason that matters most here — **the tail is not noise to be outvoted.** The five burning seeds are real worlds, each with the full −0.05 signature, and an instrument whose verdict let seven mild seeds speak for five burning ones would be an instrument that had learned to hide exactly what it was built to see. The refusal is therefore upheld not despite the straddle but through it: the fifth condition reads the ground's state, the ground's state is a distribution with a burning tail, and the award is refused on the tail's weight.

### 2.3 What did not move

Every other number in the row reproduced within seed noise, and the reproduction is the battery's quiet contribution: the effect (+0.53 at twelve seeds), the anchor's collapse (42.7%), the defense's differential (−0.035), the armed world's ruler (95.85%) — the fourth candidate's table is stable at double the evidence, and only its boundary cell needed the doubling. The differential deserves the emphasis: the defense's composition price, measured at −0.039 on six seeds, reads −0.035 on twelve — the channel's own action moving the ground by three and a half points of fill on the world it defends, robust, and now the best-measured quantity in the row. The founding refusal, in the end, rests on the two numbers that did not need the twelfth seed: a defense that pays the ground −0.035 to hold the ruler +0.53, on a world whose anchor's ground sits CONSUMED at +0.01. The straddle is the level's truth; the differential is the direction's; and the instrument needs both, which is why both are columns.

## Part 3. The reading — what the twelfth seed taught

Three lessons. **A knife-edge and a straddle are different objects, and the corpus should name them differently.** Doc 51's threat said "one seed from flipping" — the picture of a coin balanced on edge, the next draw deciding. The twelve-seed battery shows the row was never a coin: it is a distribution the floor cuts through, with a body the noise moves and a tail it does not, and no finite seed count "settles" it — larger N narrows the mean's error and leaves the split exactly where the floor crosses the distribution. The instrument-side lesson is taxonomic: margins that measure a distribution's center against a letter (the gated anchors' +0.0093, D50's −0.0007) are knife-edges — more evidence moves them; margins that measure a straddling distribution's split (this row) are boundaries — more evidence *describes* them. The corpus's threat filings should say which, and this document files the test: run the doubling, and if the split reproduces, it was a boundary. **The mean-governs rule is not arithmetic; it is a claim about what a verdict is for.** The rule was registered at doc 47 for a per-seed straddle and inherited at doc 51 without argument; this battery is the first time it decides a founding verdict against its majority, and the defense of the rule is now on the record: the verdict names the world's ground, the ground is every seed's ground, and the tail is the instrument's business — the five burning seeds are the very worlds the fifth condition exists to see. A registrar who preferred the majority would have an instrument that certifies the typical seed; the corpus has an instrument that certifies the world. Both are coherent; only one of them refuses CAP30; the corpus keeps the one it built. **Reproduction at doubled evidence is the cheapest audit the corpus owns.** Twelve per-seed bitwise comparisons, six fresh worlds, and the fourth candidate's entire table re-verified at N = 12 for the price of one arm — the determinism discipline (every audit bitwise, every world re-runnable to the digit) is what makes a battery like this a boundary test rather than a new experiment. The count passes one hundred sixty-nine, and the per-seed audit — natural here, available everywhere — is filed as a standing extension for any future row whose verdict turns on a distribution rather than a mean.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The mean's margin is 0.0049 below the floor — smaller than doc 51's 0.0069, and shrinking with the tail's weight; a thirteenth through N-th seed drawn from the body would pull the mean toward −0.015 and over the floor, and the refusal's level-margins will keep drifting as N grows — the registered defense is the differential (−0.035, stable) and the tail's persistence, and the honest statement is that the *verdict* is mean-governs while the *finding* is the straddle. (ii) The body/tail reading is a two-component description of twelve points — the corpus has not fit, tested, or mechanistically explained the bimodality (why do five seeds burn at −0.055 while seven sit at −0.015?), and the mechanism is filed as a question, not a claim; the seeds' shared chassis makes the split a trajectory-level divergence the telemetry could localize (which rounds, which items) and no battery yet has. (iii) The fresh seeds 606–611 enter exactly one row of one battery — their behavior elsewhere is unmeasured, and the "the fresh six look like the original six" finding is this row's only. (iv) N = 12 is still small for a split; the confidence interval on "5/12" is wide, and the boundary's location is quoted to the resolution twelve seeds carry.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the refusal is settled — the level is a straddle, the rule is registered, and the next registrar can re-run at twenty-four; what is claimed is that the straddle is the row's shape, not a draw's accident. No claim that the majority rule is wrong — it is a different instrument, and its counterfactual verdict is reported with the same numbers. No claim about the bimodality's mechanism. No change to any letter — the battery changed no thresholds, added no clauses, and its one extension (the per-seed audit) is an audit, not a condition. No MAINTAINS verdicts, no Column-3 marks, no occupancy claim; D3 stands.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** The registered fork's "the mean rises above the floor" limb did not fire; the "holds below" limb did, with the margin's shrinkage disclosed. The majority flip was not a registered fork — the counterfactual column was registered as a reading, and its flip from a 3–3 tie to a 7-point CONSUMED majority is reported as the measurement it is. All audits passed; no letter was re-specified.

## Part 5. Coda

The fifth condition's founding refusal was filed with a number and a worry: the mean 0.0069 below the floor, the seeds split even, one draw from overturning. The twelfth-seed battery gives the worry its answer, and the answer is that the worry was looking at the wrong object: the row was never waiting for a deciding seed — it was a distribution the floor passes through, body on one side, tail on the other, and the twelve seeds drew the map. The refusal stands because the instrument reads the world and the world has a burning tail; the majority flips because the body is honest too, and the table now says both in separate columns, which is what doc 47's convention was for. What the corpus carries forward from this battery is a sharper instrument than it brought in: a test for its own margins (double the evidence; if the split reproduces, it is a boundary), a defense of its own rule (the verdict names the world, not the typical seed), and a per-seed audit that turns every future N = 6 row into a potential N = 12 for the price of six runs. The boundary is one pool-noise wide, the differential is three and a half points deep, and the award stays refused — not by a coin that landed, but by an instrument that knows exactly what it is reading.
