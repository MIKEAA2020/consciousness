## Part 1. The audit — the additive corner, and the adjunction that fails there

**Standing notation (docs 24, 27, 62).** ChuCost is the category of triples (A, X, r) with r: A × X → [0, ∞], morphisms (f, g) with (L): s(f(a), y) ≤ r(a, g(y)); the Dialectica tensor on carriers (C × A, Z^A × X^C) with the pairing u ⊗_V r composed through the crossed readings; the residuum hom A ⊸ B = (X^Y × B^A, A × Y, h) with h = r(a, φ(y)) →_V s(β(a), y); Theorem A's adjunction ChuCost(C ⊗ A, B) ≅ ChuCost(C, A ⊸ B), proved by the condition equivalence (★) q ≤ w ⊗ p ⟺ (★★) (p → q) ≤ w under currying, with p, q, w the three pairings read at the corresponding points.

**Lemma 66 (the Łukasiewicz corner; no MV adjunction) [Derived here].** The scalar identity (★★) ⟺ (★) — equivalently p → q ≤ w ⟺ q ≤ w ⊗ p — holds in the additive quantale ([0, ∞], +) and fails in the Łukasiewicz quantale ([0, K], ⊙, →_⊙) at (p, q, w) = (0, 0, 0) and at every point with q ≤ p and w < K: the implication p →_⊙ q = min(K, q + K − p) saturates at K wherever q ≥ p, demanding w = K, while the tensor w ⊙ p = max(w + p − K, 0) truncates at 0 wherever w + p ≤ K, satisfying the bound for free. Consequently ChuCost_MV — the full subcategory of [0, K]-valued pairings with the ⊙-tensor and the MV-residuum hom, doc 62's Prop 64 — is not monoidal closed: at the dualizer ⊥ = (1, 1, 0), the tensor and hom compute to ⊥ ⊗_MV ⊥ = (1, 1, 0 ⊙ 0) = ⊥ and ⊥ ⊸_MV ⊥ = (1, 1, min(K, 0 + K − 0)) = (1, 1, K) = I, so ChuCost_MV(⊥ ⊗_MV ⊥, ⊥) is the one-morphism set (the (L) condition reads 0 ≤ 0) while ChuCost_MV(⊥, ⊥ ⊸_MV ⊥) = ChuCost_MV(⊥, I) is empty (the condition reads K ≤ 0). *Proof.* The scalar case check is two lines at the named corner and monotone extensions elsewhere; the object-level computation is doc 62's own arithmetic (Prop 64(ii) computed ⊥ ⊸ ⊥ = I), here read against the adjunction it was not checked against. ∎

**The retraction, stated.** Doc 27's Remark 18 — "Theorem A's proof consumed only associativity, commutativity, monotonicity of ⊗; the residuum adjunction; currying in V. It therefore holds for Dialectica over any commutative quantale V — the MV quantale of Part 4 included" — is **retracted in its generality claim**, per the doc 27/doc 62 precedent: the proof consumes, in addition, the additive-form identity p → q ≤ w ⟺ q ≤ p ⊗ w, which is not a quantale identity (it is not even the residuum adjunction, which reads w ≤ p → q ⟺ w ⊗ p ≤ q and does hold in every quantale; the proof's form puts the residuum's value on the left of the inequality, where only the additive scale's untruncated arithmetic supports it). The additive Theorem A itself is untouched — its proof and its theorem stand in ([0, ∞], +), where the identity holds. The consequences for doc 62, ledgered: Prop 64's objects and pairings stand (the unit (1, 1, K) and dualizer (1, 1, 0) of the ⊙-presentation, the reflected dual, the double dual (A, X, K − (K − r)) = A, and the de Morgan pairing computation r ⊙ (K − s) reflected to min(K, s + K − r) are all carrier-and-pairing statements, correct as arithmetic); the preamble's closure claim falls; Theorem V's (a) ⟺ (c) stands (its proof — closure under the dual, the tensor, and the de Morgan object identity — is adjunction-free, as an audit of doc 62's own text confirms); Theorem V's (c) ⟹ (b) falls with the closure claim, its one cited engine being "Theorem B's currying (valid in the MV scale by Theorem A's generality)." The base poset, under it all along, keeps its silence another phase; the budget, under it all along too, turns out to have been carrying not just the bridge's oldest hope but its newest counterexample — priced, as always, in the currency the instruments have been counting since the first deadband was registered.
