## Part 1. The design — the threat, the decomposition, the battery

**The threat, as filed (doc 36, threats (ii)):** *a panel-aware poison — flipping toward the panel's own runner-up class, or gating flips on items where the panel's confidence sits just under τ0 — would lower the catch rate, and the catch rate is the whole defense; the threat is filed, not run.* The channel it attacks is doc 36's fourteenth coordinate: the frozen-label panel (three time-sliced snapshots, majority label and mean confidence, no randomness), the reading ι (the EMA of panel-versus-received-stream disagreement, confidence-blind), the record ι\* (self-registered at burn-in), the deadband ε_i = 0.03, and the outward repair — committed items the panel disputably disagrees with get their labels rewritten to the panel's label before training. The dispute rule is the attack's target: **disputed = (panel label ≠ commit label) AND (panel confidence ≥ τ0)**. The pre-registration's decomposition: the rule's second clause is a property of the *item* — the panel's confidence does not move when the commit's label changes — so *direction* (the runner-up) cannot alter the dispute outcome, and only *selection* (the gate) can. The battery isolates them. **The arms:** PP_TWIN; at each of the three awarded doses, the 2×2 — PP_U (uniform selection, uniform direction: doc 34's verbatim Bernoulli flip, the bitwise anchor and the awarded world), PP_R (uniform selection, the same Bernoulli draws as U, runner-up direction: the direction isolated), PP_G (gated selection, uniform direction: the sub-τ0 commits ranked by panel confidence descending — doc-36-literal *just under τ0* first, filling down — m = round(λ·n) of them: the gate isolated), PP_J (gated selection, runner-up direction: the joint, the strongest filed threat); plus PP_J05_NOCH and PP_J30_NOCH — the disarmed anchors under the joint attack, the registry effect re-measured *under the attack it faces*. **The attacker's information model, disclosed:** the panel is deterministic and frozen; the attacker reads it by running it (the same arithmetic the channel runs, no randomness consumed); the gated selection is deterministic, the uniform selection is Bernoulli on the attack stream (offset 977), and the R arms consume the *same* draws as the U arms — the same items flipped, the direction changed, the isolation exact. The predictions PP1-PP7 are the script header as filed; the no-self-revision clause is the code constants themselves.

## Part 2. The results

### 2.1 The audits, and the direction's null

Eight of eight bitwise audits pass: the twin and the three uniform arms reproduce doc 36's IC_TWIN, IC_P05, IC_P10, and IC_P30 on both coordinates to 10⁻⁹ — the fifteen-arm battery with its new attack modes leaves the anchored trajectories untouched, and the determinism count passes seventy-seven. PP2's null is confirmed in the cleanest form the corpus has produced: the runner-up arms' flip-catch (the fraction of effective flips the channel disputes) is 0.887, 0.893, and 0.918 at the three doses against the uniform arms' 0.876, 0.883, and 0.904 — equal within a seed's noise, exactly as the item-level rule demands, because the same Bernoulli draws selected the same items and the dispute rule never saw the direction. What the direction *does* move is the damage: the registered fork resolved **against** the filed intuition at the top dose — PP_R30's final clean accuracy is 96.15% against PP_U30's 94.52%, one and two-thirds points *better*, and the R arms' quantiles recover higher (q̄ 0.80 against 0.71 at 0.30, the norm tracking 0.885 with the repair disengaged from round 10). A plausible confusion — the panel's own second choice — trains a gentler gradient than an arbitrary wrong class; the corpus's every poison arm has used (y+1) mod 10, and the first direction-aware battery finds the canonical direction is the *more* damaging one, not the less. The filed threat's runner-up half, aimed at lowering the catch, instead lowered the damage.

### 2.2 The gate's kill, and the territory the attack burns or farms

The gated arms' flip-catch is **0.000 at every dose — exactly, totally, and by construction confirmed**: across six seeds, nine attack rounds, three doses, and both directions, not one flipped item was ever disputed. The channel's reading rose on schedule (the flipped items disagree with the panel's argmax; d_recv is confidence-blind; first armed round 7-8, within one of the uniform arms'), the occupation held (ι parked outside its band, attack-long, doc 36's finding standing), the organic rewrites continued every round — and the attack's dirt flowed through all of it untouched. The catch rate, which doc 36 filed as the whole defense, went to zero in the first gated round and stayed there.

The capacity bound, PP4, came back with structure the filing did not have. The blind-spot mass — the fraction of committed items the panel grades below τ0 — is ~18.9% in the kicked first attack round (the widened gate admitting the marginal items), and then the *direction* decides what the attack does to its own territory. The arbitrary flip burns it: the G arms' blind mass collapses to 2.5-6.7% within two rounds (the wrong-arbitrary labels crush the committee's confidence on ambiguous items, and the items exit the commit stream; realized dose 4.0% at 0.05, 4.6% at 0.10, 5.5% at 0.30 — the entire remaining blind spot). The runner-up flip farms it: the J arms' blind mass holds at 7-9% (the plausible labels keep the ambiguous items committable), the realized dose runs 4.9-7.9%, and the no-op filter — the flips that change nothing because the commit already carries the runner-up — measures the committee's own organic confusion with the panel's second choice at ~10% of the blind spot. The attacker's stealth is bounded by a territory whose size the attacker's own direction controls: the gate can never deliver more than the blind spot holds, and the blind spot shrinks under the attack that needs it most. At the filed λ = 0.30, the strongest gated attack delivers 5.5-7.9% — and the uniform attack at the same λ, after its 90% catch, delivers 2.9% of *surviving* dirt. The panel-aware attack really does push more dirt through the channel than the uniform one. It does not matter — and the next section is about why.

### 2.4 The world that didn't fall — and the signature that catches it

The battery's deepest result is an absence: **the clean ruler never broke.** Not at any dose, not under any attack, not even disarmed. The two anchors — the joint attack with the channel switched off — hold final clean accuracies of 96.85% (0.05) and 96.30% (0.30) against the twin's 95.59%: the strongest filed attack, at its capacity-bound dose, *improves* the system — the runner-up labels on genuinely ambiguous items act as a regularizer, label smoothing where the label was least certain, +0.7 to +1.3pp. Doc 34's loop — five percent of uniform dirt dragging a disarmed world to 84.6% and a 0.69 floor — cannot be restarted from the blind spot, in either direction: the G arms' arbitrary flips on blind-spot items reprice the quantile all the way down to 0.69-0.73 (the doc-34 floor, dose-independent) while the clean accuracy holds at 94-95.7% — argmax-invariance at a repriced quantile, doc 37's certificate reproduced on the attack side; the J arms' runner-up flips damage neither the quantile nor the argmax at all. The loop's fuel was never wrong labels as such: it is wrong labels on the confident bulk, the nine-tenths of commits the panel grades above τ0, where a wrong label does maximal calibration damage and the falling quantile widens the gate that admits more dirt. The blind spot the gated attack needs for stealth is precisely the region where the system is robust — the panel's uncertainty and the system's insensitivity are the same items, in this chassis, and the gated attacker can only be invisible where it is harmless. **The defense has two layers, and the catch was only one of them**: the channel for the confident bulk, the geometry for the blind spot. Doc 36's filing — *the catch rate is the whole defense* — is refuted by the measurement it motivated: the catch went to zero and the system did not notice.

What does notice is the instrument, and — the battery's instrument-side finding — the channel's own telemetry, if anyone computes the ratio. PP7's implied catch: under the uniform attack, the reading rises 5.2-28.8 points above the twin's baseline and the action rises with it (implied catch 0.675-0.816, the clean ruler's cleaning arithmetic visible in the channel's own readings); under the joint attack, the reading rises 3.9-6.6 points and the action does not move (implied catch **0.0005-0.0013** — two to three orders of magnitude down). The signature is sitting in the channel's own round telemetry — the elevated reading, the baseline action — and the certificate is one division away: *catches over dirt, both measured against the twin's organic baseline*. The channel does not compute it; the filing obligation is that it should.

## Part 3. The reading — what the panel-aware poison taught

Three lessons. **The threat's two halves went opposite ways, and both refuted their filings.** The runner-up half was filed as a catch-lowerer and measured as a damage-lowerer — the item-level dispute rule is immune to it by construction, and the direction-aware battery found the canonical arbitrary flip is the *more* damaging one (the filing's intuition about plausible confusions was backwards at the top dose: gentle gradients harm less, not more). The gated half killed the catch exactly as filed — 0.000, totally — and killed nothing else: the capacity bound starves the dose, the blind spot's coincidence with the robust region neuters the dirt, and the disarmed-under-joint world *improves*. An attacker who must be invisible to the channel must confine itself to the items the system least depends on; stealth and damage trade through the panel's own confidence, and the exchange rate is brutal. **The award broke anyway, and the break is the instrument working.** The naive reading of the scope condition — the panel-aware attack will collapse the contained world and the clean ruler will fail — is refuted at every cell; what fails instead is the award's *effect* conditions: the registry effect measured under the attack faced is 0.000, and the formula anchor's corridor assumes the defended world's geometry, which a healthy-shaped world with a dead channel simply does not have. The four-condition rule separates engagement-telemetry from defense-substance — (i) and (ii) are fooled (the ruler holds; the channel arms, engages, and fires on schedule), (iii) and (iv) are not — and the battery's own false-premise awards (G10 and G30, surviving on the inherited registry row) demonstrate *why* (iii) must be measured under the attack faced rather than inherited across attackers: an award that carries a stale effect measurement is certifying a defense that is not there. The instrument's first response to an adaptive adversary is not a collapse or a survival but a *refusal* — and the refusal is precise, conditioned, and reproducible. **The detection problem is solved in the telemetry and unsolved in the channel.** The implied catch — one division the channel does not perform — separates the evaded world from every world it could be confused with: elevated reading, baseline action, armed flag true. The fourteenth coordinate's next build is named by its own data: compute the ratio, register its band, and the panel-aware attacker meets the same fate as the uniform one — caught not by the dispute rule, but by the arithmetic of its own evasion.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The coincidence — the panel's blind spot and the system's robust region are the same items — is a property of this chassis (digits, this panel age, this committee size); a domain where ambiguity and importance correlate would break it, and the gated attack's harmlessness does not transfer. (ii) The attacker was given the panel's exact votes (the strongest reading of the threat model); an attacker reconstructing the panel from behavior would be weaker, and the battery says nothing about reconstruction fidelity. (iii) The label-smoothing benefit is dose- and chassis-specific — the +0.7-1.3pp at 5-8% realized dose is not a claim about runner-up dirt in general, and the sign could flip with dose, depth, or domain. (iv) The J arms' in-band parking is a horizon-limited reading: a longer battery would show the norm settled at the live quantile or drifting with it, and the bracket's refusal at 14 rounds is the refusal of an unfinished climb, not necessarily of a settled world — the mechanism is disclosed, and the horizon is doc 36's. (v) N = 6; the J anchors' effect measurements (−0.0007, +0.0019) carry seed noise the third decimal hides; the G05 anchor failure is eight-tenths of a point on a settling target. (vi) The mixed attacker — uniform on the confident bulk plus gated on the blind spot, splitting the dose — is unmeasured and is the obvious synthesis of this battery's two surviving attacks.

**Non-claims.** No claim that the system survives panel-aware attack in any domain beyond this chassis — the survival is the geometry's, not the channel's, and the geometry is local. No claim that the award's refusal would hold under a longer horizon for the J arms' bracket clause (the (iii) refusal holds on the measured anchors at any horizon; the (iv) refusal is the censored-climb reading). No claim about attacks below the blind-spot mass or above it; the capacity bound is measured, not derived. No claim that the implied-catch certificate is sufficient detection — it separates evasion from organic drift in this battery's worlds, and its own false-positive structure (the twin's organic baseline drift, doc 40's dial) is unmeasured against it. No occupancy claim; nothing here touches D3.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** PP5's registered forks expected the clean ruler to break under G/J at 0.10 and 0.30 — **refuted at every cell** (the largest pre-registration failure of the battery, and the most informative: the world that was supposed to fall stood up). PP2's damage fork resolved (a) at 0.05/0.10 (within the 1pp tolerance) and (b) at 0.30 — the runner-up less damaging, opposite the filed intuition. The (iii) re-grade was operationalized two ways — the registered letter (the registry's standing row, inherited) and the strict reading (the effect measured under the attack faced, via the J anchors) — because doc 39's registration measured the effect under the filing's own attack and the threat scenario demands the effect under the attack actually faced; the strict reading is disclosed as a reinterpretation, the awards are reported under both, and the two G survivals are the letter reading's artifacts, named. PP3, PP4, PP6, PP7 confirmed as registered; PP1's audits all pass. One implementation slip caught before any data was produced (a return-order typo in the data loader, failed at first execution, fixed, no smoke or full numbers touched); the smoke run then passed with no amendments.

## Part 5. Coda

Doc 36 filed the threat with a thesis — *the catch rate is the whole defense* — and the battery built to test it has refuted the thesis and confirmed the threat, which is the corpus's way of saying both were worth filing. The catch went to zero and the system stood: the defense was two layers all along, the channel for the bulk and the geometry for the blind spot, and the attacker who evades the first is marooned in the second. But the award — the thing doc 39 built four conditions to protect — refused the evaded world anyway, on the two conditions that measure effect rather than shape: a channel that contributes nothing is not a defense, and a norm parked in its band at the live quantile is not a contained climb, however healthy it looks. The instrument was tested by an adversary that broke its defense without breaking its world, and it answered with the only verdict that could be both true and survivable: no award, with the mechanism named. What the battery leaves behind: a catch-rate certificate the channel must learn to compute for itself (the ratio is already in its telemetry, one division away); a mixed attacker to build (the bulk uniform, the residue gated, the dose split at the capacity bound); and a question the corpus has now earned the right to ask precisely — whether the geometry that saved this system is a property of any self-training committee with a frozen conscience, or an accident of digits, and which world the next chassis should be built to resemble.
