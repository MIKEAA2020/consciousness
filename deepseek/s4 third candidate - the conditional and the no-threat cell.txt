## Part 1. The filing — two amendments, one new cell, and the anchors to pay for them

**The inheritance (doc 44, Part 3).** The effect condition failed in both directions: doc 41's false-premise awards (effects inherited from worlds the attacker had left — the gated arms surviving on the uniform's registry row) and doc 44's true-defense refusal (a measured null of 0.0056 against an anchor holding at 95.7% — the attack too weak to need the defense). The design lesson, verbatim: *the registry effect must be read jointly with the attack's price — an effect is only meaningful against a threat that costs something — and the natural form is a conditional: if the disarmed anchor degrades, then the armed delta must clear the bar; else the row reads NO-THREAT, a verdict the current table has no cell for.* This filing builds that cell, and pays for it the only way a conditional can be paid: with anchors. Every row carries its own disarmed anchor at the same dose and the same attack — five of them fresh (the 0.05 and 0.10 stream worlds, the two gated worlds, the half-half mix at 0.10), because the conditional's whole point is that an effect measured elsewhere is a premise imported, and the corpus has now been burned by imported premises in both directions. **The threat threshold**, registered at the ruler's own letter: the anchor degrades iff its final clean accuracy falls below 93% — a world that un-defended still holds its declared ruler faces no threat the instrument's ruler can see, and the instrument declines to certify a defense against nothing. The choice is deliberately conservative and deliberately borrowed: 93% is the letter condition (i) already carries, the declared ruler's own bar, and re-using it means the conditional adds no new threshold — only a new place for the old one to stand. A stricter registrar might demand the anchor fall below the twin's level minus a margin; the filing takes the ruler's letter and discloses the choice. **The verdict priority**, registered with the same ink: AWARDED iff (i) and the amended (ii) and the conditional (iii) and (iv) all hold; NO-THREAT iff there is no threat and the armed world holds its ruler; REFUSED-(k) otherwise, the first failing condition — with the one subtlety that a ruler broken in a world with no threat reads REFUSED-(i), not NO-THREAT, because a defense that cannot even hold its own world's ruler with nothing arrayed against it has failed in the plainest way the table knows. **The (ii) amendment**, registered against doc 44's boundary case: in this chassis every round writes the calibration EMA, and the repair check happens at the round's start — so a departure that happens inside the write is one round ahead of any same-round check, and the amended letter counts the next round's repair as engagement. The precedents are the corpus's own: doc 33's moving-target transient, doc 36's threat-(iii) disclosure, doc 39's en-route bracket — each a letter corrected by the mechanism it mis-timed, and this one the smallest of them: one round, one write, the repair firing on schedule into a norm the write had already moved.

**The battery.** Nineteen arms, N = 6, fourteen rounds, the one chassis with doc 47's two certificates riding passive (they change nothing — the thirteen anchored arms audit bitwise with both aboard). The stream rows: the four doses and the twin, with the 0.05 and 0.10 anchors fresh and the 0.30 anchor auditing to doc 36. The panel rows: the gated attack at 0.10 and 0.30 with both anchors fresh. The mixed rows: the capacity mix at 0.30 and the blind-heavy and half-half at 0.10, with the capacity anchors auditing to doc 44 and the half-half anchor fresh. The pre-registered predictions TC1-TC7 fixed in the script header before any run; the no-self-revision clause the code constants themselves.

### 2.2 The 0.05 refusal — the original table's own false premise

The finding the battery was not designed for and could not have avoided. The 0.05 stream row passes (i) at 95.70%, passes the amended (ii) with zero departures, passes (iv) in the en-route bracket (τ parking at 0.9067 above its tension floor 0.8812, late slope +0.0068) — and refuses on (iii), because its anchor, measured for the first time, stands at 84.63%, and the armed delta is 11.07 points against a bar of 30. Doc 39's award of this row was filed on the registry effect 0.759 — a measurement made at 0.30, applied at 0.05, disclosed in the filing as the registry's one row, and never before re-measured at its own dose. The conditional's first act is to catch the corpus's own original inheritance: **the false-premise problem did not arrive with the panel-aware attacker (doc 41) or the mixed one (doc 44); it was in the stream row's table from the day the award was invented, and the own-anchor rule finds it where it lay.** The refusal's meaning is exact and double-sided. The threat is real — the un-defended 0.05 world degrades below the ruler's letter, so the attack costs something — and the defense is real — 11 points of clean accuracy bought back, the world held at 95.7% against an anchor at 84.6% — but the effect condition's bar stands at 0.30, and 0.111 does not clear it. The instrument thereby says something it has never said before: *this defense works and does not pay enough to certify.* Whether 0.30 is the right bar against a threat that costs 11 points is the question the refusal opens — the bar was calibrated on the 0.30 world's effect (0.759) and inherited by every row since, and the conditional has now inherited the corpus's oldest habit to the one place it had not yet looked. The 0.10 row survives the same test on a true premise: anchor 0.6207, effect 0.3452, 0.045 above the bar — the thinnest true award in the corpus, and the table says so. Doc 34 filed the sub-0.05 onset hunt as a standing dial; the anchors have now bracketed it from above, and the hunt's remaining question — whether the onset sits below 0.05 or at it — is one more anchor away.

## Part 3. The reading — what the third candidate taught

Three lessons. **The conditional's first catch is the corpus's own.** The own-anchor rule was designed against doc 41's imported premises and doc 44's measured null, and its first act is to find the same disease in the original filing: the 0.05 award, standing for nine documents on an effect measured at 0.30, refusing the moment its own anchor is run. The lesson is not that doc 39 was wrong to measure the registry at one dose — the battery was priced, and the filing disclosed the inheritance — but that the disclosure was load-bearing in a way no one had priced: an effect is a property of the *pair* (attack, dose), not of the attack, and the registry row that was one measurement was also one premise short for every row it certified. The award's honest scope, as the third candidate leaves it: the stream row at 0.10 and 0.30, the capacity mix at 0.30 — three awards, all on measured premises, one of them by 0.045. **The NO-THREAT cell completes the verdict space, and its content is negative capability.** The table can now say the four things a containment instrument needs to say: awarded (the threat is real, the defense pays), refused (the threat is real, something fails), no-threat (the threat is not real, and the ruler cannot see the attack at all), and the fork (the world's shape, decoupled). The cell's deepest content is what it refuses to do: it does not certify the defense, and it does not certify the attack's absence — doc 41's capacity bound, doc 44's ecology, and doc 47's consumption signature all stand, showing gated attack, harmless mix, and consumed territory beneath a verdict that says only *the ruler cannot see it*. The gap between NO-THREAT and "safe" is the same gap doc 34's third shell measured between declared readings and clean accuracy, and the table now carries that gap as a verdict instead of a footnote. **The write-side amendment is the smallest re-specification in the corpus's history and the model for all of them.** One round, one write, a strict count of one and an amended count of zero — and the mechanism legible in the telemetry before it is legible in the verdict. The letter was not wrong about the guarantee's substance; it was wrong about the guarantee's clock, and the fix is the clock's, not the substance's. Doc 39's en-route bracket, doc 36's threat-(iii) disclosure, doc 33's moving-target transient, and now this: the corpus's letters keep failing in the same direction — toward demanding that asynchronous mechanisms synchronize to the letter's reading — and the corrections keep having the same shape: time the letter to the mechanism, disclose the correction, keep the count.

## Part 4. Threats to validity, non-claims, and deviations

**Threats.** (i) The threat threshold is the ruler's letter borrowed, and the gated anchors clear it by 1.26 and 0.93 points — one pool-noise width at the 0.30 edge; a larger blind spot or a noisier committee flips the rows to threat-and-measured, and the NO-THREAT verdicts for the gated attack are as thin as the awards they replaced were stale. (ii) The 0.10 award clears its bar by 0.045 — the thinnest true award in the corpus, disclosed as such, and the 0.05 refusal fails by 0.19 in the other direction; the region between 0.10 and 0.05 is one anchor wide and unmeasured. (iii) The anchors are fourteen-round final accuracies, N = 6; the onset curve's shape below 0.10 (0.846 at 0.05) is a single point on a steep slope, and the loop's onset could sit anywhere between 0 and 0.05 — the bracket is from above only. (iv) The amended (ii) gives every departure one free round by construction; a defense whose repair chronically fires one round late would pass the amended letter everywhere, and the boundary between "the write moved the norm" and "the repair is slow" is not drawn by the letter — the telemetry draws it (a write-side crossing has the write; a slow repair does not), but the letter does not check. (v) The conditional prices effects at final-round accuracy only; a defense that pays late or pays in trajectory rather than endpoint is under-read, and the 0.05 row's eleven points are an endpoint price of a defense whose bracket, slope, and selectivity all say more.

**Non-claims.** No claim that NO-THREAT means safe — the gated attack's capacity, the mix's ecology, and the territory's consumption are all standing measurements beneath the verdict, and the cell says only that the declared ruler cannot see the threat. No claim that the 0.30 effect bar is right — it was calibrated on the 0.30 world and inherited everywhere, and the 0.05 refusal is the first row to price it against a small threat; the bar's own dose-dependence is now an open question, filed. No claim that the amended (ii) is final — it is timed to this chassis's every-round write, and a sparser calibration schedule would need the clause re-derived. No MAINTAINS verdicts anywhere, no Column-3 marks, no occupancy claims; D3 stands. No claim about the sub-0.05 onset beyond the bracket; the hunt is one anchor from closed and remains filed.

**Deviations from pre-registration.** TC2's letter — that SP05 would re-award — is REFUTED, and the refutation is the battery's central finding: the conditional catches the original filing's dose-inherited effect, the 0.05 row refuses on its own anchor, and no re-specification is offered; the award's scope is reported as it now measures. TC6's onset letter resolved with the 0.05 anchor *below* the ruler's letter (threat) — the registered fork allowed both sides and the below side is the finding. TC5's fork resolved to the NO-THREAT branch with the amendment validated mechanically (strict 1, amended 0) and moot for the award — both branches reported as registered. One battery amendment mid-run, disclosed: the SP10 anchor was added after the stream group's first pass revealed the 0.05 anchor degrading — the own-anchor rule could not be honestly applied to the 0.10 row on the 0.30 anchor after the 0.05 row had refused on its own, and the anchor was run before the 0.10 row was graded; no armed arm was re-run, no verdict was computed before its anchor existed, and the addition is visible in the script's history. All other verdicts met or refuted as registered; the audits pass with mechanisms; the onset curve is new measurement, not re-specification.

The candidate built what the specification asked — the conditional, the cell, the amended clock — and the first thing the conditional did was turn around and audit its own inheritance: nine documents back, the award's founding table, a 0.05 row certified on a 0.30 measurement.
