## Part 0. The demand, and why it runs the other way

The audit's Paper-1 list is quoted in full in the status paragraph, and its direction is the whole difficulty. The corpus's comparison functors run from the models into the bridge: F_info carries the information model's processes to their Hankel objects, F_phys carries the quantum model's total category to the Born pairings, and Theorem K proved that no functor carrying enough structure can carry the quantum fibers into ChuCost at all. The audit's demand is the reverse: a functor from the Chu side down to Paper 1's base poset, with the channel categories as fibers and the Grothendieck construction as the total — a projection, in the ordinary sense of a functor that throws away structure, which is why Theorem K does not forbid it and why doc 46 ledgered it open rather than pretending it paid itself. The demand's hidden assumption — visible the moment one tries to build it — is that the projection lives in the Chu construction already, that the base poset and the fibers are in there somewhere waiting to be projected out. They are not, and proving that is this document's second theorem; building what they actually are is its first.

## Part 1. The staged category, and the shadow it wears

**Definition 57 (the staged Born category Chu_Σ) [Derived here; the objects are doc 42's, the composition is doc 38's].** Fix the causal poset (P, ≼), the environment assignment E (doc 38, Definition 39: strictly monoidal, E_{pp} = ℂ), and write Born(B) = (D(B), E(B), r_B), r_B(ρ, e) = 1 − tr(eρ), for the Born pairing of a finite-dimensional system B (doc 42, Definition 48). The *staged Born category* Chu_Σ has: **objects**, the pairs (p, Born(B)) with p ∈ P and B a system — a ChuCost object with a stage grade; **morphisms** (p, Born(B)) → (q, Born(C)), the channels Φ ∈ Q(B ⊗ E_{pq}, C) when p ≼ q (none otherwise) — Paper 1's homs, doc 38's Definition 43 verbatim; **composition**, the total category's: Ψ ∘ (Φ ⊗ id_{E_{qr}}), associativity inherited from Q and the assignment's strict monoidality; **identities**, the trivial channels at E_{pp} = ℂ. The construction is the total category 𝓔 with the Born pairing displayed at every object — the same category, one forgetful choice undone — and the disclosure is the demand's first bullet answered honestly: *a precise Chu category* here means a category whose objects are ChuCost's and whose homs are not, because (Part 2) no category with ChuCost's homs can pay the audit's fourth bullet. The enrichment: each hom is a spectrahedron and composition is bilinear, so Chu_Σ is Con-enriched, doc 38's enrichment unchanged.

**Definition 58 (the shadow functor) [Derived here].** The *shadow* Σ: Chu_Σ → ChuCost sends the object (p, Born(B)) to Born(B) — the stage discarded at the source — and the morphism Φ: (p, B) → (q, C) to its prepared pair (f_Φ, g_Φ), f_Φ(ρ) = Φ(ρ ⊗ σ^{pq}), g_Φ(e) = [ρ ↦ tr(e Φ(ρ ⊗ σ^{pq}))], for σ the preparation section of doc 42's Definition 48 (a hypothesis: sections exist on chains, the running example, and the general-poset coherence remains doc 42's filed cell, inherited here as the shadow's own coherence condition). The shadow forgets exactly two things, in exactly the order the bridge's theorems priced: **the stage** — Σ is independent of p, and (p, Born(B)) and (q, Born(B)) wear the same shadow for every p and q — and **the off-slice behavior** — the prepared pair records the channel's action on the states the section prepares, and Theorem J counted what that costs: the fiber of Φ ↦ (f_Φ, g_Φ) has positive affine dimension whenever the interval carries k_{pq} ≥ 2, the unprepared degrees of freedom that Proposition 44 named irreversible and Lemma 41 priced.

## Part 2. Theorem R — the projection, paid bullet by bullet

**Theorem R (the stage projection is Paper 1's opfibration, on the nose) [Derived here; the fibration structure is doc 38's Prop 44, inherited].** The functor Π: Chu_Σ → P, (p, Born(B)) ↦ p, (Φ: (p,B) → (q,C)) ↦ (p ≼ q), is a Con-enriched opfibration, and its structure is Paper 1's, strictly: (i) *the fibers* — the subcategory Π⁻¹(p), the audit's bullet three, is the category of p-graded Born pairings and channels Φ ∈ Q(B ⊗ E_{pp}, C) = Q(B ⊗ ℂ, C) ≅ Q(B, C), the unitors of the assignment identifying it with Q canonically — **the fiber is Chan_p, bullet four paid on the nose up to the unit isomorphisms, and paid because Chu_Σ's homs are the channels; Proposition 59 below records what would have happened to this bullet on inherited homs.** (ii) *The cocartesian lifts* — the lift of p ≼ q at (p, Born(B)) is the identity channel id: B ⊗ E_{pq} → B ⊗ E_{pq}, typed (p, Born(B)) → (q, Born(B ⊗ E_{pq})); the factorization condition is Proposition 44(i) verbatim (a morphism out of (p, Born(B)) already is a morphism out of (q, Born(B ⊗ E_{pq})) under the interval algebra, and the only factorization through the identity is the trivial one), forward transport free, the identity channel costing nothing. (iii) *The reindexing agreement, bullet five* — post-composition with the lift is the transport functor Π⁻¹(p) → Π⁻¹(q): on objects Born(B) ↦ Born(B ⊗ E_{pq}), on morphisms Φ ↦ id ∘ (Φ ⊗ id_{E_{pq}}) = Φ ⊗ id_{E_{pq}} — **the carriage T_{pq} exactly, strictly, no coherence cells and no section consulted: the transport that Paper 1 filed and the audit demanded be matched is matched because it is the same functor.** (iv) *The Grothendieck agreement, bullet six* — Chu_Σ is the (covariant) Grothendieck construction of the indexed category p ↦ Q, (p ≼ q) ↦ T_{pq}: doc 38's Definition 43 built the total category as exactly this construction, the isomorphism Chu_Σ ≅ 𝓔 ≅ ∫ T is the identity on objects and homs, and the payment is inheritance, disclosed as such — the demand asked for a proof that the total category is equivalent to the Grothendieck construction, and the corpus's answer is that it has been one since Phase 3; what the reverse projection adds is not the fibration but the shadow, the Chu-side structure the file wanted the fibration to project. ∎ *Proof.* (i) is the definition of the homs plus the unitors; (ii) is Prop 44(i) with the grading displayed; (iii) is the composite law's strictness; (iv) is doc 38's Definition 43. The Con-enrichment is trivial at the base (P is a poset) and inherited on the fibers. ∎

**Proposition 59 (the fibers' honesty has a price, and the price is Theorem J's) [Derived here].** The audit's bullet four cannot be paid on the Chu side proper: no subcategory D ⊆ ChuCost containing the Born pairings of two stages' systems has fibers equivalent to Chan_p under any projection D → P built from Chu structure. *Proof.* The candidates for the fiber's homs inside ChuCost are the ChuCost homs between the graded objects — but the graded objects are not distinct in ChuCost (the grading is not Chu structure; this is Theorem S's content), and even granting a grading, the ChuCost homs between Born(B) and Born(C) are the equality-core maps, of which the channels' shadows are the completely positive prepared slice: the state-action Φ ↦ f_Φ has positive-dimensional fibers at k_{pq} ≥ 2 by Theorem J's count ((ak)²(b²−1) > a²b²), so the Chu-inherited hom-category is a strict quotient of Q(B ⊗ E_{pq}, C) — the prepared shadows — and no quotient with positive-dimensional fibers is equivalent to the thing it quotients. The channel structure must be imported, and Chu_Σ imports it by fiat of its hom- definition; the import is the staged category's whole content, and the intercepted degrees of freedom that force it are the same ones that made F_phys unfaithful (Theorem J), made the carriage adjointless (Theorem F), and made the settlement's arithmetic (Theorem P) — the intercept's fourth job, reprised as the projection's price. ∎

## Part 3. Theorem S — the stage is not intrinsic, and the file's sentence read backwards

**Theorem S (the projection does not descend along the shadow) [Derived here].** There is no functor Γ: D → P, defined on any subcategory D ⊆ ChuCost containing the shadow's image, with Γ ∘ Σ = Π. Moreover the stage is recoverable from neither side of the Chu structure: not from the objects (the shadow is constant in p), and not from the morphisms (a ChuCost morphism between Born pairings that is a prepared pair for some channel at some interval is a prepared pair for a positive-dimensional family of channels, and for channels at many intervals, the section's states being chosen per interval and the pair's values not separating them). *Proof.* The non-descent is one line: Σ(p, Born(B)) = Born(B) = Σ(q, Born(B)), so Γ(Born(B)) would equal both p and q. The morphism-side closure: the prepared-pair map's fibers are positive-dimensional at k ≥ 2 (Theorem J), and the same equality f_Φ = f_Ψ arises for channels of different intervals whenever the prepared actions coincide — the shadow's data is the function ρ ↦ Φ(ρ ⊗ σ^{pq}), which determines neither Φ off the σ-slice nor the interval that chose σ. The section is Definition 48's hypothesis, not ChuCost structure: ChuCost's homs do not know which σ organized them. ∎ **The reading.** The bridging file's claim — the base poset and the fibers *are* the external projection of the Chu construction — has its direction backwards, and the backwards direction is now a theorem. The Chu construction cannot see the stage: its objects cannot carry it (Theorem S, objects), its morphisms cannot carry it (Theorem S, morphisms; Proposition 59), and the structure that can carry it — the grading — is exactly the added data that turns the Grothendieck construction into Chu_Σ. What the file took for a projection waiting to be defined is a projection waiting to be *fed*: the base poset comes in from the physics (the causal order, the environment assignment — Paper 1's own data, doc 38's Definitions 38-39), the shadow goes down from the bridge (Definition 58), and the reverse projection is their composite arrangement — the total category wearing both. In the file's vocabulary: the external projection is not the Chu construction's shadow; **the Chu construction is the external projection's shadow** — the Born pairings are what Paper 1's total category looks like from inside a self-dual bookkeeping, and the look forgets precisely the fibration the file thought it would find.

**Proposition 60 ("the cocartesian lifts are the left adjoint of the duality" — settled, every reading) [Derived here].** (i) The Chu duality ⊥: ChuCost → ChuCost is an involution (an equivalence ChuCost^op ≅ ChuCost squaring to isomorphism on the equality core), not an adjunction, and "the left adjoint of the duality" has no referent in the category theory as stated — the audit's own remark, restated here because the settlement of the file's sentence needs it on the record. (ii) What the cocartesian lifts' shadows are: Π's lift of p ≼ q at (p, Born(B)) maps under Σ to the preparation pair (ρ ↦ ρ ⊗ σ^{pq}, e ↦ e(− ⊗ σ^{pq})) — Prop 52's correspondence, the Con-natural section s: Id ⇒ T_{pq} of the discard δ: T_{pq} ⇒ Id: a natural transformation, with a natural retraction partner in the discard, and no adjoint anywhere in the pairing. (iii) The reading that would rescue the sentence by adjacency — that the *carriage* has the left adjoint the duality allegedly supplies — fails by the same arithmetic that killed the right: a Con-enrichned left adjoint L ⊣ T_E would give affine bijections Q(L A, B) ≅ Q(A, B ⊗ E), and at B = M_1 the left side is a single point (every channel into ℂ is the trace, affine dimension l²(1² − 1) = 0) while the right side is Q(A, M_{d_E}), a convex body of affine dimension a²(d_E² − 1) > 0 for d_E ≥ 2 — dead; alive only at d_E = 1, the degenerate edge where the section, the discard, and both would-be adjoints all collapse into the identity that Theorem L already owns. The sentence's true core is (ii): the lifts map to the preparation, the forward structure is preserved as structure, and the file's intuition — that something adjoint-shaped lives in the lifts — is honored by the section-discard pairing, which is a natural transformation and its mate, not an adjunction. ∎

## Part 6. Coda

The audit's last unpaid row asked for a functor that runs the one direction the corpus never built, and the build's first discovery was why the corpus never needed to: the fibration was never missing — Phase 3 built the total category as the Grothendieck construction before the bridge had a name, and the reverse projection's six bullets pay out as inheritance, strictness, and one disclosed import. The second discovery is the one that closes the ledger's oldest sentence. The bridging file believed the base poset lived inside the Chu construction, waiting to be projected out; the truth is the mirror: the Chu construction lives inside the projection, as the shadow Paper 1's total category casts when it is read through a self-dual bookkeeping — and the shadow, by the corpus's longest theorem chain, cannot carry what it forgets: not the stage, not the unprepared channels, not the closure, not the adjoints. Everything the file wanted the Chu construction to contain — the causal order, the environment's growth, the irreversibility — is real, is theorem-grade, and is on the other side of the shadow, in the structure the shadow descends from. The audit demanded the projection be built, and it is built, and its direction is the document's last lesson: the corpus spent six phases building functors up from the models to the bridge, and the seventh motion, when it finally came, was not a bigger bridge but a return to the ground — where the base poset, the fibers, and the carriage were sitting all along, wearing no bridge at all, waiting for the shadow that would make them visible from above. The audit's ledger is now paid on every row the corpus's functors reach; what remains open is open by theorem, not by omission; and the gap between self-reference and self-experience is exactly as wide at the bottom of this document as at the top of the first.
